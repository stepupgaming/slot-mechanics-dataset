## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## Reel Area
The main reel setup is 2-3-4-4-4-4 rows across 6 reels, with 4 locked Enhancer Cells placed at the top of the last 4 reels. When a Scatter lands, it opens the locked Enhancer Cell on it’s reel.

Regular and Scatter symbols appear in the main reel area, while special symbols only appear in the locked positions.

## Bonus Symbols
A Bonus symbol can land on reel 3, 4, 5 and 6. When Bonus symbols land, they transform into Wilds. Landing 2 Bonus symbols in base game, xBoost 1 or 2 will award a respin with opened Enhancer Cells, where Bonus landed previously. Any additional Bonus symbols landing during respin will award an extra spin. Isolation/Execution Spins cannot be triggered during respin.

3 or 4 Bonus symbols in base game will trigger the Bonus modes: Isolation Spins or Execution Spins respectively.

When Isolation or Execution Spins are triggered, Bonus symbols will not transform into Wilds.

## Enhancer Cells
When triggered to open they may reveal xSplit® Reel, xSplit® Row, xWays®, Sticky Wild, Wild Reel and Doubled Inmate symbols.

- xSplit® Reel: It splits all symbols on its reel and doubles their symbol sizes.

- xSplit® Row: Lands on a random position on it’s reel and splits all symbols in the same row, doubling their size.

- xWays®: It randomly lands on its reel and transforms into a normal paying symbol. It will reveal 2-4 symbols of the same kind and increase the number of win ways. The xWays® symbol is always sticky. If more than one xWays® symbol lands on the reels, then all Ways® symbols will reveal the same symbol. xWays® may appear only during Isolation, Execution Spins and Unbarred Spin.

- Doubled Inmate: It appears as a random Inmate paying symbol and doubles the symbol size of all its instances on the main reel area.

- Sticky Wild: It randomly transforms a symbol from reel 2-6 on the main reel area into a Wild. If respin, Isolation or Execution Spins is triggered, Wild will remain sticky on its position.

- Wild Reel: Transform all symbols on its reel into Wild symbols.

## Isolation Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols in base game will trigger 7 Isolation Spins. Enhancer Cells are unlocked on reels where Bonus symbols lands and 1-3 xWays® symbols are randomly awarded on those reels. These xWays® symbols will remain sticky throughout the bonus mode.

A locked Enhancer Cell unlocks when a Scatter lands on its reel, upgrades the bonus mode to Execution Spins and awards +3 spins.

Landing a Bonus symbol on open reel awards +1 spin.

## Execution Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols in base game will trigger 10 Execution Spins. All Enhancer Cells are unlocked and 1-4 xWays® Symbols are randomly awarded which remain sticky throughout the bonus mode. 

Landing any additional Bonus symbols during Execution Spins will awards +1 spin.

## xBoost 1 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2.5 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on reel 3 or 4.

3 times more likely to hit Isolation/Execution Spins.

## xBoost 2 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 20 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 2 Scatter symbols on either reel (3 or 4) and reel (5 or 6). A respin is guaranteed.

27 times more likely to hit Isolation/Execution Spins.

## Unbarred Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 30 times the base bet one spin is awarded with all Enhancer Cells unlocked. 

Any Scatter landing will award +1 spin.

## Max Win
The max payout of the game is 44,444 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 44,444 times the base bet is awarded.
