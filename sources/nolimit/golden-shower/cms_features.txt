## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Wild Generation & Wild Burst
Symbols on the reel area matching the winning-Cluster symbols, but not taking part in the winnings, convert to Wild. A Wild symbol substitutes for any other paying symbol. If there is no win, and Wild symbols are still present on the reel, the Wild will destroy itself and the adjacent top, bottom, left, and right positions. A multiplier of 2x is added on each position where a symbol is removed.

## Special Symbols
Steam 
• Activate as soon as it lands. 
• Randomly chooses a symbol and converts all occurrences of that symbol and itself to a higher paying one. 

Floater 
• Activates when there is no win and no Wilds. 
• Randomly chooses a symbol and remove all other symbols except Bonus and Drain, and upgrades multiplier on removed positions. 
• A multiplier of 2x is added on each position where a symbol is removed. If a position multiplier already existed there its multiplier value gets doubled. 

Drain 
• Activates when there is no win, no Wilds and no Floater. 
• Removes itself and all symbols except Bonus and upgrades multiplier randomly on all removed positions.

## Bonus
Landing 3, 4 or 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 6, 8 or 10 Gold Spins respectively. 

## 6 Gold Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 6 Gold Spins. 

Position multipliers are kept during Free spins. Landing an Extra +1 Bonus symbol awards +1 spin. 

• Position Multipliers are persistent throughout the feature. 
• Steam Feature selects the two highest-occurring symbols on the reels and converts the lower-paying symbol into the higher-paying one. If multiple symbols share the highest occurrence, the two highest-paying symbols among them are selected.

## 8 Gold Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 8 Gold Spins. 

Works the same as the 6 Gold Spins, with the addition of the Floater Feature, which always selects the highest-paying symbol with the highest occurrence on the reel area.

## 10 Gold Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 10 Gold Spins. 

Works the same as the 8 Gold Spins, with the added Drain Feature, where Drain always upgrades its position multiplier to 999x.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is 5 times more likely to trigger Gold Spins.

## Steamy Spin x16 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 20 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be 16x.

## Steamier Spin x64 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 80 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be 64x.

## Steamiest Spin x128 x999 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 1969 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be 128x. Guaranteed Drain symbol and when triggered it updates its position to 999x.

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the position multipliers of the previous spin. The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the position multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented. Bonus symbols will not land during Extra Spin.

## Luke Warm

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will
