## Scatter Wins
Payouts are awarded when 7 or more symbols of same kind land on the reel area. 

Winning symbols are removed and will trigger a drop on the reel allowing new symbols to drop in.

## Multiplier Blocks
Each reel position has its own multiplier that applies to the symbol’s win. The multiplier increases only when the position is part of a win, is affected by Cell Search, or by xSplit®. 

Each increase doubles the multiplier, up to a maximum of 128x in the base game.

## Enhancer Cells
On the top and bottom of each reel there is locked Enhancer Cell positions. Each position spins independently of the main reel area. 

When unlocked, they reveal a Prisoner symbol or any feature symbol except Bonus.

## Cake Buster
• Unlocks a locked reel position and reveals a Wild symbol.

• If no locked reel positions remain, a random reel position is turned into a Wild.


## xWays®
• Transforms into a Prisoner symbol and reveals 2-4 symbols of the same kind. 

• If more than one Ways symbol, then all Ways symbols will reveal the same symbol.

## xSplit®
Doubles all Multiplier Block values on its reel and converts itself into a Prisoner symbol.

## Yard Dealer
Randomly selects an object symbol on the reels and converts it into a higher-paying object symbol. It then converts itself into a Prisoner symbol.

## Cell Search
• Gets activated when there is no win. 

• Collects the lowest-paying object symbol, adding a multiplier to itself for each collected symbol, and then converts into a Wild. 

• The multiplier values on the collected positions are doubled.

## Guard Tower Bonus
The Bonus symbol can land on reels 2-5. 

Landing any Bonus symbol in the main game unlocks the Enhancer Cells on its respective reel. 

Landing 3 Bonus symbols trigger Sewer Escape Spins. 

Landing 4 Bonus symbols trigger Manhunt Spins.

## Sewer Escape Spins
Before Sewer Escape Spins start, the Bonus Setup Mode sets the number of spins. Each +1 symbol that lands adds one spin. All locked reels get a random sticky multiplier of 2x, 4x, 8x, or 16x. 

All unlocked Enhancer states carry over from the main game. 

Multiplier Block values do not reset between spins and are capped at 128x. 

Unlocked reel positions stay unlocked between spins. 2, 3 and 4 Bonus symbols award +2, +3 and +4 extra spins respectively.

## Manhunt Spins
In addition to the features of Sewer Escape Spins, Multiplier Block values are capped at 512x. 

Corner Enhancer Cells unlock once all locked positions on the reels in their respective corners are unlocked.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 1 Bonus Symbol.

## 2x Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 20 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed minimum of 2 Bonus symbols.

## 2x Bonus + Open x64 Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 100 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 2 Bonus symbols and an initial multiplier value of x64 on all unlocked reel positions.

## 2x Bonus + All x128 Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2,000 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 2 Bonus symbols and an initial multiplier value of x128 on all reel positions.

## Jail Break
The max payout of the game is 46,532 times the base bet. When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 46,532 times the base bet is awarded.
