## Beam ‘Em Up!
Randomly activated on any spin, and will beam out all the non-organic symbols as the spaceship needs some extra fuel. Only high paying symbols and wilds will land on these spins!

## Clone Attack!
This feature is randomly triggered on any spin. It will beam back one of the farm animals, but in huge stacks! Volatile feature that will reward you handsomely or not at all...

## Abduction Spins
Triggered by 3 or more Scatters. Awards 7, 9 or 12 spins and start a Free Spin mode where all winning symbols will be abducted from the reels. Wilds will always stay on the reels, and each abducted Scatter awards one extra spin each. If the Aliens abduct all symbols, you’ll be left with only Wilds on the spins!
