TRACTOR BEAM
Something strange is going on in the fields
in the Midwest - the farm animals isn’t super
psyched about the weird activities that’s
happening both night and day....

The invisible Tractor Beam keeps bugging the farm
animals, beaming them up and down as it pleases. The
Aliens dictates the lives of the poor animals, abducting
them or any other material as they please.

Tractor Beam is a video slot that boasts three different
features, all controlled by the cloaked Alien spaceship -
Beam ‘Em Up!, Clone Attack! and Abduction Spins.

Game Features
Tractor Beam is a high-volatile slot with a
high hit frequency with a win in about 1 in
3 spins. The pay out lies within the Tractor
Beam features - you want to be kind to the
Aliens, they come in peace...

Beam ‘Em Up!
Randomly activated on any spin, and will beam out all the
non-organic symbols as the spaceship needs some extra
fuel. Only high paying symbols and wilds will land on these
spins!

Clone Attack!
This feature is randomly triggered on any spin. It will beam
back one of the farm animals, but in huge stacks! Volatile
feature that will reward you handsomely or not at all...
