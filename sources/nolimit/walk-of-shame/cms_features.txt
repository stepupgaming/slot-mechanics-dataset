## Scatters
There are two types of Scatters, Invitation and Mr. E Scatter symbols.

Freespins can be activated by combinations of Invitation and Mr E Scatter symbols.

- 3 Invitation Scatters = 10 spins of "One More!!!"
- 4 Invitation Scatters = 15 spins of "One More!!!"
- 3 invitation Scatters and 1 Mr. E Scatter = 10 spins of "All Nighter"
- 4 Invitation Scatters and 1 Mr. E Scatter = 15 spins of "All Nighter"

When a Mr. E Scatter lands in the base game it will transform into an xSplit® and either split the row or the reel. The xSplit® cannot split other Scatter symbols. 

## Loaded Wild
High paying symbols that land and cover the entire reel will convert the stack of high paying symbols into a Loaded Wild.

Loaded Wilds can only happen on reel 2-5.

All Loaded Wilds get a x4 multiplier.

## Walk of Shame
Landing a Mr. E and a Loaded Wild on reel 3, 4 and 5 will trigger Walk of Shame.

In Walk of Shame, each Loaded Wild moves one step left for the next spin. The feature ends when a Loaded Wild is present on reel 2.

##  Bonus Modes : 'One More!!!' and 'All Nighter' 
During "One More!!!", Each Loaded Wild that lands will instantly add x1 to the win multiplier.

Landing a Mr. E Scatter symbol activates "All Nighter" and awards +3 spins.

- 1 Invitation Scatter = +1 spin
- 2 Invitation Scatters = +3 spins
- 3 Invitation Scatters = +5 spins
- 4 Invitation Scatters = +7 spins

During "All Nighter", Each Loaded Wild that lands will instantly add x1 to the win multiplier and may trigger Walk of Shame.

Mr. E Scatter symbol will remain and act as an xSplit® throughout the "All Nighter" round.

- 1 Invitation Scatter = +1 spin
- 2 Invitation Scatters = +3 spins
- 3 Invitation Scatters = +5 spins
- 4 Invitation Scatters = +7 spins 

## Happily ever after
The max payout of the game is 9,822 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 9,822 times the base bet is awarded.
