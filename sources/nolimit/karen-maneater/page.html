Base Game Mechanics and Features

The base game action relies on expanding reels and infectious symbol modifiers. Whenever a Raccoon scatter symbol lands on the middle four reels, it forces all reels to its right to expand by one position. Landing just one or two scatters converts them into the proprietary xWays symbol, which reveals multiple matching symbols to increase your win ways. The powerful Infectious xWays mechanic can land on the first reel to reveal massive symbol sizes and infect the rest of the reel to match. You will also find an Infectious Wild on the last reel that nudges downward to build a multiplier for every symbol in its column. Players wanting a shortcut can activate the xBet option to guarantee a scatter on the second reel for an increased cost.

Bonus Rounds and Free Spins

Securing three or four scatters triggers the main feature where the real feast begins. Three scatters award the Value Bonus Meal with seven spins and three sticky xWays symbols. Landing four scatters unlocks the Deluxe Bonus Meal, giving you ten spins and four sticky xWays symbols. During these rounds, the sticky modifier symbols grow in size after every winning spin, maxing out at an incredible forty two symbols per position. Game xMechanics

xWays®

xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins. xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

xBet®

At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

Infectious xWays®

Any xWays symbols that appear will reveal the same symbol, and if the symbol revealed is already visible on the reels, each in view will be ‘infected’ and will expand as if they are xWays – producing additional ways to win.
