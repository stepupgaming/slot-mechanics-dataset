Game Features
Scatter
Raccoon Scatter symbol can only land on the middle four
reels. Landing a Scatter will allow all reels to the right to
expand to one extra position. Landing 1/2 Scatter(s) will
convert to an xWays symbol. Landing 3/4 will trigger Bonus
Meal.

Infectious xWays
These can only land on middle 4 reels. xWays symbols will
transform into a regular paying symbol. A symbol of 2-4 in
size will be revealed and increase the number of win ways.
Infectious xWays symbol can only land on the first reel and
will reveal a symbol of 2-7 in size. It will turn all symbols
on that reel to become 2-7 in size. xWays and Infectious
xWays symbol can only land in the main game and reveal
the same symbol.
