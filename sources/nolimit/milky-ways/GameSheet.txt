MILKY WAYS
“In space, no one can hear you scream”
is a classic tagline from Ridley Scott’s
masterpiece “Alien” from 1979. Milky Ways
and its bonus mode will however make
you scream when you hit it, and we’re sure
someone will hear your excitement...

With a 5x3 reel area with 243 ways that expands to 5x5
and 3125 ways in the bonus mode. Packed together with
multiplying Solar Wilds and Fusion Spins on all wins, you
will see wins and excitement in abundance.

Milky Ways is available with a dual RTP setup at a range of
96.14% and 94.24% (DX1).

Game Features
Respin madness, multiplier and locked-in
winning symbols - this can’t go wrong, right?

Solar Wild
Solar Wilds reveals a multiplier when it lands, ranging from
x1 to x3. Solar Wilds multiplies each other, so landing
several is very rewarding.

Milky Ways Spins
Triggered very often - 1 in 66 spins -and landing 3 Milky
Ways scatters activates the Milky Ways Spins. The reel area
will expand from 5x3 to 5x5 and increase the win ways up
to 3125. Each spin will always guarantee two Wild land on
the middle three reels, and any win will of trigger Fusion
Spins.

Fusion Spins
Whenever you get a win in the bonus mode, Fusion Spins
is triggered. All winning symbols locks in place, and a
respin is rewarded until no more winning symbols lands.        Game Information
