## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xHole™
xHole™ will activate when there are no winning combinations, Bombs or Multiplier Increasers.

All symbols from the main reels are sucked in and are spit out to random positions with next multiplier value. xHole™ then transforms itself into a Wild symbol that also gets the next multiplier value.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Wilds
The regular Wild symbol that can land on any reel.

## Bomb
All Bombs will explode when there are no winning combinations. An exploding Bomb removes all regular paying symbols on its reel and row, allowing new symbols to fall in.

Scatter and Wild symbols are unaffected by the explosion.

Bomb explosion unlocks Max Win symbol on its reel.

## Multiplier Increaser
Multiplier Increaser symbols trigger when they land and add its value to the next multiplier value. Once triggered, the Multiplier Increaser symbol gets removed.

Multiplier values of the Multiplier Increaser symbol: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x and 10x.

## Golden Multiplier Increaser
Golden Multiplier Increaser symbols trigger when they land and doubles the value of the next multiplier.

## Flee Spins
3, 4 or 5 Scatter symbols trigger 6, 9 or 12 Flee Spins respectively.

Progress of the next multiplier value is kept during spins and the Max Win symbol stays unlocked for the rest of the feature once it is unlocked.

## Scatter (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3.30 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Scatter symbol on reel 2.

## Extra Spins (Nolimit Booster)
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the multiplier state of the symbols of the previous spin.

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the next multiplier value. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the total win, then the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented. Scatter symbols will not land during Extra Spin.

## xHole™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 6 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed an xHole™ symbol on reel 2.

## Print Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 11 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with a 30x multiplier on each paying symbol.

## Printier Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 90 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with a 268x multiplier on each paying symbol.

## Printiest Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 270 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with a 911x multiplier on each paying symbol.

## God Mode
Chase max win by unlocking all 6 Max Win symbols in the bottom row with Bombs at a cost of 911 times the base bet.

## Max Win
The max payout of the game is 5,051 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 5,051 times the base bet is awarded.

Max win can also be achieved by unlocking all 6 Max Win
symbols in the bottom row.
