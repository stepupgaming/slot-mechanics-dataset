## Dead Patient
Whenever you land two Dead Patient symbols you’ve got\nthe chance to win a massive random multiplier - ranging\nfrom x5 to x9999 on a patient symbol.

## Enhancer Cells
Boost the chance of wins whenever 4 or 6 fire frames\nappears. xWays, xNudge, xSplit along with other boosters.

## Mental Transform
Symbol transform feature whenever the Spider symbol\nlands on the reels.

## Autopsy Freespins
Activated by 3 Scorpion symbols and will trigger a bonus\nmode with sticky Fire Frames. May be upgraded to\nLobotoy or Mental Freespins.

## Lobotomy Freespins
Activated by 3 Scorpions plus a Spider symbol. An\nupgraded version of Autopsy, where the Spider symbol is\nsticky and award Mental Transform for each spin. Included\nDead Multiplier that is transferred to a Patient symbol to\ntrigger some immense wins. May be upgraded to Mental\nFreespins.

## !!!This is mental!!!
Max payout of the game is set to 66.666x times the base\nbet. Trigger this, and the game round will end.

## Mental Freespins
This is where things go really mental. Triggered by 3\nScorpion symbols and 2 spider symbols. Spider symbols\nare sticky and Dead Multiplier never decrease.

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy Functionality
Buy your way straight into one of the different bonus\nfeatures, ranging from 80x to 1000x the base bet.
