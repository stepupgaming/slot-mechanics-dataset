## Win Respins
Wins awards respin on winning positions. Wins awards respin on winning positions.

## Sticky Wilds
Wild will never respin during Win Respins. Wilds may only appear on reel 2, 3, 4 and 5. Get Wilds along a betline for massive hits!

## Wild Raider
Any given spin in main game the Wild Raider feature may trigger. When feature is triggered guaranteed Wilds will be placed on the middle three reels.\nUp to 9 Wilds can appear during the feature!

## Curse of Nefertiti
Any given spin in main game the Curse of Nefertiti feature may trigger. All positions on the center three reels will sync and reveal the same symbol, all symbols except Bonus may appear on the middle reels during this feature.\nLanding the same symbol on first reel for potential big wins!\n

## Raider Spins
3 or more scatters awards Raider Spins! Each Raider spin will trigger Wild Raider feature and add at least one Wild. During Raider spins 20 additional win lines are added for a total of 40 win lines.\nDuring the Raider Spins every win will increase the multiplier by one and Win Respins feature is also active during the feature. There is no limit to the win multiplier!
