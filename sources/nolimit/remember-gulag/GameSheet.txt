Propaganda Bet
Increase the base bet by 20% to go after the Gulag Spins.
Buttons are there to be pressed - expect a more harsh,
but less rewarding base game, with a higher chance
of triggering Gulag Spins. Also guranteed that reel 5 is
unlocked during Propaganda Bet.

xWays
During the main game, the xWays® symbol can appear on
reels 1-2, and on reels 5-6 during the Gulag Spins bonus
feature. The properties of the xWays symbol range from
revealing regular symbols to doubling multiplier values.
