## Enhancer Cells
On the top and bottom of each reel, there are locked Enhancer Cell positions.

When triggered to open, they may reveal: 1 of the 5 top paying symbols, Wild, Razor Split or xWays® symbol.

Razor Split symbol will split all symbols on the regular positions on that reel to double position. If the Razor Split lands on top and bottom Enhancer Cells on the same reel it will split them twice. If the Razor Split symbol splits the Bonus symbol on its reel then the Bonus symbol transforms into Wilds and splits its row respectively.

All xWays® symbols will reveal the same regular paying symbols and 5 high stacked. When xWays® appears on both active Enhancer Cells on a reel, the entire reel will be covered by 10 single Wilds.

## Split Wild
In the main game, Split Wild can land on any reel.
The Split Wild will split all the regular symbols on the same reel, the Split Wild will not split itself.

## Bonus Scatter
The Bonus symbol can land on any reel. Landing 1 or 2 Bonus symbol in the main game will turn it to Wild and open the Enhancer Cells on its reel.

Landing 3 or more Bonuses will trigger Green Mile Spins.

## Green Mile Spins
3, 4 or 5 Bonus symbols will trigger Green Mile Spins with 1, 2 or 3 starting Jumping Wilds.

Before Green Mile Spins start, players will get the option to draw an additional Jumping Wild or collect the Jumping Wilds that are currently awarded. Maximum number of Jumping Wilds is 5.

When triggering Green Mile Spins, the player can start with the currently awarded Jumping Wilds or risk losing 5 spins for the chance of an extra Jumping Wild, up to a maximum of 5 Jumping Wilds. The number of Jumping Wilds determines the number of reels with active Enhancer Cells:

• 1 Jumping Wild: 3 reels with active Enhancer Cells.

• 2 Jumping Wild: 4 reels with active Enhancer Cells.

• 3, 4 or 5 Jumping Wild: 5 reels with active Enhancer Cells.

The 4th and 5th awarded Jumping Wild will start with a x2 multiplier on it. When reaching 5 Jumping Wilds the player is then given the choice to risk loosing 5 spins or getting the Max Win. 

When Green Mile Spins start, the reels with active Enhancer Cells are randomly drawn and remain persistent throughout the entire feature.

Landing Bonus symbols on the reel with closed Enhancer Cells will open the Enhancer Cells on its reel and transform the Bonus symbol into Jumping Wilds.

Landing 2-5 Bonus symbols will award 3-6 extra spins respectively.

## Jumping Wilds
Jumping Wilds will jump to a random regular reel position for each spin.

When a Jumping Wild lands on the reel with the Razor Split enhancer it will double its multiplier and keep it for the rest of the Green Mile Spins and also double all positions on the same row. Jumping Wilds on the rows affected by a Split Jumping Wild will also double its multiplier and keep it for the rest of the Green Mile Spins. Jumping Wild multiplier counts as the number of Wilds on that position.

## No Escape
The max payout of the game is 200,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the Green Mile Spins will end and 200,000 times the base bet is awarded.
