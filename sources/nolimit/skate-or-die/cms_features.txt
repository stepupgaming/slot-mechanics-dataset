## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## xZone®
Landing xZone™ applies +1 multiplier value on every regular paying symbol and Wild adjacent to it; vertically, horizontally and diagonally. Multiple xZone™’s can land on the same row but not on the same reel

## xCrash™
WIN up to the Max Win with xCrash™! Or lose it all.

The goal is to cash out before the multiplier crashes, keeping your multiplied bet. The longer you wait, the higher the potential winnings and the greater the risk of losing it all.

## Extra Sauce Row
The Extra Sauce Row at the top of the reel area consists of 5 potential multipliers with values of 2, 3, 4, 10 and no multiplier.

Multipliers connect with xNudge® and/or xZone™ symbols in the corresponding reels beneath them.

The Extra Sauce Row updates on each spin in base game and bonus modes.

## xZone™ Wild
If an xZone™ Wild symbol lands and there is an active multiplier in the Extra Sauce Row above its reel, then the xZone™ applies its top row multiplier on every regular paying symbol adjacent to it - vertically, horizontally and diagonally.

The xZone™ multiplier affects the xNudge® Wild and xNudge® Wild Supreme when it starts to nudge. 

Overlapping multipliers are summed together.

## xNudge® Wild
The xNudge® Wild can land on any reel. The xNudge® Wild will always nudge it until fully visible. Each nudge increases the win multiplier by 1.

Multiple xNudge® Wilds traversing the win will add their
multipliers to the total xNudge® multiplier.
The xNudge® Wild symbol and its multipliers are not
affected by xZone™ multipliers spreading to the reel that
the xNudge® has landed on, but xNudge® picks up any
multipliers that it nudges across that have been affected by
the xZone™ multipliers.
The multiplier on the xNudge® Wild increases by the
corresponding multiplier, if any, above its reel on the Extra
Sauce Row.

## xNudge® Wild Supreme
The xNudge® Wild Supreme only exists in the Boneyard Spins bonus game. In addition to xNudge® Wild features, each xNudge® Wild Supreme also adds up all the available multipliers from Extra Sauce Row to its respective multiplier.

The xNudge® Wild Supreme symbol and its multipliers are not affected by xZone™ multipliers spreading to the reel that the xNudge® has landed on, but xNudge® picks up any multipliers that it nudges across that have been affected by the xZone™ multipliers.

## Scatters
Scatter symbols can land on any reel in base game.

• Landing 3 Scatter symbols will trigger 10 Face Plant Spins.
• Landing 4 Scatter symbols will trigger 10 Dead Meat Spins.
• Landing 5 Scatter symbols will trigger 10 Boneyard Spins

## Face Plant Spins
A guaranteed xZone™ Wild symbol lands on every spin.

## Dead Meat Spin
Two guaranteed xZone™ Wild symbols land on every spin.

## Boneyard Spins
Two guaranteed xZone™ Wild symbols land on every spin. The xNudge® Wild Supreme can land on any of the reels in this boneyard.

## Max Win
The max payout of the game is 30,000 times the base bet. When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 30,000 times the base bet is awarded.

Max Win can also be achieved by winning/not dying in the Crasher Game.

## Crasher Game
The Crasher Game symbol can only land in base game. If the symbol lands fully stacked, covering all reels, it will award the player with a 30x times base bet win and the option to bet this win in the Crasher Game. If the Crasher Game symbol lands partially, the positions that it has landed on are dead.

After any win from 30x and up, the player will be asked if they want to bet their win and play the Crasher Game. The Crasher Game will then start with the multiplier of the win that enabled the Crasher Game.

If the player clicks “No” when asked to play Crasher Game, the winnings will be kept.

Bet your win and win any amount from 30x - 30,000x. The player will have to click “STOP” before the skateboarder hits the ground to win anything from 30x to 30,000x. If the skateboarder lands in the grave then all the winnings are lost but if he lands in a dumpster then the Max Win will be awarded.
