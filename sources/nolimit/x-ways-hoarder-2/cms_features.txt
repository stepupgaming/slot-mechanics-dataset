## xWays®
xWays® symbol can land on the four middle reels.

×Ways® symbol properties:

- Will reveal a random regular symbol.

- If more than one xWays®  symbol lands on the reels, then all Ways symbols will reveal the same symbol.

- The size of the xWays® symbol can range from 2-12 depending on the number of connected xWays® landed on same reel. 1 xWays® symbol can expand to a size of 2-4, 2 connected Ways symbols on same reel can expand to a size of 3-8 and 3 connected xWays® symbols on same reel can expand to a size of 4-12.

## xSplit® Wilds
xSplit® Wild symbols can land on any reel except reel 1.

xSplit® Wild symbols split all symbols to the left on the same row and double that symbol. If a symbol is split more than once, it will be displayed with a multiplier. xSplit® Wild does not split Loot symbol.

When xSplit® Wilds splits xWays® symbols and the xWays® multiplier will be doubled for every split.

## xSplit® Nudge
xSplit® Nudge symbols can land on any reel except reel 1. It lands as a stacked symbol that will always nudge and will stop just before a special symbol (if any).

Each xSplit® Nudge position will then split symbols to the left on the same row and double that symbol.

## Loot Respin
Landing Loot symbol along with any special symbol in the main game triggers Loot Respin.

During Respin, Loot symbol expands on 1st reel and lock the reel. If any special symbol (xWays®, xSplit®, Scatter or xSplit® Nudge) lands then respin retriggers and the special symbols become sticky for next spin.

When no additional special symbol lands, the 1st reel gets unlocked and one final respin is awarded. A Loot symbol may land again when the 1st reel is unlocked, this will retrigger Loot Respin.

## Scatter
A Scatter symbol can land on any reel except reel 1.

A Scatter symbol doesn’t get split by the xSplit® Wild or xSplit® Nudge, it will instead become a Super Scatter symbol.

## Raid Spins
3, 4 or 5 Scatter Symbols trigger 7, 10 or 13 Raid Spins respectively.

During the Raid Spins the xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge symbols remain sticky for the rest of the feature after landing.

Super Scatters converts itself and regular paying symbols below it to xSplit® symbols during the mode.

Radiation Level Up: For every third collected xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge symbol a new Radiation level is reached.

Binge Spins is the final level with max number of collected xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge and only 3 different symbols on the reels.

Level awards:

- Radiation Level 1 - 3 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels.

- Radiation Level 2 - 6 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The second lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels. 

- Radiation Level 3 - 9 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The third lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels.

- Radiation Level 4 - 12 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The fourth lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels.

Each level upgrade will award +1 spin.

## Plunder Spins
Landing 3, 4, or 5 Scatter Symbols from which at least 1 of them becomes a Super Scatter will trigger 7, 10 or 13 Plunder Spins, respectively.

The position of the Super Scatter and the positions below it will become sticky xSplit® symbols during this mode.

Radiation Levels stay active and upgrade in the same way as levels do in Raid Spins.

## Binge Spins
Binge Spins is triggered when Raid or Plunder Spins progresses to Level 4. Only the top 2 highest paying character symbols and the highest paying object symbol can land on the reels.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3 times the base bet, the player is 4 times more likely to trigger Free Spins.

## 1 Guaranteed xSplit® Nudge xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 10 times base bet, the player is guaranteed at minimum 1 xSplit® Nudge.

## Loot Respins xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 30 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed to trigger Loot Respin.

## Wafer Thin Mint
The max payout of the game is 25,920 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 25,920 times the base bet is awarded.
