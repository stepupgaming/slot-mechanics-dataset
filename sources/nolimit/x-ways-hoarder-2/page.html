Game Features

xWays®

xWays® symbol can land on the four middle reels. ×Ways® symbol properties: - Will reveal a random regular symbol. - If more than one xWays® symbol lands on the reels, then all Ways symbols will reveal the same symbol. - The size of the xWays® symbol can range from 2-12 depending on the number of connected xWays® landed on same reel. 1 xWays® symbol can expand to a size of 2-4, 2 connected Ways symbols on same reel can expand to a size of 3-8 and 3 connected xWays® symbols on same reel can expand to a size of 4-12.

xSplit® Wilds

xSplit® Wild symbols can land on any reel except reel 1. xSplit® Wild symbols split all symbols to the left on the same row and double that symbol. If a symbol is split more than once, it will be displayed with a multiplier. xSplit® Wild does not split Loot symbol. When xSplit® Wilds splits xWays® symbols and the xWays® multiplier will be doubled for every split.

xSplit® Nudge

xSplit® Nudge symbols can land on any reel except reel 1. It lands as a stacked symbol that will always nudge and will stop just before a special symbol (if any). Each xSplit® Nudge position will then split symbols to the left on the same row and double that symbol.

Loot Respin

Landing Loot symbol along with any special symbol in the main game triggers Loot Respin. During Respin, Loot symbol expands on 1st reel and lock the reel. If any special symbol (xWays®, xSplit®, Scatter or xSplit® Nudge) lands then respin retriggers and the special symbols become sticky for next spin. When no additional special symbol lands, the 1st reel gets unlocked and one final respin is awarded. A Loot symbol may land again when the 1st reel is unlocked, this will retrigger Loot Respin.

Scatter

A Scatter symbol can land on any reel except reel 1. A Scatter symbol doesn’t get split by the xSplit® Wild or xSplit® Nudge, it will instead become a Super Scatter symbol.

Raid Spins

3, 4 or 5 Scatter Symbols trigger 7, 10 or 13 Raid Spins respectively. During the Raid Spins the xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge symbols remain sticky for the rest of the feature after landing. Super Scatters converts itself and regular paying symbols below it to xSplit® symbols during the mode. Radiation Level Up: For every third collected xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge symbol a new Radiation level is reached. Binge Spins is the final level with max number of collected xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge and only 3 different symbols on the reels. Level awards: - Radiation Level 1 - 3 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels. - Radiation Level 2 - 6 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The second lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels. - Radiation Level 3 - 9 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The third lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels. - Radiation Level 4 - 12 xSplit®, xWays® or xSplit® Nudge collected: The fourth lowest paying character symbol and object symbol are removed from the reels. Each level upgrade will award +1 spin.

Plunder Spins

Landing 3, 4, or 5 Scatter Symbols from which at least 1 of them becomes a Super Scatter will trigger 7, 10 or 13 Plunder Spins, respectively. The position of the Super Scatter and the positions below it will become sticky xSplit® symbols during this mode. Radiation Levels stay active and upgrade in the same way as levels do in Raid Spins.

Binge Spins

Binge Spins is triggered when Raid or Plunder Spins progresses to Level 4. Only the top 2 highest paying character symbols and the highest paying object symbol can land on the reels.

Game xMechanics

xBet®

At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

xWays®

xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins. xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

xSplit®

xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.
