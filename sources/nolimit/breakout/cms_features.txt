## Syndicate Multiplier
Each high symbol has a corresponding Syndicate Multiplier symbol linked to it. When the Syndicate Multiplier lands it will
transfer the multiplier onto all of the same high paying symbols. If multiple Syndicate Multipliers of the same kind appear, each multiplier will be added to the corresponding high paying symbol.

Syndicate multipliers are not additive, they apply to the total line multiplier.Multiplier levels for high paying symbols: 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x, 25x, 50x and 100x.

## Chain Reaction Respins
Respin can be triggered by landing at least 6 high paying symbols of the same kind. During respin, high paying symbols that triggered respin will remain sticky on its
position.

Landing another high paying symbol of same kind that is already sticky or at least 6 high paying symbols of another kind will retrigger a respin.

Every Bonus symbol that appears on the initial spin or respin will be collected and contribute to advancing towards the bonus round. No wins are awarded during respins. Payout for the respin is awarded at the end of the respin feature.

## Breakout Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Breakout Spins. During this mode, Syndicate Multipliers are persistent between spins. Respin mode behaves the same way as in
base game.

Landing an additional Bonus during this feature awards 3 extra spins.

Landing a second Bonus will upgrade any remaining Breakout Spins to Clearout Spins. Multiplier levels of high paying symbols from Breakout Spins will be carried over to Clearout Spins. At the time of upgrade, number of remaining spins are added as mulitpliers to a randomly selected high paying symbol.

## Clearout Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger Clearout Spins. It starts with 1 life for each high paying symbol. During this mode, Syndicate Multipliers are persistent between spins. Respin mode behaves the same way as in base game.

When a Kill symbol lands, it will reveal either a high paying symbol or a miss. If it reveals a high paying symbol that is still active on the reels, that high paying symbol will be removed.

When the Kill symbol lands and the last high paying symbol is killed, a respin is awarded. All instances of that symbol on the reels will stick during the respins. The respin is triggered regardless of how many of the last high paying symbols are there on the reels. High paying symbols cannot be killed during respins.

Once a high paying symbol is eliminated, it will no longer appear in the remaining spins. Additionally, its multiplier will transfer to the highest paying symbol which is still active.

## Bonus Chase (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is 4 times more likely to activate the Breakout/Clearout Spins.

## Syndicate Multiplier (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 20 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a random Syndicate Multiplier on each high paying symbol.

## x100 Syndicate Multiplier (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 90 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a x100 Syndicate Multiplier on each high paying symbol.

## God Mode
At a cost of 2,000 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 1 to 3 Max Win symbols. Landing 3 Max Win symbols will trigger a respin. Any additional Max Win symbol that lands will retrigger a respin, until a total of 5 Max Win symbols land.

Player will be awarded based on the number of collected Max Win symbols:

- Collecting 3 Max Win symbols awards 1,000x the base bet.
- Collecting 4 Max Win symbols awards 2,500x the base bet.
- Collecting 5 Max Win symbols awards 20,000x the base bet.

Payout for the respin is awarded at the end of the God mode feature.

## Off The Hook
The max payout of the game is 20,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 20,000 times the base bet is awarded.
