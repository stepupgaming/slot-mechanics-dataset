## Reel Area
The game features a 3-3-3-3-3-3 reel layout across 6 reels, with an Enhancer Train positioned above the reels and a Wild Queue positioned below the reels.

## xWays®
An xWays® symbol can hold 2 to 4 of the same symbol inside and all xWays® symbols will show the same symbol.

## Wilds & Wild Queue
At the start of a new round, up to 3 Wilds with a random multiplier may appear in the Wild Queue. The possible multipliers on the Wilds are 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 20x, 1,000x. 

Wild symbols that participate in a win are stored in the Wild Queue during an avalanche. One Wild from the Wild Queue is added to the reel area during an avalanche. 

The Wild Queue can hold a maximum of 10 Wilds. If another Wild is to be added then its Multiplier is added to the 10th Wild multiplier.

## Crew Call
The Crew Call adds 3 Wilds with random multipliers to the Wild Queue, and after triggering it is removed, allowing a new symbol to fall in. Possible multipliers are 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x 20x, 100x and 1,000x.

## Fraternal Kiss
The Fraternal kiss triggers when there are no more avalanches and at least one Wild on the reel or in the Wild Queue. The following actions take place during its trigger: 

•All regular paying symbols and Wilds on the reel area and in the Wild Queue are removed. 

•A Wild with the sum of all the removed Wild multipliers is added to the reel area.

## Enhancer Train and Enhancers
Enhancers are triggered only when there is a Wild present in the same reel under them. These Enhancers may appear in the Enhancer Train. 

• Wild Adder: Wild Adder triggers if there is a Wild in the Wild Queue. It moves the Wild from the Wild Queue and positions it randomly on the reel area. The amount of Wilds moved from the Wild Queue is the same as the number of Wilds present in the same reel. 

• Multiplier: Multiplier Enhancer reveal a value of 2x, 3x or 10x. The revealed multiplier is added to the mutliplier value of each Wild present in the same reel. 

• Wild Cloner: The Wild Cloner Enhancer adds a new Wild to the queue with the same multiplier value as the Wild appearing below it in the same reel, for each Wild present in that reel. 

• +1 Spin: +1 Spin Enhancer only appear during the bonus mode. It awards an additional spin for each Wild symbol present in the same reel. 

• xQuake: xQuake Enhancer can land only during the Die Wende Spins and Mauerfall Spins. xQuake Enhancer triggers only once regardless of the number of Wilds present in the same reel. It splits only one regular paying symbol per reel into 2, 3 or 4. Once triggered it becomes inactive. xQuake doesnt split symbols on the reels which have an xWays® or an already split symbol. 

• Mega Multiplier: Mega Multiplier Enhancer can land only during Mauerfall Spins. It reveals a value of 50x, 60x, 70x, 80x, 100x, 200x, 300x or 1,000x. Revealed multiplier is added to the multiplier value of each Wild present in the same reel. Once triggered it becomes inactive. 

Enhancer Train moves one position to the left with every avalanche.

## Blitzkrieg Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 6 Blitzkrieg Spins. During this bonus mode, all Wilds on the reel area are stored in the Wild Queue at the end of each spin, this includes the spin of the base game in which the bonus mode was triggered. One Wild from the Wild Queue is moved to the reel area for each spin. 

Bonus symbols will not land during Blitzkrieg Spins.

## Die Wende Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 8 Die Wende Spins. 

It has the same features as Blitzkrieg Spins, with the possibility of xQuake appearing in the Enhancer Train.

## Mauerfall Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 10 Mauerfall Spins. 

It has the same features as Die Wende, with the possibility of Mega Multiplier appearing in the Enhancer Train.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 1 Wild with a 100x multiplier in the Wild Queue. Maximum of 3 Wilds can land.

## Punk Spins 100x (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 20 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 1 Wild with a 100x multiplier in the Wild Queue. A maximum of 3 Wilds can land.

## Punker Spins 1,000x (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 230 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 1 Wild with multiplier 1000x in the Wild Queue. 

A maximum of 3 Wilds can land.

## Punkest Spins 10,000x (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 1,989 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 1 Wild with multiplier 10,000x in the Wild Queue. 

A maximum of 3 Wilds can land.

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. During the Extra Spin: 

• All the Wilds on the reel area are stored in the Wild Queue. 

• All Symbols except Bonus symbols are removed, new symbols drop in and if available, Wild from Wild Queue is moved to the reel area. 

• Enhancer Train moved one position to the left. 

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the Wilds present in the Wild Queue and the reel area. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the total win, the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented.

## Max Win
The max payout of the game is 19,089 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 19,089 times the base bet is awarded.
