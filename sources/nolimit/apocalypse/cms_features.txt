## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xNudge® Wild
xNudge® Wild can land on reels 2-5.

When it lands, it will nudge down to the bottom position and will increase the payout multiplier by 1 for every step it nudges.

If it lands on the bottom position, it acts as a regular Wild.

## Super xNudge® Wild
Super xNudge® Wild can land on reel 3 and 4 only

Super xNudge® needs to land on the same row on both reels to activate the feature. Otherwise, it will act as regular Wild.

Once activated, it will drop down to the bottom position and will increase the payout multiplier by 1 for every step it drops, then expand to cover the entire reel.

Several Wild multipliers add to each other for a total Wild multiplier.

Maximum multiplier for Super xNudge® is x8.

## Super xNudge® Feature Spin
Awards 1 spin with 2 guaranteed Super xNudge® Wilds in the same row at a cost of 50 times the base bet.
Total Recall can be reached from Super Nudge feature spin.

## Carnage Spins \u0026 Doomsday Spins
3 or 4 Scatters in base game will trigger the Bonus modes: Carnage Spins or Doomsday Spins respectively. The bonus consists of 2 features:

Hoarding feature

- Starts with 3 spins and will reset to 3 spins every time a value symbol lands in the reel area. Scatters that triggered the feature will be replaced by random value symbols.

- All symbols are valued as times the active bet. Possible initial symbol values: ×1, ×5, x10, x20, ×40

- When a column is filled with all 4 symbols, an extra spin is granted in the Feasting feature.

- Bonus will advance to Feasting feature if there is no spin left or all the positions are filled.

Feasting feature

- Awards 5 spins in Carnage spins and 10 spins in Doomsday Spins.

- Enhanced rows on top and bottom of the reel become active which can only reveal character symbol.

- Each character symbol increases the active multiplier depending on the feature they activate.

CREATOR
Creates a new value symbol on an empty position.

TRIPLET
Randomly upgrades 3 symbol values to next level 
which can be at same or different positions.

CUTTER
Multiplies one random symbol value symbol by 2.

LICKER
Adds the highest value to the total counter.

CRAWER
Collects all symbol values and adds up to the total counter.

A persistent version of character symbols might also land, which will move right to the adjacent reel with each spin, activating the character symbol again until it reaches the last reel.

## Cataclysm Spins
Landing 5 Scatters in the base game will trigger Cataclysm Spins.

In addition to Doomsday Spins features, only persistent character symbols will be available during the Feasting Feature.

## xBet™

## Total Recall
The max payout of the game is 20000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 20000 times the base bet is awarded.
