Game Features
Casino WinSpin is a 5x3 respin slot with
three prominent features that will help you
win huge – Expanding Wilds, the Spin ‘til You
Win feature and the Hot Zones Expanding
Wilds.

Winspin Hot Zones
Both features in Casino WinSpin are triggered through
the WinSpin Hot Zones, located on reel 2 and 4. If landing
the same type of symbol in these hot zones, the WinSpin
feature will activate. If you land a Wild in the Hot Zone, the
Hot Zone Expanding Wilds feature activates.

Hot Zone Expanding Wilds
There are two types of Wilds, which both substitutes for
any other symbol. The Expanding Wild will expand and
cover the entire reel after landing. If you land a Wild inside
any Hot Zone, all regular Wilds will turn into Expanding
Wilds.
