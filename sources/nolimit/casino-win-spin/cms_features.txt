## Winspin Hot Zones
Both features in Casino WinSpin are triggered through the WinSpin Hot Zones, located on reel 2 and 4. If landing the same type of symbol in these hot zones, the WinSpin feature will activate. If you land a Wild in the Hot Zone, the Hot Zone Expanding Wilds feature activates.

## Hot Zone Expanding Wilds
There are two types of Wilds, which both substitutes for any other symbol. The Expanding Wild will expand and cover the entire reel after landing. If you land a Wild inside any Hot Zone, all regular Wilds will turn into Expanding Wilds.\nIf you land wilds in both Hot Zones, an Expanding Wild on reel 3 will be created and a massive win will be awarded. Any regular wild will also turn into an Expanding Wild.

## Spin ‘til You Win
The Spin til’ you win feature will award respins with a guaranteed win. Each non-winning spin will award a greater boost for the next respin.\nThe first respin will award all triggering symbols to be wild, the second respin will also award a random multiplier – randomly chosen between x2 to x5 - and the third level respins will award a spreading wild on reel 3. The respins won’t stop until there is a win!
