                                                                                                          ROUNDHOUSE KICK
                                                                                                          Roundhouse Kick is triggered in the main game when 3,4,5 or 6 of the same kind of paying symbols align diagonally,
                                                                                                          when there is no winning combination.

                                                                                                          The triggering symbols will be removed and create a Wild symbol in the middle positions of the alignment;

                                                                                                          - 3 symbols award 1 Wild Knockout xBomb®
                                                                                                          - 4 symbols award 2 Wild Knockout xBombs®
                                                                                                          - 5 symbols award 3 Wild Knockout xBombs®
                                                                                                          - 6 symbols award 4 Wild Knockout xBombs®

                                                                                                          Wild Knockout xBomb® explosions will increase the win multiplier by 1 for the next collapse.

                                                                                                          All other symbols will explode, except Knockout symbols and Scatter symbols.

                                                                                                          TITLE FIGHT FREESPINS.
                                                                                                          Three Scatter symbols will trigger the Title Fight Freespins. The reel area becomes a 3-3-3-2-2-2-3-
                                                                                                          3-3 with the four special title belt booster positions in the middle of the reel. Wins are paid both
                                                                                                          ways in Title Fight Freespins.

                                                                                                          The four title belt boosters are inactivated in the first Title Fight Freespins, but will activate one by
                                                                                                          one when a new title belt is won.

                                                                                                          Each fight can be won by landing a number of blows to the opponent by landing the Uppercut
                                                                                                          symbol. Each Uppercut Symbol deducts 1 hit point on the opponent. When the opponent is
                                                                                                          knocked out, the Freespins will advance to the next level's fighter. Fighters have different hit
                                                                                                          points and will be tougher to beat.

                                                                                                          Fight 1 - Jim "Big Fatty" Johnson: 3 hit points, +3 spins if beat.
                                                                                                          Fight 2 - Kenny "Ironjaw" Shambles: 3 hit points, +3 spins if beat.
                                                                                                          Fight 3 - Artie "One Glove Man" Jefferson: 4 hit points, +3 spins if beat.
                                                                                                          Fight 4 - Grayson "Boa Constrictor" Royce: 5 hit points, +5 spins if beat.
                                                                                                          Fight 5 - Wai "Little Dragon" Le: Unlimited hit points.

                                                                                                          A title belt will only activate once per spin, but if it's not used on the initial spin - it can be achieved
                                                                                                          in the following tumbles. No more Uppercut symbols will land during fight 5. The only way to beat
                                                                                                          Wai "Little Dragon" Le is to become the "Greatest of All Time".
