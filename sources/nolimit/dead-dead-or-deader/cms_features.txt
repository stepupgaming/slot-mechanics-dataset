## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## Wild
Starting from left to right, the 1st Wild to land on a reel will have a multiplier of 1x. Wilds on the following reel will have a multiplier of 2x and so on.

Multiple Wilds on the same reel will have the same multiplier. 

Maximum Wild multiplier is 5x.

## Scatters
Scatter symbols can only land on reels 2-5. Landing 3 Scatter symbols will trigger 12 Dead Spins. Landing 4 Scatter symbols will trigger 12 Deader Spins.

If only 2 Scatter symbols land in the base game, they transform into Wilds with multipliers based on the usual Wild multiplier calculation.

## xNudge® Wild
The xNudge® Wild is a stacked symbol and can only land on reel 2, 3 and 4.

Landing xNudge® Wild will always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases the win multiplier by 1.

xNudge® Wild multipliers are additive.

After nudging to fully visible the following scenarios can happen:

The Hanging: The guy at the bottom of the stacked xNudge® Wild gets hanged. This awards the player with a respin, with the xNudge® Wild being sticky throughout the respin.

The Deadlock: When the respin is over the hanged guy can either get an additional neck snap, the Deadlock, doubling the multiplier value and making the xNudge® Wild sticky for yet another respin. After each respin, the multiplier will showcase the eventual upcoming respins xNudge® Wild multiplier. 

The Dropout: The guy at the bottom of xNudge® gets hanged, but falls out. This does not award a respin. If you get the Dropout after a respin, the multiplier value will revert to its former value and no additional respin will be awarded.

## Dead Spins
xNudge® Wilds are always sticky for at least one additional spin and the xNudge® multiplier gets doubled immediately before the next spin starts.

Landing a Scatter on reel 5 will upgrade Dead Spins to Deader Spins and award +2 spins.

## Deader Spins
Same xNudge® Wild behavior as in Dead Spins but the sticky xNudge® Wild multiplier gets tripled instead of doubled.

## Just Die Already
The max payout of the game is 19,349 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 19,349 times the base bet is awarded.

Just Die Already can also be achieved in main game by landing a stacked Casket symbol on any reel. A partial Casket is a dead symbol with 0 value.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 1.5 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Scatter symbol on the second reel.

## 1 xNudge® Wild (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 7 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 1 xNudge® Wild on any of the middle 3 reels.

## 3 xNudge® Wilds (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 799 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 3 xNudge® Wilds.
