## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Atomic Wild 
A random position is selected on the reel area and is transformed into a Wild symbol having a random multiplier of 10, 20, 30, 50 or 100.

If the chosen position is already Wild, then +1 will be added to the randomly chosen multiplier.

If multiple features trigger at the same time, then Atomic Wild will always trigger first.

## Bomber
2-5 Bombers can randomly appear on different reels. One Bomber per reel.

All the paying symbols on these reels will be transformed into Wild symbols from top to bottom.

If the Anti-Air symbol is present on the chosen reel, Wild transformation stops just above that Anti-Air symbol.

If Wild is present on the chosen reel, then the multiplier increases by +1 for that Wild symbol.

## Boosted xBet®
At a cost of an extra 30%, the player is 2 times more likely to activate Freespins.

This will not be applicable for bonus modes.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Stuka
Stuka will appear diagonally either from left to right or from right to left.

It will split all paying symbols and double their size.

Maximum of 7 Stukas may appear in one spin. One Stuka per diagonal.

## Panzer
2-5 Panzers can randomly appear in different rows. One Panzer per row.

Every low paying symbol on these chosen rows will get transformed into Wild symbol except the Foot Soldier symbol.

If Wild is present on the chosen row, then the multiplier increases by +1 for that Wild symbol.

## Howitzer
It randomly chooses any 1-7 paying symbols and turns them into Wild symbols except the Commando symbol or Wild symbols.

## Scatters 
Landing 2 Scatter symbols may trigger any of the above mentioned features in the base game.

If one of the Scatter symbols lands on the 5th reel, Atomic Wild is triggered.

## Neptune Spins
Landing 3 Scatter symbols will activate Neptune Spins.

Once triggered, you will get a pick and reveal option which will reveal 2 out of 5 features.

If a Scatter lands on reel 5 while triggering Neptune Spins then Atomic Wild feature will get activated and you will get only 1 option to pick and reveal from remaining 4 features.

Each revealed feature will activate atleast once during Neptune Spins.

## Invasion Spins 
Landing 4 Scatter symbols will activate Invasion Spins.

Once triggered, you will get a pick and reveal option which will reveal 3 out of 5 features.

If a Scatter lands on reel 5 while triggering Invasion Spins, then
Atomic Wild feature will get activated and you will get only 2
options to pick and reveal from the remaining 4 features.

Each revealed feature will activate atleast once during Invasion Spins.

## Overlord Spins
Landing 5 Scatter symbols will activate Overlord Spins.

The Atomic Wild feature is guaranteed, so you will get a pick
and reveal option to reveal 3 features out of the remaining 4
features.

Each revealed feature will activate at least once during Overlord Spins.

## Mission Accomplished
The max payout of the game is 55,555 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 55,555 times the base bet is awarded.

## Nolimit Bonus Buy
Buy your way straight into the different bonus features, ranging from 68 to 2,310 times the base bet.

• Neptune Spins can be bought for 70 times bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.

• Invasion Spins can be bought for 250 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.

• Overlord Spins can be bought for 1000 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.

• Lucky Draw can be bought for 235 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.
