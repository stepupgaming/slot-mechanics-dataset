## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## Reel Area
5×5 reel setup with several locked positions.
 
Whenever a Scatter lands, it opens up the locked positions on its reel.

Only the opened positions are active symbols.

## Enhancer Cells
On the top and bottom of each reel there is a locked Enhancer Cell position.

When triggered to open, they may reveal Fisherman Wild, Big Berta, ×Ways or any normal paying symbols.

• xWays® can only land on locked positions.
• xWays® symbols will transform into a normal paying symbol. It will reveal 2-4 symbols of the same kind and increase the number of win ways.

## Fish trophy and Big Berta
Fish Trophy and Big Berta symbols have a random multiplier value which can be won during the Kill N’ Grill Wild Fishing feature in any mode.

Possible values for Fish Trophy are: 2x, 5x, 25x, 100x, 250×, 1000x, 5000x.

Possible values for Big Berta are: 100x, 250x, 1000×, 5000×.

## Kill N’ Grill Wild Fishing
In base game, landing a golden Fisherman Wild in the opened enhancer cells triggers the feature.

In any Freespin mode landing a Fisherman Wild on any open reel position triggers the feature.

During feature, multiplier values from collected Fish Trophy symbols are added and then multiplied with the bet which further adds to the symbol payout if any.

## BONUS MODES: Lunker Spins / Hawg Spins / Honey Hole Spins
Hitting 3 Scatters trigger Lunker Spins with 6 opened enhancer cells, resulting in 8 Freespins.

Hitting 4 Scatters trigger Haw Spins with 8 opened enhancer cells, resulting in 9 Freespins.

Hitting 5 Scatters trigger Honey Hole Spins with 10 opened enhancer cells, resulting in 10 Freespins.

Landing 1 extra Scatter in Lunker or Hawg Spins results in opening up
2 enhancer cells and getting 1 additional Freespin.

Troll Level

Troll levels(2x, 3x, 10x) are only available in Freespin mode. Troll level increments with every 4th Fishman Wild collected and +2 spins are awarded
In addition to Kill N’ Grill Wild Fishing features in main game, the collected Fish Trophy values are also multiplied with the Troll level in Freespin modes.

## Boosted xBet®
At a cost of an extra 75%, the player is guaranteed a Scatter symbol on the second reel.

This will not be applicable for bonus modes.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Kiss My GO-TO-HELL
The Max payout of the game is 50,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 50,000 times the base bet is awarded.
