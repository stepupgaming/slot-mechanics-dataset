## Stance
Choose between 43332 or the more volatile 23334 reel setup. Can be changed before going into the bonus mode.

## Stoudenmire xNudge Wilds
You’ve seen these before - Wilds that nudges in and award a multiplier range from x1 to x3, increase with each nudge. Stoudenmire xNudge Wilds will be upgraded in the bonus modes if landing on a reel with a Jumping Wild, and also stick in the Drunken Gundfiguth&r... Spins!

## Deputy & Marshal Raid
Triggered by the badges and will turn the low symbols and the premium symbols into one randomly chosen symbol. Landing both badges also increases the reel area to 43334 and transform the badges to wilds.

## Gunfight Spins
3 scatters that turns into Jumping Wilds and will move across the reels during the 8 Gunfight Spins. These will be upgraded by landing any badge or even both badges.

## Deputy & Marshal Gunfight Spins
3 scatters plus any of the badges, also combines the Gunfights Spins with the transforming of low or premium symbols. A very potent bonus mode!

## Drunken Gundfiguth&r... Spins
3 scatters and both badges to trigger 10 spins on a 43334 reel area with 5 Jumping Wilds - let the insanity begin! Massive payouts, but dangerous grounds to enter...

## 4 dead in 5 seconds
Landing all five characters in the main game - including Stoudenmire - triggers a respin where the Wild expands to cover all three middle reels.

## 6 feet under
The max payout of the game is 44 440 times the base bet and if this is exceeded, the game round will end.

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy Functionality
Buy your way straight into one of the 5 different bonus modes, ranging from 100x to 1000x the bet. Feelin’ lucky? Then buy the random bonus buy feature where any of the 5 different modes can be won.
