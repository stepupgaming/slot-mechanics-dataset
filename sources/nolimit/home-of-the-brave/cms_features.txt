## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xBomb®
An xBomb Wild symbol substitutes any symbol except for the Bonus symbols. An exploding xBomb Wild removes adjacent symbols except for Bonus symbols and will increase the win multiplier by one for the next collapse.

All xBomb Wilds will explode before the next collapse, except when the Wild Mining feature is triggered. An xBomb Wild will explode when there is a win, no matter if it’s a part of the winning combination or not. It will explode and trigger a new collapse if there isn’t a win, and wild Mining feature is not triggered.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## Reel Area
The game features a 4-4-4-4-4 reel layout across 5 reels, with a Conveyor Belt positioned above the top row and 2 Hot Sauce Reels located on the far left and far right sides of the game area.

## Hot Sauce Reels
The Hot Sauce Reels are triggered when the Wild Launch Button lands in the game area. In base game, one of the Hot Sauce Reels will be partially or fully filled with symbols on each spin.

Once activated, the symbols on Hot Sauce Reels will get triggered before next collapse:

- xSplit®: Splits all symbols on the adjacent row, except Bonus symbols and Red Cap. The maximum number of splits is 4.

- xBomb® Wild Multiplier: An xBomb® Wild symbol substitutes for any symbol except Bonus symbols and Red Cap.

- An exploding xBomb® Wild removes adjacent President and Alphabet symbols, and will increase the multiplier by its value for the next collapse. 

All xBomb® symbols will explode before the next collapse.

- Multiplier RPG: The Multiplier RPG contains multiplier values ranging from 2x-5x and 99x.

When activated, the multiplier(s) are transferred from the RPG symbol to the Wild that the Wild Launch Button transformed into.

## Conveyor Belt
Symbols from the Conveyor Belt drop on their respective reels if any empty space is created after win evaluation or xBomb® explosion. New symbols are dropped in to fill the empty space (if any).

The symbols that can appear are as follows:

- Wild Launch Button: This button triggers the Hot Sauce Reels and transforms into a Wild Doughnut after doing so.

- Wild Doughnut: A regular Wild symbol.

- xNudge® Wild Tower: When the xNudge® Wild Tower symbol drops into the game area, it gradually nudges down to the bottom row, becoming a stacked Wild with a multiplier.

Each nudge increases the xNudge® Wild multiplier and the multiplier size depends on how many symbols it nudges to reach the bottom row. xNudge® Symbol is always 4 symbol high. Nudge multipliers are additive. 

- Red Cap: Landing the Red Cap symbol activates The Cap Win Machine, giving the player the opportunity to get additional rewards including instant Max Win.

- Trash Can: This is a dead symbol which never drops but allows new symbols to enter Conveyor Belt.

## Covfefe Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols in base game will trigger 8 Covfefe Spins.

During Covfefe Spins, both Hot Sauce Reels contain symbols on each spin. xBomb® Wild Multiplier is sticky throughout the round.

Landing a Bonus symbol in Covfefe Spins will award +2 spins and upgrade any remaining Covfefe Spins into Dog Eating Spins.

## Dog Eating Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols in base game will trigger 10 Dog Eating Spins.

During Dog Eating Spins, both Hot Sauce Reels contain symbols and the player is guaranteed a Wild Launch Button in the conveyor belt on each spin. xBomb® Wild Multiplier is sticky throughout the round.

## Cap Win Machine
The Cap Win Machine is a simple, one-button game where you press “Push” and hope to win big.

The machine features 4 rows with multipliers on the Red Caps:

- Row 1: 30x
- Row 2: 20x
- Row 3: 15x
- Row 4: 10x

There is also a Yellow Cap that offers the Max Win.
If one or more caps fall down from their respective rows, the player collects the total multiplier(s) multiplied by the base bet as the win. If no caps fall, no win is awarded.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 1.4 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on second reel.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xBet™ Deluxe (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 5 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus on reel 2 and a Wild Launch Button in the Conveyor Belt.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## The Satan Spin (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 333 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Wild Launch Button and both Hot Sauce Reels are fully filled with symbols.

## The Cap Win Machine (God Mode)
At a cost of 6,666 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin of The Cap Win Machine feature.

## President Evil
The max payout of the game is 61,446 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 61,446 times the base bet is awarded.
