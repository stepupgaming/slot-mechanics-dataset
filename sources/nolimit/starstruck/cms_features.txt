## Multiplying Wilds
Wild symbols are stacked, 5 rows high. Each Wild may multiply the line win by x2, x3 or x5. Wilds with x5 only exists on the middle reel.\nSeveral Wilds with a multiplier on a winning line will multiply each other.\n

## Starstruck
Starstruck is a Hot Zone activated feature, where the\nHot Zone is positioned in the middle position or reel 2. Landing the Starstruck symbol in the Hot Zone will activate the Starstruck bonus game.\nStarstruck awards a pick and click option to win up to 1000 times the bet. All wins range from 5 times to 1000 times the bet.
