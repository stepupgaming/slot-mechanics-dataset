## Wilds
Ordinary wilds, but very potent together with xWays symbol.

## xWays & Infectious xWays
You’ve seen these before in East Coast vs West Coast - but this game is only dedicated to the Infectious xWays. xWays mystery symbol will reveal one symbol of the same kind, but expand the reels as they hold 2-3 symbols.\nLand a xWays symbol and a Wild, to trigger the Infectious xWays symbol - where the revealed symbol will also expand any symbol of the same kind on the reel to the same size!

## Infectious Spins
Land 3 or 4 bonus symbols to trigger the bonus mode - and choose between Toxic Hazard and Lock n’ Load bonus modes. Landing 4 bonus symbols award +1 extra spin in each mode.

## Infectious Spins: Toxic Hazard (9 or 10 spins)
For the ones that is looking for a more solid payout from Infectious Spins, where 1-5 random wilds are placed on the middle reels for every spin - which means that landing an xWays means instant Infectious Ways with a good chance of a winning connection!

## Infectious Spins: Lock n’ Load (7 or 8 spins)
The more volatile Infectious Spins option, where all Wilds lock in place for the remainder of the feature and awards +1 extra spin. Wilds may be awarded a x2 or x3 multiplier! Landing an xWays symbols means guaranteed Infectious xWays.

## Infectious Spins: Mayhem Mode (12 or 10 spins)
Triggered by 5 bonus symbols from main game, or collecting a total of 5 bonus symbols in the Infectious Spins.

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy Functionality
Buy your way straight into one of the 3 different bonus features, ranging from 75x to 555x the base bet.
