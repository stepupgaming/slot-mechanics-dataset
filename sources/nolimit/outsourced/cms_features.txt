## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## xNudge® Wild
xNudge® Wild symbol can land anywhere on the reels and will always nudge to fully visible. For every step the xNudge® Wild nudges, the Wild multiplier increases by 1.

Several xNudge® Wild multipliers add to each other for a total win multiplier.

## Print Spins
Winning symbols are paid out before triggering Print Spins (Re-spin). These winning symbols will disappear leaving an empty position for new symbols to come in. 

The multiplier on those winning positions increases by 1 for each Print Spin. Multipliers will be counted for regular paying symbols only. Print Spins ends when there are no more wins.

If xWays® lands during Print Spins, then its revealed symbol size multiplies with the increased multiplier on that position during its pay out. The symbol multiplier on the winning xWays® symbol position will revert to the previous value +1.

## Extra Spins
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the multiplier state of the symbols of the previous spin.

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the multiplier values. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then only the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented. Scatter symbols will not land during Extra Spin.

## Sweatshop Spins
Landing 3 Scatter symbols will trigger 3 Sweatshop Spins.

Any symbol multipliers from the base game are kept during spins.

## Sweatiershop Spins
Landing 4 Scatter symbols will trigger 5 Sweatiershop Spins.

This bonus works the same as Sweatshop Spins but with 2 additional spins.

## xBet™
At the cost of 1.5 times the base bet, the player is more than 4 times likely to activate Sweatshop Spins or Sweatiershop Spins.

Guaranteed Scatter symbol on the second reel.

## MAKS WIN
The max payout of the game is 5,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 5,000 times the base bet is awarded.
