## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Claw Machine
The game consists of a conveyor belt, containing five symbols
per spin, moving from right to left. A claw can pick up a symbol
randomly from conveyor belt.

If claw picks up a paying symbol without dropping it, the player will be awarded the symbols multiplier value times base bet.
Claw will not pick up the symbol with 0 multiplier value.

## Claw Spins
Claw picking up the Bonus symbol without dropping it will award 3
Claw Spins. Additional Bonus symbols picked up during Claw Spins award +3 bonus spins each.

Only symbols with multipliers greater than 2 will appear in Claw Spins.

## Claw Spins (Nolimit Bonus Buy)
At the cost of 35 times base bet, the player gets 3 Claw Spins with 1 Claw.

## Clawier Spins (Nolimit Bonus Buy)
At the cost of 150 times base bet, the player gets 3 Claw Spins with 3 Claws.

## Clawiest Spins (Nolimit Bonus Buy)
At the cost of 400 times base bet, the player gets 3 Claw Spins with 5 Claws.

## Safe Claw Mode (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 1.6 times base bet, symbol picked up by claw cannot be dropped.

## 3 Claws 1 Spin (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 4 times base bet, the player is guaranteed 3 claws per spin.

## 5 Claws 1 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 10 times base bet, the player is guaranteed 5 claws per spin.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 6 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on conveyor belt.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## God Mode (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 997 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a MAX WIN symbol on conveyor belt.

## Max Win
The max payout of the game is 10,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end an 10,000 times the base bet is awarded.

Max Win can also be achieved if claw picks up MAX WIN symbol without dropping it.
