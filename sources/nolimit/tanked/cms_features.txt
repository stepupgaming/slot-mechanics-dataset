## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## xBomb®
An xBomb Wild symbol substitutes any symbol except for the Bonus symbols. An exploding xBomb Wild removes adjacent symbols except for Bonus symbols and will increase the win multiplier by one for the next collapse.

All xBomb Wilds will explode before the next collapse, except when the Wild Mining feature is triggered. An xBomb Wild will explode when there is a win, no matter if it’s a part of the winning combination or not. It will explode and trigger a new collapse if there isn’t a win, and wild Mining feature is not triggered.

## xLoot™
Payouts are awarded when the moving character picks up symbols
of its same colour! 

Characters can also collect Wilds and special features within the game.

## Multipliers
Multipliers can be picked up by any character and apply to all wins.

The multiplier multiplies the characters payout.

Multipliers are additive.

## Tank Booster
There are 4 different types of Tank Boosters that can be picked up by characters: 

- Rocket: The character will shoot the wall close to any random enemy character. The explosion will remove all enemy characters and payout symbols in the explosion pattern as shown in image and increase the size of the grid by 1 step.

- Grenade: The character will throw the grenade targeting any random enemy character. The explosion will remove all paying symbols in the explosion pattern as shown in image.

- Power Drill: The character with a Power Drill will drive to the largest cluster on the grid that matches with the colour of character. It removes all enemy characters, payout symbols, Wilds in its path and picks up Tank Boosters, multiplier and Bonus symbols. It will not impact xBomb®, Barrel bomb and MAX WIN symbols. If the character in the Power Drill can’t move at all, the Character will explode and the explosion will remove all paying symbols in the explosion pattern.

- Airstrike: The character hits up to 3 enemy characters which will create an explosion in the explosion pattern as shown in image.

If any character is removed by any of the above Tank Boosters, then it loses a life and reappears on the reels when new symbols drop in. If the impacted enemy character collected a Tank Booster before explosion then it stays with it in next drop. Explosion will not impact Bonus, xSplit®, xNudge® and Wild Symbols.

The character will carry the Tank Booster until there is no additional movement on screen. At that time the character will use 1 Tank Booster.

## xBomb® & Barrel Bomb
xBomb® and Barrel bomb only explodes if there is no character movement and no Tank Boosters are available.

The xBomb® removes all paying symbols in the explosion pattern and increases the overall multiplier by 1. The Barrel bomb removes all paying symbols in the explosion pattern and expands the grid vertically or horizontally by 1 step.

## Booms
- Small Boom: When 3 characters are next to each other and no character can move, the characters will explode and remove all paying symbols and character in the explosion pattern.

- Big Boom: When 4 characters are next to each other and no character can move, the characters will explode and remove all paying symbols on the reel area and increase size of the grid by 1 step.

## Kill Count
When a character is in an explosion pattern of any Tank Booster, xBomb®, Barrel Bomb or a Boom, they lose 1 life.

When a character has lost 3 lives, it will be considered as killed, then gets removed from the reel area along with all of the corresponding symbols. The character is resurrected after the next evaluation.

## Grid Expansion
Grid expands by 1 step if Rocket hits the wall, a Barrel Bomb is self trigerred or Big boom is triggered. Grid expansion from 6x6 to 9x9 can happen in 4 steps as mentioned below:

- Step 1 grid expansion - Grid expands from 6x6 to 8x6 or 6x8 if there is horizontal or vertical expansion respectively.

- Step 2 grid expansion - Grid expands from 6x8 or 8x6 to 8x8 if there is horizontal or vertical expansion respectively.

- Step 3 grid expansion - Grid expands from 8x8 to 9x8 or 8x9 if there is horizontal or vertical expansion respectively.

- Step 4 grid expansion - Grid expands from 8x9 or 9x8 to 9x9 if there is horizontal or vertical expansion respectively.

Grid directly expands to the maximum size of 9x9 if an xBomb or Barrel Bomb are in the explosion pattern of any Tank Booster.

## xNudge® & xSplit®
The xNudge® Wild will nudge down to the bottom position of the reels or until it hits a non-paying symbol. If there is no multiplier when the xNudge® lands, it will set the multiplier to 2. It then increases the multiplier by +1 for every step it nudges. If additional xNudge® Wild lands, a +1 multiplier will be added.

The xSplit® symbol splits all paying symbols horizontally or vertically, doubles their sizes and then converts itself into a Wild. It also doubles the value of any multiplier that is hit by the xSplit®.

## Carnage Spins & Carnage Royale
Landing 3 Bonus symbols award 5 Carnage Spins. During Freespins, Multipliers, Kill count and grid size will be carried over from the base game.

Landing 4 Bonus symbols award 7 Carnage Royale. During Freespins, Kill count will be carried over from the base game. The grid will be set to 9x9 and a +5 multiplier on top of the current multiplier is carried over from base game.

If all characters die in the same explosion during Free Spins then it will be upgraded to Mayhem Spins and the remaining Free Spins will be played in upgraded bonus mode.

## Mayhem Spins
Mayhem Spins is triggered when all characters lose their 3 lives and die in the same explosion. The grid will be set to 9x9 and a +10 multiplier on top of the current multiplier is carried over from base game. During Mayhem spins, all characters life will be set to 1.

Mayhem Spins can be retriggered if all characters die in the same explosion. This will award the player with +1 extra spin and +10 multiplier.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3 times the base bet, the player is more than 4 times likely to activate the bonus modes.

## 4 Tank Boosters (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 40 times the bet, 4 random Tank Boosters are randomly distributed to characters.

## God Mode
Chase BYOB! by landing a MAX WIN symbol on the reels at a cost of 5,000 times bet.

## BYOB
The max payout of the game is 25,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 25,000 times the base bet is awarded.

BYOB! can also be achieved if complete Max Win symbol of size 3x3 is visible in reel area.
