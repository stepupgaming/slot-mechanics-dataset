## Lightning Rounds
Landing 3 or more Lightning Bolt symbols triggers the Lightning Rounds feature, awarding you 5 spins to collect as many Lightning Bolt symbols as possible in the 4x3 reel area. Landing additional upgrade symbols or extra spins, will enhance the payout. Payout is evaluated times your bet, and there is no cap on the number of upgrades you may get...

## Sticky Fruit Spins
Awarded by landing 4 of the same symbol on the first two reels in main game. This will start a respin and continue as long as you land additional winning symbols. All symbols will stick during the respins.

## Mystery Wrappers
Triggered by any spin in main game, where 4-9 positions will get a Mystery Wrapper overlay that will reveal one symbol before the reels stop. It may trigger the Sticky Fruit Spins if you’re lucky!
