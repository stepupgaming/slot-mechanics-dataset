Fruits has a 223333 reel area, that adapts to

Lightning Rounds
Landing 3 or more Lightning Bolt symbols triggers the
Lightning Rounds feature, awarding you 5 spins to collect
as many Lightning Bolt symbols as possible in the 4x3 reel
area. Landing additional upgrade symbols or extra spins,
will enhance the payout. Payout is evaluated times your
bet, and there is no cap on the number of upgrades you
may get...
