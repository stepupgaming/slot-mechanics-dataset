## Collapsing Mine 
Each spin begins with 3 active rows and for each collapse an additional row becomes active up till 6 rows.

A new collapse can be triggered by the following events:
• A winning combination.
• xBomb Wild explosion.
• Wild Mining feature triggered.

Winning symbols are paid out and removed before triggering next collapse, which will make all symbols fall downwards leaving empty position for new symbols to
drop in from above.

## Buried Features
Dirt symbols in main game may contain a Wild, Split or Bonus symbols.

Feature symbols buried in dirt are inactive until the dirt block they are contained in is removed or hit by an Split feature.

## xBomb® Wild Multiplier
An xBomb Wild symbol substitutes for any symbol except Bonus symbols.

An exploding xBomb Wild removes adjacent symbols except for Bonus symbols and will increase the win multiplier by its value for the next collapse.

All xBomb Wild will explode before the next collapse except when the Wild Mining feature is triggered.

## Wild Mining
Wild Mining will be triggered in main game when 3, 4, 5 or 6 of the same kind of symbols align horizontally or vertically and there is no winning combination.

The triggering symbols will be removed creating Wild symbols in the middle positions of the alignment:

• 3 symbols award 1 Wild
• 4 symbols award 2 Wilds
• 5 symbols award 3 Wilds
• 6 symbols award 4 Wilds

All other positions above the bar will explode if not a Bonus or Wild symbol and trigger a new collapse.

## xBet®
At a total cost of 250% of the base bet, at least 1 Bonus symbol is guaranteed on the last inactive row for each spin.

Additionally, all dirt positions are removed from the main game, making the buried features land without the dirt and trigger immediately when they are in the active reels.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xSplit®
An xSplit® symbol splits symbols on its row, doubling those symbols.

Symbols spliting into 8 or more will be shown with a multiplier instead.

Bonus symbols and xSplit® symbols cannot be split. Once all xSplit® symbols have activated, they all turn into one of the regular symbols.

Dirt blocks affected by a split feature are removed if there is a collapse.

## Lucky Wagon Spins
3,4 or 5 Bonus symbols trigger the Lucky Wagons Spins with 2, 3 or 4 rows of the reel area open initially. The feature starts with 3 spins and will reset to 3 spins every time a coin lands in the reel area. All coin values are valued as times the active bet.

During the feature the top row will reveal enhancers for each spin - Coin values, Multipliers, Dynamite, Beer or Dwarf. An enhancer is activated when a coin lands on the reel below the enhancer.

UPGRADE CRYSTAL
The additional 7th reel may reveal Upgrade symbols each spin. All the enhanceractivating Coins landing on the Upgrade symbol row will gain the below additional
effect:

• Coin values and Multiplier will cause the Coin to continue to be active for the next spin.
• Dynamite, Beer and Dwarf features become persistant features, activating their effects until Lucky Wagon Spins end.

The Below enhancers may appear on the top row:

COIN VALUES
Sets the value of the coin.

MULTIPLIERS
Multiplies the value of all coins on the reel below the enhancer.

DYNAMITE
Adds the Dynamite count as times bet value to the triggering coin. It then throws as many Dynamites as its count on reel positions.

• If it lands on Dirt: will remove Dirt symbol.
• If it lands on Coin: will double its Coin value.
• If it lands on Chest: will activate the Chest.

BEER
Reactivates 0-5 old Coins which makes them trigger their respective enhancers for the next spin. Triggering coin gets value 1x.

DWARF
Collects all values in the reel area at the triggering coin position.

COLLECT CHESTS
During the feature the bottom row Collect Chest can be activated by Dynamites. When a Collect Chest is activated it will collect the values in the reel area for the corresponding column above for every spin.

Lucky Wagon Spins will end if the reel area is full and there are no active features for remaining spins.
