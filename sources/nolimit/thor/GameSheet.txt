Game Features
Thor is a high-volatile slot with a high
feature hit frequency. The pay out mostly

Thor’s Lightning Bonus!
Triggered by Thor’s Lightning Bonus symbol on reel 5.
Awards one of Thor’s features - multiplier, random sticky
Wilds or sticky Wild Reels.

Thor nudges down and awards a respin and the feature is
active as long as Thor is on the reels.
