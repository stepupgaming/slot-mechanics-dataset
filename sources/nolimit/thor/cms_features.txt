## Thor’s Lightning Bonus!
Triggered by Thor’s Lightning Bonus symbol on reel 5. Awards one of Thor’s features - multiplier, random sticky Wilds or sticky Wild Reels.\nThor nudges down and awards a respin and the feature is active as long as Thor is on the reels.

## Rune Raze
Randomly triggered on any spin. When activated, all runes will be turned into one specific rune symbol.

## Hammer Time
Randomly triggered on any spin. When activated, all high paying symbols - Ring, Goblet, Axe and Helmet - will be turned into Thor’s Hammer symbols.

## Scatter Energy Collect
Landing two or more Scatters triggers Scatter Energy Collect feature, unleashing it’s power to reach the Asgard Spins. As long as there are any Scatters on the reel, the Scatters will nudge down one step and respin all reels without Scatters.\nAsgard Spins will be activated if you collect energy from three Scatters. Any additional Scatter Energy will award +2 extra spins in Asgard Spins.

## Asgard Spins
Thor’s Lightning Bonus will activate more frequently on reel 5 during the Asgard Spins. Thor can also award +2 extra spins, along with multiplier, random sticky Wild or sticky Wild reels.\nThor will award an additional feature for each nudge, stacking up multiple features as long as Thor is visible on the reels.
