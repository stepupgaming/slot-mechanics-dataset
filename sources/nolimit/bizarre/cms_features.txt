## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Chimera Spins
Landing 3 Scatter symbols will trigger 3 Chimera Spins. A Scatter landing on the middle reel transforms into a Wild and remains sticky throughout the feature.

Before the bonus round begins, the three middle reels spin individually. During this spin: Wild symbols, extra spin symbols or empty symbols can land. When Wild or extra spin symbols land then it stays on its position and respin is awarded. This continues until no new extra spin or Wild symbols land.

If Wild symbol lands then it will remain sticky throughout the feature.
If a number symbol lands, its value is added to the number of Chimera Spins. If a Wild is split by an xSplit® symbol, it gets a multiplier that also remains sticky for the entire feature.

## Super Chimera Spins
Landing 2 Scatter symbols and a Super Scatter in the base game will trigger 3 Super Chimera Spins. In addition to Chimera Spins features, all Scatters will transform into Wild symbols and will remain sticky throughout the feature.

## Coinage
Player can activate the Coinage feature at the cost of 200 times the base bet. During this feature, only coins with multiplier values and special symbols can land. If any positions are empty after a spin, a respin is triggered, filling the empty positions with new symbols. When this feature ends, sum of all multiplier values are added before multiplying with base bet.

- Coin values: 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 20x, 50x, 100x, 200x, 500x, 1,000x.

Below are the special symbols that can land during Coinage feature:

- xSplit®: It splits all coins and used Collectors on its row and doubles their values. Once triggered, the xSplit® symbol will be removed.

- Collector: Collects the total value of all visible coins and then removes them from the grid. The used Collector remains on the reel, and if another Collector lands later, it also collects the value of the previously used Collector.

- Scatters: When 3 Scatters are collected, it triggers Chimera Spins after Coinage feature ends. If one of the three Scatters is a Super Scatter, it triggers Super Chimera Spins instead.

## xBoost (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Scatter symbol on the second reel. The guaranteed Scatter might be a Super Scatter.

3 times more likely to hit Chimera Spins/Super Chimera Spins.

## Bonus Hunt (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 30 times the base bet, the player is 49 times more likely to activate Chimera Spins/Super Chimera Spins.

During this mode, only Scatters or empty symbols can land, with a maximum of one Super Scatter.

## Extra Spins
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when Chimera Spins/Super Chimera Spins ends. The additional spin will keep the Wild symbol along with it’s multiplier value of the previous spin.

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the Wild symbol multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the Chimera Spins/Super Chimera Spins, then the Extra Spin option will be presented.

## Monsters On The Loose
The max payout of the game is 20,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 20,000 times the base bet is awarded.
