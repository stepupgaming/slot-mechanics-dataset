## Power Stone
On any spin in main game the Power Stone feature may randomly trigger, a 2x2 area will become wild and all occurrences of one of the Ore symbols will also turn into Wild.

## Gem Forge
The Gem Forge feature is randomly triggered in main game. When triggered one of the Ore symbols is randomly picked and will replace all other Ore symbols on the reels. On top of that all Ore symbols that land will turn into its corresponding Gem symbol for a bigger win.

## Alchemy Spins
Landing 3 Scatters will award Alchemy Spins. The four awarded spins focuses on one Ore symbol at the time. The Ore symbol will one by one turn into wild and remain in its position during the entire feature.
