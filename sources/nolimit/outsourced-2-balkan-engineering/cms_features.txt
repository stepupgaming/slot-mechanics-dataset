## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## Infectious xWays®
Infectious Ways® reveals a regular paying symbol of size 2-4. Additionally, it may also reveal a blocker symbol which does not have a payout. 

Infectious Ways® also infects all occurrences of the revealed paying symbol into size 2-4. If more than one Infectious xWays® symbol lands on the reels, then all Infectious xWays® symbols will reveal the same symbol. 

Multiple infections on the same symbol will increase its size multiplicatively.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Elevator
An Elevator symbol can reveal either an instant bonus mode or a blocker symbol in base game. 

During the bonus modes, landing an Elevator symbol may award 1, 2, or 7 additional spins, or reveal a blocker symbol.

## xNudge® Balkan Wild
The xNudge® Balkan Wild can land in any position in the game and can nudge with a multiplier up to 999x. 

For every step the xNudge Balkan Wild nudges, the Wild multiplier increases by 1. Several xNudge® Balkan Wild multipliers add to each other for a total win multiplier. 

xNudge® Balkan Wild may malfunction and remain a 1x1 Wild.

## Print Spins
Winning symbols are paid out before triggering Print Spins (respin). 

These winning symbols will disappear leaving an empty position for new symbols to come in. The multiplier on those winning positions increases by one for each Print Spins. 

Multipliers will be counted for regular paying symbols only. Print Spins ends when there are no more wins. If an Infectious xWays® lands during Print Spins, then its revealed symbol multiplies with the increased multiplier on that position during its pay out. 

The symbol multiplier on the winning Ways symbol position will revert to the previous value +1.

## Tilt Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 4 Tilt Spins. 

Any symbol multipliers from the base game are kept during spins.

## Tiltier Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Tiltier Spins. 

This bonus works the same as Tilt Spins but with 3 additional spins.

## Filthiest Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
At a cost of 999 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 7 Filthiest Spins. 

This bonus works the same as Tiltier Spins but starts off with x2 multipliers on all positions.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 1.35 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on the top position of the second leftmost reel.

More than 3 times likely to activate Tilt Spins/Tiltier Spins.

## Balkan Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 13.5 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on the initial spin and on any respin triggered by a win. 

More than 20 times likely to activate Tilt Spins/Tiltier Spins.

## 3x Print Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 15 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x3 multipliers on all positions.

## 6x Printier Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 200 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x6 multipliers on all positions.

## 8x Printiest Spin (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 750 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x8 multipliers on all positions.

## God Mode
Chase Big Szukcess by landing “B”, “O”, “O” and “M” symbols on the reels at a cost of 1,111 times the bet.

## xTra Spin™
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the multiplier state of the symbols of the previous spin. 

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the multiplier values. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then only the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented. 

Bonus symbols can land in Extra Spin.

## Big Szukcess
The max payout of the game is 11,111 times the base bet. When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 11,111 times the base bet is awarded. 

Big Szukcess can be achieved in the main game and bonus modes by landing “B”, “O”, “O” and “M” and will award the player with an instant Big Szukcess.
