## Shadow Rows
Increase the number of rows and win lines for a additional cost for more frequent wins and bigger payouts.

## Freespin with Golden symbol
Three or more Scatters (which are also wild) grants 10 Freespins with a special Golden expand symbol. If your not happy with first draw you may make a redraw. Golden symbols expand to cover the whole reel when additional wins can be rewarded. The Freespin can be chosen with more or less Shadow rows altering the number of spins given.

## Gamble
Gamble winnings on 50/50 Gamble for double or nothing. Gamble to bonus gives the possibility to gamble the winning straight into Freespin for mysterious wins.

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy functionality
Nolimit Bonus - allows the players to buy directly into\nthe Freespin. Choose to buy Buffalo Horde and Prairie Multipliers or the more expensive Stampede Super Bonus.
