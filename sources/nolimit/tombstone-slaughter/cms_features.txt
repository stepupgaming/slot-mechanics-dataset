## Reel Setup
The game has 6 reels with row setup as 2-3-3-3-3-2.

The sixth reel has some special properties and limited symbols:

- Only 2-high stacked Revolver and Character symbols present on the reel. Reel gets inactive if half symbols land.

- No low value symbols are present on the reel.

## xNudge®
A stacked Wild symbol that will always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases the Wild multiplier by 1.

When a part of the xNudge® Wild symbol lands on the reels, it will begin to nudge up or down until it is fully visible on the entire reel.

Several Wild multipliers add to each other for a total Wild multiplier.

Only appears in the 4 middle reels.

## Slaughter Scatter
Slaughter Scatter can only land on middle 4 reels.

Landing only 2 Slaughter Scatter symbols will convert them to Reel Split Wilds as well as split itself and the other symbols on that reel in half, thus doubles the symbols on that reel.

## Slaughter Spins
Landing 3 Slaughter symbols in base game will trigger 5 Slaughter
Spins.

Last reel will always land a full Character symbol with random win multiplier (5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 15, 21, 99) or a Revolver.

El Gordo’s Revenge Meter at the bottom of the reel area starts with Level 1 and can go up to Level 5.

In addition to Cylinder features from main game, below features can also be triggered from Revolver:

- Killcount Multiplier: awards multiplier +1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21, 34, 55, or 89 to the future levels (or last level) on the meter.

- Revenge Level Up: fills all the spots on Revenge Meter for upgrading to the next level.

Landing 4 Slaughter symbols in base game will trigger 5 Slaughter Spins with 2 spots on Revenge meter filled in and an upgraded Nudge multiplier for each Revenge level as 1, 4, 5, 6, 7 respectively.

Landing a full Revolver will upgrade the remaining spins to Massacre Spins and award +2 spins.

## Massacre Spins
Landing 3 Slaughter symbols and 1 Revolver symbol in base game will trigger 5 Massacre Spins.

In addition to Slaughter Spins features, Revolver symbol will be sticky on last reel.

Landing 4 Slaughter symbols and 1 Revolver symbol in base game will trigger 5 Massacre Spins with 2 spots on Revenge meter filled in and an upgraded Nudge multiplier for each Revenge level as 1, 4, 5, 6, 7 respectively.

## Quickdraw
Revolver symbol can only appear on the last reel in main game, Slaughter Spins and Massacre Spins.

Landing a full Revolver symbol will trigger 3 to 6 Cylinder features:

- Backstab Wild: turns a randomly chosen regular paying symbol on reel 2-5 to Wild.

- Shootout Wild: turns all instances of a randomly chosen regular paying symbol on reel 2-5 to Wild.

- xSplit®: splits a randomly chosen symbol and doubles it’s size on reel 2-5. In case of Nudge, doubles it’s multiplier.

- Infectious xSplit®: splits all instances on reel 2-5 of a randomly chosen symbol and doubles it’s size.

- Justice Wild: places an Nudge Wild at the position of a randomly chosen character symbol on reel 2-5, which then will nudge up until the top of the reel. Each nudge up increases the Wild multiplier by 1. 

- Boothill Multiplier: randomly selects a Character symbol and gives a win multiplier ranging from x5 - x999 (5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 22, 36, 44, 45, 54, 58, 90, 310, 523, 718, 805, 999).

If Massacre Spins is triggered from Nolimit Bonus tool, Quick Draw feature will not activate for the main game spin.

## El Gordo’s Revenge Meter
El Gordo’s Revenge Meter at the bottom of the reel area starts with Level 1 and can go up to Level 5. Starting multiplier for xNudge® is set for each level starting from 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 respectively.

Bonus symbols can land on reels 2-5. Each Bonus symbol awards +1 spin to the current level, fills the El Gordo’s Revenge Meter by 1 spot, and increments the multipliers for the future levels by +1. By collecting 4 Bonus symbols, Revenge Meter advances to next level.

Next level will activate when all the current spins are played.

## xRIP®
Any spin in main game or bonus mode that generate combinations where the total payout according to the paytable is below the base bet triggers xRIP®.

When xRIP® is triggered there is no payout.

## El Gordo Revenged
The max payout of the game is 500,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 500,000 times the base bet is awarded.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 1.2 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Slaughter symbol on the second reel.

## Super xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 15 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 2 Slaughter symbols.

## xNudge® Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 35 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 2 xNudge® Wilds.

## xNudge® & Revolver Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3,000 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 1 xNudge® Wild and 1 fully stacked Revolver symbol.
