## Collapsing Mine
Each spin begins with 3 active rows and for each collapse, an additional row becomes active, up to 6 rows. A new collapse can be triggered by a winning combination, xBomb Wild explosion or a Wild Mining feature.\nWinning symbols are paid out and removed before triggering the next collapse, which will, in turn, make all symbols fall downwards, leaving the empty position for new symbols to drop down to.

## Lucky Wagon Spins
3, 4 or 5 bonus symbols will trigger the Lucky Wagons Spins feature with 2, 3 or 4 rows of the reel area open initially. The feature starts with 3 spins and will reset to 3 spins every time a coin lands in the reel area. All coin values are valued times (x) the active bet.\nDuring the Lucky Wagon Spins feature, the top row will reveal enhancers for each spin - Coin values, Multipliers, Dynamites, Bag, Chest or the Dwarf. An enhancer is activated when a coin lands on the reel below the enhancer.

## xBomb Wild Multiplier
An xBomb Wild symbol substitutes any symbol except for the Bonus symbols. An exploding xBomb Wild removes adjacent symbols except for Bonus symbols and will increase the win multiplier by one for the next collapse.\nAll xBomb Wilds will explode before the next collapse, except when the Wild Mining feature is triggered. An xBomb Wild will explode when there is a win, no matter\nif it’s a part of the winning combination or not. It will explode and trigger a new collapse if there isn’t a win, and wild Mining feature is not triggered.\n

## Wild Mining
Wild Mining will be triggered in the main game when 3, 4 or 5 of the same type of symbol align horizontally and there is no winning combination. 3, 4 and 5 symbols, awards 1, 2 or 3 wilds respectively. All other positions above the bar will explode as long as it’s not a Bonus or a Wild symbol, and it will trigger a new collapse.
