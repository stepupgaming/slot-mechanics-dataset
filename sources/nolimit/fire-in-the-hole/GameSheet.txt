Game Features
Collapsing Mine
Each spin begins with 3 active rows and for each collapse,
an additional row becomes active, up to 6 rows. A new
collapse can be triggered by a winning combination,
xBomb Wild explosion or a Wild Mining feature.

Winning symbols are paid out and removed before
triggering the next collapse, which will, in turn, make all
symbols fall downwards, leaving the empty position for
new symbols to drop down to.
