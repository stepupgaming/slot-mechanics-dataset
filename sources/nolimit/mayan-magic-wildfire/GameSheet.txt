Mayan Wilds
Activated by landing a Wild on the middle three reels. Will
also transform the center position on the middle reel to
a sticky Wild that remains throughout the entire feature.

multiplier.

Mystery Sync

synchronize the symbols on the three middle reels to dish
out really nice wins, as this machine pays both ways.
