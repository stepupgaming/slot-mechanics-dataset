## Mayan Wilds
Activated by landing a Wild on the middle three reels. Will also transform the center position on the middle reel to a sticky Wild that remains throughout the entire feature. All additional Wilds awards extra spins and an increased multiplier.

## Mystery Sync
This feature is randomly triggered on any spin. It will synchronize the symbols on the three middle reels to dish out really nice wins, as this machine pays both ways.

## Mystery Wild
This feature is also randomly triggered on any spin. The symbol that lands on the center position of the middle reel will turn into a Wild, and any additional of that specific symbol on the three middle reels will also transform into Wilds.\nThis will activate Mayan Wilds, hopefully with a major head start!
