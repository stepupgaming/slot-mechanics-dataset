## Riot Respins
Riot Respins are awarded when landing 2 Bonus symbols.

Bonus symbols are converted to Molotov Wilds and lock the reels.

All Reels with Wilds are locked.

Additional Bonus symbols landing during Riot Respins award an additional respin and are converted into Molotov Wilds, after payout.

## DisOBEY Spins
Bonus symbols award 8 DisOBEY Spins, played with 3 Jumping Wild Symbols and the Top Train Enhancer.

## ChAos Spins
Bonus symbols award 8 ChAos Spins, played with 4 Jumping Wild Symbols and both Train Enhancers.

## Mass Transit BBQ Spins
Bonus symbols award 8 Mass Transit BBQ Spins, played with 4 Jumping Wilds, both Train Enhancers and the Burning cart stopping every spin.

## Jumping Wilds
Jumping Wild symbols and Burning Jumping Wild symbols are active during Free Spins.

They change positions on the reels between each spin.

All Wild Multiplier enhancers triggered by Burning Jumping Wild symbols will become persistent during the remainder of Free Spins.

Wild Multipliers are additive.

## xWays®
An xWays® symbol can hold 2 to 4 of the same symbol inside and all xWays® symbols will show the same symbol.

When xWays® in base game are expanded to 4 high, the symbol can be super expanded up to 20 high.

xWays® Mystery Symbol is activated in main game and Free Spins.

xWays® in Free Spins will expand to 4 high and contains 4, 6 or 8 of the same symbol.

## Train Enhancers
Train Enhancers are awarded when a Jumping Wild Symbol or Burning Jumping Wild symbol is on the same reel.

Top Train Enhancers: Wild Multiplier, xWays®, Molotov Wilds, Extra spins, all reels become 6 high, Upgrade Free Spins, Enable Bottom Train Enhancer.

Bottom Train Enhancers: Wild Multiplier, Extra spins, Burning Jumping Wild.

If the Burning cart contains a Burning Jumping Wild then it will convert all Jumping Wilds into Burning Jumping Wilds on it’s reel.

Wild Multipliers are additive.

## Wild Symbols
Molotov Wild turns all positions above into Wild symbols.

Molotov Wilds may land in main game, Riot Respins and may be activated during Free Spins.

Pipe Bomb Wild symbols contain 2 to 4 Wild symbols and turns all positions above into Pipe Bomb Wilds of the same size.

Pipe Bomb Wild may land in main game and Riot Respins.
