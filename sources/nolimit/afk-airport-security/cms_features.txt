## xZone®
Landing xZone™ applies +1 multiplier value on every regular paying symbol and Wild adjacent to it; vertically, horizontally and diagonally. Multiple xZone™’s can land on the same row but not on the same reel

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## Reel Area
The game features a 5 reels and 3 rows layout with a feature meter placed at the top. Feature symbols appear in the feature meter.

## Wild
A Wild symbol substitutes for any other symbol except a Bonus symbol.

## Quarantine
Once activated, any 1 or 2 random low paying symbols will not appear on the reel area.

## Full Body Scanner
Any 1 or 2 low paying symbols are selected and all of them are replaced with a random high paying symbol.

## Not My Bag
3 to 6 mystery symbols land randomly on the reels and they reveal any regular paying symbol. All mystery symbols will reveal the same symbol.

## Arm Slides
Any Wild present on the reel area expands to fill the entire reel with Wild symbols.

## Class Upgrade
Any regular paying symbol is selected and all their instances on the reel area get a multiplier of x3, x5, x8 or x10.

## Extra Seat Space
Any regular paying symbol is selected and all their instances on the reel area are split into 2.


## Full Row Booking
Any 1, 2 or 3 reels are selected and all symbols in the chosen reels are split into 2, except a Bonus symbol.

## Lithium-ion Batteries
Any 1 or 2 regular paying symbols will be replaced by Wild symbols and all adjacent symbols will be split into 2, except Bonus symbol.


## Turbulance
All non-winning symbols on the reel area are shuffled, except Bonus symbol. Symbols will retain their multiplier and symbol size after the shuffle.

## "Random" Security Search
Any 1, 2 or 3 regular paying symbols will be replaced by Wild symbols.

## Air-Mile Multiplier
A random win multiplier is revealed and applied to all wins. Possible multipliers values are x3, x5, x8 and x10.


## Connecting Flight
Win combinations are paid both ways.

## Economy Class Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Economy Class Spins. It starts with 1 randomly selected feature that stays active for the remaining spins. 

Any additional feature that activates will award +1 spin and will stay active for the rest of Economy Class Spins.

## Business Class Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Business Class Spins. It has the same features as Economy Class Spins, but starts with 2 random features.

## First Class Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 First Class Spins. It has the same features as Business Class Spins, but starts with 3 random features.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 1.4 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus on the second reel.

## 3 Mile High Club (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 12 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 3 features.

## 12 Mile High Club (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 4,500 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed all 12 features.

## The Captain's Cap
The max payout of the game is 19,693 times the base bet. When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 19,693 times the base bet is awarded.
