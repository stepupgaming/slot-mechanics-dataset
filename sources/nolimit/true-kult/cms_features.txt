## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Baptism By Fire
Stacked symbol that will reveal a random high paying Devotee symbol of the same type or Scatter symbol in base game.

Skull Wilds may be revealed during the bonus modes.

## Scatters
Landing 2 or more Scatter symbols opens reel 6 immediately, expanding to 729 win ways.

Landing 3 Scatter symbols activates ‘The Seance’ bonus mode.

Landing 3 Scatter symbols activates ‘The Sacrifice’ bonus mode.

Landing a Scatter on reel 6 during The Seance will activate the Sacrifice bonus mode.

## True Believer Wilds
The three stacked true Believer symbol only lands on reel 6. Only activates if the sixth reel is opened.

The True Believer symbol will randomly turn low paying symbols on reels 2-5 into Wilds.

The True Believer symbol will transform into the same Devotee symbols.

## Tarot Multipliers
Tarot Multiplier symbols land on reel 6, and will only activate when reel 6 is open. These Tarot symbols will hold a ways multiplier, and will reveal one of the Devotee symbols.

These multipliers will be collected in the bonus modes if not used when they land. The collected multiplier is sticky throughout the Sacrifice bonus mode.

Tarot Multipliers can be the following: x5, x10, x15, x20, x50, x99

## Left Hand Path
The maximum payout of the game is 23,237 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 23,237 times the base bet is awarded.

## The Seance
Landing 3 Scatter symbols awards 7 spins of The Seance bonus mode.

Three All Seeing Eye frames will be activated at the start of the bonus mode and all symbols that land within the frame will be split into two symbols. The All Seeing Eye frames will move randomly across reels 2-5 on every spin.

The sixth reel will be open during the Seance, which will hold True Believer symbols and Tarot multipliers. Tarot Multiplier symbols will turn into one of the Devotee symbols with an additional symbol multiplier. If the symbol multiplier isn’t used, it will be saved for next time the same symbol lands. Once transferred to the reel, the multiplier will be removed.

If an additional Tarot Multiplier lands and reveals the same Devotee symbol as already collected, the highest multiplier will be collected and the symbol multiplier count will be updated. The count signifies how many of that symbol that may be upgraded with the Tarot Multiplier.

Landing the Skull Wild symbol will award +1 spin and award an additional All Seeing Eye frame. If the Skull symbol lands within an All Seeing Eye frame it will turn into a double wild, but won’t award an additional All Seeing Eye.

## Sacrifice Spins
Landing 4 Scatter symbols awards 10 Sacrifice Spins.

Four All Seeing Eye frames will be activated at the start of the bonus mode and all symbols that land within the frame will be split into two symbols. The All Seeing Eye frames will move randomly across reel 2-5 on every spin.

The sixth reel will be open during The Sacrifice, which will hold True Believer symbols and Tarot Multipliers. Tarot Multiplier symbols will turn into one of the Devotee symbols with an additional symbol multiplier. The symbol multiplier will be sticky and saved for the remainder of the bonus mode and transferred to the reel every time the symbol lands.

If an additional Tarot Multiplier lands and reveals the same Devotee symbol as already collected, the highest multiplier will be collected and the symbol multiplier count will be updated. The count signifies how many of that symbol may be upgraded with the Tarot Multiplier.

Landing the Skull Wild symbol will award +1 spins and award an additional All Seeing Eye frame. If the Skull symbol lands with an All Seeing Eye frame it will turn into a double wild, but not award an additional All Seeing Eye.

## Nolimit Bonus Buy*
Buy your way straight into the different bonus features, ranging from 68x to 400 times the base bet.

The Nolimit Bonus feature may however be removed in some regulated markets.

• 7 ‘The Seance’ Spins can be bought for 68 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.

• 10 Sacrifice Spins can be bought for 400 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.

• Lucky Draw can be bought for 234 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.
