## Manhattan Freespins
Getting 3 or more scatters will transport the game to an evening setting where all high paying symbols can turn to Wilds. Collect all high paying symbols to fill its corresponding meter, to turn the symbols into Wilds for that spin and award extra spins.

## Bugsy / Betty Goes Wild
Randomly triggered on each spin and during this spin all Bugsy/Betty symbols will become Wilds. With the symbols being stacked and the regular Wild as well it can give some nice hits.

## Wild Party Respins
Landing both Bugsy and a Betty symbols covering the entire reel will trigger a respin with the landed stacked become locked in Wilds. Any additional stacked Bugsy and Betty covering the entire reel will have the same behaviour for a massive respin potential.
