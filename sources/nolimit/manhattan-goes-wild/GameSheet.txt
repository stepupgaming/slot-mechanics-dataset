MANHATTAN GOES WILD
Welcome back to the 1920 and flourish in
the golden age of the Art Deco era. This
game brings you to a world when every
night was a cocktail party!

With the beatiful art deco style and fancy glittery dresses
Manhattan Goes Wild brings a little old fashion rich flavour
to Nolimit City game portfolio.

Focus is on the two main characters Bugsy and Betty
being the main attractions. Both are stacked on the reels
and may go Wild and act as a Wild symbol, combined with
stacked Wilds in the game and the 243 win ways there is
great potential!

Manhattan Freespin allows all high paying symbols turn
Wild! Enjoy the cocktail party and hope they all go Wild!
