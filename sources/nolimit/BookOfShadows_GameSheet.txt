BOOK OF SHADOWS
Hidden in the shadows lies the book of
enchantments to alter the reality of “reel”
world. The book will give the power to
change the setup for every spin in this
interactive title.

A 5x3 reel area that can expand to 5x5 setup in both main
game and freespins giving additional paylines and wins.
This game gives multiple choices for the player to dwell
in the shadows choosing between the number of rows,
locking reels and gamble.

The bonus round gives a Golden symbol that may award
addtional wins for big payouts with full screens of symbols.

Book of Shadows is available with a dual RTP setup at a
range of 96.01% and 94.09% (DX1).
