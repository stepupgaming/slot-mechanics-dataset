## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## Reel Area
The reel displays one symbol per spin, which can either be a multiplier or blank.

If a blank symbol appears, there is no win.

When a multiplier appears then its value applies to the base bet.

## It’s Alive Spins
Landing a Bonus symbol in main game will trigger 10 It’s Alive!! Spins.

## Devour Spins
Landing a Bonus symbol during It’s Alive!! Spins will trigger Devour Spins and award 5 extra spins per Bonus symbol.

The highest multiplier value which lands during this bonus mode gets locked above reel area and awarded to player with each spin.

## Linked Wins
The reel order when played with 2 or 4 reels are starting from the top leftmost reel. In case of a multiplier is higher than its subsequent reel, it transfer it’s value to subsequent reel as shown in the images.

Multiplier value of all reels are added before payout.

## 2 Reels (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 4 times the bet, 2 reels will become active.

## 4 Reels (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 16 times the bet, 4 reels will become active.

## God Mode
Chase the Max Win by landing 10,000x symbol on the reel at a cost of 2,500 times the bet.

## Max Win
Max payout of the game depends on number of reels as mentioned below:

1 active reel - 10,000x
2 active reels - 20,000x
4 active reels - 40,000x

The Max Win is awarded depending on the number of active reels and the game round ends if the total win exceeds the max payout.
