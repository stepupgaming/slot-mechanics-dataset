Base Game Mechanics and Features

Winning combinations are formed by landing matching symbols across the reels, but the primary engine behind the gameplay involves tracking the four designated Hot Zones located at the corners of the central grid. Landing specific symbol or color combinations inside these corner positions determines which of the main features will activate. For instance, the Disco Wild can occasionally trigger during standard spins, landing exclusively on the center position of the middle reel before spreading to all adjacent positions to create sudden multi-line wins. If you land four symbols of the same type across all the corner zones, it initiates the Colossal Spins feature where the middle three reels merge into one massive symbol to match the triggering icon type, significantly boosting your payout chances.

Bonus Rounds and Free Spins

The premium bonus tiers are entirely dictated by coordinating the symbols that drop into the corner structures. Achieving an identical match of symbol colors across all four corners activates the Lucky Wheels bonus, which presents a three-leveled prize wheel capable of dispensing instant wins up to 1,000x the total stake. For the ultimate reward sequence, landing the exact same identical symbol in every single corner triggers the Super Freespins mode. This feature grants seven highly lucrative rounds where the triggering symbols transform into sticky wild icons that lock in place for the remainder of the session to foster massive combination potential. Game Features

Disco Wild

Disco Wild will keep occassionaly produce some big wins in the main game. Disco Wild may only land on center position on middle reel in the main game and it will spread to adjecant positions next to it.

Hot Zones

The 4 Hot Zones triggers the features. Depending on what symbols that lands within the Hot Zones will trigger one of the three features – Super Freespins, Colossal Spins and Lucky Wheels.

Colossal Spins

Landing 4 of the same symbol type – i.e. all cherries, sevens or diamonds – will trigger the Colossal Spins where reel 2-4 will become one colossal symbol. The colossal symbol will substitute for all symbols of its type.

Lucky Wheels

Landing the same colored symbols – I.e all blue, all green or all red symbols - in all 4 Hot Zones awards the Lucky Wheels feature, that triggers a 3 level chocolate wheel that awards instant cash wins up to 1000x the bet.

Super Freespins

Landing the same symbol in all 4 Hot Zones awards 7 Super Freespins where trigger symbols will become Sticky Wilds throughout the feature. Expect disco fever wins!
