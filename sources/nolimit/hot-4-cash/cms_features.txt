## Disco Wild
Disco Wild will keep occassionaly produce some big wins in the main game. Disco Wild may only land on center position on middle reel in the main game and it will spread to adjecant positions next to it.

## Hot Zones
The 4 Hot Zones triggers the features. Depending on what symbols that lands within the Hot Zones will trigger one of the three features – Super Freespins, Colossal Spins and Lucky Wheels.

## Colossal Spins
Landing 4 of the same symbol type – i.e. all cherries, sevens or diamonds – will trigger the Colossal Spins where reel 2-4 will become one colossal symbol. The colossal symbol will substitute for all symbols of its type.

## Lucky Wheels
Landing the same colored symbols – I.e all blue, all green or all red symbols - in all 4 Hot Zones awards the Lucky Wheels feature, that triggers a 3 level chocolate wheel that awards instant cash wins up to 1000x the bet.

## Super Freespins
Landing the same symbol in all 4 Hot Zones awards 7 Super Freespins where trigger symbols will become Sticky Wilds throughout the feature. Expect disco fever wins!

## Colossal Spins 
Landing 4 of the same symbol in all 4 Hot Zones will turn reel 2-4 into one colossal symbol. 

## Lucky Wheels 
Landing the same colored symbols in all 4 Hot Zones awards a multi tiered instant cash Lucky Wheel.
