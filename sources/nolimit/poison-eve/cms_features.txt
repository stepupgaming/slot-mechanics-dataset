## Expanding Vine Wilds
Wilds that land will always expand to cover entire reel for exciting main game wins..

## Liquid Magic
This feature starts when Poison Eve lands fully on the first reel, and alla bottles will transform into the Poison Eve symbol. A feature that may pay up to 2000 times the bet.

## Flower Power
Triggered by landing a bonus symbol in the Hot Zone on reel 5. The features opens up a portal on the left most reel, and any additional bonus symbols that lands will create additional portals.\nSymbol landing in the Hot Zone will populate all portals and all of that symbol type will expand on all reels, which will pay 2000x in total for Poison Eve herself.
