## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## Reel Area
The reel area consists of 6 reels with 7 rows. The top 3 rows are locked and can be unlocked by Forsaken Fingers.

Cascading reels: Any winning symbols will explode and
be removed and let new symbols fall into place which may
award additional wins.

Symbols in locked positions stay in place and do not drop
during cascades.

## xNudge® Wild
When an xNudge® Wild symbol lands, it gradually nudges
down to the bottom row, becoming a stacked Wild with a multiplier.

Each nudge increases the xNudge® Wild multiplier and the
multiplier size depends on how many symbols it nudges to reach the bottom row. xNudge® multipliers are additive.

xNudge® Wild multiplier applies only to wins involving the xNudge® Wild.

A Wild symbol substitutes for any symbol except Scatter symbol.

## Forsaken Fingers
Forsaken Fingers unlocks the nearest position on the
locked rows of its reel and any adjacent reels, if present.
It then converts itself into a Wild.

## Eye Collector & Spider
Lowest-paying Dead Legend symbol has 1 eye, and all others have 2 eyes.

Eye Collector

Collects eyes from Dead Legend symbols in the active reel area. For every 5 eyes collected, the total win multiplier is increased by 1. It then converts itself into a Wild. 

Spider

Gets activated only when an Eye Collector symbol is present. Spider symbol randomly generates 4, 8, or 12 eyes, which are collected by the Eye Collector. It then converts itself into a Wild.

## Entombed Spins
Landing 3 or more Scatter symbols triggers Entombed Spins.

Any Scatter may upgrade to a Red Moon Scatter. Spins awarded are +2 for each Scatter and +3 for each Red Moon Scatter.

Each progression level has a Dead Legend symbol that must collect 4 gems to advance to the next level. On the first level, all Dead Legend symbols on the reels have the same number of eyes as in the base game. With each level upgrade, the Dead Legend symbol for that level doubles its number of eyes.

Each level upgrade awards +2 spins.

Unlocked positions from Forsaken Fingers and the total win
multiplier remain persistent between spins.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is 3 times more likely to trigger Entombed Spins.

## Booster Forsaken Fingers (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 20 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed minimum of 3 Forsaken Fingers.

## Booster xWays® + 7 Rows (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 30 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed
at least 2 xWays with 7 active rows.

## Booster xNudge® + 7 Rows (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 90 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed
at least 1 Nudge with 7 active rows.

## Booster Eye Collector + 7 Rows (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 199 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed an Eye Collector and Spider symbol with 7 active rows.
