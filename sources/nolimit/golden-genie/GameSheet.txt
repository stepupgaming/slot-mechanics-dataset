GOLDEN GENIE
The worst kept secret in the mysterious
Middle East, is the Genie in the Lamp,
who’s owner will summon the Genie and be
granted his most desirable wishes.

With 5x3 reel area and packed with remarkable features,
you will experience the riches of the Middle East. Will you
see your wishes come true?

The Genie Parade bonus round will go on until no new
Genies appear, and the multiplier will stack for each
new Genie that leaves the reels. Get ready for multiplier
madness!

Golden Genie and the Walking Wild is available with a dual
RTP setup at a range of 96.03% and 94.11% (DX1).

Game Features
Search for the long lost lamp and its Genie
to activate the three features.

Genie Wishes
Align Aladdin with his lamp and activate the feature - all
Aladdin and Lamp symbols turn into Wilds and get get a
random multiplier between x2-x10.

Genie Lamps
This feature may trigger during any spin in the main game
with reel 2 and 4 will be covered with Wilds, and there’s
something extra - all wins pays both ways!

Genie Parade
This feature may grant your wishes. Activate with 3 Genie
symbols to start of the bonus round. All Genie Wilds will
walk to the left and increase its multiplier for each step.
The bonus round continues as long there’s a Genie on the
Reels. The multiplier value will increase for each step, and
the Golden Genie multiplier value will be passed on once       Game Information
to another Genie Wild, when it leaves the reels.
