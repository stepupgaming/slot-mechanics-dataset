## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xNudge® Wild
Nudge Wild symbols can land on reel 3-4 during the main game and the Spirit Spins bonus mode.

A stacked Wild symbol that will always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases the Wild multiplier by 1.

When a part of the xNudge Wild symbol lands on the reels, it will begin to nudge up or down until it is fully visible on the entire reel.

Several Wild multipliers add to each other for a total Wild multiplier.

When Split Wild splits an xNudge Wild, it will add one more Wild to that reel.

## xSplit® Wilds & Wilds
Regular Wild symbols can land on all reels.
xSplit Wild symbol can land on reel 5-6 during the main game, and reel 1-2 during the
Spirit Spins bonus mode.
xSplit® Wild symbols split all symbols to the left and right and double that symbol. If a
symbol is split more than once, it will be displayed with a multiplier.
When xSplit® Wilds splits Ways symbols, the Ways symbol multiplier will be doubled for
every split.
xSplit® Wild that splits Scatter symbols, will transform the Scatter to an expanding Wild
that fills the entire reel with Wild symbols. Scatter symbols doesn’t split when Spirit Spins
is activated.
xSplit® Wild symbols converts to two regular Wild after landing and can be split by
another xSplit® Wild.

## Scatters
Landing 3 or more Scatter symbols activates Spirit Spins.
Landing 4 Scatter symbols activates “Spirit Spins: All Aboard” mode,
Landing 5 Scatter symbols activates “Spirit Spins: Double Joy” mode
where all symbol win multipliers are doubled.
In main game, a Scatter symbol may be split by xSplit Wilds if the
Spirit Spins isn’t activated. The Scatter symbol will then transform into
expanding Wilds, that covers the entire reel.
A Scatter symbol is always split in the Spirit Spins.

## Spirit Spins
Before the bonus mode starts, a setup spin will be triggered. This setup
mode will decide the number of spins awarded and what win multiplier
each of the four top paying character symbols will get.
Before the Spirit Spins starts, one of the character symbols will be
randomly chosen and active during the bonus mode.
Landing 4 or more Scatter symbols, all characters will be active during
the bonus mode.
Landing 5 Scatter symbols will award double the win multiplier value
during the setup spins and during the bonus mode.
During the bonus mode, the top reel may award an increase win
multiplier value to the active characters.
In Spirit Spins: Randomly awarded +1x, +2x or double the current
collected win multiplier value.
In Spirit Spins Double Joy: Randomly awarded +2x, +4x or double the
current collected win multiplier value.
The win multiplier value is updated for the current spin and remain for
the rest of the bonus mode.
Collect additional Scatter symbols, which awards +1 extra spin each.
Collect 3 Scatter symbols during Spirit Spins to upgrade the bonus
mode to Spirit Spins: All Aboard, which activates the win multipliers on
all character symbols.
