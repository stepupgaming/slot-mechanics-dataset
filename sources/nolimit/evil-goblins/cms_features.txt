## Resurrection Wilds
You need 3 Dead Wilds to activate and transform them into\nResurrection Wild Symbols. The last Wild symbol to land will also\ncount as a hefty multiplier.

## Goblin Sacrifice
Match the goblin on top of the reels with the symbol on the reel\nbeneath that same goblin to trigger a Goblin Sacrifice. get a match\nfor a grenade to be thrown as a sacrifice, turning him into a Wild\nsymbol.

## xBomb
The inevitable explosion removes all symbols except Scatter\nsymbols, Dead Wilds and Resurrection Wilds whilst also increasing\nthe win multiplier with +1. Every time an xBomb symbol appears,\none row will be added after the explosion, can go up to 7 rows.

## Evil 4
Evil 4 feature is triggered when all goblins at the top of the reels\nare matched. The throned goblin will be sitting on reel 1, where\na multiplier of x4 or higher is boosted. The symbols are counted\nas 4 of each, multipliers will increase by 1 each time a matching\ngoblin appears on the top reel.

## Goblins Feast Spins
4 spins with the Evil 4 multiplier activated. Each matching goblin\nwill stay in its position with the rest of the bonus round, whilst the\nmultiplier will increase +1. Any new matching goblins will turn into\na skeleton and stay for the remainder of the feature, all the while\nstill turning matching symbols into Wilds. Explosive Goblins Feast\nSpins has an upgrade of an xBomb on every new spin.

## All Hail the King
Max payout of the game is set to 31.969x times the base bet.\nTrigger this, and the game round will end.

## Fresh Meat Spins
8 spins will provide you with Dead Wilds on each spin, and a\nhigh chance of getting a Goblin Sacrifice along with an xBomb to\ntrigger. An upgrade to Goblin Feast Spins is earned by triggering\nthe Evil 4 feature. 

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy Functionality
Buy your way straight into one of the different bonus features,\nranging from 60x to 666x the base bet
