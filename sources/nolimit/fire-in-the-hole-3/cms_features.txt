## Collapsing Mine
Each spin begins with 3 active rows and for each collapse an additional row becomes active up till 6 rows.

A new collapse can be triggered by the following events:

- A winning combination
- xBomb® Wild explosion
- Wild Mining feature triggered
- xHole™ feature triggered

Winning symbols are paid out and removed before triggering next collapse, which will make all symbols fall downwards leaving empty position for new symbols to drop in from above.

## xBomb® Wild Multiplier
An xBomb® Wild symbol substitutes for any symbol except Bonus symbols.

An exploding xBomb® Wild removes adjacent symbols except Bonus symbols and will increase the win multiplier by its value for the next collapse.

All xBomb® Wild will explode before the next collapse except when the Wild Mining feature is triggered.

## Wild Mining
Wild Mining will be triggered in main game when 3, 4, 5, or 6 of the same kind of symbols align horizontally or vertically and there are no winning combinations, active xHole™ or active xBomb® Wild.

The triggering symbols will be removed creating Wild symbols in the middle positions of the alignment:

- 3 symbols award 1 Wild
- 4 symbols award 2 Wilds
- 5 symbols award 3 Wilds
- 6 symbols award 4 Wilds

All positions above the bar, except for Bonus and Ice Buried symbols, will explode and trigger a new collapse. Ice Buried symbols will be revealed and become active with the collapse.

## Buried Features
Ice symbols in main game may contain a Wild, xSplit®, Win Multiplier, xHole™, Bonus or a Max symbol. Feature symbols buried in ice are inactive until the ice block they are contained in is removed by an exploding xBomb® Wild.

Symbols contained in Ice blocks will also be revealed when split by xSplit® except for win multiplier.

Empty Ice blocks and Ice blocks containing win multiplier affected by a split feature are removed if there is a collapse.

The Ice block can contain win multipliers of 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x, 20x, 50x, or 100x.

A “Max” symbol appears as a 2x2 symbol in ice block. Max Win is awarded as soon as all the ice is cleared from the symbol.

## xHole™
Activates when xHole™ symbol appears on the reel.

Only regular paying symbols are drawn from the reels, then scattered to random positions with split symbols of size 1 to 3. xHole™ leaves an empty space on its position which triggers a collapse.

Winning symbols are paid and removed before the xHole™ triggers.

Landing multiple xHole™ symbols causes the symbol size to increase multiple times.

## xSplit®
An xSplit® symbol splits all other symbols on its reel, doubling the value of those symbols.

Bonus, xSplit® and xHole™ symbols cannot be split.

Once all xSplit® symbols have activated, they all turn into one of the regular symbols.

Ice blocks affected by xSplit® are removed if there is a collapse.

## Gamble
Triggered by a win of at least 5 times the base bet in the main game.

An option to win a level of Lucky Wagon Spins costing more than the total round win is offered. The player can gamble their winnings for a chance to win a level of Lucky Wagon Spins.

After winning a Gamble, the player is presented with the option to gamble for the next level of Lucky Wagon Spins.

Choose Collect to stop gamble at any time.

The player can toggle the Gamble button to enable or disable the Gamble feature.

## Lucky Wagon Spins
3, 4 or 5 Bonus symbols trigger the Lucky Wagon Spins with 2, 3 or 4 rows of the reel area open initially. The feature starts with 3 spins and will reset to 3 spins every time a coin lands in the reel area. All coin values are valued as times the active bet.

6 Bonus symbols will trigger the Lucky Wagon Spins with a Persistent Dwarf guaranteed on the first spin and 4 rows of the reel area open.

During the feature the top row will reveal enhancers for each spin - Coin values, Multipliers, Dynamite, Persistent Dynamite, xHole™, Evil Dwarf or Persistent Dwarf. An enhancer is activated when a coin lands on the reel below the enhancer. Below enhancers may appear on the top row:

Coin values 
Sets the value of the coin.

Multipliers
Multiplies the value of all Coins, Persistent Dwarf and the coin value of Persistent Dynamite on the reel below the enhancer.

Dynamite
- Adds the Dynamite count as times bet value to the triggering coin. It then throws as many Dynamites as its count on reel positions.
- If it lands on Ice block: will remove the Ice block.
- If it lands on Coin: will double its Coin value.
- If it lands on Chest: will activate the Chest.
- If it lands on Ice containing a symbol: symbol will be revealed.
- If it lands on Dynamite/Persistent Dynamite: will double its Coin value.
- If it lands on Persistent Dwarf: will double its Coin value.

Persistent Dynamite
On each spin, it drops a number of Dynamites equal to its count on the reel positions.

## Persistent & Evil Dwarf
Persistent Dwarf

Collects all values in the reel area at its triggering position for each spin.

Evil Dwarf

Triggers a Golden Spin which reactivates all the coins on reel area. Coins will trigger their respective enhancers, boosting their existing values.

## xHole™ & Frozen Wagon Spins
xHole™

Triggers 3 Frozen Wagon Spins and transforms itself into a 1x coin. After the 3 Frozen Wagon Spins, Lucky Wagon Spins are resumed and the spin left count is reset to 3.

Frozen Wagon Spins

Top enhancer row will only contain Coins. All the existing Coins in the reel area will collect the Coin values from the enhancers and add them to their existing values.

## Guaranteed Dwarf (Nolimit Bonus)
Awards Lucky Wagon Spins with 6 Bonus symbols and a guaranteed persistent Dwarf at a cost of 4,000 times the base bet.

## Evil Dwarf Forever (Nolimit Bonus)
Awards Lucky Wagon Spins with 3 Bonus symbols and a guaranteed Evil Dwarf on top row during each spin at a cost of 700 times the base bet.

## Booster 1 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on reel 2.

## Booster 2 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 5 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed at least 3 Bonus symbols in ice blocks.

## Booster 3 (Nolimit Booster)
Activate all 6 rows for a cost of 5 times the base bet.

## Golden Nugget (Nolimit Bonus)
Chase the Max Win (Fire in the Bowl) by landing guaranteed Max symbol in the ice block, available for 7,000 times the base bet.

Gamble option will not be available.

## Fire In The Bowl
Winning 70,000 times the base bet will clean the mine out and the game round will be over.
