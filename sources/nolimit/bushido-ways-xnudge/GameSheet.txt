Shogun Spins
When landing 3 Bonus symbols, 8 Shogun Spins are
awarded. Any additional Bonus symbol that lands awards
+1 Shogun Spin. For every spin, at least one guaranteed
single wild will land on reel 2, 3, 4 or 5. It is possible to
upgrade to Geisha Spins by landing The Wild Geisha
during Shogun Spins. Extra Geisha Spins are awarded
equal to the multiplier achieved.

Geisha Spins
When landing 3 Bonus symbols at the same time as the
The Wild Geisha lands, 8 Geisha Spins are awarded.
For each nudge the The Wild Geisha does, a sticky
multiplier is awarded.
