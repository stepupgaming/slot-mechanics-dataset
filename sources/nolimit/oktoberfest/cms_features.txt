## Party Spins
Each spin can randomly trigger a special mode where the player will be rewarded super-stacked reels with one of the mid SYMs. This spin will increase your heart rate where you’ve got a very good chance of landing the entire reel with only one medium symbol!

## Pretzel Party Spin
Each spin can randomly trigger the special Pretzel Party Spin. If you’re lucky to trigger the Pretzel Party Spin, all the low win symbols (10 to A) turns into wilds for a massive win!

## Prost Win-spins
If you land the stacked wilds on reel 2 and 4, you will trigger a special wild-mode with respins with guaranteed wins! The two characters will toast and create a single wild on reel 3, that will give you two more guaranteed win-spins that ensures a massive win!

## Bierspins
If you trigger 3 scatters you will be awarded 10 Bierspins. In this high-paying free spin feature the scatters becomes collectibles that will award you additional spins and increased multipliers - up to 5x multiplier and 12 extra free spins!
