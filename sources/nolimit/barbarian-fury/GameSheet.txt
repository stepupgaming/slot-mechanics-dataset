Game Features
Barbarian Fury is all about the xNudge®

xNudge® Roaring Wild
A 4-row high Wild symbol exists on reel 2, 3 and 4 and will
always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases the
Wild multiplier by 1.

Several Wild multipliers add to each other for a total Wild
multiplier on the line win for huge wins!

Barbarian Respin
May be triggered when 3 or more of the same Barbarian
symbol lands on the reels. When triggered the barbarians
will nudge to fully visible and lock for a respin. Wilds will
also lock and be present in the respin.
