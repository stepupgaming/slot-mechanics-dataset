## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Cut Reel
The reel setup is 4-4-4-4-4 rows across 5 reels and a Progression bar beneath the bottom row.

Winning combinations and the symbols below them, on each reel, falls down allowing the new symbols to fall in.

## Progression Bar
There are 5 levels of progressions. The Progression Bar resets for each level up. Exceeding collect will be carried over.

Level 1: 15
Level 2: 25
Level 3: 50
Level 4: 80
Level 5: 110

Fallen Wild, High or Medium paying symbols advances the Progression bar.

Wild symbol = +3 per winning symbol
High paying symbol = +2 per winning symbol
Medium paying symbol = +1 per winning symbol

Each level converts medium paying symbols to high paying symbols.

Three dropped Scatters will progress the player to the Tzantza Spins rewarding +6 spins, each skipped level rewards the player +2 Free spins.

Progression bar resets every new game round.

## Soul Wilds
There are three Soul Wilds with individual Soul Collectors which collect Souls from dropped High Paying symbols after a win.

The Soul Wild feature get enabled in the below order:

Level 1: Happy Soul Wild - Requires a total of 4 souls to be activated.
Level 3: Sad Soul Wild - Requires a total of 10 souls to be activated.
Level 5: Angry Soul Wild - Requires a total of 18 souls to be activated.

Soul Wilds can only be activated if no more Souls can be collected and its collector has been filled.

The number of activated Soul Wilds determines the size of the Wild zone: 1x1, 2x2 or 3x3 and multiplies the general multiplier by 1, 2 or 3 respectively. The general multiplier resets after this event.

All Scatters within the Wild Zone, awards +1 Free spins each.

The Soul Wilds Collector resets whenever it fails to collect enough Souls or gets activated until next spin.

## Ayahuasca Spins
Reaching a new level of the progression bar awards +2 Free spins till level 4, +6 Free spins when advancing to Tzantza Spins.

Buying Ayahuasca Spins instantly progresses the progression bar to level 3 and awards +6 Ayahuasca Spins.

Three of the medium paying symbols are replaced with three high paying symbols.

## Tzantza Spins
Buying Tzantza Spins instantly progresses the progression bar to level 5 and awards +10 Tzantza Spins.

All the medium paying symbols are replaced with high paying symbols.

## Pick A Potion
When Tzantza Spins has ended without a Max Win, the player will have two choices:

Butterfly Bottle: Guarantees a win ranging from 5 - 8,000 times the base bet.

Skull Bottle: Gamble with your accumulated win for a chance of winning the Max win or lose it all. The chance is determined by your accumulated win amount, higher the better.

## Headful Collection
The max payout of the game is 15,950 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 15,950 times the base bet is awarded.
