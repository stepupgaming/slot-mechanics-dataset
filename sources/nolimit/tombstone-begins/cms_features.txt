## Win Respins
Wins will award a respin with sticky symbols. Winning symbols and all their instances on the reel area will remain sticky during respin.

When a new symbol gets sticky an additional respin is awarded. Below are the symbols that can become sticky:

- New winning symbols
- Contains a symbol type from the current win
- Wild
- Bonus (only in base game)
- Revolver

Payout is awarded when there are no respins left.

## Unleash Symbol
When the Unleash symbol lands, all Revolvers immediately fire all remaining bullets one after another. 

Only one Unleash symbol can land per spin.

## Refill Symbol
When the Refill symbol lands, all Revolvers are refilled to 6 bullets.

Only one Refill symbol can land per spin.

## Revolver Symbol
Revolver symbols can land on any reel. Each Revolver contains 3 to 6 bullets, and only one bullet is fired by each Revolver per spin. A bullet targets regular paying symbols or Wilds if it has the xSplit® feature.

If there are multiple Revolvers, they fire one by one from left to right and top to bottom. Each bullet can trigger one of the following features:

- Wild: Once fired, that target symbol turns into a Wild. Wild symbols can also land normally on the reel area.

- xSplit®: This Bullet can hit regular paying symbols or Wilds. It doubles the multiplier value of the target symbol. 

- Upgrade: This bullet can hit any regular paying symbol except the highest paying symbol. Once fired, the target symbol an all their instances on reel area upgrades into a random higher-paying symbol.

- xNudge® Wild: xNudge® Wild activated when its bullet hits above the top row of reels 2 and 4 at random. After activation, it will always nudge to fully visible on the target reel, when there are no respins left. Each nudge increases the win multiplier by 1. 

- xNudge® Multiplier: This bullet hits above the top row of reels 2 to 4 at random. If xNudge® Wild activates on the target reel, its xNudge® multiplier increases. The possible multiplier increases are 2x, 3x, 10x or 100x. xNudge® multipliers are additive. 

- Random: Once fired, the bullet changes randomly to a different bullet. If there are no valid targets available, the bullet fires without any effect.

## Gunslinger Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 6 Gunslinger Spins. Each additional Bonus symbol awards +1 spin.

The xNudge® multiplier does not reset between Gunslinger Spins.

## Outlaw Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 8 Outlaw Spins. In addition to Gunslinger Spins features, all Revolvers land as fully loaded.

## Widowmaker Spins
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 10 Widowmaker Spins. In addition to Outlaw Spins features, all Revolvers will fire all their remaining bullets before xNudge® activation or when no respins are left, after respin is awarded. 

Once all Revolvers have fired, win combinations formed by firing a bullet will not award additional respins.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus on second reel.

## xNudge® Showdown (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 30 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Revolver loaded with 2 xNudge® bullets and 2 xNudge® multiplier bullets.

## xNudge® Fully Loaded (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 190 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Revolver loaded with 3 xNudge® bullets and 3 xNudge® multiplier bullets.

## Revolver Mayhem (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 999 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 6 Revolvers with random number of bullets on top and bottom positions of reel 2, 3 and 4. 

Among these Revolvers, 3 will always contain xNudge® multiplier bullets and one xNudge® bullet.

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a bonus round ends. The additional spin will keep the xNudge® multipliers of the previous spin. It is guaranteed to land a Revolver with xNudge® bullet.

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the Nudge® multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the total win, then the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented.

## Vengeance
The max payout of the game is 20,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 20,000 times the base bet is awarded.
