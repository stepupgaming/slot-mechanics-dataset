## xBet®
At a cost of an extra +10% on the base bet, the last reel will be blocked with increased chances of landing Scatter.
Increases the chance of entering Middle Management/Ivory Tower Spins by more than 2 times.
The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xNudge® Wild
The xNudge® Wild symbols can only land on reels 2-5.
Landing xNudge® Wild will always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases the win multiplier by 1. Once it covers the reel, it will also nudge additional 0-9 steps.
Several xNudge® Wild multipliers multiply with each other for a total Nudge multiplier.

## Wilds
Wild symbols can only land on reels 2-5 and transform into:
Green Wild: an active Wild symbol with a random symbol multiplier ranging 1×- 5x.

Red Wild: a dead symbol.

## Scatters

Scatter symbols will only land on reels 2-5.
Landing 3 Scatter symbols will trigger 10 Middle Management Spins.
Landing 4 Scatter symbols will trigger 12 Ivory Tower Spins.

## Middle Management Spins
Layoff and Lawsuit symbols can land. Lawsuit symbol acts before the Layoff symbol.
Each Layoff symbol will increase the win multiplier by 1 whereas each Lawsuit symbol will decrease it by 1. Tech Layoff multiplier which will act during Ivory Tower Spins gets doubled when Layoff symbol lands.
The multiplier value can never go below 1 or above 9999. The win multiplier is applied at the end of the round.
Landing Overtime symbol will award +1 spin per symbol. Landing 2 or more Overtime symbols in the same spin will also upgrade the bonus mode to Ivory Tower Spins.

## GOD MODE 
Chase Big Win Not Found by landing a Mega symbol on the reels at a cost of 1370 times bet.

## Ivory Tower Spins
Instead of Layoff and Lawsuit symbols, Tech Layoff and ‘CtrI+Alt+Del’ symbols can land.
‘Ctrl+Alt+Del’ symbol acts before the Tech Layoff symbol.
The Tech Layoff symbol will add a win multiplier and then the value of the next Tech Layoff multiplier is doubled. The initial and minimum value of these multipliers is 1, the maximum value is 9999. The win multiplier is applied at the end of the round.
The ‘Ctrl+Alt+Del’ symbol divides the value of the next Tech Layoff by 4 (rounded down to nearest integer).
Landing Overtime symbol will award +1 spin per symbol.

## Big Win Not Found
The max payout of the game is 9217 times the base bet.
When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 9217 times the base bet is awarded.
Big Win Not Found could also be achieved in main game by landing the mega symbol fully stacked on reels.
