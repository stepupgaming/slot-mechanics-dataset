Enhancer Cells
On the top and bottom of each reel there is locked
enhancer cell positions. When triggered to open they may
reveal 1 of the top 5 paying symbols, Wild, Razor Split or
xWays symbol. Razor split with split all regular positions
into double, landing on top and bottom will split them
twice. xWays symbol will all reveal the same regular paying
symbols, stacked at 4.
