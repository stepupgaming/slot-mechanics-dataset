## Enhancer Cells
On the top and bottom of each reel there is locked enhancer cell positions. When triggered to open they may reveal 1 of the top 5 paying symbols, Wild, Razor Split or xWays symbol. Razor split with split all regular positions into double, landing on top and bottom will split them twice. xWays symbol will all reveal the same regular paying symbols, stacked at 4.

## Bonus triggers
Land 1 or 2 bonus symbols will open the Enhancer cell on its respective reel, also turning the bonus symbol into a wild. Landing 3 or more bonus symbol will trigger the Lockdown Spins.

## Lockdown Spins
3, 4 or 5 bonus symbols will trigger Lockdown Spins with either 1, 2 or 3 starting Jumping Wilds. The Enhancer reels will be open and active throughout the whole feature, with the ability to add additional Jumping Wilds and spins by landing another bonus symbol.\nThe amount of spins will be decided before the start of the feature, being revealed in the enhancer cells along with\nan additional multiplier on a random convict premium symbol.

## Split & Jumping Wilds
In the main game, Split Wilds can land on any reel. The Split Wild will split all the regular symbols on the same reel, but not itself. Jumping Wilds will jump to a random regular reel position for each spin. When a Jumping Wild lands on a reel with a Razor Split enhancer it will double its multiplier, which will stay for the remainder of the feature. The maximum multiplier for a Jumping Wild is 512x.

## Psycho Manhunt
The maximum payout of the game is 150,000 times the base bet. When the total exceeds this, the lockdown spins will end.

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy Functionality
Buy your way straight into the allusive Lockdown Spins, with an option to trigger it with 3, 4 or 5 Bonus symbols at 100x, 400x or staggering 2000x the bet.
