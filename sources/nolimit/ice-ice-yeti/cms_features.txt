## Yeti Shake
On any spin in main game the Yeti Shake may trigger and will break 2 to 10 ice blocks for an instantly increased game area for huge wins, right of the bat.

## Ice Ice Respins
This feature is triggered by the Yeti Wild, that will let out a powerful roar to break 2 to 3 ice blocks and award a respin. Any additional winning symbols will award yet another respin, and another chance to break even more ice blocks for that chillin’ 16 807 win ways!
