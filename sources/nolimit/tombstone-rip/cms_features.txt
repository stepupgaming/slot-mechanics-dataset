## Enhanced Bet
By increasing the basic stake by 10%, you may chase the gallows with a guaranteed Scatter symbol. Harsh, but higher reward.

## xNudge Wilds
Make sure to hang on - a stacked Wild symbol will always nudge to be fully visible with each nudge increasing the Wild multiplier.

## xSplit Wilds
Take cover, xSplit Wild only appears on the last reel and counts as 2 symbols high. When landing it will cut symbols to the left in half.

## xRIP
The dead aren’t mourned here; xRIP is triggered by any spin if the total win is less than the base stake, then there is no payout...

## Hang ‘em High Freespins
Third times the charm - Landing three Hang ‘Em High Scatter symbols will activate 8 Hang ‘Em High Freespins. The win multiplier in the bonus mode will be increased by all Wild symbol types and it will remain for the entire bonus round.

## Boothill Freespins
Hold your horses! This is were things go batshit crazy. Land 3 Scatters plus a Boothill Scatter to trigger 10 spins. Only Cowboy symbols lands on the last reel, but it’ll transform the other of the same symbol to Wilds. It’s time to let them hang and burn in hell - so let’s add a multiplier, from 5x-999x, on a random Cowboy for each spin. Nuts!

## El Gordo’s Revenge
The max payout of the game is 300 000 times the base bet.

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy Functionality
Buy your way straight into one of the different bonus features, ranging from 70x to 3000x the base bet.
