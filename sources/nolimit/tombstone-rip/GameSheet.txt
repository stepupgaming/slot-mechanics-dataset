Game Features
Enhanced Bet
By increasing the basic stake by 10%, you may chase the
gallows with a guaranteed Scatter symbol. Harsh, but
higher reward.

xNudge Wilds
Make sure to hang on - a stacked Wild symbol will always
nudge to be fully visible with each nudge increasing the
Wild multiplier.

xSplit Wilds
Take cover, xSplit Wild only appears on the last reel and
counts as 2 symbols high. When landing it will cut symbols
to the left in half.

xRIP
The dead aren’t mourned here; xRIP is triggered by any
spin if the total win is less than the base stake, then there
is no payout...
