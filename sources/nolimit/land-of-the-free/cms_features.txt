## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## Reel Area
The game has 5 reels with reel 5 locked in the main game.

The reel setup is 4-4-4-4-4 and a Conveyor Belt beneath the bottom row. All reels are unlocked during the Tsunami Spins and Submerged Spins.

## xBet®: Asscar Mode
At a cost of an extra 25%, the player is guaranteed a Scatter symbol on reel 1.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Wild and Stacked Symbols
In main game the Wild symbols can land on any reel.

Partial and fully stacked character symbols may land. Fully stacked character symbols convert into stacked Wild symbols.

## Conveyor Belt
A wide range of features may appear on the Conveyor Belt on each spin, which alters the reels somehow.

## Bottles
A crate with 1 or 2 bottles.

1 bottle will convert any random paying symbol on the same reel into
Wild.

2 bottles — The first bottle will convert any random paying symbol on the same reel into Wild and the second bottle will split that Wild symbol into two parts.

## Protein Powder Keg
PROTEIN POWDER KEG

Gets activated if there is a partially stacked symbol on the same reel.

It converts the partially stacked symbol to a fully stacked symbol.

## Benzo Bear
BENZO BEAR

A half stacked Benzo Bear only exists in main game and will convert 3-
8 random symbols into Wild and/or Scatter.

A fully stacked Benzo Bear only exists in Submerged Spins and will convert all symbols below water surface into Wild.

## Mr. SPLIT \u0026 Chainsaw
Mr Split can only appear on reel 5 if it’s unlocked and will split all symbols except Scatter from reels 1-5 on a row according to its size. It can appear half or fully stacked in the main game.

During Tsunami Spins and Submerged Spins, Mr Split appears as a fully stacked symbol and will split all symbols that are below water surface.

The chainsaw will be active if Mr Split is present on unlocked reel 5.

Chainsaw will add 1 more split to the already split positions on the same reel where it appears.

## Rubber Ducks
When appears during Tsunami Spins and/or Submerged Spins, it awards +2 spins per Rubber Duck.

Each Rubber Duck increases the level of water by one row during Tsunami Spins.

## TITAN SPIN (Idiot Spin in the Bonus Buy) 
When a submarine enters during any spin in the main game or Tsunami Spins/ Submerged Spins, Titan Spin will be triggered. One of three ‘Presidants’ may land in the submarine window and the multiplier of that ‘Presidant’ will be applied to the base bet. If no ‘Presidant’ lands, it is a no win.

The three ‘Presidants’ and their multipliers are:

Presidant 3: 15000x
Presidant 2: 25000x
Presidant 1: 57000x

Titan Spin is termed as Idiot Spin for bonus buy.

## Scatters
In main game the Scatter symbols can land on any reel.
Landing one Scatter anywhere on reel 1-4 unlocks reel 5.

Landing 3/4/5 Scatter will trigger Tsunami Spins and will award
12/15/20 spins respectively.

## Tsunami Spins
All reels are active. It starts with bottom 2 rows submerged under water.

When all 4 rows are covered by water, Tsunami Spins are upgraded to Submerged Spins.

## Submerged Spins
All 4 rows are covered by water level.

## Pearly Gates
The max payout of the game is 57,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 57,000 times the base bet is awarded.

## Nolimit Bonus Buy

• Submerged Spins can be bought for 258 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.

• Lucky Draw can be bought for 115.5 times the bet, maximum possible bet is €100.00.

• Idiot Spin can be bought for 2,310 times the bet, the maximum possible bet is €100.00.
