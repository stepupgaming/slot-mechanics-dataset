## xBomb®
An xBomb Wild symbol substitutes any symbol except for the Bonus symbols. An exploding xBomb Wild removes adjacent symbols except for Bonus symbols and will increase the win multiplier by one for the next collapse.

All xBomb Wilds will explode before the next collapse, except when the Wild Mining feature is triggered. An xBomb Wild will explode when there is a win, no matter if it’s a part of the winning combination or not. It will explode and trigger a new collapse if there isn’t a win, and wild Mining feature is not triggered.

## Cluster Payout
Cluster wins are achieved with 5 or more matching symbols that are connected horizontally and/or vertically.

After a winning Cluster, the symbols are destroyed allowing new ones to drop in.

## Big Symbols
There are 3 types of symbol sizes: 1x1, 2x2 that corresponds to 4 symbols and 3x3 that corresponds to 9 symbols.

If there is no room for Big Symbol to drop, the gap underneath it will be filled with the same symbol as the Big Symbol.

## Multiplier
Every new avalanche after a win increases the multiplier by x1.

## Trapped Wild
The Trapped Wild is shown with a highlight effect animation on a symbol. When a symbol with a Trapped Wild highlight is part of a win or is removed by an xBomb® Wild explosion, it transforms into a Wild symbol. The transformed Wild will be kept for the next avalanche.

Trapped Wild highlight effect can be triggered on all symbols and symbol sizes.

## Wild Rush
Once triggered, it randomly transforms 2-5 regular paying symbols into Wild symbols.

## Force Shift
Once triggered, it randomly transforms 1, 2 or 3 regular paying symbols into another regular paying symbols of same or different kind.

## xBomb® Wild
Once triggered, it randomly transforms 1-3 regular paying symbols to xBomb® Wild symbols.

An xBomb® Wild symbol substitutes for any symbol except for Tsar Side Bonus symbol.

An exploding xBomb® Wild removes adjacent symbols except for Tsar Side Bonus and Wild symbols increasing the win multiplier by one for the next collapse. If a big symbol is adjacent to exploding xBomb® Wild, the entire big symbol will be removed.

All xBomb® Wilds will explode before the next collapse.

## Destruction Spin
Destruction meter is filled when 25 win symbols are collected.

When the meter is full and there are no additional wins, the player is awarded a Destruction Spin where Wild Rush, Force Shift and xBomb® Wild are guaranteed to trigger at least once.

The Destruction meter resets after each spin.

## Revolution Spins
When the Destruction meter is filled with 25 win symbols during Destruction Spin, Revolution Spins are triggered.

Once triggered, the player can choose one feature: Wild Rush, Force Shift or xBomb® Wild and receives 5, 6, or 7 Revolution Spins based on the choice. The selected feature triggers at least once on each spin, while the other features may trigger randomly.

Revolution Spins starts with a x15 win multiplier. When Destruction meter is filled with 30 win symbols, +2 spins are awarded and total win multiplier gets doubled. The Destruction meter resets after each spin.

## Tsar Side Spins
When a Tsar Side Bonus symbol lands during the Destruction Spins and the Destruction meter is filled with 25 win symbols, 6 Tsar Side Spins are triggered. All 3 features (Wild Rush, Force Shift and xBomb® Wild) will trigger at least once on each spin.

Tsar Side Spins starts with a x15 win multiplier. When Destruction meter is filled with 30 win symbols, +2 spins are awarded and total win multiplier gets doubled. The Destruction meter resets after each spin.

## xBoost 1 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 5 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 1 feature (Wild Rush, Force Shift or xBomb® Wild) randomly.

## xBoost 2 (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 12 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 2 features (Wild Rush, Force Shift or xBomb® Wild) randomly.

## xBoost 3 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 30 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Destruction Spin.

## xBoost 4 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 60 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed at least 1 Trapped Wild symbol of size 2×2 or 3x3.

## Bombs Away!
The max payout of the game is 19,775 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 19,775 times the base bet is awarded.
