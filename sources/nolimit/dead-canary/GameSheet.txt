                                                                                       FEATURES IN THE GAME




                                                                                                        REEL AREA
                                                                                                        A 3-4-3-4-3-4 reel area starting as a 3-3-2-2-1-1 with three rows blocked by barriers.


                                                                                                        One blocked row will open up after a win, an xBomb® explosion adjacent to the barrier, or a Wild Mining feature.


                                                                                                        The multipliers in the bottom positions are awarded whenever the barrier is removed and the symbol is destroyed or if the removed barrier
                                                                                                        reveals a Scatter symbol.

                                                                                                        WILD MINING
                                                                                                       Wild Mining is triggered in the main game when 3,4 or 5 of the same kind of paying symbols align diagonally, when there is no winning
                                                                                                       combination.


                                                                                                       The triggering symbols will be removed and create a Wild symbol in the middle positions of the alignment:
                                                                                                       - 3 symbols award 1 Wild bomb.
                                                                                                       - 4 symbols award 2 Wild bombs.
                                                                                                       - 5 symbols award 3 Wild bombs.


                                                                                                       Wild bomb explosions will increase the win multiplier by 1 for the next collapse.


                                                                                                       All other symbols will explode, except xBomb® symbols. If a Scatter symbol gets blown away, it reappears in a random position in the next
                                                                                                       collapse.

                                                                                       FEATURES IN THE GAME




                                                                                                        SCATTERS
                                                                                                       In the main game, the Scatter symbols can land on any reel, but if it lands behind the barrier it will not come in play until it is unblocked.


                                                                                                       Scatter symbols will stick to their positions and remain there during Canary Freespins, unless any Scatter symbol lands in the vault position.
                                                                                                       It will then be pushed to an available space towards the centre.


                                                                                                       There are two types of Scatter symbols:
                                                                                                       - Silver cage Scatter symbol.
                                                                                                       - Gold Cage Scatter symbol.


                                                                                                       Three Scatter symbols of any kind will trigger Canary Freespins.

                                                                                                        BONUS MODE: CANARY FREESPINS
                                                                                                        Three Scatter symbols of any kind triggers Canary Freespins. The reel area becomes a 4-3-4-3-4-3-4 with the four vaults in the corners being
                                                                                                        locked.


                                                                                                        The four vaults are locked in freespins but collect coins and multipliers from the three neighbouring positions. The vaults can be opened
                                                                                                        by xBomb explosions. When a vault gets unlocked, everything that is collected will be awarded as a win at the end of the bonus mode.


                                                                                                        Scatter symbols start with 1x and can collect coins, multipliers, upgrades, the rat and the dwarf enhancer.
                                                                                                        - Silver cage Scatters: Collect in an x-shape 1 position from itself.
                                                                                                        - Gold cage Scatters Collect in an x-shape from the entire rows.


                                                                                                        All coin values are valued as ( X ) the active bet.


                                                                                                        Scatter symbols and vaults will stop collecting after 65,000x.


                                                                                                        The Scatter symbol furthest to the left will pick up first.


                                                                                                        Scatter symbols start with 3 hit points and the collected wins will only be awarded if the birds die.


                                                                                                        xBombs and gas makes the Scatter symbol lose one hit point. When the bird dies it will respawn in its cage.


                                                                                                        Canary Freespins start with 3 spins. xBombs reset the spin count to 3.


                                                                                                        Scatter symbols will be randomly positioned from any bonus buy
