## Free Spins
Three or more scatters activates a free spin bonus mode. During the Free spins scatters can re-trigger and award you up to 50 free spins! During free spins the slot will enable an extra 20 fixed betlines, plus a x5 multiplier on all wins and use the same respin behaviour on any wins.

## Star Spin
In addition, each base game win on high symbols is stored in the start spin bar, a feature that will give one free spin with extra wilds, with the symbols you’ve previously won on. When the star spin bar is filled you will activate this feature.
