## Reel Area
5x5 reel setup with bottom row locked and a Ritual Bar beneath the bottom row. Bottom locked row is filled with Wild symbols and each level progression unlocks a Wild symbol starting from the left

Winning combinations will be destroyed and remaining symbols will tumble down, allowing the new symbols to fall in.

## Ritual Bar
There are 5 levels of progressions. The Ritual Bar resets for each level up. Exceeding collection will be carried over.

- Level 1: 20
- Level 2: 30
- Level 3: 40
- Level 4: 60
- Level 5: 80

Landing the Scatter symbols and winning combinations of medium and high paying symbols advances the Ritual bar.

- Medium paying symbol = +1 per winning symbol
- High paying symbol = +2 per winning symbol
- Scatter symbol = +10 per Scatter symbol

Each level removes the lowest paying symbols and also converts medium symbols to high paying symbols.

Ritual bar resets for every new game round.

## Candle Spins
Reaching a new level of the Ritual Bar awards +2 Candle Spins till level 4, +6 Free Spins when advancing to Reign of Terror Spins.

Before any new level, any plausible wins pay out before progressing to the next level.

During Candle Spins, the Ritual Bar performs same as in base game.

## Sticky Wild
Sticky Wild can land on any reel.

Candle Spins & Reign of Terror Spins: The Wild drops down to the bottom of the reel and becomes Sticky Wild, until it is part of the winning combination or a new bet is made.

## xSplit®
There are 2 types of xSplit® symbols:-

- Dagger xSplit®: Splits it’s row.
- Guillotine xSplit®: Splits all symbols below it on its reel.

×Split® will always appear with x2 multiplier on it. Symbols that are split by xSplit® also get their multiplier values respectively. Both Dagger and Guillotine will turn to a Wild after the split. Multiplier value reflects the symbol count on that reel.

Maximum symbol size can be 32x.

## Halifax Gibbet Spins
Buying Halifax Gibbet Spins instantly progresses the Ritual bar to level 3 and awards +6 Candle Spins.

3 of the low and medium paying symbols are replaced with 3 high paying symbols.

## Reign of Terror Spins
+6 Free Spins are awarded when entering Reign of Terror Spins.

Any potential wins has to be completed before entering Reign of Terror Spins.

The Ritual Bar is removed and the reel area becomes a 5x5 reel with only high paying symbols.

Landing a Scatter symbol gives a +1 spin.

Buying Reign of Terror Spins awards you 10 Free Spins
