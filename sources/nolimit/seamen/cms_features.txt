## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Win Respins
Wins award a respin on winning positions. Winning symbols are paid out before triggering the respin. These winning symbols will disappear leaving an empty position for new symbols to come in. The multiplier on those winning positions increases by one for each respin.

Multipliers will be counted for regular paying symbols only.
On each respin, one extra Fire Frame is added to a position where there is no Fire Frame.

## Fire Frames
On each spin, Fire Frames are randomly added to positions of the reel area. Each position that gets a Fire Frame has its multiplier increased by +1. Winning symbols with Fire Frames have their position multipliers increased by +2.

## Bombs
If there are no winning symbols, Bombs will explode and remove symbols based on their type. Any symbol removed by a Bomb has its position multiplier increased by +1, or +2 if there’s a Fire Frame on that position.

There are 3 types of Bombs that explode in below listed sequence:

- Coconut: Removes itself and increases the position multiplier by 5, 10, 15, 20 or 100, in addition to the standard increase.

- Cross Bomb: Removes itself and all regular symbols on its row and column.

- Naval Mine: Removes itself and all low paying symbols on the reel area.

## Molotov Fire
Transforms itself into a Wild and adds Fire Frames to all symbols on its column.

## Rigged Spins
Landing 3 Bonus Symbols will trigger 5 Rigged Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Free Spins.

## Super Rigged Spins
Landing 4 Bonus Symbols will trigger 7 Super Rigged Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Free Spins.

Additionally, more Fire Frames are awarded per spin on average.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 2.5 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on the top position of the second leftmost reel.

## Big Load Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 9 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with 3x multipliers on all positions.

## Bigger Load Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 80 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with 6x multipliers on all positions.

## Coconut Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 2,000 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with Coconuts on all positions.

## Extra Spins
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the position multipliers, Fire Frames and Bonus symbols of the previous spin.

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the position multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented.

## Riding Moby-Dick
The max payout of the game is 20,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 20,000 times the base bet is awarded.
