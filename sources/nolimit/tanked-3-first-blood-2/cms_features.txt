## xLoot™
Payouts are awarded when the moving character symbol picks up gem symbols of its same colour.

Wild symbols can be collected by all characters.

- xLoot™ Upgrade: Each gem has 7 payout levels, which increases when its corresponding character kills other character symbols. The payout level increases by 1 for each character killed.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xGlitch™
xGlitch™ may trigger when a character is limited to picking up only one symbol, causing only feature symbols to fall during avalanches.

This can repeat 4 to 50 times in a row, creating the appearance of a glitch in the game.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Coin & Coinburst
Coins can appear on the reels with the following values: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000, 2,000 or 5,000.

When a character picks up a Coin, the player is awarded the Coin’s value multiplied by the base bet. If the character is killed, any Coins it collected during that spin will be dropped on the grid - however, the player retains any winnings already awarded from the Coins.

- Coinburst: When a character collects a Coinburst symbol, all positions traversed by any character - while landing or collecting symbols during all avalanches in the current spin - are transformed into Coin symbols. Only empty positions, Gems, and Wilds can be transformed.

If a Wild symbol position is transformed into a Coin, it will not be available for collection by any other character symbols.

## Bombs
There are two types of Bombs:

- Bomb: expands in 1 direction.
- Three Way Bomb: expands in 3 directions.

They trigger when no Characters can move and all possible Tank Boosters have been activated.

It will explode in the direction/directions where it points. All payout symbols and Character symbols are removed from the reels, and the grid expands in the direction of the bomb’s impact.

If any character is removed by any of the above Bombs, it will reappear on the reels when new symbols drop in.

## Tank Boosters
There are 5 different types of Tank Boosters that can be picked up by characters: Rocket, Loot Rocket, Grenade, Hatchet and Airstrike.

The character will carry the Tank Booster until there is no additional movement on the screen and then it uses one. Each Tank Booster’s function is explained below:

- Rocket: The character will shoot the wall close to any random enemy character. The explosion will remove all enemy characters and payout symbols in the explosion pattern as shown in image and increase the size of the grid section by 1 step. Feature symbols will remain on the reels.

- Loot Rocket: It will act like the Rocket and is additionally guaranteed to pick up all feature symbols dropped by any character that is killed, along with any new feature symbols (except Bombs) that appear during grid expansion.

- Grenade: The character will throw the grenade targeting any random enemy character. The explosion will remove all characters and payout symbols in the explosion pattern as shown in the image.

- Hatchet: The Hatchet targets the character holding it and removes all characters and payout symbols within the explosion pattern shown in the image. The character holding the Hatchet will remain on the reel.

-  Airstrike: The character hits 2 to 3 enemy characters and removes all characters and payout symbols within the explosion pattern shown in the image.

If a character is removed by any of the above Tank Boosters, it will reappear on the reels when new symbols drop in. Any Tank Boosters the enemy collected before the explosion stay with it when it reappears. Explosion will not impact Coins, Bombs, Coinburst, Tank Boosters, Bonus and Wild Symbols.

## Pickpocket & Booms
- Pickpocket: When 2 characters are next to each other and no character can move, there is a chance that one character is awarded a Tank Booster, Coinburst or a Bonus symbol.

- Small Boom: When 3 characters are next to each other and no character can move, the character that can remove the most symbols on the grid will explode and remove all character and paying symbols in the explosion pattern. Gems matching the exploding character’s colour will gain +1 payout level.

- Big Boom: When 4 characters are next to each other and no character can move, the characters will explode and remove all paying symbols on the reel area and increase size of each grid section by 1 step. All gems will gain +1 payout level.

## Grid Expansion
The grid is in 4 sections top left, bottom left, top right, and bottom right. Each explosion that increases the grid will do so in 1 step.

It is possible to expand each of the 4 sections a total of 3 times to a maximum grid of 9-10-11-12-13-12-11-12-13-12-11-10-9.

- The Bombs increase the grid section that is in the direction/directions of the explosion.
- A Big Boom will expand all sections by 1 step.
- The Rocket and Loot Rocket expand the section that is closest to the explosion of the rocket.

When the grid expands, it can reveal one or more feature symbols in the newly expanded section.

## Kill Drop
When a character is killed, it is guaranteed to drop a Coin symbol and any Coins it collected before being killed on the adjacent positions on the grid.

It may also drop one of the following feature symbols on its position: Tank Boosters, Bomb, Three Way Bomb, Bonus symbol or Coinburst symbol.

## Thresher Spins
When a character symbol collects 3 Bonus symbols, 7 Thresher Spins will be awarded with below features:

- Level of Gems and grid size will be carried over from the base game and will be persistent.
- Each collected Bonus symbol will award +1 spin.

## Reaper Spins
When a character symbol collects 4 Bonus symbols, 7 Reaper Spins will be awarded. It operates just like Thresher Spins, but with below enhancements:

- Coins present on the reels will be sticky and will stay between spins until collected. Any coins present when Reaper Spins are triggered will also stay sticky throughout the feature.

## The Dead Pay Well Spins
When a character symbol collects 5 Bonus symbols, 7 The Dead Pay Well Spins will be awarded. It operates just like Reaper Spins, but with below enhancements:

- Coins collected by characters are paid out and then carried by them across spins as well - even those collected during the trigger.

- When a character is killed, all held Coins will be dropped onto adjacent positions on the grid and they will payout if picked up again.

## Bonus Blitz
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed to land 1 Bonus symbol.

## Guaranteed Coinburst
At a cost of 50 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed to land 1 Coinburst symbol.

## Maxed Out
At a cost of 200 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a reel setup featuring the maximum grid size and all gems at their highest value.

## God Mode
Chase ‘A Different Perspective’ by landing a Max Win symbol on the reels at a cost of 4,000 times bet.

## A Different Perspective
The max payout of the game is 25,584 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 25,584 times the base bet is awarded.

‘A Different Perspective’ can also be achieved if the complete Max Win symbol is visible in the reel area.

## xTra™ Spin
At the end of the game round, players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin with the available Tank Booster options to choose from. The selected Tank Booster will be assigned to any one of the Characters. 

The additional spin will retain grid size and the symbol positions of the current reel setup. The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the reel setup. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented.
