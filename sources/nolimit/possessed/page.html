Base Game Mechanics and Features

The base game is built entirely around the inverted cross area in the center of the grid. Landing a Wild in the joint of the cross transforms the entire cross area into a Wild with a two times multiplier. The game also features xNudge Wilds that bump to fill the reel while increasing your multiplier. You will frequently encounter xWays and Infectious xWays mechanics. These special symbols reveal matching paying symbols and infect other instances on the grid to expand them. A major component of the gameplay is the xActivation feature. Landing this symbol triggers a respin, activating sections of the cross and filling them with high value symbols from the special xCrucifix reel. Players can also utilize the xBet option to increase their base stake and greatly boost the chances of triggering the bonus rounds.

Bonus Rounds and Free Spins

Surviving the base game can lead you to three intense tiers of free spins. Landing three scatter symbols triggers nine Necromancy Spins, which keeps three positions of the cross permanently active. If you find four scatters, you enter the Atonement Spins with twelve spins and four active cross positions. The ultimate nightmare is the Beelzebub Spins, unlocked by landing five scatters. This top tier bonus awards fifteen spins with the entire cross area fully activated for massive payout potential. Landing additional scatters during any of these rounds will award extra spins and upgrade you to the next tier. Game Features

Wild and xNudge® Wild

Wilds can land only on the middle reel. If Wild lands in the joint of the Cross, the whole Cross area is transformed into a Wild with a multiplier of 2. If the Wild lands in any other position of the Cross, the middle reel of the Cross area is transformed to Wild symbols and a corresponding multiplier is given.

xWays® and Infectious xWays®

xWays® symbols will transform into any 2 or 3 similar paying symbols except Wild, Scatter and xActivation. Infectious Ways will transform into 2 similar paying symbols except Wild, Scatter and xActivation and will infect all of the other same symbols on the reel area to expand to the same size.

xActivation

In the Main game, the Landing Activation symbol will trigger a Respin. The active area will be filled with a random symbol from the remaining symbols of the xCrucifix reel. Only 1 xActivation can land per spin. When 1st xActivation symbols lands, it will activate 3 positions of the Cross. It can reappear in the following Respin and activate one more position of the Cross until all positions of the Cross are activated. Each time an xActivation symbol lands the lowest paying symbol is removed from xCrucifix reel. The activation symbol can also appear in bonus modes but does not activate the positions of the Cross.

xBet®

At a cost of 2.5 times the base bet, the player is 5 times more likely to trigger Free spins. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

Necromancy Spins

Landing 3 Scatter symbols will trigger 9 Necromancy Spins and activate 3 positions of the Cross. Landing one additional Scatter will award +1 Spin and upgrade to Atonement Spins.

Atonement Spins

Landing 4 Scatter symbols will trigger 12 Atonement Spins and activate 4 positions of the Cross. Landing one additional Scatter will award +1 Spin and upgrade to Beelzebub Spins.

Beelzebub Spins

Landing 5 Scatter symbols will trigger 15 Beelzebub Spins and activate all positions of the Cross. Landing one additional Scatter will award +1 Spin.

BLASPHEMOUS

The max payout of the game is 50000 times the base bet. When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 50000 times the base bet is awarded.

Game xMechanics

xBet®

At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

Infectious xWays®

Any xWays symbols that appear will reveal the same symbol, and if the symbol revealed is already visible on the reels, each in view will be ‘infected’ and will expand as if they are xWays – producing additional ways to win.

xNudge®

xNudge®: A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

xWays®

xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins. xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.
