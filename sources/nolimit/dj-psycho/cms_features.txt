## xCluster®
2 or more unique symbol clusters in the same avalanche trigger xCluster - which increases the win multiplier by +1 for each unique symbol cluster, from the second cluster onwards.

## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Cluster Payout
Cluster wins are achieved with three or more matching symbols that are touching horizontally and/or vertically.

After a winning Cluster, the winning symbols are destroyed allowing new ones to appear.

## Crossed Symbols 
Whenever a symbol gets transformed by one of the following listed features, the symbol gets a second value.

On first transformation any Medium paying symbol turns into an additional Medium paying symbol.

On first transformation any Low paying symbol turns into an additional Low paying symbol.

If the newly formed symbol gets transformed again, it becomes a Wild.

## xKnob
xKnob symbol turns into a Wild and adds a value of minimum +1 to the overall multiplier affecting all wins.

The achieved value is sticky in Psycho Spins.

## Break
BREAK symbol gives itself and neighbouring symbols (left, right, below and above) an additional selected symbol value.

Medium paying symbols turn into an additional Medium paying symbol, called Crossed symbol.

Low paying symbols turn into an additional Low paying symbol, called Crossed symbols.

## Bite 
BITE symbol gives itself and diagonal neighbouring symbols an additional selected symbol value.

Medium paying symbol turn into an additional Medium paying symbol, called Crossed symbols.

Low paying symbols turn into an additional Low paying symbol, called Crossed symbols.

## Drop 
The DROP symbol only activates if there are 2 pieces of this symbol on the reel area.

If only one DROP symbol is present, it is regarded as a dead symbol.

In case 2 DROP symbols land, the shortest path between them is selected and all symbols in this path transform into a second symbol.

## xBPM 
xBPM symbol turns into a Wild when landing and will multiply the symbol size by 2 for each symbol on that row only

## Beat Bar 
The Beat Bar tracks the progress of multipliers on all coloured symbols.

Each time a coloured symbol connects, +1 is added to that specific colour.

Beat Bar resets for every new game round in base game.

Each spin, from 1 to 5 colours are chosen as freespins triggers.

Beat Bar becomes sticky during avalanches, Geez Spins and Psycho Spins, until a new bet is made.

## GEES Spins
In base game if a chosen colour or colour reaches x3 multiplier, Geez Spins are triggered with 5 initial spins.

That specific colour or colours need to have freespins activated on the Beat Bar in order to trigger Geez Spins.

Reaching x3 multiplier for any other coloured symbol will reward an additional 2 spins.

## PSYCHO Spins
Reaching x5 multiplier for ALL coloured symbols in Geez Spins will trigger Psycho Spins.

If x5 multiplier is achieved for all colours through base game, 17 spins are granted in Psycho Spins.

In Psycho Spins the general multiplier is sticky and any hit will guarantee a feature symbol in the next avalanche.

## Boosted xBet®
At a cost of an extra 235%, the player is 3 times more likely to hit Geez Spins and more than 7 times likely to hit Psycho Spins.

This will not be applicable for bonus modes.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Remixed 
The maximum payout of the game is 25,420 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 25,420 times the base bet is awarded.
