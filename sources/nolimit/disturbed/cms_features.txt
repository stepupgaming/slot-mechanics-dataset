## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## Enhancer Cells
Each spin can light up to 4 Enhancer Cells.
When lit, one of the following features will occur;
xWays®;
- A random position will become xWays®.
- xWays affect all symbols on an xSplit® position.
xSplit®;
- Mr. Split is a Wild symbol
- Random symbol on reel 3 turns into Mr. Split, the same symbol can be chosen again
- xSplit will split ( add 1 extra symbol) on all positions in the same row, except for itself. - If xSplit® splits an xWays® position, an extra xWays® symbol will be added.
Wild:
- A random position will become a regular wild symbol.
Disturber (Respin);
- Each Disturber activates a respin.
- During a triggered Disturber, the activated features are re-triggered.
- Positions affected by xWays®,Wilds, or split by xSplit® do not reset between Disturber.
Gravestones:
- Landing 4 gravestones may trigger Angel of Death.

## Mrs. Nudge (xNudge®)
Mrs. Nudge can only land on reel 3.
Landing Mrs. Nudge will always nudge to be fully visible. Each nudge increases the win multiplier by 1.

## Scatters
Scatter symbols can land on any reel in basegame.
Executioner Scatter symbol can only land on reel 3 in Pre-Op Spins. Landing the Executioner Scatter symbol upgrades the Pre-Op Spins into Annihilation Spins and awards +3 spins.

## Ramp Access (xBet®)
At a cost of an extra 100%, the player is guaranteed the top left Enhancer Cell to be lit up, plus a Scatter symbol on the first reel.
The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Pre-op Spins
Landing 3 or more Scatter symbols activates Pre-Op Spins.
- 3 Scatters: Activates 12 spins of Pre-Op Spins. - 4 Scatters: Activates 15 spins of Pre-Op Spins. - 5 Scatters: Activates 20 spins of Pre-Op Spins.
In Pre-Op Spins, 1 Enhancer Cell will always be lit. Activating a Disturber will grant an extra spin.
Landing the Executioner Scatter symbol upgrades the Pre-Op Spins into Annihilation Spins and awards +3 spins. The Executioner Scatter symbol can only land on reel 3 in Pre-Op Spins.

## Annihilation Spins
Landing 3, 4 or 5 Scatter symbols with all Enhancer Cells lit activates Annihilation Spins.
- 3 Scatters: Activates 12 spins of 'Annihilation Spins'. - 4 Scatters: Activates 15 spins of 'Annihilation Spins'. - 5 Scatters: Activates 20 spins of 'Annihilation Spins'.
During Annihilation Spins, all Enhancer Cells are lit. Activating a Disturber will grant an extra spin.

## Angel of Death
Landing 4 lit gravestones while also getting a stacked Dr. Death Symbol on reel 3 will trigger Angel of Death, which directly leads to Six Feet Under.

## Six Feet Under
The max payout of the game is 54,391 times the base bet.
When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 54,391 times the base bet is awarded.
