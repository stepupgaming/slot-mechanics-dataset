## Fifth Reel
The game has 5 reels with a row setup of 3-4-4-4-2.

The fifth reel has some special properties and limited symbols:

All symbols counts as a double symbol when they land fully.

No low value symbols are present on the reel.

Gunslinger symbols and Hog Me Tight Scatter symbols are present in main game and Redemption Spins.

During Salvation Spins it only holds the Hog Me Tight Scatter symbol.

## xNudge®
A stacked Wild symbol will always nudge to fully visible. Each Nudge Wild increases the win multiplier by 1.

They only appear in the three middle reels.

When a part of the Nudge Wild symbol lands on the reels, it will begin to nudge up or down until it is fully visible on the entire reel.

In base game: Several Nudge Wilds will add to each other for a total win multiplier.

In bonus modes: Several Nudge Wilds will multiply each other for a total win multiplier.

## xSplit®
Landing 2 Diamondback Scatter symbols will activate the Reel Split Wild. After landing, it splits itself and the other symbols on that reel in half, thus doubling the symbols on that reel. 

The Reel Split Wild can only activate on the middle three reels.

The Hog Me Tight Scatter only appears on last reel and counts as 2 symbols high. It also acts as a Wild. When landing it will cut symbols to the left middle rows:

Reel 1: Symbol in the middle row will be split.
Reel 2-4: Symbols in the two middle rows will be split.

When an Nudge Wild is split, it will double the Nudge Wild multiplier.

## Quick Draw
Landing 1 revolver cylinder on reel 1 will activate Quick Draw.

Quick Draw will randomly shoot 3 to 6 times - depending on the number of bullets held in the cylinder 

Each shot doubles the symbol multiplier which is hit.

## Shootout
Landing 2 Sheriff Badges on reels 2 and 4 will trigger the Shootout feature.

All low win symbols on center 3 reels will act as Wild symbols.

## Scatter Symbols
Diamondback Scatter symbols only appears on the middle three reels in main game.

The Hog Me Tight Scatter symbol can only appear on the last reel in main game and Freespins.

Landing 3 Diamondback Scatter symbols will activate 5 Redemption Spins. If the Hog Me Tight Scatter symbol also lands, then 6 Salvation Spins are awarded.

Landing only 2 Diamondback Scatter symbols will convert them to Reel Split Wilds.

Landing Hog Me Tight Scatter symbol without activating Salvation Spins, it will split symbols to the left in the middle rows. It also acts as Wild.

Landing the 3 RIP Scatter symbols during the bonus modes will retrigger the bonus.

## Redemption Spins
Landing 3 Diamondback Scatter symbols will activate 5 Redemption Spins.

The win multiplier will be increased by Nudge Wilds (x1-x4) and will remain on the reels throughout the feature. Any collected win multiplier will be multiplied by another xNudge® Wild.

Upgrade to a Salvation Spins if you land Hog Me Tight Scatter symbol on last reel and 1 additional spin will be awarded.

Landing the 3 RIP symbols will retrigger the Free Spins, awarding +5 spins.

## Salvation Spins
Landing 3 Diamondback Scatter symbols with a Hog Me Tight Scatter symbol will activate 6 Salvation Spins.

The win multiplier will be increased by Nudge Wilds (x1-x4) and will remain throughout the feature. Any collected win multiplier will be multiplied by another ×Nudge® Wild.

Reel 5 will always hold the Hog Me Tight Scatter symbol, that is a Wild and will split all symbols on the middle row after every spin.

Landing the 3 RIP symbols will retrigger the Free Spins, awarding +6 spins.

## xRip®
Any spin in main game or bonus mode that generate combinations where the total payout according to the paytable is below the base bet triggers xRIP®.

When xRip® is triggered there is no payout

## Final Judgement
The max payout of the game is 100,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 100,000 times the base bet is awarded.
