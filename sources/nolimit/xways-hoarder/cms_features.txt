## xSplit Wilds
Not an ordinary wilds, and super potent as it’ll split all symbols to the left on the same row. Boom!

## xWays
Just as previously seen in a slot by Nolimit City - it’ll reveal 2-4 of the same symbol and all xWays symbols will reveal the same symbol for a major punch in the gut.

## Scatter symbols
Triggers the Bunker Raid Spins, but can also be upgraded to a Super Scatter symbol.

## Bunker Raid Spins
All xWays symbols will be sticky for the remainder of the feature, and always stored in the bottom of the reels. If the FS is triggered by Super Scatters, then all symbols, incl. the scatter, below the Super Scatter automatically converts to xWays symbols when entering the feature.\nCollect xWays symbols to level up the Bunker Raids Spin from level 1 to level 3 - each level with its upgrade of low paying symbols being removed.\nAwards 7 or 10 free spins if triggered by 3 or 4 scatters. Any additional xWays symbols award +1 spin retrigger.
