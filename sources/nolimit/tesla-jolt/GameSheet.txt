Game Features
A classic setup with a base game feature
with a nice potential of bagging some
electric wins in the hunt for the Tesla Spins
free spin mode.

Tesla Jolt
The Tesla Jolt symbol lands on reel 5 and triggers a
randomly chosen symbol on the reel area to become
Wild..


Tesla Spins
Three or more scatters awards 10, 15 or 20 Tesla Spins.
There are two features in the Tesla Spins; Charged Wilds
and the Time-lapse Trigger.


Charged Wilds
Any Wilds landing on reel 2, 3, 4 will be stored. The feature
is fully charged once 3 Wilds are stored, and the wilds will
be dispersed back on its reel.
