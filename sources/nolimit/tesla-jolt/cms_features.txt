## Tesla Jolt
The Tesla Jolt symbol lands on reel 5 and triggers a randomly chosen symbol on the reel area to become Wild..

## Tesla Spins
Three or more scatters awards 10, 15 or 20 Tesla Spins. There are two features in the Tesla Spins; Charged Wilds and the Time-lapse Trigger.

## Charged Wilds
Any Wilds landing on reel 2, 3, 4 will be stored. The feature is fully charged once 3 Wilds are stored, and the wilds will be dispersed back on its reel.

## Time-lapse Trigger
The Time-lapse Trigger symbol releases all charged wilds and awards 1-3 extra Tesla Spins. The dispersed wilds will randomly be placed on the reels they were charged from.
