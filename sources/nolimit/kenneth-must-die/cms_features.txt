## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xHole™
Activated when xHole™ symbol lands on the centre position of the main reel area.

Symbols only from main reels are sucked in and are spit out to random positions with splitted symbols of size 2 to 8. In case of xNudge® Wild, it changes size and win multiplier. xHole™ then converts to Wild.

Symbols only from main reels are sucked in and are spit out to random positions with splitted symbols of size 2 to 8. xHole™ then converts to Wild.

Gimp, Gimpier, Wild, xNudge® Wild, Kinky Kenneth, Kinky Wife and winning symbols before xHole™ suck go back to the same position. Gimp and Gimpier symbols get a multiplier for their splitted symbol size.

xHole™ will not suck any sticky Wild and sticky xNudge® Wild in bonus game modes. It will also not suck Gimp symbols when exactly 3 Gimps are present on the reels.

## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

The lower max win version increases the probability of below features:

When playing on the 10,000x version, the mathematical model of the game will alter slightly.

## Big Balls Multiplier
Symbols in the bottom special row come in from the right. At spin start special row symbols get an incremental Big Balls multiplier: first symbol gets x1, second gets x2 and so on.

Symbols gets split as per their respective Big Balls Multiplier.

Avalanche will be triggered only when symbol(s) on the special row is part of the win. Winning positions on special row will be occupied by the symbols from right. New symbol’s Big Balls Multiplier will be incremented by +1 on the highest existing multiplier on the special reel.

## Kinky Hookup
Triggered when Kinky Kenneth and Kinky Wife symbols appear on the normal reels.

All the symbols on the shortest path between these special symbols will convert to either only Wife, Kenneth or Wild.

Win before the Kinky Hookup will be awarded first, followed by the Kinky Hookup win if any.

## Gimp Transform
Landing exactly 2 Gimp symbols will trigger Gimp Transform.

Gimp symbol gets transformed to one of the following symbols: xWays®, xNudge®, Wild, Kinky Kenneth, Kenneth, Kinky Wife or Wife.

Gimp symbols will respawn on its original position in case of avalanche.

## Gimpier Transform
Gimpier symbol can only land on reel 5.

Gimpier symbol gets transformed to one of the following symbols: Kinky Kenneth, Wild or Kinky Wife.

Gimpier symbol will respawn on its original position in case of avalanche.

## xNudge® Wilds
XNudge® Wild will always nudge upwards until the top of the reel.

For every step the xNudge® Wild nudges, the win multiplier increases by 1. While nudging over a regular Wild symbol, xNudge® Wild gets +1 additional win multiplier per Wild symbol.

In case of multiple xNudge® on same reel - already nudged xNudge® Wild’s multiplier will be added to total win multiplier.

Sticky xNudge® Wild resets the position beneath it to its original size.

xNudge® landing on the bottom special row will get its respective Big
Balls multiplier.

Several xNudge® Wild multipliers on different reels add to each other for a total win multiplier.

## Beefy Spins
Landing 3 Gimp symbols in base game will trigger 8 Beefy Spins.

Big Balls multiplier will not reset.

Normal Wild symbols landing on the main reels will be sticky.

Landing a Gimpier symbol will upgrade bonus mode to Beefier Spins and award +1 additional spin.

Landing a Gimpier symbol with an activated Hole in the same avalanche will upgrade bonus mode to Beefiest Spins and award +2
additional spins.

## Beefier Spins
Landing 3 Gimps and 1 Gimpier symbol in base game will trigger 9 Beefier Spins.

In addition to the Beefy Spins features, Gimpier symbol and Nudge Wild landing on the main reels will be sticky.

Landing an activated Hole will upgrade bonus mode to Beefiest
Spins and award +1 additional spin.

## Beefiest Spins
Landing 3 Gimps, 1 Gimpier and an activated xHole™ in base game will trigger 10 Beefiest Spins.

In addition to the Beefier Spins features, xHole™ will reappear in its position at the start of each new spin.
