COINS OF FORTUNE
Coins of Fortune is inspired by a
mesmerizing south-east asian city and
its old traditional heritage, all mixed into
a mysterious melting pot of mythical
creatures and neon lights.

Coins of Fortune has 20 fixed betlines on a 5x3 reel area.
The Dragon Nudge feature and the Lucky Respin feature
will keep the players on their toes in their chase of these
high-potential features.

The Dragon Nudge will award three respins with a stacked
wild that nudges and the Lucky Respin will award respins
with walking wilds that progress as long as there are wilds
present.

Game Features
Coins of Fortune is a 5x3 respin slot with
two prominent features that will keep the
excitement going for more an more spins
– Dragon Nudge stacked wilds and the
running bonus mode Lucky Respin.

Dragon Nudge
When you land the same three symbols on one reel, the
Dragon Nudge respin is triggered. On the reel where the
symbols landed reveal a stacked Dragon Wild overlay
during the respin. The Dragon Nudge will move downward
for each respin, any additional Dragon Nudge pro-longs
the respin mode!



Lucky Respins
Land 3 or more scatters and you will be awarded the
Lucky Respins. All scatters during the Lucky Respins walks
left, and be collected once they move outside the reel
area. The win multiplier increases for every third collected
