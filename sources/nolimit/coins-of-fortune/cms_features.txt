## Dragon Nudge
When you land the same three symbols on one reel, the Dragon Nudge respin is triggered. On the reel where the symbols landed reveal a stacked Dragon Wild overlay during the respin. The Dragon Nudge will move downward for each respin, any additional Dragon Nudge pro-longs the respin mode!

## Lucky Respins
Land 3 or more scatters and you will be awarded the Lucky Respins. All scatters during the Lucky Respins walks left, and be collected once they move outside the reel area. The win multiplier increases for every third collected scatter. Any Dragon Nudge that is triggered will turn into coins, for one massive multiplier bump!
