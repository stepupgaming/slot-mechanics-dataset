Game Features
Kitchen Drama is a 5x3 freespin slot with
two prominent features that will help you
get the Wild Sushi freespins – triggered by
winning combinations from the collapsing
reels.

Free Spin Trigger
The collapsing reels feature in Kitchen Drama allows for
an exciting free spins trigger. Given that you win on a
combination of three sushi ingredients, will enable the
Wild Sushi Free Spin.


Wild Sushi Free Spins
When you trigger Wild Sushi Free Spins mode, you will
be rewarded 10 free spins where you may turn all sushi
ingredients into wilds!


Bunshin Wilds
The Bunshin Wilds will clone themselves and randomly
spread to two additional positions on the reels.
