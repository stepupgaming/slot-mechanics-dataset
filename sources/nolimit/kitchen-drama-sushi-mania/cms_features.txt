## Free Spin Trigger
The collapsing reels feature in Kitchen Drama allows for an exciting free spins trigger. Given that you win on a combination of three sushi ingredients, will enable the Wild Sushi Free Spin.

## Wild Sushi Free Spins
When you trigger Wild Sushi Free Spins mode, you will be rewarded 10 free spins where you may turn all sushi ingredients into wilds!

## Bunshin Wilds
The Bunshin Wilds will clone themselves and randomly spread to two additional positions on the reels.

## Kamisabi Attack
Three or more collapses will activate the Kamisabi Attack! This feature will help you get even more collapses and hopefully make you win the free spin mode.
