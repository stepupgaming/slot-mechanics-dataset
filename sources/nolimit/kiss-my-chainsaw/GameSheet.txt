                                                                                       FEATURES IN THE GAME




                                                                                                       SCATTER
                                                                                                       In the main game, the Scatter symbol can only land on reel 1,3, and 5.


                                                                                                       Burnout is triggered by landing exactly 2 Scatter symbols, both on the same row.


                                                                                                       Once all winnings are executed, Burnout will destroy all symbols including the 2 Scatter symbols, allowing new symbols to fall in.


                                                                                                       Depending on what row the Scatter symbols land on, one of the bonus modes will be awarded.


                                                                                                       - 3 Scatter symbols on different rows: Activates 8 spins of "The Ride".
                                                                                                       - 3 Scatter symbols on the same row: Activates 9 spins of "The Last Ride".

                                                                                       FEATURES IN THE GAME




                                                                                                       CHAINSAW LARRY SPINS
                                                                                                       Landing 3 Scatter symbols during "The Ride" or "The Last Ride" will award +3 spins and activate Chainsaw Larry spins.


                                                                                                       An extra enhancer reel is added and the symbol payout gets upgraded in this mode. It follows the Chainsaw Larry Spins paytable.


                                                                                                       Chainsaw Larry will appear in the form of a jumping Wild, jumping to a new position each spin on reels 2-4. If Chainsaw Larry jumps on any
                                                                                                       of the character symbols, the win multiplier is increased by +1.


                                                                                                       Chainsaw Larry will reveal the enhancer symbols on the row it lands, always triggering one of following enhancers: Smiley, xSplit® or Lucky
                                                                                                       Feet.


                                                                                                       Smiley will award +1 Spin.


                                                                                                       xSplit® will double all multipliers between the xSplit® and Chainsaw Larry symbols. Each character symbol split will increase the win
                                                                                                       multiplier by +1.


                                                                                                       Lucky Feet will cause Chainsaw Larry to move to a different row and random position on reels 2-4, leaving behind a regular Wild symbol. If
                                                                                                       Chainsaw Larry moves to a position with a Charged Wild, the Afterburner feature is triggered which doubles the symbol multipliers on the
                                                                                                       same row. If it jumps on initial Charged Wild, all symbols become x2.
