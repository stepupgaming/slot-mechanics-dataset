## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Reel Area
The game has 5 reels and 3 rows, with three fire pits above the reels, each linked to a unique base game feature. When any feature is triggered, the corresponding fire pit ignites.

These features appear as scrolls. When they land there is a chance the corresponding feature triggers:

- xWays® (Orange scroll): xWays® symbols will transform into a normal paying symbol. It will reveal 2-4 symbols of the same kind and increase the number of win ways.

- Infectious Wild (Red scroll): Transforms a random symbol and all its instances on the reels into Wilds.

- xSplit® (Grey scroll): xSplit® splits all symbols on it’s row and doubles the symbol size, converting itself into Wild. xSplit® can split any symbol, except for itself and Bonus.

## xNudge® Wilds
A stacked Wild symbol that will always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases the Wild multiplier by 1. Once fully visible, it triggers Dead Man’s March and moves 1 step left, further increasing its multiplier by 1.

If an xNudge® Wild reaches the first reel during a Dead Man’s March and other xNudge® Wilds are present, it transfers its multiplier to the nearest one. Dead Man’s March will be awarded until the last xNudge® Wild reaches the first reel.

## Bonus Spins
Dead Man’s Gold

Landing 3, 4 or 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 3, 4 or 5 Dead Man’s Gold Bonus Spins respectively. Total count of Bonus symbols can be seen on Bonus symbol board.

Bonus Spins starts on 5x5 reel grid where only coins will land. Spin count remains intact if a coin lands. All coin values are valued as times the active bet.

When 3 or more coins align vertically or horizontally, they are collected and are paid out. Feature coins are triggered only when they are collected.

Below are the 4 feature coins that can also land and might get triggered:

- Collector: Collects the value of all active coins.

- Cloner: Copies the highest coin value to all active coins.

- Multiplier: Doubles the value of all visible coins.

- King’s Grace: Resets the bonus spin count to its initial value. 

Reaper’s Gold

Landing 3, 4 or 5 Gold Bonus symbols trigger 3, 4 or 5 Reaper’s Gold Bonus Spins respectively.

In addition to features of Dead Man’s Gold, Feature coins are triggered as soon as they land.

## Booster 1 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 1.5 times the base bet, the player is more likely to activate Dead Man’s Gold. When at least 1 Bonus symbol lands on reel area, 1-4 additional Bonus symbols may be added to the Bonus board.

## Booster 2 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 2.5 times the base bet, Bonus symbols are upgraded to Gold Bonus symbols to trigger Reaper’s Gold.

## Booster 3 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 5 times the base bet, the player is more likely to activate Reaper’s Gold. When at least 1 Gold Bonus symbol lands on reel area, 1-4 additional Gold Bonus symbols may be added to the Bonus board.

## Booster 4 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 40 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed minimum of 1 xNudge® Wild.

## Extra Spins
Bonus Spins: Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the multiplier state of the symbols of the previous spin.
The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the multiplier values.

If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then only the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented.

## Keep Your Hood On
The max payout of the game is 25,313 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 25,313 times the base bet is awarded.
