                                                                                                         LOCK 'N' LOAD
                                                                                                          Every time a winning combination occurs; the next group of bullets will be loaded into the cylinder. The feature
                                                                                                          will activate once it's fully loaded with six bullets and no more winning combinations occur.


                                                                                                          Once activated, the bullets will be randomly fired at the high paying character symbol on the reels. The
                                                                                                          characters that are hit, will be replaced by an overlay Wild.


                                                                                                          If there are five or less high paying symbols on the reels, the unused bullets will be saved in the cylinder for later.

                                                                                       FEATURES IN THE GAME




                                                                                                         WHACKED SPINS
                                                                                                         Whacked Spins start with three spins. The spins will reset to three as soon as any win is
                                                                                                         collected. Coin values, xWays® symbols and xNudge® symbols® all reset the spin count to three.


                                                                                                         Coins will hold a value times the base bet. All winnings during the spins will be added to the
                                                                                                         counter in the bottom of each reel. All collected values will be added together for a total win
                                                                                                         after Whacked Spins end.


                                                                                                         If the xRevolver cylinder symbols lands, it will award one of the 6 options within the cylinder. It
                                                                                                         can any of the following symbols;


                                                                                                         • Coin Value
                                                                                                         • xWays®
                                                                                                         • xNudge®
                                                                                                         • Russian Roulette.
