## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge. 

## Lock 'n' Load
Every time a winning combination occurs; the next group of bullets will be loaded into the cylinder. The feature will activate once it's fully loaded with six bullets and no more winning combinations occur.
Once activated, the bullets will be randomly fired at the high paying character symbol on the reels. The characters that are hit, will be replaced by an overlay Wild.
If there are five or less high paying symbols on the reels, the unused bullets will be saved in the cylinder for later.

## Scatters
Three Scatter symbols will trigger Whacked Spins. Whacked Spins always start with a collected value of x1 the base bet for each reel.

## Whacked Spins
Whacked Spins start with three spins. The spins will reset to three as soon as any win is collected. Coin values, xWays® symbols and xNudge® symbols® all reset the spin count to three.
Coins will hold a value times the base bet. All winnings during the spins will be added to the counter in the bottom of each reel. All collected values will be added together for a total win after Whacked Spins end.
If the xRevolver cylinder symbols lands, it will award one of the 6 options within the cylinder. It can any of the following symbols;
• Coin Value
• xWays®
• xNudge®
• Russian Roulette.

## Whacked Spins: xWays®
In Whacked Spins, the xWays® symbol will expand to always reveal three coin values.

The xWays® symbol will hold coin values ranging from 1 to 20 times the base bet.

## Whacked Spins: xNudge®
xNudge® symbol will expand and award three multipliers of x1, x2 or x3 - depending on where it lands. For each nudge the symbol makes, it will award +1x on its multiplier.

Each xNudge® value will multiply the collected value at the bottom of the reel.

## Whacked Spins: Russian Roulette
Russian Roulette can only be awarded from the xRevolver symbol. When the Russian Roulette is activated, the spins will be reset to three once again, but will not be able to reset again.
Instead of adding up the winnings on each reel, the winnings will instead be multiplied with eachother into a total win.

## Last Man standing
The max payout of the game is 11,912 times the base bet.
When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and 11,912 times the base bet is awarded.
