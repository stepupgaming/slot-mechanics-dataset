Drive By Respins
A Drive-By Respin is a spin that grants you one of the
enhancers from either West Coast or East Coast Spins.
Drive-By Respins are triggered when 2 Bonus Symbols
lands during a game round.

East Coast Spins

every spin. The top bar will reveal the enhancers during
the bonus mode.

the beginning of the East Coast Spins – landing additional

more position. Unlocking all five positions turns the East
