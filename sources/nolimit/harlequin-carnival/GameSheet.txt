Game Features
Harlequin Carnival is focused around its
heart-pounding walking xNudge wild.

Harlequin xNudge® Wild
A 3-row high Harlequin Wild symbol exists on all reels and
will always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases
the Wild multiplier by 1. Several Wild multipliers add to
each other for a total Wild multiplier on the line win for
huge wins! Landing this symbol will trigger the Harlequin
Respins!

Harlequin Respins
All Harlequin xNudge Wilds will walk to the left and award
a respin on the other reels, up until it reaches the first
reel. Landing additional xNudge Wilds will create a truly
festive respin!

Scatters may be collected during the respins, to activate
the Harlequin Bonus.
