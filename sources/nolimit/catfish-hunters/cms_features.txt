## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xTra™

## Fish \u0026 Electrical Features
At the start of each spin, one or more Electrical Frames appear in random positions on the reel area. 

Fish symbols can appear on the reel area and each Fish has an associated value. A Fish win is awarded when a Fish symbol gets an Electrical Frame on its position. The global Fish multiplier starts at x1 and is applied to the Fish value before multiplying by the base bet. 

Each time a Fish win is awarded, the global Fish multiplier is doubled, with the maximum value being 1024x. Fish wins are evaluated from top to bottom, and left to right. 

Possible Fish values are 0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 20x and 100x.

## Imposter Wild
Impostor Wild symbols can land on any reel. When a Impostor Wild lands, it transforms into one of the following Wild symbols randomly: 

• Stacy Wild: Increases the global Fish multiplier by 2, 4 or 8 times randomly. 

• Karen Wild: Adds Electrical Frames to itself and to all its adjacent positions. 

• Granny Wild: Adds Electrical Frames to all positions on the reel area. 

• Cletus Wild: Transforms all winning regular paying symbols into random Fish symbols. 

• Bubba Wild: Transforms all regular paying symbols into random Fish symbols.

## Dark Water Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 8 Dark Water Spins. Each additional Bonus symbol awards +1 spin. Global Fish multiplier from the base game is kept during bonus spins and will be halved on each new spin, with the minimum value being x1. 

During this feature, one of the following 3 upgrades is randomly awarded: 

• Upgraded Bonus: Awards +2 spins instead instead of +1. 

• Upgraded xWays ®: xWays ® always reveal a Fish or a Impostor Wild. 

• +1 Electrical Frame: Adds a sticky Electrical Frame in the middle position of the reel area.

## Deep Water Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 10 Deep Water Spins. It has the same features as Dark Water Spins but 2 upgrades are randomly awarded instead.

## Overcharged Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 12 Overcharged Spins. It has the same features as Deep Water Spins but all 3 upgrades are awarded instead.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on the second reel.

## Grand Catch (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 90 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a minimum of 1 Imposter Wilds. Only Fish symbols with values of 10x, 20x and 100x can land.

## Legend Catch (Nolimit Booster)

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the Electrical Frames and global Fish multiplier of the previous spin. 

If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the total win, then the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented. Bonus symbols will not land during Extra Spin. New Electrical Frames can be only awarded by Karen or Granny Wilds during the additional spin.

## Max Win
The max payout of the game is 20,000 times the base bet. 

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 20,000 times the base bet is awarded.
