## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Wilds
A Wild symbol can land on any reel except reel 1. 

## Connected Ways
Payouts are awarded when 3 more matching symbols connect horizontally or diagonally starting from the leftmost reel. 

## Brown Nudge Delight
The Brown Nudge Delight symbol can land on any reel except 1.

It is a horizontally expanding Wild that turns all symbols on its right on the same row into Wilds.

## Clone
The Clone symbol creates a copy of the reel it lands on. The copied reel is inserted to the right of the original reel. Upon cloning, the Clone symbol becomes a dead symbol. 

Maximum number of reels can be 10. 

## Press
The Press symbol can land on any reel except reel 1. The Press symbol removes all symbols below it on the same reel and transforms into a Wild when it reaches the bottom row. After removal, new symbols fall in. 

Press symbol does not tigger if it lands on the bottom row. 

## Germinator
The Germinator symbol may land during bonus mode. Each time it lands, it removes the lowest paying symbol for the next spin and awards +1 spin. Multiple Germinator symbols can land in a spin, removing more paying symbols. 

If all ten paying symbols are removed by Germinator symbols during the bonus mode, the max win is awarded. 

## Big Win
Winnings are awarded when a WIN symbol lands along with one or more BIG symbols. BIG symbols are dead if they land without a WIN symbol. 

The payout is determined by how many BIG symbols land along with a WIN symbol: 

- 1 BIG symbol = 15x base bet
- 2 BIG symbols = 25x base bet
- 3 BIG symbols = 50x base bet
- 4 BIG symbols = 100x base bet
- 5 BIG symbols = 500x base bet 
- 6 BIG symbols = Max Win.

## God Mode
Chase "Supersized" by landing 6 or more BIG symbols and a WIN symbol at the cost of 2,000 times the bet. 

## 5 Fat Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 3 Fatty symbols will trigger 3 Fat Spins. 

Cloned game area goes back to 5 reels on each new spin. 

## 7 Fatter Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Fatter Spins. If a Clone symbol lands, all cloned reels are kept for the next spin. Otherwise, the game area resets back to the 5-reel setup. 

## 9 Fattest Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 9 Fattest Spins. 

If more than 5 Fatty symbols land during the feature trigger, each additional Fatty symbol will award +2 spins. 

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 3 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Fatty symbol on reel 2. 

## Germinator Spin (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 6 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin with no low paying symbols.

## Germinator Deluxe Spin
At the cost of 100 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin with only the top 2 paying symbols. 

Fatty and special symbols (Wild, Clone, Brown Nudge Delight, Press, BIG & WIN) can still land. 

## Germinator Ultra Spin
At the cost of 1,234 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin with only the top paying symbol. 

Fatty and special symbols (Wild, Clone, Brown Fudge Delight, BIG & WIN) can still land. 

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the position multipliers of the previous spin. 

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the position of the multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented. Bonus symbols will not land during Extra Spin.  

## Supersized
The max payout of the game is 12,345 times the base bet. 

When the total win exceeds this amount, the game round will end and the 12,345 times the base bet is awarded.
