## Wild Transform
Match the symbol in the top reel and the symbol underneath on the same reel to transform both into a Wild symbol.

## xNudge Wild
Stacked Wild symbol in the bottom reel that will always nudge to be fully visible, each nudge increased a wild multiplier by 1. Get a 2nd chance with an even higher multiplier if you don’t win on it the first time...

## xWays & Infectious xWays
You’ve seen it before - xWays reveals 2-3 of the same symbol. Infectious xWays symbol will affect and multiply all symbols of the revealed type for massive wins.

## Reel Split
Reel split will double all symbols above it on the main reel. It then vanishes, causing a bottom reel collapse for new symbols to appear.

## Transform ‘em
Will transform all of the same symbol type into the same symbol, meaning that all character symbols will turn into the same one!

## Grit Girl
Scatter paying symbol that pays as soon as she appears on 3+ reels. Grit Girl has the base payout of x25, four reels have a base payout of x500, but it will also have the chance of a massive multiplier....

## Torture Spins
If you get three scatter symbols, you’ll get 8+ Torture Spins. The active game area expands, and locked positions from main game will be available for the duration of the bonus round.

## Dead Spins
Landing 4 Scatter symbols will trigger 10+ Dead Spins. The active game area will be expanded, and all previously locked spots in\nthe main game, including the sticky Wild with a multiplier in the bottom right corner, will be available for the duration of the bonus round.

## No More Grit to be Found
The max payout of the game is 20220 times the base bet.

## Nolimit Bonus - Feature Buy Functionality
Buy your way straight into one of the different bonus features, ranging from 69x to 669x the base bet.
