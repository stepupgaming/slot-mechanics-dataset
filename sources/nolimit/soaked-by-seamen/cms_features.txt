## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xTra™
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the position multipliers and Fire Frames of the previous spin. 

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the position multipliers and Fire Frames. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the total win, then the Extra Spin opportunity will be presented. 

Extra Spin requires a ways win to be presented in the main game; Side Hustle wins alone don’t count.

## Win Respins
Winning symbols are removed, their multipliers increase by +1, and new symbols respin into those positions. Multipliers apply only to symbols in winning positions. 

On each respin, one additional Fire Frame is added.

## Fire Frames
On each spin, Fire Frames are randomly added to positions of the reel area. Each position that gets a Fire Frame has its multiplier increased by +1. Winning symbols with Fire Frames have their position multipliers increased by +2.

## Lucky Lighter
Reveals a regular paying symbol. Each reel position displaying that symbol receives a Fire Frame, increasing its multiplier by +1.

## Banana
The Banana symbol may randomly land during a spin and appear with a number: 1 to 3. After the indicated number of respins, the Banana expands to cover all positions on its reel with Banana Wilds.

## Bombs
If there are no winning symbols, Bombs will explode and remove symbols based on their type. Empty positions respin, landing new symbols. Any symbol removed by a Bomb has its position multiplier increased by +1, or +2 if there’s a Fire Frame on that position. 

There are 2 types of Bombs that explodes in below listed sequence: 

• Coconut: Removes itself and increases the position multiplier by 5, 10, 15, 20 or 100, in addition to the standard increase. 

• Blowfish Bomb: Removes itself and all adjacent regular paying symbols. It will not trigger if there is a Coconut symbol present on the reel area.

## Rigged Spins
Landing 3 Bonus Symbols will trigger 5 Rigged Spins. 

Position multipliers are kept during Freespins.

Landing a Bonus symbol awards +1 spin.

## Super Rigged Spins
Landing 4 Bonus Symbols will trigger 8 Super Rigged Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Freespins. 

Additionally, more Fire Frames are awarded per spin on average. 

Landing a Bonus symbol awards +1 spin.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 2.50 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on the top position of the second leftmost reel.

## Nutty Spin (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 40 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with 4 Coconuts and 1 Banana.

## Nuttier Spin (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 400 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with 10 Coconuts and 1 Banana.

## Big Load Spin 3x (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 9 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x3 multipliers on all positions.

## Bigger Load 6x (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 80 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x6 multipliers on all positions.

## Biggest Load 15x (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 3,000 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x15 multipliers on all positions.

## Side Hustle™
An optional add-on bet that increases the total cost of the spin. It allows the player to bet on a specific outcome, awarding a fixed win on top of other wins if that outcome occurs. The cost and win amount depend on the choice of feature bet. Multiple Side Hustles can be placed simultaneously. 

Side Hustle Options: 

• 5 Fire Frames: Pays out if at least 5 Fire Frames appear in a single spin. 

• 7 Fire Frames: Pays out if at least 7 Fire Frames appear in a single spin. 

• 10 Fire Frames: Pays out if at least 10 Fire Frames appear in a single spin. 

• 20x Multiplier: Pays out if a position multiplier of 20x or higher is achieved. 

• Banana Expand: Pays out if a Banana symbol expands. 

• Dead Spin: Pays out if the base game spin ends with no win.

## King Dong
The max payout of the game is 30,000 times the base bet. When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 30,000 times the base bet is awarded.
