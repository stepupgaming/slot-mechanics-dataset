## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## Stacked Symbols
The character symbols can land in various sizes, with stacked symbols reaching up to 4 positions high.

Stacked symbol will be counted as the number of positions it covers on the reel.

## Chained Reels
Activated randomly on any spin.

Two or more reels will be linked, causing the same symbols to land adjacent to each other on all linked reels.

## xNudge® Wild
A stacked Wild symbol that will always nudge to fully visible. Each nudge increases the xNudge® Wild multiplier by 1.

When a part of the xNudge® Wild symbol lands on the reels, it will begin to nudge up or down until it is fully visible on the entire reel.

Several xNudge® Wild multipliers add to each other for a total xNudge® Wild multiplier.

The xNudge® Wild multiplier acts as the win multiplier.

## xNudge® Desire
Activated randomly on any spin in main game.

From 1-5 xNudge® Wild symbols will be added to the reels.

If an xNudge® Wild is added to a Chained Reel, all the Chained Reels will contain the xNudge®.

## Oppression Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 5 Oppression Spins.

The third Bonus which is a Big Bonus symbol will award one of the below upgrade feature during the bonus trigger:

- Additional spins
- Additional win multiplier
- Additional guaranteed Chained Reels
- Guaranteed Nudge Desire on each spin
- Guaranteed Nudge on each spin
- Max win

During Oppression Spins, at least 2 reels will always be chained on each spin. Any multiplier gathered from an xNudge® Wild or Big Bonus symbol will be persistent throughout the feature.

Up to 3 Big Bonus symbols can land in a spin during the bonus mode and each will award one of the upgrade features.

Each additional xNudge® Wild from the Big Bonus symbol guarantees an extra xNudge® Wild on every spin.

## Domination Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 5 Domination Spins.

In addition to Oppression Spins features, each of the 2 Big Bonus symbols will award an upgrade feature during the bonus trigger.

## Enslavement Spins
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 5 Enslavement Spins.

In addition to Oppression Spins features, each of the 3 Big Bonus symbols will award an upgrade feature during the bonus trigger.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on reel 1 or 2.

## Chained Reels xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 7 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed to activate Chained Reels.

## xNudge® (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 35 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed an xNudge® Wild.

## xNudge® Desire (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 500 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed to activate xNudge® Desire.

## The Coronation
The max payout of the game is 14,780 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 14,780 times the base bet is awarded.
