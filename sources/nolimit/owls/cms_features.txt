## Scatter Loot
Landing the stacked scatter fully visible on reel 3 will trigger a scatter payout, which can pay up to 6- and 7-hits, given that you receive Wilds on the reel.

## Dream Spins
Awarded when landing three scatters on reel 2, 3 and\n4. Middle scatter will always nudge to fully visible. Will randomly award one of the three Dream Spins – Ember Dream, Frost Dream or Emerald Dream. All bonus modes will always have a stacked Wild on reel 3.

## Ember Dream
Will award 5 spins where Flame Shard and Flame Chest symbols will transform to Wild on each spin.

## Emerald Dream
Will award 5 spins where Emerald Shard and Emerald Book symbols will transform to Grove Owl symbol on each spin.

## Frozen Dream
Will award 5 spins where Frost Owl, Frost Shard and Frost Sword symbols will transform into the most occurring symbol of the Frost set on each spin. If there are an equal amount of symbols, the symbol with the highest value will be chosen.
