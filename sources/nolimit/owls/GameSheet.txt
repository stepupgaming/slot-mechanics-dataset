OWLS
Granted with the gifts of nobility, strength
and wisdom, our beloved Owl warriors
defend the forgotten ancient forests.
Destined to bring stability to their Kingdom,
their combined bravery and wit is needed to
keep peace and order.

Owls is a video slot with 15 fixed lines on a 5x3 reel area,
with three prominent bonus features and a scatter payout
feature. The different bonus features are all decided by the
huge scatter on reel 3 – will it award the Frozen Dream,
Ember Dream or Emerald Dream?

Game Features
Our brave Owls will do whatever they can
to assist the player to get his beloved rich
wins, and the key to the big wins is the fully
stacked scatter on reel 3. The major wins are
hidden in the mysterious dream world of
the free spins.

Main game consists of two prominent features - Scatter
Loot and the Dream Spins. Dream Spins will randomly
pick on of the three different Free Spin modes - Ember
Dream, Emerald Dream and Frozen Dream.
