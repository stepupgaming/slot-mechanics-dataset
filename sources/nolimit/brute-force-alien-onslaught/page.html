Brute Force: Alien Onslaught summary

Nolimit City unleashes an intergalactic battle with the Brute Force: Alien Onslaught slot. After barely surviving their last encounter, a fearless squad of human mercenaries must assemble again to defend Earth from Xylox and his alien swarm. This heavy sci-fi action takes place on a massive six reel grid with five rows across the board. Base Game Mechanics and Features

Surviving the alien assault relies entirely on the powerful xNudge mechanics that dominate the reels. The game features four distinct xNudge wilds, each representing a character in the fight. These stacked symbols always drop down to become fully visible on the grid. Joshua, Jason, and Jade each increase their multiplier by different values with every step they nudge. Jade is the most potent of the human team, adding a massive multiplier jump with each movement. However, the alien overlord Xylox is the real threat. When the Xylox xNudge wild lands, it absorbs and combines the multiplier values of all the other character wilds currently on the screen. To help you connect these massive multipliers, regular wild symbols can land and instantly upgrade all low paying symbols into mid paying symbols to create larger winning combinations.

Bonus Rounds and Free Spins

Triggering the bonus features requires collecting a mix of red and blue scatter symbols. Landing three scatters launches either REDemption Spins or BLU Genesis Spins, depending on the dominant color of the triggering symbols. These rounds introduce sticky xNudge wilds that remain on the board to build persistent multipliers. Landing four scatters upgrades these features to their Super versions, increasing the frequency of sticky wilds. If you manage to perfectly balance the scatter colors by landing two red and two blue symbols, you activate the ultimate Stellar Punishment Spins. This top tier feature drops you directly into the crossfire with guaranteed sticky wilds right from the first spin. Game Features

xNudge® Wild

There are 4 types of xNudge® Wilds which land as a stacked symbol and always nudge to be fully visible. For Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds, a random multiplier value updates at the bottom of the xNudge® Wild when it’s fully visible and adds to the xNudge® Wild multiplier value, respectively. Xylox xNudge® Wild combines the multiplier values of all other xNudge® Wilds and adds them to its own. xNudge® Wilds have different nudge multiplier values: - Joshua xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier value by +1 and the maximum multiplier value that can be awarded is 7x. - Jason xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier value by +2 and the maximum multiplier value that can be awarded is 15x. - Jade xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier value by +5 and the maximum multiplier value that can be awarded is 40x. - Xylox xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier by + 1.When Xylox xNudge® Wild lands for the first time, it collects multiplier values from Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds. If it becomes sticky, it continues collecting multipliers from non-sticky Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds until the round ends. Another +1 is awarded once the nudge is complete. - xNudge® Meter: The xNudge® Meter summarises the overall xNudge® multiplier values on the winning symbols.

Wilds

When a Wild symbol appears, it randomly transforms all low-paying symbols on the reel into mid-paying symbols.

REDemption Spins

Landing 3 Scatter symbols, including at least 2 red Scatters will trigger 10 REDemption Spins. Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands. Joshua xNudge® Wild will not land during this feature.

Super REDemption Spins

Landing 4 Scatter symbols, including at least 3 red Scatters will trigger 10 Super REDemption Spins. Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands. Joshua and Jason xNudge® Wild will not land during this feature.

BLU Genesis Spins

Landing 3 Scatter symbols, including at least 2 blue Scatters will trigger 10 BLU genesis Spins. Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds may become sticky when they land. Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands.

Super BLU Genesis Spins

Landing 4 Scatter symbols, including at least 3 blue Scatters will trigger 10 Super BLU genesis Spins. Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds may become sticky when they land however a sticky Joshua xNudge® Wild will always land on 1st spin and remains sticky throughout the feature. Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands.

Stellar Punishment Spins

Landing 2 red and 2 blue Scatters will trigger 10 Stellar Punishment Spins. Jade xNudge® Wild may become sticky when it lands however a sticky Jade xNudge® Wild will always land on 1st spin and remains sticky throughout the feature. Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands. Joshua and Jason xNudge® Wild will not land during this feature.

Game xMechanics

xNudge®

xNudge®: A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.
