## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xNudge® Wild
There are 4 types of xNudge® Wilds which land as a stacked symbol and always nudge to be fully visible. For Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds, a random multiplier value updates at the bottom of the xNudge® Wild when it’s fully visible and adds to the xNudge® Wild multiplier value, respectively. Xylox xNudge® Wild combines the multiplier values of all other xNudge® Wilds and adds them to its own.

xNudge® Wilds have different nudge multiplier values:

- Joshua xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier value by +1 and the maximum multiplier value that can be awarded is 7x.

- Jason xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier value by +2 and the maximum multiplier value that can be awarded is 15x.

- Jade xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier value by +5 and the maximum multiplier value that can be awarded is 40x.

- Xylox xNudge® Wild: Each nudge increases the win multiplier by + 1.When Xylox xNudge® Wild lands for the first time, it collects multiplier values from Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds. If it becomes sticky, it continues collecting multipliers from non-sticky Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds until the round ends. Another +1 is awarded once the nudge is complete. 

- xNudge® Meter: The xNudge® Meter summarises the overall xNudge® multiplier values on the winning symbols.

## Wilds
When a Wild symbol appears, it randomly transforms all low-paying symbols on the reel into mid-paying symbols.

## REDemption Spins
Landing 3 Scatter symbols, including at least 2 red Scatters will trigger 10 REDemption Spins.

Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands. 

Joshua xNudge® Wild will not land during this feature.

## Super REDemption Spins
Landing 4 Scatter symbols, including at least 3 red Scatters will trigger 10 Super REDemption Spins.

Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands.

Joshua and Jason xNudge® Wild will not land during this feature.

## BLU Genesis Spins
Landing 3 Scatter symbols, including at least 2 blue Scatters will trigger 10 BLU genesis Spins.

Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds may become sticky when they land. Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands.

## Super BLU Genesis Spins
Landing 4 Scatter symbols, including at least 3 blue Scatters will trigger 10 Super BLU genesis Spins.

Joshua, Jason and Jade xNudge® Wilds may become sticky when they land however a sticky Joshua xNudge® Wild will always land on 1st spin and remains sticky throughout the feature.

Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands.

## Stellar Punishment Spins
Landing 2 red and 2 blue Scatters will trigger 10 Stellar Punishment Spins.

Jade xNudge® Wild may become sticky when it lands however a sticky Jade xNudge® Wild will always land on 1st spin and remains sticky throughout the feature. Xylox xNudge® Wild will always be sticky when it lands.

Joshua and Jason xNudge® Wild will not land during this feature.
