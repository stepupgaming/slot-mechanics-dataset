## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## Wild
A Wild symbol can land on any reel except reel 1.

## Rebound Spins
Wins award respins on non winning positions. Winning symbols and all their instances on the reel area will remain sticky during respin.

Every respin that either improves the win, contains a symbol type from the current win or a Wild/Bonus symbol will award an additional respin. During each respin, the multiplier value of all non-winning positions increases by +1 except for Wilds, Bonus and sticky symbols.

Rebound Spins end when there are no more wins.

## Plot Twist
The Plot Twist symbol upgrades all sticky symbols to random higher paying symbols and transforms into the same upgraded symbol. Only one Plot Twist symbol can land per spin. Plot Twist symbol will not affect Wild and Bonus symbols.

If no winning combinations land on the first spin, the Plot Twist symbol transforms into a random mid paying symbol.

## Bonus
Bonus symbols can land on any reel.

Landing 2, 3, 4 or 5 Bonus symbols during a bonus spin will award 2, 3, 4 or 5 additional spins respectively.

## Carpe DIEm Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 5 Carpe DIEm Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Carpe DIEm Spins.

During this feature, Wild symbol will remain sticky on top position of second reel.

## Super Carpe DIEm Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Super Carpe DIEm Spins.

Position multipliers are kept during Super Carpe DIEm Spins. During this feature, Wild symbol will remain sticky on top and bottom position of second reel.

## Mega Carpe DIEm Spins
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 10 Mega Carpe DIEm Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Mega Carpe DIEm Spins.

During this feature, Wild symbols will remain sticky on all positions of second reel.

## Speedrun (Nolimit Bonus)
All Carpe Diem modes in sequence at a cost of 2,800 times the base bet.

Position multipliers are kept.

## God Mode (Nolimit Bonus)
Chase ‘Love Hurts’ by landing GOD symbols on each position at a cost of 1,337 times the bet.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on reel 2.

## Print Spins x20 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 9 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x20 multipliers on all positions.

## Printier Spins x250 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 90 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x250 multipliers on all positions.

## Printiest Spins x1000 (Nolimit Booster)
At the cost of 350 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a spin that starts off with x1000 multipliers on all positions.

## Love Hurts
The max payout of the game is 9,999 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 9,999 times the base bet is awarded.

The max win can also be achieved instantly in the main game by landing one God symbol on each position in a single spin. This overrides any Booster buys.
