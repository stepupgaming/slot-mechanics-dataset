## Infectious xWays®
Any xWays symbols that appear will reveal the same symbol, and if the symbol revealed is already visible on the reels, each in view will be ‘infected’ and will expand as if they are xWays – producing additional ways to win.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xTra™
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the position multipliers of the previous spin. Alternatively, the player may choose to pay a smaller cost to get an Extra Spin where the multipliers are halved. 

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the position multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented. Bonus symbols will not land during Extra Spin. 

Extra Spin requires a Scatter Win to be presented in the main game; Side Hustle wins alone don’t count.

## Scatter Wins
Payouts are awarded when 8 or more symbols of same kind land on the reel area. Winning symbols are removed and will trigger a drop on the reel allowing new symbols to drop in. 

A multiplier of x2 is added on each position where a symbol was removed. If a position multiplier already existed there and that position gets a win on next drop, its multiplier value gets doubled. Additionally, random multipliers are awarded on random positions at the start of each spin. 

The multiplier on a win position can reach up to x16,384.

## Wilds
Wild symbols substitute for any normal paying symbol to help form winning combinations.

## xWays® and Infectious xWays®
xWays® symbols transform into a regular paying symbol and increases the position multiplier by 2, 4 or 8 times. 

Infectious xWays®: The revealed xWays® symbol will infect all of the other same symbols on the reel area to expand to the same size. 

If more than one xWays® or Infectious xWays® symbols land, then all those symbols transform into the same symbol.

## Bomb
The Bomb symbol triggers when no win combinations remain on the reels. 

Once activated, it removes itself and all symbols above it on its reel. The Bomb then increases the multiplier of its position by x2, the position one row above by x4, the next position by x8, and so on. 

All Bombs will explode before the next collapse. 

Bonus symbols are unaffected by the explosion.

## Duck Hunt Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Duck Hunt Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Duck Hunt Spins. 

Landing an Extra +1 Shot symbol awards +1 spin. During this feature, any 1 of the following 6 upgrades is randomly awarded: 

• Upgraded xWays®: xWays® symbols are replaced by Infectious xWays®. 

• Upgraded Bomb: Removes itself and all symbols above AND below it on its reel. Increases its position multiplier by x2, the positions one row above and below by x4, the next positions by x8, and so on. 

• Extra +2 Shots: Replaces the Extra +1 Shot symbol and awards +2 spins upon landing. 

• +2 Upgrades: Awards additional +2 upgrades for the Duck Hunt Spins. 

• Extra Multipliers: Awards 5 additional multipliers on random positions on 1st spin of the Duck Hunt Spins. 

• +1 Spin: Awards an extra spin at the start.

## Hawk Eye Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 8 Hawk Eye Spins. 

It has the same features as Duck Hunt Spins but 2 upgrades are randomly awarded instead.

## Big Game Spins (Nolimit Bonus)
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 10 Big Game Spins. 

It has the same features as Duck Hunt Spins but 3 upgrades are awarded instead.

## xWays® Juicer (Nolimit Bonus)
At a cost of 500 times the base bet, the player is awarded with 7 spins of xWays® Juicer. 

It has the same features as Duck Hunt Spins but Infectious xWays® is guaranteed to land on each spin. No other upgrades are awarded during this feature except the Upgraded xWays®.

## Bomb Juicer (Nolimit Bonus)
At a cost of 2,800 times the base bet, the player is awarded with 7 spins of Bomb Juicer. 

It has the same features as Duck Hunt Spins but the Upgraded Bomb is guaranteed to land on each spin. No other upgrades are awarded during this feature except the Upgraded Bomb.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus on the second reel.

## Bonus Hunt (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 30 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on the initial spin and on each avalanche triggered by a win or Bomb explosion.

## Day 8 Spins (Nolimit Booster)

## Day 64 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 90 times the base bet, the initial multiplier value of all positions will be x64.

## Day 512 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 800 times the base bet, the initial multiplier value of all positions will be x512.

## Day 2048 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 4,000 times the base bet, the initial multiplier value of all positions will be x2048.

## DUCK MAN RETURNS
The max payout of the game is 40,000 times the base bet. 

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 40,000 times the base bet is awarded.

## Side Hustle
An optional add-on bet that increases the total cost of the spin. It allows the player to bet on a specific outcome, awarding a fixed win on top of other wins if that outcome occurs. The cost and win amount depend on the choice of feature bet. Multiple Side Hustles can be placed simultaneously. 

Side Hustle Options: 

• Kill Santa: Pays out if at least 1 Santa is removed. 

• Kill Granny: Pays out if at least 1 Granny is removed. 

• Kill Phil: Pays out if at least 1 Phil is removed. 

• Kill Gamer: Pays out if at least 1 Gamer is removed. 

• Kill Fanboy: Pays out if at least 1 Fanboy is removed. 

• Kill Duck: Pays out if at least 1 Duck is removed. 

• Max Multiplier: Pays out if at least 1 position reaches a multiplier value of 16384x. 

• Double Bonus: Pays out if at least 2 Bonus symbols land in the base game. 

• Dead Spin: Pays out if the base game spin ends with no win.
