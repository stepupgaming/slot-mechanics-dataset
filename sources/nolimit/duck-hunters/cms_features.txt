## Infectious xWays®
Any xWays symbols that appear will reveal the same symbol, and if the symbol revealed is already visible on the reels, each in view will be ‘infected’ and will expand as if they are xWays – producing additional ways to win.

## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Scatter Wins
Payouts are awarded when 8 or more symbols of same kind land on the reel area. Winning symbols are removed and will trigger a drop on the reel allowing new symbols to drop in.

A multiplier of x2 is added on each position where a symbol was removed. If a position multiplier already existed there and that position gets a win on next drop, its multiplier value gets doubled.

The multiplier on a win position can reach up to x8192.

## xWays® \u0026 Infectious xWays®
xWays® symbols transform into a regular paying symbol and increases the position multiplier by 2, 4 or 8 times.

Infectious xWays®: The revealed xWays® symbol will infect all of the other same symbols on the reel area to expand to the same size.

If more than one xWays® or Infectious xWays® symbols land, then all those symbols transform into the same symbol.

## Bomb
An exploding Bomb removes adjacent regular paying symbols in a 3x3 pattern. Symbol size gets doubled on the positions affected by Bomb.

All Bombs will explode before the next collapse.

Bonus and Wild symbols are unaffected by the explosion

## Duck Hunt Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Duck Hunt Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Free spins. Landing a Extra +1 Shot symbol awards +1 spin.

During this feature, any 1 of the following 3 upgrades is randomly awarded.

- Upgraded xWays® : xWays® symbols are replaced by Infectious xWays®.

- Upgraded Bomb: An exploding Bomb removes adjacent regular paying symbols in a 5x5 pattern.

- Extra +2 Shots: Replaces the Extra +1 Shot symbol and awards +2 spins upon landing.

## Hawk Eye Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 8 Hawk Eye Spins.

It has same features like Duck Hunt Spins but 2 upgrades are randomly awarded instead.

## Big Game Spins
Landing 5 Bonus symbols will trigger 10 Big Game Spins.

It has same features like Hawk Eye Spins but all 3 upgrades are awarded instead.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is 5 times more likely to trigger Free Spins.

Guaranteed Bonus symbol on the second reel.

## Day 2 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2.8 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be x2.

## Day 64 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 90 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be x64.

## Day 1024 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3,000 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be x1024.

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the position multipliers of the previous spin.

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the position multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented. Bonus symbols will not land during Extra Spin.

## Sky is the Limit
The max payout of the game is 30,000 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 30,000 times the base bet is awarded.
