## xBet®
At an extra cost per spin, the player gets an increased chance of getting into the Bonus Modes. The way this happens varies from game to game. The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xNudge®
xNudge®:  A simple yet effective feature that turns any game into a massive potential bearing title! xNudge works alongside wild reels, wherein landing a wild in view has the potential to increase the multiplier attached, as the wild nudges to fill the entire reel. If the wild is not centred in the reel, it will either nudge up or down, incrementing its multiplier by 1 for each nudge.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## Reel Area
The game has 6 reels and 4 rows, with locked Enhancer Cells placed at the bottom of the middle 4 reels.

Winning combinations will explode and allow new symbols to fall in. With each win, an Enhancer position unlocks, starting from left to right.

## Avasplit Effect
When winning symbols explode, some of the remaining symbols are randomly selected for Avasplit Effect which increases their symbol size by 1.

## Wilds \u0026 xNudge® Wilds
Regular Wilds and xNudge® Wilds can land on any reel. When a part of the xNudge® Wild symbol lands on the reels, it will begin to nudge up or down until it is fully visible on the entire reel. Each nudge increases the xNudge® Wild multiplier by 1.

If a nudged over symbol already has a multiplier then it’s value will also be added to xNudge® Wild multiplier. xNudge® Wild will not nudge down on locked Enhancer positions.

xNudge® can also receive a higher starting multiplier if it originates from a Hellevator Booster that already has a multiplier.

## Enhancer Cells
Each Enhancer reveals a symbol once unlocked. If the revealed symbol is removed then a new symbol appears on it’s position on next avalanche.

## Demon
When a Demon Symbol lands, it removes a random number of symbols from the reel area. Symbols larger than size 1 cannot be removed by the Demon symbol. It then transforms into a random regular paying symbol with a multiplier equal to number of symbols it removed.

If there is any win, Demon symbol will be triggered after winning symbols are removed. If no symbol is removed then it just transforms into any regular paying symbol with a multiplier value 1x.

## Hell Spins
Landing 3 Scatter or Hellevator Booster in base game will trigger 8 Hell Spins. If Hellevator Booster lands during bonus trigger or during bonus round then it persists on reel area throughout the round.

Hellevator Booster reveals a random symbol and cannot be removed by Demon symbol. If the revealed symbol takes part in winning then it gets removed and a new symbol is revealed on next avalanche.

When Hellevator Booster gets a multiplier, it’s value is stored and the progress is carried over throughout the round. When xNudge® increases the Hellevator Booster’s multiplier, the boost only lasts for the current spin and reverts to its previous value after the spin ends. Enhancer symbols resets on each spin. 

During Bonus mode, Scatters are collected if they land and progresses the Scatter meter. When 3 Scatters are collected then +2 spins are awarded and a Hellevator Booster adds to the reel area from the next spin. Scatter meter resets after collecting 3 Scatters.

## xBet™ (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2.4 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed a Bonus symbol on reel 1.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## Bonus Hunt (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 15 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed either blank symbols, Scatter symbols or Hellevator Boosters.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## God Mode
Chase Max Win by landing a Max Win symbol on Enhancer position at a cost of 3,800 times the base bet.

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when Hell Spins ends. The additional spin will keep the Hellevator multipliers of the previous spin.

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the Hellevator multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the Hell Spins/previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented.
