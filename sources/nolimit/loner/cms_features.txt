## xBet®
At a cost of an extra 50%, the player is guaranteed a Monitor symbol on reel 1.

Double Feature: At the cost of 35 times the base bet, the player is guaranteed 2 Monitor symbols on reels 1 and 3.

The payout for each symbol is not affected.

## xSplit®
xSplit® was first made available within xWays Hoarder xSplit, released in July 2021. On this first occasion, xSplit symbols go a long way towards assisting wins through the feature, as any xSplit symbols in view to the right of a Scatter symbol will turn the Scatter into a Super Scatter symbol – which within this release assists in progressing the feature through several levels. However, aside from the progression through bonus levels, xSplit as individual symbols increase win ways during base play. Any xSplit symbol in view will split all symbols to the left of it into two, increasing the ways to win. After this, the xSplit symbols will turn into wilds – for the potential of huge wins.

## Infectious xWays®
Any xWays symbols that appear will reveal the same symbol, and if the symbol revealed is already visible on the reels, each in view will be ‘infected’ and will expand as if they are xWays – producing additional ways to win.

## xGOD®
The most potent feature to date in the Nolimit City xMechanic portfolio. xGod® was first introduced in the game Nine to Five, released in November 2023.

xGod® is a powerful single feature capable of delivering the max win in an instant. May trigger the max win in the base game or the bonus modes - without the assistance of any other features in the game or wins in the game.

## Monitors
Monitor symbol can land on reels 1, 3 and 5.

If 1 or 2 Monitors land, they’ll reveal one of the following symbols:

Wild: The regular 1x1 Wild symbol.

xSplit®: Splits all other symbols to the left and right and doubles the value of that symbol. It turns into a Wild during a split but does not split Monitor symbols.

Infectious xWays®: Transforms into a normal paying symbol of size 2-4. It infects all occurrences of that symbol on the reels, turning them into size 2-4 as well. Infected symbols can also be split by Split.

Max Win: Triggers Max Win.

If 3 Monitors land, they’ll reveal one of the following:

- Pin The Win
- Tick Tick Boom
- Shoot Shoot Duck
- Max Win

## Big Wild
The 2x2 or 3x3 Big Wild symbol can land fully stacked or partial on reels 2-4 during any spin. 

xSplit® splits the Big Wild on all of its affected positions.

## Pin The Win
Triggered when 3 Monitors reveal Pin The Win.

The Pin The Win logo moves around the game area. When hitting a corner the win shown is awarded and the round is over. Any other position will award a random win multiplier between 1x-500x that will be applied to the awarded win.

Wins displayed in the corners are 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1000 and Max Win.

## Shoot Shoot Duck
Triggered when 3 Monitors reveal Shoot Shoot Duck.

The game starts with 5 shots. Shoot the ducks to collect awards.

Duck symbols contain:

- Symbols: With awards X times the base bet (1x-1000x).

- Bomb: Collects all visible awards when shot.

- Ammo clip: Increases shots back up to 5.

- Van: Increases its value each step by collecting all visible awards on the game area.

- Max Win: Award Max Win when shot.

The round ends when there are no shots left or Max Win is awarded.

## Tick Tick Boom
Triggered when 3 Monitors reveal Tick Tick Boom.

Choose a target to blow up. A reward between 5-1000 times the base bet will be revealed and there is also a possibility of Max Win.

## God Mode
Monitors may reveal M A X symbols awarding Max Win.

## xGod™
xGod™ is when you collect a Max Win symbol, triggering a Max Win. 

xGod™ can be achieved in the following ways:

- Base game: Monitor reveals a Max Win symbol.
- Shoot Shoot Duck: Shoot a Max Win symbol.
- Pin The Win: Collect a Max Win symbol.
- Tick Tick Boom: Reveals the Max Win.

## Max Win
The max payout of the game is 14,999 times the base bet.

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 14,999 times the base bet is awarded.
