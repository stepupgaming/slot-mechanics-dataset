## xWays®
xWays® unlocks unprecedented win potential for players. Usually seen in a fixed Ways game, any xWays symbols that land reveals a stack of the same symbol, increasing the ways available to win, and when working alongside some of the other mechanics available in this series, such as xNudge or xSplit, can pave the way for some huge wins.

xWays® first made an appearance in Pixies Vs Pirates, although many players will more likely recognise this mechanic from Punk Rocker, an early 2020 release. Alongside these two releases, xWays has made an appearance in a host of other NolimitCity games, including Dragon Tribe, East Coast vs West Coast, and San Quentin – the early 2021 release which rocked the community with news of a 150,000x maximum win availability.

## Infectious xWays®
Any xWays symbols that appear will reveal the same symbol, and if the symbol revealed is already visible on the reels, each in view will be ‘infected’ and will expand as if they are xWays – producing additional ways to win.

## xTra™
At the end of your round, you might get the chance to buy an extra spin without resetting your current progress. Keep your momentum and go for bigger rewards instead of starting over.

## xWays® and Infectious xWays®
xWays® symbols transform into a regular paying symbol and increases the position multiplier by 2, 4 or 8 times. Infectious xWays®: The revealed Ways® symbol will infect all of the other same symbols on the reel area to expand to the same size. If more than one Ways® or Infectious Ways® symbols land, then all those symbols transform into the same symbol.

## Wilds
A Wild symbol substitutes for any other symbol except for Bonus symbols.

## Scatter Wins
Payouts are awarded when 8 or more symbols of same kind land on the reel area. Winning symbols are removed and will trigger a drop on the reel allowing new symbols to drop in. A multiplier of x2 is added on each position where a symbol was removed. If a position multiplier already existed there and that position gets a win on next drop, its multiplier value gets doubled. The multiplier on a win position can reach up to x8192.

## Bomb
An exploding Bomb removes adjacent regular paying symbols in a 3x3 pattern. Multiplier gets doubled on the positions affected by the Bomb. After exploding, it reveals a random symbol which can be a mid paying symbol, Wild, Infectious Ways® or another Bomb. 

If multiple Bombs appear, their random symbols will be revealed only after all Bombs are exploded.  

Bonus and Wild symbols are unaffected by the explosion.

## Duck Hunt Spins
Landing 3 Bonus symbols will trigger 7 Duck Hunt Spins. Position multipliers are kept during Free spins. Landing an Extra +1 Shot symbol awards +1 spin. 

During this feature, any 1 of the following 3 upgrades is randomly awarded. 

• Upgraded Ways®: Ways® symbols are replaced by Infectious Ways®. 

• Upgraded Bomb: Behaves the same as a regular Bomb but never reveals mid paying symbols. 

• Extra +2 Shots: Replaces the Extra +1 Shot symbol and awards +2 spins upon landing.

## Hawk Eye Spins
Landing 4 Bonus symbols will trigger 8 Hawk Eye Spins.

It has the same features as Duck Hunt Spins but 2 upgrades are randomly awarded instead.

## Big Game Spins
Landing 5 Bonus Symbols will trigger 10 Big Game Spins. 

It has the same features as Hawk Eye Spins but all 3 upgrades are awarded instead.

## Bonus Booster (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 2 times the base bet, the player is 5 times more likely to trigger Free Spins. 

Guaranteed Bonus symbol on the second reel.

## Day 8 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 10 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be x8.

## Day 64 Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 90 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be x64.

## Happy Hour Spins (Nolimit Booster)
At a cost of 3,000 times the base bet, initial multiplier value of all positions will be x8 and the middle two reels are guaranteed to be completely filled with Bombs on the initial drop.

## Extra Spin
Players might get the opportunity to purchase an additional spin when a round ends. The additional spin will keep the position multipliers of the previous spin. 

The cost of the spin will be calculated based on the position multipliers. If the cost of the Extra Spin is less than or equal to the win on the previous spin, then the Extra Spin option will be presented. Bonus symbols will not land during Extra Spin.

## Too Drunk To Miss 
The max payout of the game is 33,333 times the base bet. 

When the total win exceeds this amount the game round will end and 33,333 times the base bet is awarded.
