## Base Game Mechanics and Features

The base game is fueled by a tumbling mechanic where winning combinations disappear so new symbols can fall into place. Every time you score a win with medium or high paying symbols, or land a scatter, you feed the Ritual Bar located at the bottom of the screen. Advancing through the five levels of the Ritual Bar removes lower paying symbols and converts medium symbols into high paying demonic entities. You will also encounter Sticky Wilds that drop to the bottom of the grid and remain there until they contribute to a winning connection.
