Book of Shadows (Nolimit City) Review

This is NoLimit City’s first game in the ever-popular book slot series, and we guess it was about time these guys also joined the club of “bookworms”. We give a standing ovation to the fact that this developer has decided not to play it safe with their debut, and Book of Shadows is bound to make some waves in the community. This is not least due to the massive 30,338x max win potential, but also because it comes with some rather unorthodox features.

The theme itself is far from your typical Ra-style copycat, and you’ll be taken to a spooky graveyard with cartoon-style symbols here. The game comes with the standard 5x3 reel setup with 10 paylines, but you can alter this from the get-go to a 5x4 or 5x5 setup via the Shadow Rows feature. This will cost a bit more per spin, however, but it also slightly increases your RTP and, of course, the number of paylines (from 10 to 15 or 20). Keep in mind that the customizable RTP ranges can be adjusted from casino to casino.

It’s refreshing enough to not get yet another trip to some ancient Egyptian tomb, and this game also allows you to lock reels in place for an extra cost. In addition, you can gamble all wins and there is even a special gamble feature where you can win the bonus round. All in all, you have a lot of different options to play with here, and this will suit players who enjoy taking destiny into their own hands. The bonus round is surprisingly standard though, which is perhaps the biggest drawback we can find in this game.

Book of Shadows

What are the bonus features?

First of all, you have the Lucky Locks feature, which is similar to what Microgaming calls Hyperspins. You can lock up to 4 reels in place after every spin, and spin only the remaining unlocked reel(s).

The cost of each Lucky Locks spin will, of course, depend on how many reels you’ve locked, what symbols are on the locked reels, and so forth. You can, for example, lock 2 scatters in the hope of landing a third one, but this will naturally cost more than a spin that cannot trigger such a potentially lucrative outcome.

Of course, you also have the ability to gamble your winnings here, a feature that most book slots offer. You can take your chances in the double or nothing game after regular wins or bonus round wins, and it’s as simple as picking the correct card color, red or black. You can gamble 5 times in a row if you want, but the winning amount cannot go above €500,000.

What makes this game stick out is the fact that you can also gamble to win the free spins bonus round. The feature is called the Wheel of Bonus, and your chances of winning increases with the size of your bet. You cannot bet above 60x your stake, however, and you will see a satanic, pentagram wheel of fortune with a red segment that spins. The size of the red segment increases with your bet size, and you can win 10 free spins via this feature.

Free spins in Book of Shadows

You need to land at least 3 book scatter symbols anywhere on the same spin to trigger the free spins bonus round here. How many spins you get depend on your number of rows:

  • 3 rows and 10 paylines equals 14 free spins.
  • 4 rows and 15 paylines equals 12 free spins.
  • 5 rows and 20 paylines equals 10 free spins.

Like with all book slots, a special symbol will be chosen before the feature starts. This is called the Golden symbol here, and you have a chance to reshuffle the deck if you’re not happy with the outcome the first time. This might also downgrade the golden symbol though, and you have to accept whatever comes on your second try.

The golden symbols will expand to cover the full reel after the regular win calculation, and the good news is that these expanded reels do not have to be adjacent to win. Get 5 stacked goth girl reels, and you’ll win 10,000x your stake. You can also retrigger the bonus round by landing 3+ scatters on the reels.

On average, the free spins round is triggered on every 150 spins or so, but you also have the option to buy the feature (not available for UK players). The bonus buy option will set you back 60x, 90x or 120x your stake for 10 free spins with 3, 4, or 5 rows, respectively.

How to play

Undoubtedly, Book of Shadows is a bit different from other games in the series. It comes with some unusual options that you will not find anywhere else, and here we’ll take a closer look at how you can best play this game. The interface is simple enough, and we will guide you through everything you need to know here.

The dollar sign icon down to the left allows you to choose a bet level between 10p and £100 per spin, but that’s only if you go with the default 3 rows option. There is a black semi-transparent blind covering rows 4 and 5 (shadow rows is the in-game name for it), and you can click the upwards pointing arrow to activate these extra rows. It will, however, increase your bet by either 1.5x or 2x, and also the RTP increases slightly the more rows you unlock (96.01 %, 96.07 %, or 96.19 %, respectively).

Keep in mind that the game comes with customizable RTP ranges, so you might see lower values than this from certain casinos. Anyway, the volatility remains very high no matter how many rows you go with, and the base game is all about landing matching symbols on 1 or more of the 10 paylines from left to right to create winning combos. Of course, when you add the 4th or 5th row, the ways to win will increase to 15 or 20 paylines, respectively.

Book of Shadows

You can check out the paytable via the hamburger menu, but you’ll need to scroll through the feature information first. The highest value symbol is the book scatter/wild and the goth girl. You need only 2 of the premium symbols on a payline to win, while 3 are needed for the low-value royals. The 5 regular premium symbols pay between 500x and 50x, which is solid, and the book scatter serves double duty as a wild symbol (which is common for the book slot series).

Finally, you can switch on the fast play option via the lightning icon next to the spin button. You can also set up the autoplay feature with between 10 and 1,000 autospins. The feature can be set to stop when your balance has increased or decreased by a certain percentage, and the bonus buy option is found via the golden star icon on the left-hand side.

The 200 Spins Book of Shadows Experience

The visuals of Book of Shadows slot immediately caught our attention, as the game certainly looks very different than other games in the series. We also liked the twists NoLimit City has added to this game, and not least the insane 30,338x potential. For all of these reasons we could hardly wait to take Book of Shadows for our 200 test spin experience, and below we’ll tell you all about how it went.

We decided to immediately unlock the 2 shadow rows, which made the game into a 5x5 grid. This increased our bet size from €4 to €8, but we just had to play this one at maximum capacity. We won around 3x our stake after a few spins, and decided to check out the gamble feature. We chose the red card and won, and immediately decided to see if this winning streak could carry us all the way into the bonus round.

We chose the Wheel of Bonus option, and spun the pentagram wheel with the 3-eyed goat in the middle. Lady Fortuna put her foot down however, and we lost the gamble. After a few more spins we landed a book scatter on the middle reel, and decided to use the Lucky Locks feature to keep it in place. We landed yet another scatter on the second reel, and decided to lock that reel also.

Incredibly enough, we actually landed the sorely needed third scatter on reel 5 on the next spin, and the bonus round was ours. We got the 10 royal symbol as our chosen symbol, and decided to go with that. We had several teaser spins with 2 book scatters present, and won smaller wins each time. With only 4 spins left we had our first golden symbol expanding reels feature, and this landed us a 10x win.

In the end we won a total of €98.8, but getting the feature via the locked reels cost us €166.63, so not exactly profitable this time. A bit hooked, we immediately bought the 5 rows bonus round for €480, and crossed our fingers once again. This time the A royal symbol was chosen, and we immediately won 16.8x with 3 expanded rows. We kept getting minute wins after that, until we got 4 expanded A rows with only 2 spins left. This landed us 120x our stake, which was decent, and in total we won €627.2 from the bought bonus round.

Review Summary

Very seldom do we see such a creative take on the already crowded and overused book genre. NoLimit City has bravely decided to stray pretty far from the common path most developers take, and there is a lot more to see here than a simple little twist to the tale. Book of Shadows is literally a dream come true for every book fan who wants a massive potential and the ability to tweak and play with different options.

Most book slots are limited to 5,000x your stake, so a 30,338x potential is certainly bound to raise some eyebrows in the community. The ability to lock reels is clever, but it can be quite expensive if you lock scatters. Also, the option of expanding the reel set is fun to play with, but might also frustrate indecisive players. The bonus round is the least innovative thing in this game, paradoxically, as this is where the twist usually comes from most developers.

Book of Shadows (Nolimit City) Slot FAQs

What is the RTP of Book of Shadows slot?

The default Return to Player depends on the number of rows you chose, but beware that this game also comes with customizable RTP ranges. This means that operators can lower the RTP ranges, which is something you need to check with each casino you play at. The payout percentages are as follows: 96.01 % with 3 rows (96.2 % with bonus buy); 96.07 % with 4 rows (96.29 % with bonus buy); 96.19 % with 5 rows (96.43 % with bonus buy).

How volatile is Book of Shadows?

It’s a very high volatility game that scores 10 out of 10 on NoLimit City’s own scale, something they call “extreme volatility”.

What is the biggest win possible?

You can win up to 30,338x your stake, which is a lot more than the standard 5,000x book slot max win. Play with the highest possible bet of £100, and you can pocket up to £3,033,800 via the bonus round here.

How does the Lucky Locks feature work?

You can choose to lock up to 4 reels, and only spin the remaining reels again. This will, however, cost you extra per spin, and how much will vary depending on what you stand to win or trigger.

How does the Shadow Rows feature work?

The default setup is 5 reels, 3 rows and 10 paylines. However, you can unlock extra rows and paylines for a price as follows: 4 rows and 15 paylines costs 1.5x your regular stake (bet range: 15p to £150); 5 rows and 20 paylines costs 2x your regular stake (bet range: 20p to £200).

Is there a free spins mode in Book of Shadows?

Yes, it comes with a randomly chosen expanding symbol and the possibility for retriggering the feature.
