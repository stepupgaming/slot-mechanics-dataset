Chase for Glory Review

Wild Streak Gaming takes you to the Colosseum in ancient Rome this time, and you get to meet 4 fierce-looking gladiators on both sides of the grid. The visuals are decent overall, and the symbols land with a marble backdrop. We can reveal up front that there are no actual fighting animations in this game, so don’t get your hopes up in that regard. The base game is about as barebones as it gets, with only wilds to help you out here and there.

Luckily, the Chase for Glory bonus round isn’t all that hard to trigger, and it comes along every 117 spins on average. You get to choose your favorite gladiator for the battle that is about to play out, and he or she corresponds to 1 of 4 grids. Each gladiator collects coin symbols, and if your choice wins the battle you get the winnings from all 4 grids. Each gladiator grid kicks in after a certain amount of free spins, and you get multiplier wilds to boot. The 7,887x potential is decent as well, but overall the experience wasn’t all that memorable.

Wild Streak Gaming - Slot Developer

Wild Streak Gaming - delivers physical slot machines as well as online slots, and they have been in the business since 2015. With 27 video slots to their name at this point, this is a mid-level developer with a somewhat dated style that’s clearly inspired by their brick and mortar games.

Slot Theme And Storyline

We have seen plenty of installments in the Gladiator genre over the years, and Chase for Glory slot does not stick out all that much, to be honest. The animated characters do lift the presentation a tad above average, perhaps, but there is no actual fighting going on. It’s more of a Mexican standoff, really, but we did like the Caesar thumb symbol that plays an important part in the bonus round.

Chase for Glory RTP, Volatility, And Max Win

The top-tier Chase for Glory RTP of 96 % is about as average as they come. However, operators can adjust the RTP down to 95 or 94 %, due to customizable RTP ranges. This game scores 5 out of 5 on the in-game volatility scale, so it’s safe to say that you’re in for a highly volatile experience. The Chase for Glory max win is 7,887x the stake, and it comes with a hit rate of 1 in 68,910,481 spins.

Chase for Glory Rules And Gameplay

You can play with bet levels of £0.3 to £240, which is a decent range. However, this is one of these games where you cannot play with £1 (or £0.1), which is a bit annoying. You win when matching symbols land in any position on 3 to 5 adjacent reels starting from the leftmost reel, which is how the 243 win ways system works. Wild symbols step in for any pay symbol to help complete and/or improve wins, but the wild symbol holds no value of its own in Chase for Glory slot.

Chase for Glory Bonuses And Special Features

The base game does not come with any bonus features unless you count wild symbols, so Chase for Glory slot is all about the bonus round.

Chase for Glory Free Spins

You need 3, 4, or 5 scatters in view to trigger the Bonus Round, and this gives you 25 free spins no matter the amount of triggering scatters. The bonus round comes with 4 different 5x3-sized reel sets, and you get to pick 1 of the 4 gladiators up front. Only the golden helmet gladiator’s grid will be active from the start. By default, you only get wins from the reel set that corresponds to your chosen fighter, and each gladiator’s grid comes with different multiplier wilds when they become active. It works as follows:

  • Golden Helmet Tripod Gladiator (green faction) - is active for all 25 free spins, and you can land x2 multiplier wilds on reels 2 and 4.
  • Armored Sword Gladiator (red faction) - is active for the last 20 free spins, and you can land x2 and x3 multiplier wilds on reels 2 and 4.
  • Net & Spear Gladiator (blue faction) - is active for the last 15 free spins, and you can land x3 and x5 multiplier wilds on reels 2 and 4.
  • Female Gladiator (purple faction) - is active for the last 10 free spins, and you can land x5 multiplier wilds on reels 2 and 4.

You will also land special Coin symbols during the bonus round, and these are collected by the gladiator associated with the grid. The Ceasar’s thumb-down symbol is known as the Reset symbol, and it resets the coin collection meter of the gladiator with the highest number collected to zero. Don’t sweat it, however, as your chosen fighter’s coin tally will never reset.

All regular wins are on display per grid as well, and the gladiator with the most collected coins becomes the winner as the bonus round ends. If your chosen gladiator becomes the winner, all winnings from the other gladiators are added to your total win. The winner is chosen randomly if there is a tie between gladiators. There is no way to retrigger the bonus round, and you cannot win extra Chase for Glory free spins either.

Chase for Glory Bonus Buy (not UK)

Eligible players will find the Buy Free Spins button above the reels, and purchasing the feature will set you back 100x the stake. This gives you 3+ triggering scatters on the spin that follows, and the bonus round comes with an organic hit rate of 1 in 117 spins on average. This is well below average according to our stats, and the RTP does not change when you go for the bonus buy option.

Review Summary And Verdict

In this day and age, it really shouldn’t be that difficult to create a slot game where you can place bets of £/€0.1 and £/$1 per spin. However, games like Chase for Glory slot do come along once in a while, and it bothers the OCD in us every time. Looking past that, Wild Streak Gaming has created a decent-looking Gladiator slot, but the fighters don’t do much else than stare each other down at any stage of the game. There are no actual battle animations like you get in some games, but you can’t really expect that every time.

The base game could definitely have been spiced up a bit, and even adding the multiplier wilds you get in the bonus round to the mix would’ve helped. The good news is that you’ll trigger the bonus round every 117 spins on average, which is a solid hit rate well below average. It might be tempting to go with the gladiator who gets the most free spins, but we had no trouble winning the coin collection battle with the female warrior who gets only 10 spins. It’s all random, of course, and any fighter can land you the decent 7,887x max win. It comes with a 1 in 68.9m hit rate though, which is a lot higher than many Pragmatic Play games.
