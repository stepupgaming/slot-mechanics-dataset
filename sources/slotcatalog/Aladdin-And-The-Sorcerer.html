Aladdin and the Sorcerer Review

In Aladdin and the Sorcerer slot from Pragmatic, you get to partake in the epic battle between Aladdin and his archvillain, the evil Sorcerer. These animated characters are found on either side of the reels, and every time they battle you will gain both respins, wilds and potentially multiplier wilds up to 3x. The game comes with a max win potential of 6,250x your stake.

It all plays out on 5 reels, 3 rows and 20 ways to win, and you can place bets up to £100 per spin across all platforms and devices. The base game will give you 3 to 6 randomly placed wilds whenever you land either the Aladdin symbol or the Sorcerer symbol alone, and the battle between them is triggered when you land both on the same spin.

This is a highly volatile game, so expect some dry spells between bigger wins, and don’t expect to trigger the all-important 2 level Respins feature too often. However, when it happens, and Aladdin wins the first round, you can expect big payouts in level 2 if you get lucky with the 2x and 3x multipliers. This is a fun game with plenty of action, and well done animated characters.

What symbols are there?

The higher value symbols on this game are different colored gemstones with golden edge. The low value symbols are the exact same gemstones, but without the golden edge. You will need between 3 to 5 identical symbols on a payline to win, and here we present you with the paytable for Aladdin and the Sorcerer slot:

  • Wild symbol - Pays 50x for 5 on a payline
  • Gold edged red gemstone - Pays 50x for 5 on a payline
  • Gold edged purple gemstone - Pays 20x for 5 on a payline
  • Gold edged blue gemstone - Pays 12.5x for 5 on a payline
  • Gold edged light blue gemstone - Pays 10x for 5 on a payline
  • Gold edged green gemstone - Pays 7.5x for 5 on a payline
  • Same gemstones with no golden edge - Pay between 6.25x to 2x for 5 on a payline

What are the bonus features?

You will see Aladdin with his lamp to the left of the reels, and the evil Sorcerer with his crystal ball to the right. You will want to get these two into an epic battle, cause the more they fight, the more you benefit and win.

The Aladdin bonus symbol will only land on reel 1, while the Sorcerer bonus symbol will only land on reel 5. If either of these symbols land on the reels alone, you will be rewarded with 3 to 6 wild symbols randomly added onto the reels.

The real magic happens when you land both the Aladdin symbol and the Sorcerer symbol on the same spin, as this triggers the Battle respins bonus feature. Firstly, you will see that both reel 1 and 5 become fully stacked wild symbols, and you will be given a re-spins where these 2 reels are frozen in place.

Aladdin and the Sorcerer are now in battle mode, and you will see that each of them have a health meter with 3 bars each. If you land an Aladdin symbol without a Sorcerer symbol, Aladdin will use his lamp to attack, and 1 health bar is removed from his archenemy. The same goes the other way around.

The re-spins feature goes on until Aladdin has lost all of his 3 health bars, but if Aladdin wins you will move on to level 2 of the Battle respins feature. All wilds will then become sticky for the rest of the feature, and you will get 3 respins in level 2. The sticky wilds get a 2x multiplier on the second spin, and this will increase to a 3x multiplier on the third and final spin.

Free spins in Aladdin and the Sorcerer

Aladdin and the Sorcerer actually does not come with any dedicated free spins feature, and this is probably due to the many respins you will benefit from here. Some players might not like this, but we can assure you that the free spins are not missed all that much for the above mentioned reason.

What is the jackpot (max win)?

There is no progressive or fixed jackpot to be won on Aladdin and the Sorcerer slot, but you can still win decent payouts here. With the 3x multiplier you can actually win up to 6,250 times your stake on this game, and that is a decent potential even for a highly volatile slot.
