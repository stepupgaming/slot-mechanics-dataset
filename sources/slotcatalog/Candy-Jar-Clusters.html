Candy Jar Clusters Rules And Gameplay

The Candy Jar Clusters game is played on a purple-framed board with 7 reels and 7 rows. The RNG machine uses the Cluster Pays system, and each combination of at least 6 identical symbols, connected horizontally or vertically, pays. Moreover, symbols explode, causing Tumbles, and new ones fall from above to fill out the gaps.

Therefore, more than one payout can be collected per single spin, and there is a screen in the upper left side where you can see all the winnings in a streak. The Candy Jar Clusters slot is packed with Autospin, Quickspin, Turbo, and several other features. The betting range is really wide, although a round costs only 20 credits - €0.20 to €240 per spin.

Candy Jar Clusters Bonuses & Special Features

Pragmatic Play was not too lavish in the Candy Jar Clusters slot in terms of bonus minigames, but I think the desired effect has been achieved. There are no Gamble games and progressive jackpots, but a bonus round with 3 exciting modifiers and 5 jackpot prizes. You can find the details below:

Tumbles & Random Multiplier

I already mentioned that the slot machine features Tumble wins, but the Candy Jar Clusters by Pragmatic Play also comes with a random multiplier. It may activate on any spin that will result in a win. The Random Multiplier can be x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x25, x50, or x100. The feature affects all rewards during the spin and consecutive tumbles and remains active during main and bonus modes.

Random Free Spins

Besides the regularly paying symbols and wilds, three types of candies may also land on any spin and tumble. Yellow, Red, and Green scatters are collected in the Wild, Free Spins, and Jackpot jars, respectively. They represent the 3 modifiers that can be active during the Candy Jar Clusters bonus round.

It can be triggered randomly after the collection of one or more candies and is played with 1, 2, or all 3 modifiers. You'll receive 10 Candy Jar Clusters free spins if the bonus is triggered with the Wild or/and Jackpot modifiers and 11 to 14 rounds if it was triggered with the Free Spins modifier.

The Wild feature adds 6 to 12 wilds on every free spin and a special Golden Wild joins the action. Whenever it hits the board, the Wild Multiplier increases by x1 and affects all rewards from that moment until the end of the minigame.

As I already noted, the Free Spins modifier awards from 1 to 4 additional free spins but also activates an Extra Free Spins scatter for the bonus duration. Each one adds a round to the remaining balance, and each 5 Extra Free Spins scatters awards one Super Spin. These highly lucrative rounds will be played with high-valued symbols at the end of the minigame.

The Jackpot feature adds 5 fixed prizes in the game, and you can win each one several times. Every time a scatter with the respective Jackpot tag appears, it will be collected. Here are all the pots and the required tags:
