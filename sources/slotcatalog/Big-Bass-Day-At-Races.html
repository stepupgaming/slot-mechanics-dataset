Big Bass Day At The Races Review

You can leave your fishing rod and tackle box at the door when entering into Big Bass Day At The Races slot from Reel Kingdom. This is the bearded fisherman’s day off, and he plans to spend it with a champagne bucket or at least some pints at the race track. All the modifiers, features, and mechanics you loved from Big Bass Amazon Xtreme are back, but the scenery has definitely changed.

This is a reskin job in other words, but surely one that will please a certain crowd of horseracing enthusiasts. We are a bit worried about where this will end if Reel Kingdom plans to flood every popular slot genre with Big Bass copycats, but we have our fingers crossed for this being a one-off. The money symbols are racehorses in this game, and you get a random chance of big wins in the base game. The bonus round is all about different modifiers from the pre-pick game, as well as random modifiers and the enhanced version of the multiplier and extra spins trail.

Reel Kingdom - Slot Developer

Reel Kingdom, the Pragmatic Play super partner studio, exploded onto the scene with the iconic Big Bass Bonanza, and they haven't slowed down since. Their relentless focus on expanding this beloved series has flooded the genre with follow-ups, and seemingly now also reskin jobs judging from the game at hand.

Slot Theme And Storyline

You’ll leave the peaceful lakeside behind in this surprising installment, as Big Bass Day At The Races game takes a detour from fishing and plunges headfirst into the high-energy world of horseracing. The familiar fishing visuals are replaced by a race track, with pints and champagne on the menu. Accompanied by a pleasant soundtrack, your trusted bearded fisherman seems to enjoy his day off, and every jockey seems to be his twin brother or different versions of him.

Big Bass Day At The Races Rules And Gameplay

You win by landing 3 to 5 matching symbols across up to 10 paylines, although the top-tier Champagne bucket symbol also pays for 2 of a kind. All wins start from the leftmost reel, and the game plays out on 5 reels with 3 positions per reel. You can place bets between €0.1 and €250 per spin, which should cater to most player types, and the so-called Man Wild substitutes for any pay symbols to help complete or improve line wins. He appears only in the bonus round though, where he serves as the cash prize collector as well.

Big Bass Day At The Races Bonuses And Special Features

You get a random cash collection with random multiplier feature in the base game, but other than that, Big Bass Day At The Races slot is all about the multiplier and extra spins trail bonus round. It comes with plenty of modifier action though, so let’s dive in!

Ante Bet

The Ante Bet will cost you 50 % more per spin to keep on, and the benefit is a “higher chance of triggering the bonus round”. Unfortunately, Reel Kingdom does not reveal any stats regarding this, but the regular bonus round hit rate is an already solid 1 in 148 spins.

Money Symbols

The jockey-on-racehorse symbols in different variants serve as the Money Symbols in Big Bass Day At The Races slot, and they appear with cash prizes between 0.2x and 1,000x your stake. All money symbols in view may be collected at random times in the base game, which means you win the sum total of the cash prizes. The sum total will then be boosted by a random multiplier between x1 and x50.

Big Bass Day At The Races Free Spins

You need 3+ scatters in view to trigger the Bonus Round, which awards 10 free spins for starters. The crucial 3rd scatter may come into view via a random lasso or respin feature if exactly 2 scatters appear in the base game.

You get to pick different horse names before the bonus round starts, and these reveal different modifiers you bring with you into the free spins round. One or two modifiers are revealed upfront if you trigger the bonus round with 4 or 5 scatters, respectively, and below is an overview of possible modifiers:

  • More Free Spins - you can get up to +6 extra free spins at best.
  • Fixed Horse - gives you up to 3 guaranteed money symbols per free spin.
  • Already Collected Man Wilds - up to 3 wilds are collected in the multiplier and extra spins trail from the get-go.
  • Remove Lowest Horse - eliminate the 0.2x money symbol first, and the 0.5x money symbol the second time it’s revealed.

Man Wilds collects the cash prize symbols in view as usual, and the Man Wild is himself collected in the multiplier and extra spins trail seen above the grid. Every 4th man wild awards +10 extra free spins and increases the cash prize multiplier. The multiplier increases to x2 the first time, and x3, x10, x20, x30, x40, x50 for the following 7 triggers, respectively. You cannot get more extra spins once you’ve reached the x50 multiplier.

Let’s now look at some random modifiers that can trigger from different outcomes in the bonus round. The presence of the collector Man Wild with no money symbols may trigger money symbols to appear at random times, and vice versa. A stampede may transform symbols if the Man Wild is present without money symbols. A lasso can drag both money symbols and the man wild onto the screen if neither is present at the end of any given free spin.

Big Bass Day At The Races Bonus Buy (not UK)

Eligible players can pay 100x the stake to purchase the bonus round via the bonus buy menu seen on the left-hand side. This awards 3 to 5 triggering scatters on the spin that follows, and the bonus buy RTP is the same as the overall RTP of the game.
