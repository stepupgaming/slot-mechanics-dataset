Bear Crazy Review

Bear Crazy is an upcoming picnic-themed online slot crafted by Reel Kingdom in partnership with Pragmatic Play. It offers a light-hearted experience with a rather simple base game and feature-packed bonus. Providers have added a solid Buy Feature set and a bonus bet for those who want to improve the odds. All is packed in a potentially high-RTP bundle and offers heart-warming audio-visuals.

Reel Kingdom is popular among the slot audience with a number of enticing titles, but mostly with the Big Bass Bonanza series. This is one of the longest and most appreciated franchises that already boasts dozens of sequels and spin-offs. Reel Kingdom partners with Pragmatic Play for many of its projects, including BBB. The online slot in focus today is another joint venture and uses Pragmatic’s distribution network.

Bear Crazy comes with colorful designs and a standard layout of 5x3, plus 20 paylines. Symbols pay from left to right, starting from the leftmost side of the grid. Players can use Autoplay, Turbo, Super Turbo, Ante Bet, and several Buy Feature options. The base game looks rather “empty”, but scatters can trigger a bonus game with 3 separate wild features. Try them all via the Bear Crazy demo, or continue with my dedicated review!

Bear Crazy Slot - Reels Screen

Bear Crazy pays when 3, 4, or 5 identical symbols land on a payline and the goofy-looking bear acts as a wild symbol. It is the one with its tongue out, and it can replace any of the regulars to create winning combinations. Wilds also pay the most when landing on a payline - 2x, 4x, and 16x the bet. The other bear-designed symbol, with the big smile, is a scatter, and it does not pay cash.

Picnic Basket is the highest-valued regular, along with the blueberry cheesecake - 0.60x to 4.80x. Next are Honey Pot and Picnic Sign, both awarding between 0.40x and 3.20x. The low-valued symbols are A - 10, and they pay from 0.20x to 1.60x the stake. The betting range is as wide as usual when we discuss Reel Kingdom / Pragmatic Play slots. Bets start at just €0.05 and reach €250 per spin, or up to €500 via the Ante Bet.

The Bear Crazy RTP ranges and goes as high as 96.52%, which is actually a quite decent rate. Due to the high levels of volatility, you’d better avoid the alternative variants of the slot - 95.52% and 94.52%. Reel Kingdom has chosen a balanced math model and the Hit Frequency rate is 24.69% or 1 in 4.05 spins on average. However, fans of the provider will notice the relatively low Bear Crazy max win of 3,900x the bet.

Bear Crazy Features

Bear Crazy offers no grid modifiers and special features during the base game, so the bonus is where we all want to be. The scatter symbol is the trigger as usual, and you will need at least 3 to get yourself near the honey pots. But don’t worry if you land 2 scatters only, there is a chance for the reels without scatters to respin for a second chance of bonus activation. Here is what happens next:

Free Spins Bonus

Free Spins starts when 3, 4, or 5 scatter symbols hit the reels at the same time. They award 10, 15, and 20 free spins, respectively and a feature selection screen. Pick one of the Honey Pots to reveal either Mega Bears, Multiplied Bears, or Expanding Bears. PAW is a special symbol that comes into play during the bonus. At the start of the minigame, a trail will be displayed above the reels.

Every PAW in view will be collected there and each 7th collected PAW will activate a retrigger. You will receive 10 extra free spins and extra wild symbols will be added to the reels. This will repeat for 9 retriggers, and after that, no more extra wild symbols will be added. Still, every 7th collected PAW will add 10 extra rounds with no virtual cap. Here is what the wild features do:

  • Mega Bears - reels 3, 4, and 5 merge to show Mega Symbols of 3x3. If a 3x3 PAW lands, it will mark off 5 positions on the Trail. Each retrigger will increase the win multiplier by +1, and it’s applicable to all wins.
  • Multiplied Bears - a multiplier of x2, x3, or x5 will appear on every wild symbol. It will be applied to the wins affected by the wilds. If more than one wild is part of a winning combo, multipliers multiply each other.
  • Expanding Bears - if a wild symbol can complete a winning combination, it will expand vertically to cover the entire reel.
Bear Crazy Slot – Free Spins Win Screen

Ante Bet & Buy Feature

According to the technical characteristics of the Crazy Bear online slot, the Free Spins triggering frequency is 1 in 130.09 spins on average. Ante Bet can help you with that, as if activated, it will add more scatters on the reels. However, it will also increase the selected bet by 100% and will disable bonus buys. Buy Feature, on the other hand, provides instant access to the bonus with any of the wild features in a Regular or Super variant:

  • Buy Mega Bears - 100x the bet - get the bonus with Mega Bears.
  • Buy Super Mega Bears - 300x the bet - a higher chance to hit wild symbols, and the starting win multiplier is x5.
  • Buy Expanding Bears - 100x the bet - get the bonus with Expanding Bears.
  • Buy Super Expanding Bears - 300x the bet - a higher chance for landing wild symbols.
  • Buy Multiplied Bears - 100x the bet - get the bonus with Multiplied Bears.
  • Buy Super Multiplied Bears - 300x the bet - a higher chance for landing wild symbols.

Theme & Graphics

Bear Crazy looks quite simple at first, with cartoonish design, several bees flying around, and a beaming bear to the right. It is a forest in the back, perhaps where it has come from. Butterflies, daisies, and sunny weather seem like the ideal setting for a picnic. Well, I suppose without the bear, although it's not an aggressive bear, but a smiling one and ready to join a feast.

Despite the generic artwork, animation effects, and audio set, the online slot actually feels great. And much better than most of the most recent Big Bass Bonanza spin-offs. Reel Kingdom has used the latest technology to deliver fine graphics and full compatibility with Android and iOS mobile devices.

Pros And Cons Of Bear Crazy Slot

Pros

  • Light-hearted theme with premium audio-visuals
  • 2 scatter symbols in the base game may activate respins
  • Free Spins with Mega, Expanding, or Multiplier wilds
  • Trail in the bonus awards extra free spins and wilds
  • Ante Bet and Buy Feature (optional)
  • Super Free Spins modes via Bonus Buy

Cons

  • Be aware of adjustable RTP rate
  • High levels of volatility can be tricky
  • No base game modifiers except scatter respins

Our Verdict

Bear Crazy provides a straightforward base game with a potentially high payout rate (96.52%) for balanced wins despite the high volatility. The scatter respins are a nice touch, although they are not guaranteed. Yet, this feature proved to be quite frequent during my test of the slot. And so it almost all comes down to free spins, as it almost always does when Reel Kingdom is involved.

Here, 3 modifiers come into play - Mega, Multiplier, and Expanding, and all these adjectives refer to the wild symbol. Moreover, PAWs get collected and add free spins, also increasing the number of potential wilds on the board. It’s a pity that the wild modifiers can’t combine and that the Bear Crazy max win is just 3,900x the bet. It is of little consolation that the probability of it dropping is significantly better than usual - 1 in 934,741 spins.

So, in conclusion, the game does possess a certain entertaining potential, but only for players looking for a fun way to invest a few coins. High rollers won’t activate the higher stake levels as the potential return is that much covered by honey. The bonus features feel great, but they are few and feel incomplete if no extra free spins come in aid. Bear Crazy is no bad online slot, but it’ll hardly be remembered for anything but the charming theme!
