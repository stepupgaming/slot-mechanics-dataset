Punk Rocker Review

Punk Rocker from NoLimit City certainly has all the loud and in-your-face attitude of the punk era, and it’s backed up by a rather brutal math model that hits like a knuckle-duster. The soundtrack and visuals are a match made in punk heaven, and the game comes packed with all sorts of different and exciting bonus features.

The regular setup is 5 reels, 4 rows and 243 paylines, but you can unlock a 6th reel and play with up to 46,656 ways to win here. The RTP is a decent 96.54 %, and if you haven’t guessed already, this is a highly volatile game from NoLimit City. You can place bets between 25p and £100 per spin across all platforms and devices, and the game’s max win is 15,072x your stake.

As for the bonus features, you will benefit from both xWays mystery symbols and respins with sticky wilds in the base game. The main attraction, however, are the 2 free spins with moving wilds features. Here you will benefit from 5 extras (including wild multipliers up to 25x), and you can also opt to buy these bonus rounds depending on your jurisdiction.

What symbols are there?

All the high value symbols are punk versions of famous icons that look like they could have been painted by Andy Worhol. There is also a finger flipping monkey among them, and the lower value symbols have all the items you’ll need to start a revolution. You will normally win with 3 to 5 matching symbols on a payline, but there is a 6th bonus reel that will open up sometimes. Here is the paytable for Punk Rocker slot:

  • Churchill - Pays 6x for 5 and 20x for 6 on a payline
  • Mona Lisa - Pays 3x for 5 and 10x for 6 on a payline
  • Monkey - Pays 2x for 5 and 6x for 6 on a payline
  • Einstein - Pays 1.6x for 5 and 4x for 6 on a payline
  • Sean Connory - Pays 1.2x for 5 and 4x for 6 on a payline
  • Anarchy symbol - Pays 1x for 5 and 3x for 6 on a payline
  • Mixer switch - Pays 1x for 5 and 3x for 6 on a payline
  • Molotov cocktail - Pays 0.8x for 5 and 2.4x for 6 on a payline
  • Knuckle-duster - Pays 0.8x for 5 and 2.4x for 6 on a payline
  • Boots - Pays 0.8x for 5 and 2.4x for 6 on a payline

What are the bonus features?

Look out for the skull with a lit molotov cocktail in its mouth, as this is the wild symbol on Punk Rocker. It will step in for all regular symbols to give you wins, and it will also turn all symbols above it into wilds.

You will also benefit from special symbols called xWays symbols, and these contain between 2 and 4 of the same symbol when they land. This matching symbol will then expand on the reel, and help you land winning combos. This also expands the actual size of the reel, and you can get up to 46,646 ways to win if all reels are expanded to the max.

The biggest bonus feature in the base game is the Riot Respins, and you trigger this by landing 2 scatters on the same spin. The scatter symbols will turn into sticky wilds, and you get a respin with the 6th reel unlocked and 729 ways to win. If you land more wilds, they become sticky too, and you get a new respin. This goes on as long as you keep landing new wilds, and can lead to some very nice payouts.

Free spins in Punk Rocker

There are 2 different free spins features you can trigger on this game, and they are the Anarchy free spins and Civil War free spins. The first one is triggered when you land 3 scatters, and this gives you 8 spins with the 6th reel unlocked and 729 paylines. The scatters become moving wilds, shifting positions per spin.

You need 4 scatters on the same spin to trigger the Civil War bonus round, and you get 8 spins here also, with 6 reels and 729 ways to win. The only real difference is that you now have 4 moving wilds, instead of just 3.

Both free spins features come with a set of 5 different extras. Above the reels you’ll see a subway train pull in, where the wagons are different reel modifiers. These modifiers will only affect reels with moving wilds on them, and they can give you the following benefits:

  • Wild Multiplier – adds a multiplier value of either 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x, 15x or 25x to the moving wild
  • 4-High xWays – gives you xWays symbols
  • Extra Free Spins – gives you 1 to 3 extra free spins
  • 6-High Reels – all reels land 6 symbols, which means 46,656 paylines
  • Jumping Wild – gives you an extra moving wild

What is the jackpot (max win)?

You will not find any progressive jackpot on Punk Rocker slot, and the key to winning big is the free spins multiplier. If you are lucky with the multiplier, you can win the game’s max win of 15,072 times your stake.
