Drago - Jewels of Fortune Review

We were quite surprised when we first loaded in this game, as we really didn’t expect something like this from Pragmatic Play. We applaud the fact that they have broken away from their usual style and pattern, and Drago - Jewels of Fortune took us for quite a volatile thrill ride in our 200 spins test session. You can read all about that further down in the review, but we’ll start by covering the basics first.

The audiovisual presentation is stunning here, and far from the more generic style we’re used to from this developer. The reels are set right in the dragon’s lair, and you’ll see the 3D animated purple dragon curiously checking you out as you spin the reels. The cave is filled with treasures and gold coins, glimmering temptingly in the background, and it all plays out on an unusual 5-4-4-4-5 reel setup. This is done to highlight each of the corner positions, as these are key when it comes to triggering both of the main bonus features.

You have 1,600 fixed ways to win in this game, and you can place bets between 20p and £100 per spin across all platforms and devices. The base game comes with a very exciting Dragon Super Spin feature, where you’ll collect dragons and climb up a multiplier ladder that can reach as high as 5,000x (or 10,000x if you land a special symbol that doubles all the values). You get to play a similar feature as a prelude to the bonus round, and here you’ll collect spins, multipliers, sticky wilds and mystery symbols to bring with you into a feature that offers a 48,000x potential.

Logo

What are the bonus features?

The first thing you’ll probably notice about this game is the 4 corner reel positions that stick out like sore thumbs. Each corner position is elaborately framed, and these positions are key to triggering the main bonus features in Drago - Jewels of Fortune slot. We’ll start with the base game features first, and then move on to the main attraction right after.

Whenever you land 4 identical dragon symbols in the 4 corner positions, you trigger the Dragon Super Spin feature. There are 4 different dragon symbols in this game, and each one of them can trigger the feature. You will only land matching dragon symbols during the feature, or blank symbols of no value. The multiplier paytable is displayed on the right-hand side, and the respins tally is reset to 3 every time you land at least 1 dragon.

The dragons you land become sticky to the end of the Super Spin round, when your total winnings are summed up. Each dragon symbol comes with a unique paytable of different multipliers, and the more sticky dragons you land, the bigger your multiplier boost will be. You can also land an upgrade symbol, and this effectively doubles all the multiplier values on the paytable. Here is the multiplier range you can look forward to for each of the 4 dragons:

  • 5 to 20 yellow dragons give you a multiplier between 2x and 100x.
  • 5 to 20 green dragons give you a multiplier between 4x and 250x.
  • 5 to 20 red dragons give you a multiplier between 5x and 500x.
  • 5 to 20 purple dragons give you a multiplier between 10x and 5,000x.

Free spins in Drago - Jewels of Fortune

You need to land a white crystal bonus symbol in all 4 corners on the same spin to trigger the free spins feature here. This symbol can only land on reels 1 and 5, and a respin game will play out before the bonus round starts. You start out with 3 respins, but the tally resets to 3 again whenever a colored crystal symbol lands.

The point is to collect these crystal symbols that give you different extras in the bonus round, which makes the free spins feature quite unique each time you trigger it. Here is an overview of what the different color crystal symbols will give you:

  • Green crystals give you a random number of free spins between 1 and 10.
  • Red crystals give you between 1 and 5 sticky wilds.
  • Purple crystals give you a random number of 5 to 50 mystery symbols.
  • Blue crystals increase the win multiplier by +1 or +2.

When the respins feature is over, you will bring with you whatever extras you collected and the bonus round will finally start. You’ll see the bonus goodies you brought with you displayed on either side of the reels, and there is no way to retrigger the free spins feature or get extra spins once it starts.

How to play

This is one of those games that sound a bit complicated when you read about them, but it’s really quite simple once you get into it. Don’t worry, we’ll run you through everything you need to know here, and you might wanna skim through this part if you are an experienced player.

The hamburger menu down to the left-hand side takes you into the game settings. Here you can turn on the Quick spin option, and this makes the game pace ultra-fast if you hold down the spacebar. You can also turn on a battery saver mode, which might be relevant if you play on mobile or tablet, and the intro splash screen can also be turned off to save time next time you load the game.

Game workflow

Of course, you can also toggle on/off the game sound effects and the background music if you wish, and check out the game history after you’ve played a while. You can adjust your bet level in the game settings too, or use the + and - buttons next to the spin button. Either way, the bet range is between 20p and £100 per spin, which is nothing out of the ordinary.

The “i” icon takes you into the game’s paytable, where you can check out all the different symbol values and also read about the bonus features. Click the autoplay button to set up the autospins feature with between 10 and 100 spins. You also have a lot of options for the feature to stop, like when a given amount is lost or won, or when you trigger the bonus round.

The 200 Spins Drago - Jewels of Fortune Experience

Drago - Jewels of Fortune is a very untypical game for Pragmatic Play, and it comes with a massive potential. This was more than enough to trigger anticipation in us, and we really looked forward to testing our beginner’s luck with our 200 spins test session. We set the bet level to £1, and loaded up the autoplay feature with our first 100 spins.

With the turbo spin switched on the pace of this game is dangerously fast. You can easily burn through 100 spins in a matter of minutes with nothing but measly wins and dead spins. Our first 150 spins was over in a blink, but then the game completely shifted gears on us. It all started when we triggered the Super Spins feature with the green dragon, which gave us a 10x win with multiplier values doubled.

We braced ourselves for another dead spin session for the rest of our final 50 spins, but instead we triggered the free spins feature a whopping 3 times almost in a row. The prelude respins feature where you get to build your free spins feature is pure genius in our view. It’s actually really entertaining and cool to see what mix of spins and features you can rack up each time.

Win

In our first bonus round we got a 3x win multiplier, 40 mystery symbols, 4 sticky wilds and 8 free spins. We landed big to massive wins on each and every of those 8 spins, mostly thanks to the sticky wilds and the 3x multiplier, and we ended up with a solid payout of 630x our stake. It came as a shock after all those dead spins before, and we could feel the hair stand up at the back of our necks.

Our second bonus round came right after the first one, and we got a 3x multiplier this time also. However, we only got 20 mystery symbols, 2 sticky wilds and 6 spins this time, but we still won an okay 61x our stake. The final free spins round came towards the very end, and we got 2 sticky wilds, 2x multiplier and 20 mystery symbols. This time we won only 19x, which tells you that big wins are certainly not guaranteed in any way here.
