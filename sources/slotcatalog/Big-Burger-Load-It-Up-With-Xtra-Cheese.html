Big Burger Load It Up With Xtra Cheese Rules And Gameplay

Big Burger Load It Up With Xtra Cheese slot plays out on 5 reels with 3 positions per reel and 10 paylines. You can place bets between €0.1 and €250 per spin across all devices, and you win by landing 2 to 6 matching symbols across at least one payline. Big Burger Load It Up With Xtra Cheese Bonuses And Special Features

You get instant cash prizes in the base game of Big Burger Load It Up With Xtra Cheese slot, while the bonus round offers a new pay system and 2 special symbols in addition to 2 random modifiers. Let’s take a closer look!

Ante Bet (not UK)

Eligible players can turn on the Ante Bet between any base game spin, and keeping it on means you pay 50 % Xtra per spin. The benefit is not specified, but you get Xtra scatters added to the reel strips which “increase your bonus round chances”.

Hot Dog Symbols

The Hot Dog symbols serve as instant prize scatters in the base game. You can see the prize paytable on the left-hand side, and landing 3 to 10 Hot Dogs in view pay the corresponding prize between 1x and 1,000x your stake.

Big Burger Load It Up With Xtra Cheese Free Spins

You need 3 Hamburger scatters in view to trigger the Bonus Round in Big Burger Load It Up With Xtra Cheese slot, and scatters can appear on reels 1, 3, and 5 only. This gives you 10 free spins and a new win system replaces the base game win system. You win by landing matching symbols on adjacent reels starting from the left, instead of having fixed paylines.

In addition, you’ll benefit from 2 special symbols:

The Expand symbol expands the grid each time it appears. It adds an Xtra horizontal row the 2 first times it appears, and a vertical row the third time it appears. After that, it turns into a wild symbol each time it lands. You also win +1 Xtra spin each time the grid expands.

The Pop symbol selects a random pay symbol after any regular wins are paid. All instances of the selected symbol are removed and pay 0.5x your stake each. A new spin follows, as there is no tumble wins mechanic in any stage of this game.

In addition to the special symbols, you will also benefit from 2 different random modifiers during the bonus round:

The Ketchup Bottle can appear to add a multiplier of x2, x3, or x5 to a randomly selected winning symbol, and this boosts the win accordingly. The Mustard Bottle will rearrange symbols on the grid to guarantee a win when it appears, and landing 3 scatters awards +10 Xtra free spins.
