Dead Man's Crew

Raise a glass and join the Dead Man’s Crew

Dead Man’s Crew brings the crew ashore for a night of wild celebrations, cascading wins, and plenty of treasure-
fuelled chaos. Between adventures, the crew has taken over the tavern — and with growing Wild Scatter
multipliers, powerful Crew Features, and a maximum win of 10,000x your bet, the party can get seriously
rewarding.

Played on a 6×6 Avalanche grid, wins are formed by matching 5 or more identical symbols, sending winning
symbols crashing away and making room for new ones. Wild Scatters join the action as substitutes and grow their
multiplier every time they contribute to a win, while multiple Wilds can combine their multipliers to make each
winning cluster worth even more.

The tavern gets even rowdier when Feature Hotspots appear across the grid. Land a winning symbol on a Hotspot
and the Crew can unleash one of their tricks, from transforming or removing symbols to spawning new Wild
Scatters and Hotspots. Mystery Trails add even more chaos, turning paths across the grid into Mystery Symbols
before revealing their hidden rewards.

When the party really gets going, 3 or more Wild Scatters trigger 8 Free Spins — and this time, the Wild multipliers
stay with the Crew from spin to spin. With Wild Boosters, Wild Doublers, Extra Spins, and Crew Features joining the
festivities, the longer the party lasts, the more powerful the Wilds can become.

The voyage can wait. Tonight, the Crew is celebrating!
