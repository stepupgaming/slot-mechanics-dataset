ABOUT THE GAME

Chicken Man is a 5-reel, 5-row paylines game. Its main feature is the collection phase where wilds are collected and tiles they land on are highlighted and receive a multiplier starting at 2x. After the collection phase is over, all collected wilds land in the one and only free spin and receive the multiplier of the tile they land on.

The maximum win in this game is 12,500x your bet, and can be achieved in the bonus games.

FEATURES

WILD SYMBOL

The Wild Symbol substitutes for all symbols in the paytable.

When multiple wild symbols participate in the same win line their multipliers are added together.

In the bonus games, Wild symbols are collected, leaving a highlighted tile on the grid with a starting multiplier of 2x. If a Wild is collected from an already highlighted tile, its multiplier doubles.

In the PAYOUT SPIN, Wild symbols will inherit the multiplier of any highlighted tiles they land on.

EGGVENGER’S ASSEMBLE

Trigger the EGGVENGER’S ASSEMBLE bonus game by landing 3 SAFE scatter symbols at the same time in the BASE GAME.

COLLECTION PHASE

This bonus game starts with a collection phase where only non-paying and wild symbols can appear on the grid.

The player starts with 3 lives and loses 1 life each time no wilds land. Landing at least 1 wild symbol refreshes the lives back to 3.

Wilds are collected and the tiles they land on receive a 2x multiplier, which doubles each time a new wild lands on the same tile. The collection phase ends after 3 consecutive dead spins.

PAYOUT SPIN

All available wilds appear on the grid randomly in the one and only free spin and receive the multiplier from the tile they land on.

SUPER EGGSPLOSION

Trigger the SUPER EGGSPLOSION bonus game by landing 3 BANK scatter symbols at the same time in the BASE GAME.

This bonus has the same mechanics as the EGGVENGER'S ASSEMBLE bonus, with one exception!

The bonus starts with a super spin, where only non-paying or multiplier symbols can land. Every tile has a chance of receiving a starting multiplier of 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x or 10x.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 12 500 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
