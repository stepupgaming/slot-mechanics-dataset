ABOUT THE GAME

Ahoy, matey! Unsheath yer sabers and load yer pistols, it’s time to return to the keel-hauling adventure on the high seas in Pirate Bonanza 2! In this 6-reel, 5-row drop cascades game with cluster wins all winning squares are highlighted waiting for the Cannon symbol to fire and transform them into explosive Multiplier bombs! Are you ready? We’ve got some plunder to claim!

The max win in this game is 20 000x your bet and can be achieved in all game modes.

FEATURES

TUMBLE WIN

All wins are added to the total Tumble Win above the grid. After adding the win amount to the Tumble Win, all winning symbols are removed and new ones drop in to take their place and possibly award additional wins.

The total Tumble Win amount is awarded at the end of every spin.

CANNON FEATURE

After a winning combination occurs, the squares behind the winning symbols will be highlighted.

If there is a Cannon symbol on the grid it will activate any highlighted squares after winning combinations have been awarded.

The Cannon symbol activates only if there have been winning combinations in the current spin.

When activated, squares will reveal Multiplier Bomb symbols and possibly the special R-Bomb. All Multiplier Bombs will then explode and reveal a multiplier which is added to a total multiplier one by one from left to right, top to bottom.

Multiplier Bombs can either be additive or multiplicative. Additive Bombs add their value to the total multiplier, while multiplicative Bombs multiply the value of the total multiplier.

There are three different regular Multiplier Bombs and a special Golden Multiplier Bomb:

Low-Value Bombs

Low-Value Bombs are additive and can have one of the following multipliers: 2x, 3x, 4x or 5x.

Medium-Value Bombs

Medium-Value Bombs are additive and can have one of the following multipliers: 10x, 15x, 20x or 25x.

High-Value Bombs

High-Value Bombs are additive and can have one of the following multipliers: 50x, 75x or 100x.

Golden Multiplier Bomb

Golden Multiplier Bombs are multiplicative and can have one of the following multipliers: x2, x3, x5 or x10.

After all Multiplier Bombs have been revealed, if the special R-Bomb has been revealed, it will first collect all additive multipliers to the total win bar before replacing those positions with new bombs. New bombs may be of any type (additive, multiplicative or possibly another R-Bomb). The R-Bomb is also replaced with a new bomb. Golden Multiplier Bombs are not replaced and remain until the end.

Only 1 R-Bomb can be present on the grid at a time.

Once all Bombs are revealed, the Golden Multiplier Bombs reveal their multipliers, further multiplying the total collected multiplier.

At the end the total multiplier amount will be used to multiply the value of the Tumble Win and the new Tumble Win value is then awarded.

CANNON BLAST

Trigger the CANNON BLAST bonus game with 10 free spins by landing 3 BONUS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game.

During this bonus there is a higher chance of landing the Cannon symbol and highlighted squares persist until being activated by the Cannon feature!

BOMBS AWAY

Trigger the BOMBS AWAY bonus game with 10 free spins by landing 4 BONUS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game.

During this bonus there is a higher chance of landing the Cannon symbol. Highlighted squares persist for the entire duration of the bonus round!

SPIN

The game is started by pressing the SPIN symbol button. The spacebar on the keyboard can also be used to initiate a spin.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). Separate wins in a winning combination will be displayed during the win presentation. Symbol payouts are displayed in the paytable and reflect the current bet configuration. Only the highest win per winning way is paid.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 20 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

AUTOPLAY

Using Autoplay will let you play a number of game rounds automatically. Press the AUTOPLAY button and choose a number of game rounds to initiate this feature. The remaining number of spins is displayed. Autoplay is stopped by pressing the STOP button.

ADVANCED AUTOPLAY SETTINGS

The Advanced Autoplay settings allow the player to set number of plays, total session lost limit and single win limit. The autoplay feature will stop when any of the selected limits are exceeded.

TURBO PLAY

The Turbo Play functionality is used to get the fastest game round possible (not available on all operators and jurisdictions). Turbo Play is activated from the menu.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.

HISTORY

The result of a completed game may be viewed in History immediately after closing the game window. Results of unfinished games are not displayed in History.
