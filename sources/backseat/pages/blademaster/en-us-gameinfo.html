ABOUT THE GAME

Welcome to Blademaster! In this 5-reel, 5-row paylines game you'll have a chance to encounter the Blademaster himself! Step into the arena of deadly combat and test your mettle!

The maximum win in this game is 13,000 times your bet and can be achieved in the base game and bonus game.

FEATURES

BLADEMASTER

The Blademaster feature can occur randomly during both the base game and bonus game. When the feature activates, 2-5 Wild Multipliers will appear on the reels (at most 1 per reel). In the base game, the value of each Wild Multiplier can be x2, x3, x4, x5, or x10. If the feature occurs during the bonus game, the sum of the Wild Multipliers will be equal to the total of the Global Multiplier.

Wild Multipliers multiply any winning combination they are part of. If more than one Wild Multiplier is part of the same winning combination their values are multiplied with each other and then applied to the win.

GLOBAL MULTIPLIER

The bonus game has a progressive Global Multiplier. It starts at x2 and can be increased up to a maximum of x50 by landing Blue Flag symbols. Blue Flags can have the following values: +2, +3, +4, +5, +10. Each spin in the bonus game will have a Wild Multiplier equal to the Global Multiplier appear on one randomly selected reel, unless the Blademaster feature activates. When the Blademaster feature occurs the Global Multiplier will instead be split into 2-5 Wild Multiplier symbols that spread onto the reels!

BONUS

Trigger the bonus game with 10, 12 or 14 free spins by landing 3, 4 or 5 BONUS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

Land Blue Flags to boost the Global Multiplier. When the Blademaster feature activates, the Global Multiplier is split into 2 to 5 multiplicative Wild Multipliers! Their combined value equals the Global Multiplier.

Landing 3 BONUS symbols at the same time during the bonus game awards 5 additional spins!

SPECIAL SYMBOLS

Wild symbols can be landed in the base game and bonus game. Wild symbols substitute for all other paying symbols.

SPIN

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). Separate wins in a winning combination will be displayed during the win presentation. Symbol payouts are displayed in the paytable and reflect the current bet configuration. Only the highest win per winning way is paid.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 13,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

AUTOPLAY

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in Game History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.

GAME HISTORY
