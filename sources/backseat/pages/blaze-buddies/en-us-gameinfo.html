ABOUT THE GAME

Blaze Buddies is a 5-reel, 4-row paylines game featuring 4 cute dragon friends! They can be boosted by 3 distinct modifiers - Wild Dragon, Expanded Dragon and Dragon Multiplier. These modifiers along with Extra Spins can be acquired during a picking phase before the bonus games start or after landing 2 BONUS symbols on reels 1 and 5 at the same time during a bonus game.

The maximum win in this game is 10,000 times your bet, and can be achieved in the bonus games.

FEATURES

PICK MECHANIC

Central to the game is the Pick Mechanic. In the beginning of the bonus games or following a retrigger, players get to pick a number of eggs that can award dragon symbol modifiers or 2, 3 or 4 extra spins.

Extra spins can only be picked once when there are 3 initial picks available. It is possible to get more free spins from additional picks after the retrigger or when 4 or more picks are available outright.

The dragon symbol modifiers are applied to the four dragon symbols for the duration of the bonus game. All 3 modifiers can be applied to a single dragon.

There are 3 types of modifiers:

Expanded Dragon

This modifier ensures that the dragon symbol, when applied, always lands as a fully expanded reel, except if it's a golden wild symbol.

Only one type of dragon can get the Expanded Dragon modifier and this modifier can be picked only once during a bonus game.

Dragons with Expanded Dragon modifier cannot land more than once per reel and can only land during the bonus games.

Wild Dragon

This modifier transforms the dragon symbol it’s applied to into a golden wild symbol. If the dragon symbol is expanded, the entire expanded reel becomes wild.

Wild dragon symbols substitute for all symbols in the paytable.

Wild dragon symbols can only land during bonus games.

Dragon Multiplier

This modifier adds a multiplier to the dragons symbol it’s applied to.

The possible multiplier values are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, or 100x.

Multipliers on a single payline are added together.

Dragons with multipliers can only land during bonus games.

DRAGON’S DELIGHT Bonus

Trigger the DRAGON’S DELIGHT bonus by landing 3 BONUS scatter symbols at the same time in the BASE GAME.

Start this bonus game with 3 picks and at least 10 free spins. This bonus feature maintains the mechanics of the base game and has the added bonus of the Pick Mechanic. Every time you land BONUS scatter symbols on reels 1 and 5 at the same time you are granted an additional pick, up to 6 in total.

BLAZE OF GLORY Bonus

Trigger the BLAZE OF GLORY bonus by landing 4 or 5 BONUS scatter symbols at the same time in the BASE GAME.

Start this bonus game with 4 or 5 picks and at least 10 free spins. This bonus feature maintains the mechanics of the base game and has the added bonus of the Pick Mechanic. Every time you land BONUS scatter symbols on reels 1 and 5 at the same time you are granted an additional pick, up to 6 in total.

FREE SPINS & RETRIGGER

In the base game BONUS scatter symbols can land on all reels. In bonus games they appear only on reels 1 and 5.

Landing BONUS scatter symbols during bonus games on reels 1 and 5 grants an additional pick for up to 6 in total. After 6 picks have been made no more BONUS scatter symbols will appear on the grid.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player.
