ABOUT THE GAME

Beware the unseen danger that lurks in the shadows… prepare to strike, or be stricken! Shadow Strike is a 5-reel, 4-row paylines game featuring two Ninja Reels that flank the main grid on the top and bottom. The Ninja Reels give you more chances to enhance your gameplay by landing various special symbols. The game also features 3 unique bonus games built around the Ninja Reels mechanic which add additional functionality to the symbols that land on the main grid or the Ninja Reels!

The max win in this game is 15,000 times your bet and can be achieved in all game modes.

FEATURES

NINJA REELS

Above and below the main grid there are 2 additional Ninja Reels to enhance your gameplay. When unlocked, these Ninja Reels can introduce random elements to the game by revealing Wild, MultiSplit, or +FS symbols. Apart from these special symbols, the other symbols on the Ninja Reels are non-paying dead symbols.

In the BASE GAME both Ninja Reels are locked at the start of each spin, and are unlocked if any of the three different BONUS symbols lands on the main grid.

In THE MYSTERIES OF THE DOJO and THE SECRETS OF THE SCROLL both Ninja Reels are always fully unlocked.

In THE WISDOM OF THE KOI the bottom Ninja Reel is locked and each position on it must be individually unlocked, and the top Ninja Reel is replaced with reel multipliers.

WILD SYMBOL

When a Wild symbol lands on one of the Ninja Reels it will create a new Wild symbol in a random position on the corresponding reel in the main grid.

Wild symbols that land or are created on the main grid have a chance to get one of the following multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x.

If more than one Wild multiplier symbols are part of the same winning combination, their multiplier values are added together before being applied to the win.

MULTISPLIT SYMBOL

MultiSplit symbols only appear Ninja Reels, and when one of these symbols lands it will multiply the multiplier value of all Wild symbols on the corresponding reel in the main grid with the displayed multiplier.

The possible MultiSplit multiplier values are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x.

If a MultiSplit symbol crosses over a Wild that does not have a multiplier value, the multiplier value of the MultiSplit will be added to the Wild.

+FS SYMBOL

During THE MYSTERIES OF THE DOJO and THE SECRETS OF THE SCROLL bonus games you can land +FS symbols in either of the two Ninja Reels, granting an additional 1-3 free spins per symbol.

THE MYSTERIES OF THE DOJO BONUS GAME

Trigger THE MYSTERIES OF THE DOJO bonus game with 10, 12 or 14 free spins by landing 3, 4 or 5 DOJO BONUS scatter symbols at the same time during the BASE GAME.

During this bonus both Ninja Reels are unlocked on every spin, allowing you to get Wild, MultiSplit and +FS symbols.

Wild symbols that land or are created on the main grid remain sticky throughout the entire bonus round.

THE SECRETS OF THE SCROLL BONUS GAME

Trigger THE SECRETS OF THE SCROLL bonus game with 10, 12 or 14 free spins by landing 3, 4 or 5 SCROLL BONUS scatter symbols at the same time during the BASE GAME.

During this bonus both Ninja Reels are unlocked allowing you to land Wild, MultiSplit and +FS symbols.

Wild symbols on the main grid expand into full Wild reels.

THE WISDOM OF THE KOI BONUS GAME

Trigger THE WISDOM OF THE KOI bonus game by landing 3 KOI BONUS scatter symbols at the same time during the BASE GAME.

The bonus consists of 2 phases:

COLLECTION PHASE

The goal of the Collection Phase is to collect as many wilds and reel multipliers as possible, and unlock slots in the bottom Ninja Reel to be able to get MultiSplit symbols in the Payout Phase!

In the Collection Phase starts you have 3 lives and the game transforms so that only non-winning, Wild, multiplier and MultiSplit symbols appear on the main grid. Lives get reset whenever a Wild, multiplier or MultiSplit symbol lands. The Collection Phase continues until you get 3 non-winning spins in a row.

These are the non-winning symbols in the Collection Phase:

Above each reel is a reel multiplier that starts at 1x. By landing multiplier symbols, the reel multipliers above the main reels are progressively increased!

Below the main grid there is a Ninja Reel which is initially locked. Each position in the Ninja Reel can be unlocked for the Payout Phase by landing a MultiSplit symbol on the corresponding reel in the main grid.

PAYOUT PHASE

After the Collection Phase, 3 final free spins will be awarded for the Payout Phase.

All collected wilds will land randomly on the main grid on each spin. Each Wild will have a multiplier value corresponding to the reel multiplier above the reel where it lands.

The unlocked positions on the bottom Ninja Reel can also reveal MultiSplit symbols to boost the multipliers of the Wild symbols!

SPECIAL SYMBOLS

The DOJO BONUS, SCROLL BONUS and KOI BONUS symbols do not appear during any bonus game.

Wild symbols substitute for all symbols in the paytable and appear in all game modes. Wild symbols do not appear on Ninja Reels in THE WISDOM OF THE KOI bonus game.

When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
