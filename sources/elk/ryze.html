The Realm of Ryze

Deep beneath a temple lost, the Realm of Ryze lies. Halls upon halls of immense treasures, a wealth too great to neglect. To part any coin from the rest, one must master the craft of thievery, or meet the wrath of its protector, Ryze!

Ryze is a 6 row, 5-to-6 column mischievous cluster slot that comes with Multiplier Wilds, coins, symbol transformations, a Bonus and a Super Bonus, all packed into a max potential of 10 000x bet.

Bonus game

A bonus symbol that reaches the bonus key mould below the game grid triggers the bonus game with 7 free drops. Multiplier Wilds are sticky in the bonus game. If the bonus symbol reaches the super bonus key mould, the super bonus is triggered where both coins and Multiplier Wilds are sticky.

Game Features

Multipliers Wilds
Winning clusters of payout symbols creates a Multiplier Wild in one of the vacant positions created after a win. The multiplier value of a Multiplier Wild increases for each winning cluster it is part of.

Coin Win + Merge
A cluster of coins pays out its aggregated value and that value is then given to a new coin that is left behind when the cluster is removed.

Multiplier Merge
After the win, the individual multipliers are increased before being multiplied and merged into a new Multiplier Wild.

The Burst
The Burst changes itself and all adjacent symbols to the same payout symbol or coins.

X-iter
X-iter™ allows players to jump straight into the action with 5 different game modes ranging from a 2x bet with more than triple the chance of triggering a bonus, to a 500x bet Super Bonus where both coins and Multiplier Wilds are sticky.  More info about X-iter™
