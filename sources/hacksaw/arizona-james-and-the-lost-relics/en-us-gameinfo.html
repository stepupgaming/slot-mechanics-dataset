SUPER CASCADES

When a winning combination occurs, all regular paying symbols of the type that were part of the winning combination are removed from the grid, and new ones drop down to fill the empty spaces.

PISTOL REVEALS

When a winning Cluster occurs with a Wild symbol, the Wild symbol will transform into a Pistol Cylinder symbol. The Pistol Cylinder will have between 2-6 available shots. The Pistol Cylinder will activate after all wins and cascades have been settled. When the Pistol Cylinder fires, it will hit as many symbols on the grid as there are shots in the Pistol Cylinder. If there is more than one Pistol Cylinder on the grid, they will activate in order, from top to bottom, left to right. Pistol Cylinders can shoot the same position more than once. Once the Wild symbol has transformed into a Pistol Cylinder, it is no longer Wild.

The symbols that the Pistol Cylinder hits reveal Special symbols: Coins, Amulets, Sacred Relic, and Reload symbols. After all shots are fired, if a Reload symbol appears on the grid, it will activate and reload all the Pistol Cylinders with between 2-6 shots, revealing new Special symbols. If a Coin symbol gets shot again, that Coin symbol will be upgraded to the next Coin type. If an Emerald Amulet symbol gets shot again, it will be upgraded to a Gold Amulet symbol. Diamond Coins, Gold Amulets, Sacred Relics, and Reload symbols cannot be shot again. Only one Reload symbol can be on the grid at the same time.

When all shots are fired, and no new Reload symbols are revealed, the Coin symbols will reveal their values. All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet.

  • Bronze Coins can have the following values: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x
  • Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x
  • Gold Coins can have the following values: 25x, 50x, 100x
  • Diamond Coins can have the following values: 150x, 250x, 500x

After all Coin symbols have revealed their values, the Amulet symbols will activate. Emerald Amulet symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Sacred Relics on adjacent squares with one of the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20. Gold Amulet symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Sacred Relics on the grid with one of the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20.

Once all Amulet symbols have applied their Multipliers, Sacred Relic symbols will activate. Sacred Relic symbols collect the values of all Coins and other activated Sacred Relic symbols, storing their total value. Sacred Relic symbols activate top to bottom, left to right. When each Sacred Relic symbol has activated, Amulet and Reload symbols disappear, and all shot positions activate again to reveal new Coin, Amulet, and Sacred Relic symbols, and the reveal process above repeats.

Once all reveals and activations have been completed, all Coins and Sacred Relic symbols will be added together and multiplied by your bet amount to determine the final awarded win.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: {maximumWinMultiplier} times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when a winning Cluster occurs containing a Wild Symbol, which transforms into a Pistol Cylinder that fires on as many symbols on the grid as there are shots available, revealing Coin, Amulet, Sacred Relic and Reload symbols that combine their cash prize value, which is paid as a multiple of the bet until the maximum win of 25 000x is reached. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player.
