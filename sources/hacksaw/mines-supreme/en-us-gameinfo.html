ABOUT THE GAME

MINES SUPREME is the supreme version of the classic game, where the objective is to pick squares from a grid. Behind each square there can be either a Mine, a Diamond, a Shield, a Multiplier or the incredible Max Win Diamond! For each Diamond you find, your collectable win increases, but if you hit a Mine, you lose it all. You have to dare to win, and collect your win at the right time!

Start a round by pressing BET and pick squares until you are happy with the win, and then collect it - or until you hit a Mine and the round ends. If there are no more squares to pick, the win will be automatically collected. Win or lose, you can then start a new round.

FEATURES

SHIELDS

Finding the Shield symbol gives you an active Shield that will defuse the next revealed Mine. This means that even after hitting a Mine, you can continue playing. Each Shield defuses only one Mine, then becomes inactive.

If you have an active Shield, the COLLECT button will remain inactive until a Mine is defused and the Shield becomes inactive. You can collect your collectable win immediately after defusing the Mine, or you can continue playing. The Shield symbol can only appear if you are playing with at least 2 Mines on the grid. There are always fewer Shield symbols than Mines.

MULTIPLIERS

Finding a Multiplier boosts your current collectable win! Multipliers can have one of the following values: 2x, 3x, 5x, 10x, 100x.

Your current collectable win will be multiplied by the shown value. If you keep playing and find additional Diamonds, the win value of each Diamond is added to the new collectable win.

Example: You have 1.5x as your collectable win. Finding a multiplier of 3x increases the collectable win to 4.5x. You pick another square and find a Diamond that adds an additional 0.5x to the collectable win for a total of 5x. (Numbers not indicative of real gameplay.)

MAX WIN DIAMOND

The elusive Max Win Diamond instantly awards the game’s max win of 10 000 times your bet or {maxExposure} (whichever is lower), and ends the current round.

PAYOUT DISPLAY

Above the grid are a series of bars with numbers. The left-most highlighted bar shows the collectable win you will receive if you collect immediately, expressed as a multiplier of your bet amount for the current round. The bar immediately to the right of the highlighted one shows the potential payout if you successfully find another Diamond, and so on.

GAME CONFIGURATION

The size of the grid and the number of Mines are configurable in the menu panel. The more Mines you play with, the higher the risk, but the larger the potential payout! On the other hand, if you increase the size of the grid or reduce the number of Mines, the payout per Diamond will go down, but the risk decreases. You can configure the game to fit your own playstyle!

AUTOPLAY MENU

The autoplay settings are configurable in the autoplay menu. Before starting autoplay, you must select how many squares to pick and the number of rounds to play. You may also change the size of the grid and the number of Mines before starting. There are two options for autoplay, RANDOM where squares are picked at random or CUSTOM where squares are picked using your own custom pattern.

RANDOM PATTERN

In this autoplay mode you decide how many picked squares you would like per round. The maximum allowed number of picks is the grid size minus the number of Mines you have configured. For example, if you have a 5x5 grid with 3 Mines, the maximum number of picks allowed will be 22. If you reach your selected number of picks with an active Shield, autoplay will continue with randomly picked squares until a Mine is defused and the Shield becomes inactive. Autoplay will then immediately end and your win will be credited.

CUSTOM PATTERN

In this autoplay mode you select the squares on the grid that will be picked. Click a square again to remove it from the pattern. The maximum allowed number of picks is the grid size minus the number of Mines you have configured. For example, if you have a 5x5 grid with 3 Mines, the maximum number of picks allowed will be 22. If you reach the end of your selected squares with an active Shield, autoplay will continue with randomly picked squares until a Mine is defused and the Shield becomes inactive. Autoplay will then immediately end and your win will be credited.

MAX WIN

The maximum win in this game is 10 000 times your bet or {maxExposure}, whichever is lower. If your selection causes your total collected win to reach or exceed the maximum win amount, the round will end, and your maximum win will be credited immediately.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.

HISTORY

The result of a completed game may be viewed in History. Results of unfinished games are not displayed in History.
