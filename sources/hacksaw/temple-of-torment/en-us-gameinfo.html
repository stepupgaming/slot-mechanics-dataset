ABOUT THE GAME

Witness the divine clash between the two Egyptian gods Ra and Anubis in this 5-reel, 4-row game with payline wins. Wild Golden Scarab symbols expand either upwards or downwards depending on which god prevails! The max win in this game is 10,000 times your bet! The maximum win can be achieved in the base game, the Anguish of Anubis bonus game and the Reign of Ra bonus game.

FEATURES

WILD GOLDEN SCARAB SYMBOLS

All Wild Golden Scarab symbols in view expand if at least one expanded Golden Scarab symbol would be part of at least one winning combination once expanded. If it is possible to create a winning combination by expanding in a certain direction, Golden Scarabs will expand in that direction.

Expanded Golden Scarab symbols count as wild on every position that the expanded symbol covers. Golden Scarab symbols expand either upward or downward, to the edge of the grid. All Golden Scarabs expand in the same direction. Golden Scarab symbols that do not expand are counted as a single wild symbol. Each reel can have only one Golden Scarab symbol at a time.

WILD SYMBOL

If a Golden Scarab symbol expands through a WILD symbol, the WILD activates and turns into a multiplier that is applied to the whole expanded symbol.

WILD multipliers can have the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x

If a winning combination includes more than one expanded Golden Scarab with a multiplier, the multiplier values will first be added together and then applied to the win.

ANGUISH OF ANUBIS BONUS GAME

Land at least 3 Anubis FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate this bonus feature.

3 symbols awards 10 free spins.

4 symbols awards 12 free spins.

5 symbols awards 14 free spins.

In this feature there is a better chance of landing Golden Scarab and WILD symbols than in the base game! All landed WILD symbols will be sticky for the remainder of the feature.

Land additional Anubis FS scatter symbols at the same time during the feature for extra free spins.

2 symbols awards 2 extra free spins.

3 symbols awards 4 extra free spins.

REIGN OF RA BONUS GAME

Land at least 3 Ra FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate this bonus feature.

3 symbols awards 10 free spins.

4 symbols awards 12 free spins.

5 symbols awards 14 free spins.

In this feature, all activated WILD multipliers are added to the total multiplier above the reels. Whenever a WILD multiplier is added to the total multiplier, the total multiplier is activated and then applies to all wins on the current spin.

Land additional Ra FS scatter symbols at the same time during the feature for extra free spins.

2 symbols awards 2 extra free spins.

3 symbols awards 4 extra free spins.

SPECIAL SYMBOLS

The Wild symbol substitutes for all symbols in the paytable.

The Golden Scarab symbol substitutes for all other symbols in the paytable.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
