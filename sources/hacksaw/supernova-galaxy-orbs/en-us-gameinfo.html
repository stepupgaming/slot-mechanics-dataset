RED ORB SYMBOLS AND HOLD & WIN SEQUENCES

When a Red Orb symbol lands, it triggers a Hold & Win sequence after any regular wins have been handled. The Hold & Win sequence starts with 3 refilling lives that represent 3 spins.

During the Hold & Win sequence, only Red Orb, Jackpot, Dead symbols and Bronze, Silver, Gold or Diamond Coins will land. Every time a Red Orb, Coin or Jackpot symbol lands, the lives are refilled and new spins are awarded. Dead symbols are non-paying symbols and have no other effect.

When Red Orb, Coin and Jackpot symbols land, they stick to the grid. All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet. Coin symbols award wins when they are collected by Orb symbols.

  • Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.4x, 1x, 2x
  • Silver Coins can have the following values: 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x
  • Gold Coins can have the following values: 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x
  • Diamond Coins can have the following values: 100x, 150x, 200x, 250x, 500x, 1000x

After the final life has been used or there are no available positions on the grid, any Jackpot symbols will reveal their values from the Jackpot sign outside of the grid. Only one Jackpot symbol can land at the same time during a spin. Jackpot symbols award wins when they are collected by Orb symbols. Jackpot values revealed from the Jackpot sign are awarded as a multiple of your bet. Jackpot symbols can reveal the following values from the Jackpot sign:

  • Mini: 10x
  • Major: 50x
  • Mega: 500x
  • {maxWinLabel}: {highestJackpotMultiplier}x

Once the Coin symbols have landed and the Jackpot symbols have revealed their values, the Red Orb symbols activate. Activated Red Orb symbols collect the values of all Coin and Jackpot symbols and the sum is paid out as a multiple of your bet.

1 Red Orb symbol can land during the same spin. During a Hold & Win sequence, there can be a maximum of 3 Red Orb symbols on the grid at the same time.

When there are multiple Red Orb symbols on the grid at the same time, they separately collect the values of all Coin and Jackpot symbols once. The values collected by the Red Orb symbols are then added together and the sum is awarded as a multiple of your bet. Red Orb symbols collect from left to right, top to bottom.

BLUE ORB SYMBOLS

When a Blue Orb symbol lands, any regular wins are handled before the Blue Orb is activated. When activated, Blue Orb symbols collect the values from all Coin and Jackpot symbols on the grid and the sum is paid out as a multiple of your bet.

When there are multiple Blue Orb symbols on the grid, each Blue Orb symbol collects the values of each Coin and Jackpot symbol once. The values collected by each Blue Orb symbol are then added together and the sum is paid out as a multiple of your bet. Blue Orb symbols collect from left to right, top to bottom.

3 Blue Orb symbols can land during the same spin. Red and Blue Orb symbols cannot be on the grid at the same time.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
