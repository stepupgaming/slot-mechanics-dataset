ABOUT THE GAME

It's the most wonderful time of the year! Join Santa on his adventure to deliver all the gifts in time for Xmas in Xmas Drop and get your own Xmas gift in this 5x5 game with paylines and a maximum win of 12,500 times your bet!

The max win can be achieved in the base game, Night Before Xmas and Santa Claus Is Coming To Town game modes.

FEATURES

WILD SANTA SYMBOL

Wild Santa symbols expand if the expanded symbol would be part of at least one winning combination once expanded.

Expanded Wild Santa symbols count as wild on every position that the expanded symbol covers. Wild Santa symbols only expand downward, to the bottom of the grid. Wild Santa symbols that do not expand are counted as a single wild symbol. Each reel can have only one Wild Santa symbol at a time.

WILD GIFT SYMBOL

If a Wild Santa symbol expands through a Wild Gift symbol it turns into a multiplier that is applied to the whole expanded Wild Santa symbol.

Wild Gift multipliers can have the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x

If a winning combination includes more than one expanded Wild Santa with a multiplier, the multiplier values will first be added together and then applied to the win.

NIGHT BEFORE XMAS

Land 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to trigger this bonus feature with 10 free spins!

In the Night Before Xmas feature you have a better chance of landing Wild Santa symbols and Wild Gift symbols than in the base game!

Landing 3 FS symbols at the same time during the feature awards 4 additional free spins.

Landing 4 FS symbols at the same time during the Night Before Xmas feature awards the Santa Claus Is Coming To Town feature at the end of the current spin. If the remaining number of free spins is less than 10, the number of free spins will be set to 10. Otherwise, the current number of remaining free spins is unchanged.

SANTA CLAUS IS COMING TO TOWN

Land 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to trigger this bonus feature with 10 free spins!

In the Santa Claus Is Coming To Town feature each reel can be activated by landing a Wild Santa symbol on that reel! Once a reel is activated, a Wild Santa symbol is guaranteed to land on that reel on all remaining free spins.

Landing 3 FS symbols at the same time during the feature awards 4 additional free spins.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 12 500 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 12 500 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win is reached by landing 1 Wild Santa symbol at the top of each reel at the same time that will expand through a regular Wild symbol to display a combined multiplier value of at least 27x when all the multipliers are added together. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
