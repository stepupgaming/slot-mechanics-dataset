ABOUT THE GAME

Join Hercules, the demi-god of strength, as he takes on the trials of legend in a quest for glory, power, and riches. With his trusty lion’s skin and a heart full of courage, Hercules is ready to conquer foes and unlock divine rewards in this epic 5-reel, 5-row adventure with ways wins. FEATURES

ROTOGRID™

Landed RotoGrid™ symbols are activated after wins on the current grid have been awarded, giving 1 - 3 clockwise grid rotations that are collected and stored in a meter outside the grid. The RotoGrid™ symbol will then turn into a Wild symbol.

Each grid rotation turns the grid a quarter turn clockwise and unlocks the possibility of new wins being created on the grid. Each rotation is awarded one at a time, processing any new wins before the next rotation.

If multiple RotoGrid™ symbols are present on the grid at the same time, they will be activated one at a time, after all rotations from the previously activated RotoGrid™ symbol have been completed. Maximum 3 RotoGrid™ symbols can land on the same spin.

CONNECTING WILDS

After each grid rotation, any Wild symbols that are on the same reel will connect, filling all positions between them with additional Wild symbols. If the grid rotates again, both the original and the new Wild symbols can connect with other Wild symbols on their respective reels, potentially filling the grid with Wild symbols.

BONUS CHOICE

Landing 3 or 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game brings you into the Bonus Choice menu, where you are able to choose between two bonus game options depending on the number of landed FS symbols.

With 3 FS symbols, you may choose either the LABORS or the LABYRINTH bonus games.

With 4 FS symbols, you may choose either the GODLY LABORS or the GODLY LABYRINTH bonus games.

LABORS BONUS GAME

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will get 10 free spins. This bonus feature maintains the mechanics of the base game with a higher chance of landing RotoGrid™ and Wild symbols!

Landing additional FS scatter symbols during the feature awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols awards 2 additional spins,

3 FS symbols awards 4 additional spins.

GODLY LABORS BONUS GAME

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will be awarded a variant of the LABORS bonus. In this variant, RotoGrid™ and Wild symbols will appear more frequently.

LABYRINTH BONUS GAME

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will get 8 free spins.

The following symbols appear in this bonus: Skull Block symbols, Cracked Stone symbols, FS symbols and Adding and Multiplying Coin symbols. Skull Block symbols and Cracked Stone symbols are non-paying.

The aim of the bonus is to collect Coin and FS symbols in the Treasure Chests at the bottom of the grid.

When a Coin or FS symbol lands above a Cracked Stone symbol, that Stone symbol will be crushed and disappear from the grid, letting the Coin or FS symbol move down into its position. This process repeats until a Skull Block symbol gets in the way, or the Coin or FS symbol reaches the bottom row of the grid. Whenever a Coin or FS symbol reaches the bottom row, they will be collected in the Treasure Chest for the corresponding reel.

Each Treasure Chest has a progressive multiplier which is paid out at the end of the bonus and multiplied with your bet amount to determine the final win. Collected Coin symbols either add their value to the existing value on the Treasure Chest, or multiply the existing value on the Treasure Chest. All Treasure Chests start at 0x.

Adding Coins can have the following values: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x

Multiplying Coins can have the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20

Collected FS symbols each award 1 extra free spin.

Once all Coin and FS symbols on the grid have moved as far as they can, there is a chance that the grid will rotate either clockwise or counter-clockwise. When the grid rotates, it may allow Coin symbols and FS symbols to move to a new position by breaking Cracked Stone symbols, leading them through the “labyrinth” of Skull Block symbols towards the bottom of the grid.

If the Might of Hercules feature triggers in this bonus game, a random number of Skull Block symbols will be destroyed and sometimes a random number of Cracked Stone symbols will be destroyed. If Skull Block symbols or Cracked Stone symbols are destroyed, additional FS or Coin symbols may appear on the grid.

GODLY LABYRINTH BONUS GAME

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will be awarded a variant of the LABYRINTH bonus with 10 free spins. In this variant, Coins with higher values may land more frequently.

WAYS TO WIN

The specified requirements for a winning combination in the base game are also used during bonus features or free spins play unless otherwise stated. Each wager is eligible for all winning combinations unless otherwise stated.

You win if matching symbols appear on adjacent reels from left to right, starting with the leftmost reel. Each winning combination of matching symbols creates a winning way. The number of possible ways in this game is 3125. Please consult the paytable for the number of each symbol required to win.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
