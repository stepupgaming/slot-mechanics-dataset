ABOUT THE GAME

When he’s not lurking in the back-streets of Paris, he’s pulling tricks on tourists at the Great Pyramid! Smokey got smoked, so now he has moved on and assumed a new identity as Le Pharaoh™!

Join us on this new adventure in the sands of Egypt, still on the hunt for the riches at the end of the rainbow. Le Pharaoh™ is a 6-reel, 5-row paylines game with a familiar face in a new place and a maximum win of 15,000 times your bet!

The maximum win can be reached in all game modes.

FEATURES

STICKY RE-DROPS

When a winning combination lands, the symbols in that combination will stick and a re-drop will be awarded. All paying symbols that stick in this way create Golden Squares on their positions.

During the re-drop, paying symbols that either extend existing or create new winning combinations will also stick and another re-drop will be awarded. This continues until no further extensions or new winning combinations are created. Once no further re-drops are awarded, all wins will be paid out.

Rainbow symbols and FS symbols that land together with a sticky winning combination will also stick but do not create Golden Squares.

Sticky paying symbols are removed at the start of the next spin or when the Golden Riches feature activates.

Golden Squares, sticky Rainbow symbols and sticky FS symbols are all removed at the start of the next spin.

GOLDEN RICHES

If there is a Rainbow symbol on the grid at the end of the Sticky Re-drops feature, it will activate the Golden Riches feature. When this happens, all Golden Squares on the grid will activate and reveal either Bronze, Silver or Gold Coins, and have a chance to show special Pot Of Gold, Green Clover or Golden Clover symbols.

All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet.
Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x
Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x
Gold Coins can have the following values: 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x, 500x

After all Coins and special symbols have been revealed, Clover symbols will activate. There are two types of Clover symbols: Green Clover and Golden Clover.

Green Clover symbols multiply Coins or Pot Of Gold symbols on adjacent squares with one of the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20

Golden Clover symbols multiply all Coins or Pot Of Gold symbols on the grid with one of the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20

After all Clover symbols have awarded their multipliers, Pot Of Gold symbols will activate. Pot Of Gold symbols collect the values of all Coins and other Pot Of Gold symbols on the grid and store their total value. Pot Of Gold symbols activate top-to-bottom, left-to-right.

When all values from Coins and Pot Of Gold symbols have been collected, all other Golden Squares will activate again to reveal new symbols and the process above repeats.

Once all reveals and activations have been completed, all Coins and Pot Of Gold symbol values will be added together and multiplied with your bet amount to determine the final awarded win.

Coins and special symbols are removed at the start of the next spin.

BONUS CHOICE

Landing 3 or 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game brings you into the Bonus Choice menu, where you are able to choose between two bonus game options depending on the number of landed FS symbols.

With 3 FS symbols, you may choose either the LUCK OF THE PHARAOH or the LOST TREASURES bonus games.

With 4 FS symbols, you may choose either the SUPER LUCK OF THE PHARAOH or the SUPER LOST TREASURES bonus games.

LUCK OF THE PHARAOH

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will be awarded 10 free spins.

This bonus retains the Sticky Re-drops and Golden Riches mechanics from the base game.

In this bonus all Golden Squares remain highlighted until the end of the feature, even after being activated by a Rainbow symbol.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time awards extra free spins:
2 FS awards +2 free spins;
3 FS awards +4 free spins.

SUPER LUCK OF THE PHARAOH

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will be awarded a variant of the LUCK OF THE PHARAOH bonus with an increased number of free spins! In this variant, 12 free spins will be awarded and there are better chances of landing the higher-value Coins and Pot Of Gold symbols.

This variant guarantees that a Clover symbol is revealed whenever the Golden Riches mechanic activates!

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time awards extra free spins:
2 FS awards +2 free spins;
3 FS awards +4 free spins.

LOST TREASURES

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will enter the bonus with 3 refilling lives.

The following symbols appear in this bonus: dead symbols, Coins, Green and Golden Clover, Pot Of Gold, and two different Stone Tablet symbols. Normal paying symbols do not appear. Dead symbols are non-paying and have no other effect.

Each spin in the bonus consumes 1 life. Whenever any non-dead symbol lands, your lives are refilled to 3. After 3 spins without a refill, the bonus will end.

All Coin symbols and Pot of Gold symbols that land become sticky until collected by a Pot Of Gold or the end of the feature.

Stone Tablet symbols that land also become sticky until the end of the feature. There are two types of Stone Tablet symbols: Adding Stone Tablet and Multiplying Stone Tablet.

The Adding Stone Tablet symbol adds a value to one random Coin on each spin, from one of the following values: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x, 500x

The Multiplying Stone Tablet symbol multiplies the value of one random Coin on each spin, with one of the following multiplier values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20

If the grid fills completely with Coins, Pot Of Gold, or Stone Tablet symbols, the bonus ends.

At the end of the bonus game, all Coin and Pot Of Gold symbol values will be added together and multiplied with your bet amount to determine the final awarded win.

SUPER LOST TREASURES

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will be awarded a variant of the LOST TREASURES bonus. This variant guarantees that an Adding Stone Tablet symbol lands on the first spin. And there are better chances of landing higher-value Coins and Pot Of Gold symbols.

RAINBOW OVER THE PYRAMIDS

Landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game awards the RAINBOW OVER THE PYRAMIDS Epic Bonus with 12 free spins!

This bonus retains the Sticky Re-drops and Golden Riches mechanics from the base game.

During this bonus, all Golden Squares remain highlighted until the end of the feature, even after being activated by a Rainbow symbol!

In addition, a Rainbow symbol is guaranteed to land on every spin!

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time awards extra free spins:
2 FS awards +2 free spins;
3 FS awards +4 free spins.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 15,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 15 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win is reached by forming winning combinations that highlight as many grid positions as possible, then landing a Rainbow symbol to activate the Golden Riches feature that will reveal Bronze, Silver and Gold Coins as well as Green and Golden Clover and Pot of Gold symbols with a total combined value of 15,000x. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account.
