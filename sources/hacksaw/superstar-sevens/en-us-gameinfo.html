FEATURES

SUPER CASCADE

When a winning combination occurs, all regular paying symbols of the type that were part of the winning combination are removed from the grid, and new symbols drop down to fill the empty spaces.

TOTAL WIN BAR

All wins during each spin are collected in the Total Win Bar outside of the grid. The collected wins are awarded at the end of the spin.

CASCADE COUNTER

Activate the Cascade Counter by landing a Lightning symbol and a win on the same spin. When activated, all non-winning symbols that are removed through Super Cascades are collected and the collected symbols are converted into a Multiplier value. Each collected low-paying symbol adds a 1x Additive Multiplier per winning cluster, and each collected high-paying symbol adds a 2x Additive Multiplier per winning cluster, which is visible in the Total Win Bar. The Cascade Counter stays active until the end of the spin.

When all wins and Super Cascades have been handled on that spin, the final collected win will be multiplied by the Multiplier value from the Cascade Counter in the Total Win Bar to determine the payout for that spin.

If an Epic Lightning symbol lands and there is a win in the Total Win Bar, the total win is multiplied by an additional Multiplier value at the end of the spin. Epic Lightning symbols can have the following Multiplicative Multipliers x5, x6, x7, x8, x9, x10, x25, x50, x77, x100. Only 1 Epic Lightning symbol can land on each spin.

If 6 FS scatter symbols land at the same time, a Max Win is awarded instantly.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 10 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when a high enough win amount is collected in the Total Win Bar, alongside landing a Lightning symbol which activates the Cascade Counter, adding multipliers that combine with the collected win to reach the maximum win of 10 000. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability if bonus rounds are excluded.

Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
