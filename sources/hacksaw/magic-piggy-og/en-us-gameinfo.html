ABOUT THE GAME

Prepare to be enchanted by the magic of pigs! Magic Piggy OG is a 5x3 game with payline wins and a magical game show theme! The maximum win in this game is 2,500 times your bet!

The max win can be achieved in the base game, the Pig Bonus and Epig Bonus game modes.

FEATURES

Magic Piggy & Magic Hat

The Magic Piggy symbol is a revealing symbol that reveals either a Wild symbol or a Bill Stack symbol when activated by the Magic Hat symbol! All Magic Piggy symbols reveal the same type of symbol.

When the Magic Hat symbol lands, it first shows what all Magic Piggy symbols will reveal and then transforms into the revealed symbol.

Bill Stack scatter symbols are cash prizes based on your current bet. All Bill Stack symbols are added together and the total amount is multiplied by your current bet to produce the win amount.

Bill Stacks can have the following values: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 250, 500

When revealing Bill Stack symbols, there is a chance that a Magic Piggy symbol instead reveals a Multiplier Coin scatter symbol.

Multiplier Coins reveal a multiplier value, and then multiply 1 - 5 Bill Stack symbols with the revealed value. The same Bill Stack symbol can be affected multiple times by the same Multiplier Coin, or by different Multiplier Coins.

Multiplier Coins can have the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5

Pig Bonus

Land 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to trigger this feature, which awards 10 free spins!

In this feature the Magic Piggy symbols will only reveal Bill Stack symbols or Multiplier Coin scatter symbols when activated by the Magic Hat symbol!

Land Star and Super Star scatter symbols to collect Piggy Points above the reels!

Star symbols add 1 - 3 Piggy Points to the meter above the reel where it landed.

Super Star symbols add 1 - 3 Piggy Points to all reels!

When the Magic Hat symbol lands, an equal amount of Magic Piggy symbols will fill random positions on the corresponding reels.

Piggy Points are reset after each Magic Hat trigger.

Completely filling the Piggy Points meter above all reels at the same time awards 10 additional free spins!

Landing 3 FS symbols at the same time in the feature awards 4 additional free spins.

Landing 4 FS symbols at the same time in the feature instantly triggers the Epig Bonus. If the remaining number of free spins is less than 10, the remaining number of free spins will be set to 10. Otherwise, the current number of remaining free spins is unchanged.

Epig Bonus

Land 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to trigger this feature, which awards 10 free spins!

In this feature the Magic Piggy symbols will only reveal Bill Stack symbols or Multiplier Coin scatter symbols when activated by the Magic Hat symbol!

The Epig Bonus game has progressive Piggy Points that do not reset after a Magic Hat trigger! See description of Pig Bonus above for other rules.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 2 500 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 2 500 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{baseGameMaxWinOdds}. The maximum win occurs when a Magic Hat symbol lands together with multiple Magic Piggy symbols that will reveal Bill Stacks and Multiplier Coins which will combine together to reach the max win. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
