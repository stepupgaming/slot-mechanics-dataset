ABOUT THE GAME

The skies belong to us!

Up above the smoke-stacked streets and broken alleys, the Steamrunners own the air. FEATURES

WILD GAS CANISTER SYMBOLS

When a Green Gas Canister symbol lands, it spreads and transforms itself along with all adjacent low-paying and Wild symbols into Wild symbols. The spread occurs horizontally and vertically, if the transformed symbols will be part of a win. If additional low-paying or Wild symbols are connected to the newly transformed ones, the spread continues, turning those connected symbols into Wilds as well.

When a Purple Gas Canister symbol lands, it will spread and transform itself and all low-paying and Wild symbols on the grid into Wild symbols if the transformed symbols will be part of a win.

When low-paying symbols are transformed into Wild symbols, there is a chance that a random Multiplier value is added to that new Wild symbol. Wild symbols created by Gas Canisters can have the following Additive Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 200x. Wild symbols already on the grid that are affected by Gas Canister symbols will receive a 2x Multiplier value.

When a Wild symbol with a Multiplier participates in a winning combination, the value of the win is multiplied by the Wild Multiplier value. If more than one Wild Multiplier is part of the same winning combination, their values are added before being applied to the win. The Wild symbol substitutes for all symbols in the paytable. Only 1 Gas Canister symbol can land on the grid at a time.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 10 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when a Purple Gas Canister symbol lands and transforms itself and all low paying symbols on the grid into Wild symbols with random Multipliers being added to the transformed symbols which will form winning combinations until the max win of 10 000x is reached. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
