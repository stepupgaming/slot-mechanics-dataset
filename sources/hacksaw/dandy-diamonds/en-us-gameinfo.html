DIAMOND SYMBOLS AND DANDY RESPINS

When a Diamond symbol lands, it acts as a Wild, substituting for all symbols in the paytable. Diamond symbols can also land with an Additive Multiplier. Multipliers can have the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x.

When the Diamond symbol has landed, it will trigger a Dandy Respin after all winning combinations have been handled. During a Dandy Respin, all Diamond symbols are Sticky, meaning that they keep their grid position and Multiplier value, while new symbols spin in. Every time a new Diamond symbol lands, another Dandy Respin is awarded. The Respin sequence continues until no new Diamond symbols land or there are no more available positions on the grid.

Wins are awarded after every Dandy Respin. When a Diamond Multiplier participates in a winning combination, the value of the win is multiplied by the Diamond’s Multiplier value. If more than one Diamond symbol is part of the same winning combination, their Multiplier values are added together before being applied to the win.

COLOSSAL DIAMOND SYMBOLS

If 4 or more Diamond symbols land in a square formation of 2x2, 3x3, 4x4 or 5x5 they merge to form a Colossal Diamond symbol. When the Colossal Diamond is formed, any Diamond Multiplier values are added together and applied to the Colossal Diamond symbol. Colossal Diamond symbols act as a Wild symbol, substituting for all symbols in the paytable. The Multiplier value of the Colossal Diamond symbol is applied to each winning combination that the Colossal Diamond symbol is part of.

A Colossal Diamond symbol can grow if additional Diamond symbols land around the existing Colossal Diamond in such a way that they collectively form a larger square formation, for example growing from 2x2 to 3x3.

BOLT SYMBOLS

A Bolt symbol is activated when it lands within a 3x3 square formation surrounding a Diamond symbol or Colossal Diamond symbol. When activated, the Bolt symbol applies a Multiplier value or increases the Multiplier values of all Diamond symbols and Colossal Diamonds inside the surrounding 3x3 square formation before any wins are rewarded. When the wins have been awarded, the Bolt symbol spins out from the grid along the regular paying symbols. Bolt symbols can land during regular spins and Dandy Respins. Bolt symbols add one of the following Additive Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x.

An Epic Bolt symbol will activate when it lands while there is any Diamond on the grid. An activated Epic Bolt symbol applies a Multiplier value or increases the Multiplier values of all Diamond symbols and Colossal Diamond symbols on the grid before any wins have been handled. When the wins have been handled, the Epic Bolt symbol spins out from the grid along the regular paying symbols. Epic Bolt symbols can land during regular spins and Dandy Respins. The Epic Bolt symbol adds one of the following Additive Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x.

Only 1 Bolt and Epic Bolt can land during the same spin. When a Bolt and Epic Bolt symbol land and activate during the same spin, the Bolt symbol will activate before the Epic Bolt symbol.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
