ABOUT THE GAME

The most wonderful time of the year is upon us and it’s time to get in the holiday spirit! Join the penguins Ruth and Rupert and take the time to celebrate with a classic snowball fight right here in Snow Slingers. The game features include the Snowball mechanic and two jolly bonus games! Snow Slingers is a 5-reel, 4-row paylines game featuring a max win of 10,000 times your bet! The max win can be achieved in all game modes.

FEATURES

SNOW-VOLVER SYMBOL

Normal and Epic Snow-volver symbols can land with 1 - 6 Snowballs. When a Snow-volver lands, the shown number of Snowballs are thrown at random positions on the grid.

When a position without Snow is hit for the first time by a Normal Snowball it sticks to the grid and turns into a Wild Snow symbol. When a Snow symbol is hit by another Snowball, a random multiplier is added to the Snow and turns it into a Wild Snow Multiplier.

When any position is hit by an Epic Snowball it immediately becomes a Snow Multiplier!

The possible multiplier values that each Snowball can add to a Snow Multiplier are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x.

If more than one Snow Multiplier is part of the same winning combination, their multiplier values are added together before being applied to the win.

Subsequent hits on existing Snow Multipliers increases the multiplier value.

In the base game and YOU REAP WHAT YOU SNOW bonus, Epic Snow-volvers always activate before Normal Snow-volvers to maximize the chances of stacking more multipliers on each Snow Multiplier.

Once a Snow-volver is empty, it is replaced by a WILD symbol.

THROW SNOW! BONUS FEATURE

Land 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the THROW SNOW! bonus feature.

When entering this bonus, the game transforms and only Snow-volver symbols, Reload symbols and non-paying symbols will appear on the reels!

The bonus has 3 refilling lives that reset each time any Snow-volver or Reload symbol lands. Each life represents one spin. Once all 3 lives are consumed without a new reset, the bonus feature ends. If all positions on the grid are filled with used Snow-volver symbols, the feature will end immediately.

There are six Gift Boxes with multiplier values displayed above the grid. Gift Boxes can have either multiplying or adding multipliers:

Possible values for adding multipliers are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 30x, 40x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 150x, 200x, 250x, 500x

Possible values for multiplying multipliers are: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20

In this bonus, when a Snow-volver lands the shown number of Snowballs are thrown at random Gift Boxes instead of onto the grid. When a Snowball hits a Gift Box, its multiplier value is added or multiplied to the Snow-volver’s total value.

Normal Snow-volvers stick to the grid and keep their multiplier value until the end of the feature.

Epic Snow-volver total multipliers are added to all existing sticky Normal Snow-volvers and Reload symbols that are present on the grid. All Epic Snow-volvers then disappear on the next spin.

In this bonus Epic Snow-volvers always activate after Normal Snow-volvers and Reload symbols, to maximize the possible multiplier values that are added to the existing symbols.

The Reload symbol reloads Normal Snow-volvers so you can collect more multiplier values!

Landed Reload symbols activate after newly landed Normal Snow-volvers. When a Reload symbol activates it collects all the multipliers from all the sticky Normal Snow-volvers on the reel and reloads the Normal Snow-volvers with 1 - 6 new Snowballs. These Snowballs are then immediately thrown at the Gift Boxes, and the collected values are added to the Normal Snow-volvers. Reload symbols also stick to the grid and keep their collected multipliers until the end of the feature.

At the end of the bonus feature all sticky Normal Snow-volver and Reload symbol multiplier values are added together and multiplied by your bet to give you the final win!

YOU REAP WHAT YOU SNOW BONUS FEATURE

Land 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the YOU REAP WHAT YOU SNOW bonus feature with 10 free spins.

This bonus feature maintains the mechanics of the base game and has the added bonus that all Snow Multipliers become sticky and stay on the grid until the end of the feature!

Landing additional FS scatter symbols during the feature awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols awards 2 additional spins;

3 FS symbols awards 4 additional spins.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
