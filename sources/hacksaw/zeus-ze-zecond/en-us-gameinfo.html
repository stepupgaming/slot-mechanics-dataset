FEATURES

WONDER REVEAL

Wonder symbols land vertically on reels during a spin, revealing all high-paying symbols, all Wilds, or a Wonder Reel. Wonder symbols only land on reels 2, 3, 4, and 5 during normal play.

If a Wonder symbol reveals high-paying or Wild symbols that are part of a win, a respin sequence is triggered where all revealed symbols revert back to Wonder symbols and remain sticky throughout the respin. Once the respin is complete, new symbols are revealed and any new wins are awarded. The respin sequence continues until no new wins are awarded with the revealed Wonder symbols or a Wonder Reel is revealed.

Wonder Reels are formed when one or more Wonder symbols land on the same reel; those symbols then spin. Once they stop spinning, each position will reveal Coin symbols, Diamond symbols, Clover symbols, or Pots of Olympus symbols. When the Wonder Reel has revealed all its symbols, all Coin and Diamond symbols reveal a random multiplier depending on the symbol type.

All Coins and Diamonds are cash prizes paid as multiples of your bet.

Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x

Gold Coins can have the following values: 25x, 50x, 100x

Diamonds can have the following values: 150x, 250x, 500x

After all Coins and Diamonds have been revealed, the Clover symbols will activate. Green Clover symbols will multiply all Coins, Diamonds or existing Pots of Olympus on adjacent squares with one of the following Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20. Gold Clover symbols will multiply all Coins, Diamonds or existing Pots of Olympus on the grid with one of the following Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20.

Once all Clover symbols have applied their Multipliers, Pot of Olympus symbols will activate. Pots of Olympus collect the values of all Coins, Diamonds, and other Pots of Olympus on the grid and store their total value. Pots of Olympus activate top to bottom, left to right.

When all values from Coins, Diamonds, and Pot Of Olympus symbols have been collected, all other symbols in the Wonder Reels except the Pot of Olympus will spin and activate again to reveal new symbols, and the process above repeats.

Once all reveals and activations have been completed, all Coins, Diamonds, and Pots of Olympus values will be added together and multiplied by your bet amount to determine the final awarded win. The Wonder Reels are removed at the start of the next spin.

BONUS GAME - GATES OF HADES

Activate this bonus with 8 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game's mechanics, and all Wonder symbols that land are Sticky and remain in the same position throughout the bonus! The minimum Coin value that can land in this bonus is 1x.

BONUS GAME - GODLY ASCENSION

Activate this bonus with 8 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game's mechanics. In addition, a Wonder Meter is displayed outside the grid. Each time a Wonder symbol lands on the grid, the Wonder Meter increases by one step. 25 Wonder symbols are needed to fill the Wonder Meter.

When the Wonder Meter is full, a reward spin is triggered after the current free spin is complete. On a reward spin, every position on the grid will be covered by a Wonder Reel, which will only reveal Coins, Diamonds, Clover symbols, or Pots of Olympus symbols.

During the first reward spin, all types of Coin symbols can land, including Diamonds, with Bronze as the minimum Coin type and Diamond as the highest. With each subsequent reward spin, the minimum Coin type increases by one step, first Bronze, then Silver, then Gold and finally Diamond, removing the lower-value Coin symbols each time. The minimum Coin value that can land in this bonus is 1x.

Once all reveals and activations from the reward spin have been completed, the grid will reset, the Wonder Meter returns to zero, and the new minimum Coin symbol type will update for the next reward spin. The Wonder Meter will continue to fill and trigger reward spins until there are no more remaining free spins.

HIDDEN EPIC BONUS - ZEUS UNLEASHED

Activate this bonus with 8 free spins by landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus keeps the mechanics of the GATES OF HADES bonus. In addition, reels 2, 3, 4, and 5 are filled with Wonder symbols from the start! Low-paying symbols cannot land in this bonus. The minimum Coin value that can land in this bonus is 5x.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 20 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

Maximum win: 20,000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when Wonder symbols land and form Wonder Reels which in turn, reveal Coin, Diamond, Green Clover, Gold Clover and Pots of Olympus symbols that combine over multiple respins until the maximum win of 20 000x is reached. INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player.
