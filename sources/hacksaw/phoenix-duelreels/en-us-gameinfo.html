FEATURES

DUELREELS™

When a VS symbol lands it will expand into a wild DuelReel if it would be part of or create a winning combination.

Expanded DuelReels™ cover their entire reel and a duel will take place on each DuelReel. Each DuelReel has two multipliers, connected to the Phoenix and the Water Dragon. The surviving duelists multiplier will be applied across the entire reel and affects any winning combinations that includes the DuelReel.

Fully expanded DuelReels™ are counted as full wild reels and substitute for all symbols in the paytable.

The possible multiplier values that can appear on DuelReels™ are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 200x.

If more than one DuelReels™ are part of the same winning combination, their multipliers are added together before being applied to the win.

Only 1 VS symbol can land on a reel at a time.

RESURRECTION SPINS

When the Phoenix loses the duel on at least one DuelReel, you will be awarded a Resurrection Spin re-spin. During this re-spin, it is guaranteed that at least 1 VS symbol will land.

The process above will repeat during re-spins until no DuelReels™ with losing Phoenixes appear.

BONUS GAMBLE

Whenever you enter a bonus game, you have the choice to either continue directly to the bonus game or gamble. If you choose to gamble, you will be presented with 5 Phoenix Eggs to choose from.

You can choose up to 3 Eggs, with each Egg possibly containing 2 to 10 free spins, or a Bonus Upgrade if you are in the FIRE AND WATER bonus. If a Bonus Upgrade is found, the bonus will be immediately upgraded to the RISE OF THE PHOENIX bonus with 10 free spins.

You may gamble once per bonus and after a Bonus Upgrade.

FIRE AND WATER BONUS GAME

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game retains the features of the base game with a better chance of landing more VS symbols!

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbol award +2 free spins, 3 FS symbols award +4 free spins.

RISE OF THE PHOENIX BONUS GAME

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game has the same enhanced features as the FIRE AND WATER bonus game!

Additionally, this bonus has enhanced RESURRECTION SPIN re-spins where the amount of VS symbols that land during the re-spin will be equal to the number of DuelReels™ with losing Phoenixes during the triggering spin.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbol award +2 free spins, 3 FS symbols award +4 free spins.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. When a win amount is displayed in this format, the displayed value may be rounded down. The actual amount awarded will never be less than the displayed value.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
