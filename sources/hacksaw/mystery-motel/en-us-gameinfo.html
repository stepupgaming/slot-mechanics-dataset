ABOUT THE GAME

Please enjoy your stay at the Mystery Motel! Mystery Motel is a 5x4 video game with 1024 ways to win. This game is packed with mysterious and spooky features. The game features a bonus wheel, wild mystery multipliers and free spins!

WILD MYSTERY MULTIPLIERS

Spooky Mystery Multipliers can drastically increase your winnings. Every time you get a wild symbol as part of a winning way, the wild will be replaced by a multiplier symbol. If you get several multipliers as part of a win, the multipliers shown will multiply. The multipliers will randomly take on a value of either 2, 3 or 5.

Example: If you get a win of 10 times your bet from the winning symbols and your win contains two wilds with multipliers 5 and 3, your win will be 10 * 5 * 3 = 150 times your bet! The max multiplier from this feature is 625x your win.

The wild symbol substitutes for all symbols except the BONUS and FREE SPINS symbols.

BONUS GAME

Three or more "bonus" scatter symbols in the same spin triggers the spooky bonus wheel!

The bonus wheel consists of three levels. Each level contains a number of possible outcomes - an arrow, a multiplier or a free spin win. If the wheel comes to a stop on an arrow symbol, you advance to the next level with even higher potential winnings and free spin possibilities!

If you hit a free spin symbol, you will be awarded with the FREE SPIN game with the shown number of free spins.

If you hit a multiplier, you will be awarded a fixed win of the shown number times your bet.

The bonus symbol appears in the base game only.

FREE SPINS

If you land three or more FREE SPIN symbols in the main game the free spin mode is triggered. 3 symbols gives 8 spins, 4 symbols 12 spins and 5 symbols 20 spins. If you win free spins in the bonus game, you will return to the reels but now in free spin mode. The free spin mode has even more wilds with multipliers. If you land three or more FREE SPIN symbols, you will be awarded with an additional 5 freespins. This feature can award extra free spins up to 10 times. Free spins will play automatically until there are no free spins left.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in Game History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
