ABOUT THE GAME

The wild north beckons! Here the mighty eagle rules, soaring high above land and sea. The beauty of this environment has been lovingly captured in this 5x4 game with payline wins and a maximum win of 10,000 times your bet!

FEATURES

STACK’N’SYNC™

Landing the S’n’S scatter symbol activates the Stack’n’Sync™ feature.

The reel where the symbol landed is copied to up to 4 other reels and these reels are then stacked with one kind of symbol. The stacked symbols replace existing symbols on the reels. The game then awards a respin, with the stacked reels synced to each other so they stop in the same position.

Landing another S’n’S symbol during the respin will repeat the stacking on additional reels and sync them, and award an additional respin.

The stacked symbol and the stack amount is shown by the symbol and stars that appear in the upper right corner above the reels. Each filled star represents one quarter of the reels. Reels can be a quarter, half, three-quarters or fully stacked!

All regular paying symbols, the Wild symbol, and the FS symbol can be stacked. The Eagle Claw bonus symbol can not be stacked.

GOLDEN STACK’N’SYNC™

Landing the golden S’n’S scatter symbol activates the Stack’n’Sync™ feature and guarantees that all 5 reels are stacked. Only high-paying symbols and the Wild symbol can be stacked by the golden S’n’S.

The golden S’n’S also activates a global multiplier that is applied to any wins on the respin. The multiplier is shown in the upper left corner above the reels.

The multiplier can have one of the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x

FREE SPINS

Trigger Free Spins by landing at least 3 FS scatter symbols simultaneously in the base game. For each landed FS symbol, 1 free spin is awarded. Up to 20 free spins can be awarded.

Free Spins increase the chance of landing S’n’S and golden S’n’S symbols!

The FS symbol is only available through the Stack’n’Sync™ feature.

EAGLE LINK

Trigger the Eagle Link bonus by landing 3 Eagle Claw scatter symbols simultaneously in the base game.

This bonus has two phases: a collect phase and a payout phase. Only Eagle symbols, Golden Eagle symbols and non-paying symbols appear in this bonus.

During the collect phase all landed Eagle and Golden Eagle symbols are sticky. The collect phase has 3 spins that refill when a new Eagle or Golden Eagle sticks. After 3 spins in a row without sticking new symbols, the collect phase ends and the bonus transitions to the payout phase. No wins are awarded during the collect phase.

Every landed Golden Eagle symbol activates the Eagle Link feature. The Eagle Link connects to all Eagle symbols that land vertically or horizontally adjacent to a Golden Eagle or another Eagle symbol already connected to the Eagle Link. When the collect phase ends, all Eagle symbols that are not connected to the Eagle Link are removed.

The payout phase has a global multiplier that applies to all Eagle symbol wins. The multiplier value is equal to the number of stuck Golden Eagle symbols.

SPIN

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

AUTOPLAY

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
