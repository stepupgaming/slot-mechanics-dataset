ABOUT THE GAME

Come and enjoy a snack in Panda’s kitchen… if you dare! Eye of the Panda is a cascading drop game with a 5x5 grid and winning paylines. The game features the Wild Panda Multiplier symbol that will substitute for other paying symbols, and multiply your wins. The max win in this game, which can be reached in all game modes, is 10,000 times your bet!

FEATURES

SUPER CASCADE

Whenever a win occurs, all symbols of the winning type(s) are removed from the reels and new symbols drop in to take their place.

WILD PANDA MULTIPLIERS

The Panda symbol is a Wild Multiplier with the possible values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10.

The Panda symbol substitutes for other paying symbols, and will apply their Multipliers to any winning combination that the Panda symbol is a part of. If more than one Panda symbol is part of the same winning combination, the Multipliers will multiply each other, before being applied to the win.

Landed Wild Panda Multipliers are persistent during the round until they are part of a winning combination. Once a Panda symbol is part of a win, it is removed on the next cascade together with the winning symbols.

NEVER SAY NO TO PANDA! BONUS

Land 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the NEVER SAY NO TO PANDA! bonus feature with 10 free spins.

When a Panda symbol is not part of a winning combination, it will be saved in one of the 5 Panda Collector spaces outside the reel. If there are no empty spaces in the Collector when a Panda symbol lands on a non-winning spin, that Panda will not be saved.

When a win occurs with at least one winning payline, Pandas in the Panda Collector will jump down onto the reels again to randomly selected existing winning positions in a winning combination and apply their Multiplier to any wins they are part of, in the same manner as in the Base Game. Only the number of Pandas that can fit within the existing wins will jump down from the Panda Collector. If there are no more winning positions to fill, the Pandas will be saved in the Panda Collector.

Since Pandas are Wild symbols, they can create new wins when they have jumped down from the Panda Collector. These wins will also be multiplied by the Multiplier.

On the last spin of the feature, all saved Pandas that were not part of any win will jump down to randomly selected losing positions on the reels. If they create winning combinations, those wins will be multiplied by the Panda’s Multipliers and then cascade.

JUMPING PANDA BONUS

Land 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the JUMPING PANDAS bonus feature with 10 free spins.

The feature keeps all the mechanics of the base game, but the first 5 Wild Panda Multiplier symbols that land become sticky and stay on the reels until the end of the feature. The sticky Pandas will jump to a new random position on each free spin and cascade and get a new Multiplier value on each jump. The Panda Multiplier activates whenever the Panda symbol is a part of a winning combination.

SPIN

The game is started by pressing the SPIN symbol button. The spacebar on the keyboard can also be used to initiate a spin.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). Separate wins in a winning combination will be displayed during the win presentation. Symbol payouts are displayed in the paytable and reflect the current bet configuration. Only the highest win per winning way is paid.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

AUTOPLAY

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
