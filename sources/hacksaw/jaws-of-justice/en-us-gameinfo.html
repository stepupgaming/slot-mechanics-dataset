ABOUT THE GAME

On a toxic planet deep within the cosmos, a deranged experiment unleashed horrors beyond imagination. Twisted by radiation and engineered for destruction, these apex predators have only one instinct — to kill. Having long since hunted their world to extinction, these beasts now turn their gaze to the stars. They prowl the void, seeking new nebulae to conquer… and new prey to destroy.

Conquer the cosmos in Jaws of Justice, a 5-reel, 4-row game with payline wins. Fight fin and fang for a max win of 10 000 times your bet, available in all game modes!

FEATURES

LASER SHARK SYMBOLS

When a Laser Shark symbol lands, it can only land on the 4th row of the grid. Once the grid has settled, all Laser Sharks fire a Laser in 1 of 3 directions: straight up, diagonally left, or diagonally right. Laser Sharks activate in order from left to right.

When a Laser is fired, every paying symbol along its path transforms into a Wild symbol. These transformed symbols can remain Wild or reveal an Additive Multiplier value of: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x. If a Laser hits an existing Wild, that Wild gains a Multiplier. If a Laser hits a Wild symbol with a Multiplier, the Multiplier value of that Wild will increase. If more than 1 Wild Multiplier is part of the same win, their values are combined.

Activated Laser Shark symbols transform into Wild symbols.

FORCE FIELDS

There are 3 Force Fields located on the left, top, and right edges of the grid. If a Laser Shark activates and hits a Force Field, the Laser will bounce off the Force Field and continue in another direction at the same angle. The Laser will continue to transform paying symbols in its path into Wild symbols.

When a Laser Shark hits a Force Field straight on, the Laser will bounce back the same way it came from, adding Multipliers to the transformed Wild symbols. If a Laser hits a Laser Shark symbol, it adds a Multiplier.

Once all wins have been awarded, the Laser Shark and all Wild symbols will be removed from the grid for the next spin.

When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account.
