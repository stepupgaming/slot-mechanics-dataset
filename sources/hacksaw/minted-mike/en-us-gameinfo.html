STICKER SYMBOLS

The Pink, Blue and Green Stickers are activator symbols that activate their respective mechanics. When multiple mechanics are activated during the same spin, the Pink Marker will be activated first followed by the Blue Spray Can and finally the Green Sticker Roll.

When the Pink Sticker symbol lands, it has a chance of randomly activating the Pink Marker after any wins have been awarded. When the Pink Marker activates, the following number of Hot Lines can be randomly painted onto the grid along the predetermined paylines: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 10, 12, 15, 20 or 26.​​ The maximum amount of Hot Lines that can be on the grid at the same time is 26. After the Hot Lines have been added to the grid, a respin is triggered and new symbols land. No new Sticker symbols can land during the respin.

All Hot Lines have a shared Hot Line Multiplier shown in the Multiplier Bar on top of the grid. Hot Lines can be painted onto the grid with the following Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 150x, 183x. When a winning combination occurs on a payline marked by an Hot Line, the value of the Hot Line Multiplier is applied to the win.

When the Blue Sticker symbol lands, it has a chance of randomly activating the Blue Spray Can before any wins are awarded. When the Blue Spray Can activates, the following number of Wild symbols can be added to the grid: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 15 or 20. Wild symbols can only be added to the grid when the Blue Spray Can is activated.

When the Green Sticker symbol lands, it has a chance of activating the Green Sticker Roll after any wins have been awarded. When the Green Sticker Roll activates, FS symbols and FS Upgrade symbols can be added to the grid.

Landing at least 8 FS symbols will award the STEEZY SKATER bonus. Landing at least 7 FS symbols and 1 FS Upgrade symbols will award the INK THE CITY bonus. Landing at least 6 FS symbols and 2 FS Upgrade symbols will award the TAG AND DASH bonus. The number of free spins awarded in the bonus is determined by the number of FS symbols that land. Each FS and FS Upgrade symbol that lands represents 1 free spin. FS and FS Upgrade symbols can only be added to the grid when the Green Sticker Roll is activated.

The Sticker activation animation does not influence the activation of the Pink Marker, Blue Spray Can or Green Sticker Roll mechanics.

BONUS GAME - STEEZY SKATER

Activate this bonus with 8-20 free spins by landing FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game’s mechanics. In addition, there is a higher chance of landing Pink, Blue and Green Sticker symbols.

The number of FS and FS Upgrade symbols that land when the Green Sticker Roll activates determines the number of extra free spins awarded in this bonus. If 1 FS Upgrade symbol lands, it awards an upgrade into the INK THE CITY bonus. If 2 FS Upgrade symbols land, an upgrade into the TAG AND DASH is awarded. When upgrading to a new bonus, the existing free spins and the awarded free spins will be transferred to the new bonus. In this bonus, the additional free spins awarded from the Green Sticker Roll are between 8-20.

BONUS GAME - INK THE CITY

Activate this bonus with 8-20 free spins by landing 1 FS Upgrade symbol and FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the base game, with all Hot Lines being progressive, meaning that they will keep their grid position with an increasing shared Multiplier value throughout the bonus. Each time the Pink Marker is activated during the bonus, the Hot Lines on the grid are activated and new Hot Lines are added to the grid. At the same time, the shared Multiplier value randomly increases with one of the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 150x, 183x.

If the Pink Marker is activated multiple times, the following number of Hot Lines can be added to the grid during every subsequent activation: 1-24.

If the Pink Marker activates when there is already a full grid of Hot Lines, the Hot Line Multiplier value increases, while no new Hot Lines are added to the grid.

The number of FS and FS Upgrade symbols that land when the Green Sticker Roll activates determines the number of extra free spins awarded in this bonus. If an FS Upgrade symbol lands, it awards an upgrade into the TAG AND DASH bonus. When upgrading to a new bonus, the Hot Lines, the shared Multiplier value and the existing free spins will be transferred to the new bonus along with the awarded free spins. In this bonus, the additional free spins awarded from the Green Sticker Roll are between 8-20.

HIDDEN EPIC BONUS - TAG AND DASH

Activate this bonus with 8-20 free spins by landing 2 FS Upgrade symbols and FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the INK THE CITY bonus. In addition, 1 Sticker symbol is guaranteed to land on every spin.

The number of FS symbols that land when the Green Sticker Roll activates determines the number of extra free spins in this bonus. In this bonus, the additional free spins awarded from the Green Sticker Roll are between 8-20.
