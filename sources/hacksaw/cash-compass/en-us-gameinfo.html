ABOUT THE GAME

Join this adventure set in the Caribbean Sea! Cash Compass is a 6x6 cluster win game with a max win of over 7300x. Trigger the Spreading Compass feature to multiply the symbols for massive wins!

COMPASS SYMBOL

Five compass symbols trigger the compass in the logo. The needle starts to spin and once it stops all compass symbols multiply and spread in the direction shown. Regular wins are awarded before the compass feature is triggered.

FREE SPINS

If 3 FREE SPINS symbols land on the grid, the free spins feature is triggered. In this mode, every time a compass symbol lands on the grid the compass starts spinning. This feature has added compass symbols on the reels. After the compass stops spinning, the compass symbol will multiply and spread in the direction shown. Regular wins are awarded before the compass feature is triggered.

3 FREE SPINS symbols award 10 free spins

BONUS WHEEL

If 3 BONUS symbols land on the grid, the bonus game feature is triggered.
The bonus game consists of a bonus wheel with 8 sections. The player gets up to 50 spins.
Every time that the hit marker comes to a stop at a particular section of the wheel, the displayed multiplier is added to the total and the multiplier on that section is increased to the next level. All multipliers start at 1x and can increase to 10x, 25x, 100x and finally 250x.

The bonus feature stops once the wheel comes to a stop on the skull symbol. The total win will be calculated as the displayed total multiplier multiplied with the base game bet level.

The maximum achievable win in this game is 7,300 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in Game History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.

GAME HISTORY
