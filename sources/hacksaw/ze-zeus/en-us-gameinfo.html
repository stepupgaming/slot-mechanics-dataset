ABOUT THE GAME

Never fear, Ze Zeus is here! Don your favorite wreath and join us in this 6-reel, 5-row game with cluster wins and a maximum win of 10,000 times your bet.

The max win can be reached in the base game and in the WHAT IF ZEUS WAS ONE OF US?, ZE ZEUS TAKE THE WHEEL and ZE ZEUS MIGHT SUPERSTAR game modes.

FEATURES

SUPER CASCADE

Any time a winning combination occurs, all regular paying symbols of the type that were part of the winning combination will also be removed from the grid and new ones will drop down to fill the empty spaces.

WIN TO WIN

After a winning combination occurs, the squares behind the winning symbols will be highlighted as Divine Squares.

If there is a Hand of Zeus symbol on the grid it will activate any Divine Squares after all wins have been completed. Activated Divine Squares reveal either Bronze, Silver or Gold Coins, and have a chance to show Vessel of Wealth or Zap of Zeus symbols.

All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet.

Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x

Gold Coins can have the following values: 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x, 500x

After all symbols have been revealed, any Zap of Zeus symbols will activate and multiply all Coins or existing Vessel of Wealth symbols on adjacent squares with one of the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10

Vessel of Wealth symbols will activate after all Coins have been revealed and all Zap of Zeus symbols have been activated. Vessel of Wealth symbols collect the values of all Coins and other Vessel of Wealth symbols and store their total value. Vessel of Wealth symbols activate top-to-bottom, left-to-right.

After the last Vessel of Wealth on the current grid has activated, all other Divine Squares will activate again to reveal new symbols and the process above repeats.

Once all reveals and activations have been completed all Coins and Vessel of Wealth symbols will be added together and multiplied with your bet amount to determine the final awarded win.

WHAT IF ZEUS WAS ONE OF US?

Activate this bonus game with 8 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game.

In this bonus all Divine Squares remain highlighted between spins until they are activated by the Hand of Zeus symbol.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus game awards extra free spins:

2 FS awards +2 free spins;

3 FS awards +4 free spins.

Landing 4 FS symbols at the same time upgrades the bonus to the ZE ZEUS TAKE THE WHEEL bonus and awards +4 free spins.

ZE ZEUS TAKE THE WHEEL

Activate this bonus game with 12 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game.

In this bonus all Divine Squares remain highlighted throughout the whole bonus, even after being activated by the Hand of Zeus symbol.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus game awards extra free spins:

2 FS awards +2 free spins;

3 FS awards +4 free spins.

ZE ZEUS MIGHT SUPERSTAR

Activate this bonus game with 12 free spins by landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game.

In this bonus all Divine Squares remain highlighted throughout the whole bonus, even after being activated by the Hand of Zeus symbol. A Hand of Zeus symbol is guaranteed to land on every free spin in this bonus.

In addition, Bronze Coins do not exist in this bonus.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). Separate wins in a winning combination will be displayed during the win presentation. Symbol payouts are displayed in the paytable and reflect the current bet configuration. Only the highest win per winning way is paid. Some win amounts may be displayed in an abbreviated format using letters to represent large numbers. When a win amount is displayed in this format, the displayed value may be rounded down. The actual amount awarded will never be less than the displayed value.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 10 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win is reached by forming winning combinations that highlight as many grid positions as possible, then landing a Hand of Zeus symbol to activate the Divine Squares feature that will reveal Bronze, Silver and Gold Coins as well as Zap of Zeus and Vessel of Wealth symbols with a total combined value of 10,000x. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
