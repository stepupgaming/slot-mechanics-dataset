DUELREELS™

When a VS or Bounty VS symbol lands, it expands into a DuelReel if part of a winning combination once expanded. Fully expanded DuelReels™ count as Wild reels and substitute for all symbols in the paytable. Once expanded, the DuelReel will cover the entire reel, triggering a Duel between two duelists. Each DuelReel will have 1 Adding Multiplier assigned to each of the 2 duelists.

When a VS symbol expands, it expands into an Outlaw DuelReel. The Multiplier from the surviving duelists will be applied across that entire DuelReel and is added to all wins connected to that reel. If more than 1 Outlaw DuelReel is part of the same winning combination, their winning Multipliers are added together before being applied to the win.

The possible Multiplier values for Outlaw DuelReels™ are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x.

When a Bounty VS symbol expands, it expands into a Bounty DuelReel with a Duel between the Bounty Hunter and an Outlaw. If the Bounty Hunter wins a Duel, she collects the Multiplier from the Outlaw and combines it with her existing Multiplier, and another Duel is triggered on the same Bounty DuelReel. On the new Duel, the Bounty Hunter will display her combined Multiplier from the previous Duel. This repeats until the Bounty Hunter loses a Duel or after 3 Duels have been played. When the Bounty Hunter loses a Duel, her collected Multiplier is applied to that Bounty DuelReel. The Multiplier values for Bounty DuelReels™ start at 2x.

Only 1 VS symbol or 1 Bounty VS symbol can land on a reel at a time.
