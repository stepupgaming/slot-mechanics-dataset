FEATURES

3 PIÑATAS

Blue, Red and Green Piñata Skulls are located outside the grid. When Blue, Red and Green Piñata scatter symbols land, they can activate the Skull with the corresponding color at the end of the spin after all wins have been handled. When a Skull is activated, it triggers a bonus. When entering a bonus, all Piñata symbols that were on the grid become sticky and are carried over into the bonus. Landing all three Piñata symbols at the same time will activate a bonus.

One Piñata symbol of each color can be on the grid at the same time, and different Piñata symbols can land on the same reel. The animation does not influence the activation of the 3 Skulls or the 3 Piñatas.

BONUS GAME - PIÑATA PARTY

Activate this Hold & Win bonus with 3 refilling lives by activating 1-3 Piñata Skulls during the base game. In this bonus, only Coin, Jackpot, Piñata and Dead symbols can land. Dead symbols are non-paying symbols and have no other effect.

Lives are displayed outside the grid, where 3 lives equal 3 free spins. When a Coin, Jackpot or any Piñata symbol lands, the lives and free spins are refilled to 3. Once all lives are consumed, the bonus ends.

When Coin symbols land, they stick to the grid until the end of the bonus or until collected. All Coin symbols are paid as a multiple of your bet.

Bronze Coins can have the following values: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x

Gold Coins can have the following values: 20x, 30x, 40x, 50x

Diamond Coins can have the following values: 100x, 150x, 250x, 500x

When Jackpot symbols land, they stick to the grid and award one of four different Jackpot prizes with a value based on your current bet at the end of the bonus. The current value of each Jackpot is displayed outside the grid on the Jackpot Signs. The four Jackpot Markers are:

MINI: 25x

MAJOR: 50x

MEGA: 250x

{maxWinLabel}: {highestJackpotMultiplier}x

When Piñata symbols land, they stick to the grid. Blue Piñata symbols will multiply one random Coin value by x2 every time the lives are refilled.

Red Piñata symbols will multiply all Coin values then any activated Green Piñata symbols in a 3x3 area around the Red Piñata symbol when all lives are consumed. Red Piñata symbols cannot affect Jackpot values. Red Piñata symbols can apply the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2-x10.

Green Piñata symbols activate when all lives are consumed for the first time. Activated Green Piñata symbols collect the values of all Coin and Jackpot symbols, storing their total value. Once a Coin or Jackpot symbol has been collected, it is removed from the grid and the lives are refilled.

If there is a full grid of Coin, Jackpot and all three Piñata symbols, once all activations are complete, all values are paid out, all Coin and Jackpot symbols are removed before the next spin and lives are refilled.

If there is a full grid of Coin, Jackpot, Blue and Red Piñata symbols, Blue Piñata symbols will activate, then Red Piñata symbol, then all values are paid out and the bonus ends.

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player.
