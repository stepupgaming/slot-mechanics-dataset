ABOUT THE GAME

Enter the dark mansion and explore what rests inside. Just be careful not to touch anything… it might awaken!

Immortal Desire is a 5-reel, 4-row game with ways-wins, featuring two different bonus games with a max win of 10,000 times your bet! The max win can be achieved in all game modes.

FEATURES

BLOOD REEL

At the start of each spin, one or more reels can turn into Blood Reels. All winning High-paying symbols on a Blood Reel are turned into Vampire symbols and have multipliers added to them. The total win amount in each winning combination is multiplied by the sum of the multipliers included in that win to determine the final win value.

For example, if a winning combination consists of 5 winning symbols of a certain type, of which 2 are on Blood Reels and both are given a multiplier of 10x, the total multiplier for that win will be 20x and the final win amount will be determined by counting the base win value for those 5 symbols times the multiplier of 20x.

Blood Reels can add the following multiplier values to Vampire symbols: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 25x, 50x, 100x.

A Blood Reel always applies the same multiplier value to the Vampire symbols on the reel. Blood Reels may appear during the base game and Blood Moon bonus game. Maximum 5 Blood Reels can appear at the same time.

BLOOD MOON

Land 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the BLOOD MOON bonus feature with 10 free spins.

In this bonus, all landed Blood Reels are persistent until the end of the feature. Blood Reel multipliers are refreshed on each spin.

Landing additional FS scatter symbols during the feature awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols awards 2 additional spins;

3 FS symbols awards 4 additional spins.

DARK AWAKENING

Land 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the DARK AWAKENING bonus feature with 10 free spins.

During this feature, only non-paying symbols, Awakened Multipliers and Coffin symbols appear. Above each reel is a multiplier value that can be increased by landing AWAKENED MULTIPLIER and COFFIN symbols. At the end of the feature, the multipliers above the reels will be added together and multiplied with your bet amount to determine the final award.

AWAKENED MULTIPLIER SYMBOLS

Landed Awakened Multiplier symbols increase the value of the multipliers above the reels. They can be adding or multiplying and can affect one or all the reel multipliers.

Normal multipliers only affect the reels they land on, while Epic multipliers are applied to all the reels.

Normal adding multipliers can have the following values: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 250x, 500x.

Epic adding multipliers can have the following values: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 250x, 500x.

Normal Multiplying multipliers can have the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x6, x7, x8, x9, x10.

Epic Multiplying multipliers can have the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x6, x7, x8, x9, x10.

COFFIN SYMBOLS

Coffin symbols are sticky until the end of the feature. At the end of the bonus game, landed Coffin symbols will reveal a multiplying multiplier which will be applied to their respective reel multiplier.

Coffin symbols can have the following multiplier values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x6, x7, x8, x9, x10.

Landing additional FS scatter symbols during the feature awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols awards 2 additional spins;

3 FS symbols awards 4 additional spins.

WAYS TO WIN

The specified requirements for a winning combination in the base game are also used during bonus features or free spins play unless otherwise stated. Each wager is eligible for all winning combinations unless otherwise stated.

You win if matching symbols appear on adjacent reels from left to right, starting with the leftmost reel. Each winning combination of matching symbols creates a winning way. The number of possible ways in this game is 1024. Please consult the paytable for the number of each symbol required to win.

SPIN

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). Separate wins in a winning combination will be displayed during the win presentation. Symbol payouts are displayed in the paytable and reflect the current bet configuration. MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

AUTOPLAY

Using Autoplay will let you play a number of game rounds automatically. Press the AUTOPLAY button and choose a number of game rounds to initiate this feature. The remaining number of spins is displayed. Autoplay is stopped by pressing the STOP button.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
