FEATURES

3 CURSED CHESTS: HOLD & WIN

A Green, Red and Blue Chest are located outside of the grid. When Green, Red and Blue Pot symbols land, they will charge the Chest with the corresponding color. When a Chest is charged, there is a chance that it will activate at the end of the spin after all wins have been handled. Only one Chest can activate per spin. The animation does not influence the activation of the 3 Cursed Chests.

When a Green Chest is activated, a Cursed Wilds Respin is triggered, where any combination of regular paying symbols, Wild or Wild Multipliers will land. The Wild symbol substitutes for all symbols in the paytable. When a Wild Multiplier symbol participates in a winning combination, the value of the win is multiplied by the shown value. If more than one Wild Multiplier symbol is part of the same winning combination, the values are first added together before being applied to the win. Wild Multipliers can have the following Additive Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x.

When a Red Chest is activated, a Cursed Coins Bonus sequence is triggered, with 3 refilling lives equaling 3 free spins. Lives are displayed outside of the grid. During the Cursed Coins Bonus sequence, Dead symbols can land that are non-paying and have no other effect.

Bronze, Silver, Gold and Diamond Coins can also land and stick to the grid until the end of the Cursed Coins Bonus sequence. All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet.

Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.4x, 1x

Silver Coins can have the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x

Gold Coins can have the following values: 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x

Diamond Coins can have the following values: 50x, 100x, 150x, 200x, 250x, 500x, 1000x

When any Coin symbol lands, the lives and free spins are refilled to 3. Once all lives are consumed or there are no more available positions, the Cursed Coins Bonus sequence ends.

When a Blue Chest is activated, a Cursed FS Respin is triggered, where only Dead and FS symbols can land. Landing at least 7 FS symbols will award the TREASURE COVE bonus. Landing at least 6 FS symbols and 1 FS Upgrade symbol will award the GHOSTLY GALLOWS bonus. The number of free spins awarded in the bonus is determined by the number of FS symbols that land. Each FS and FS Upgrade symbols that lands represents 1 free spin.

BONUS GAME - TREASURE COVE

Activate this bonus with 7-15 free spins by landing FS scatter symbols at the same time during the Cursed FS Respin in base game.

This bonus keeps the base game's mechanics. In addition, when the 3rd Pot symbol of the same color lands, the corresponding Chest is guaranteed to activate. Chests can activate before the 3rd Pot symbol lands; in this case, the count resets, and 3 new Pot symbols of that color are required to guarantee an activation.

When activated, the Cursed Coins Bonus sequence counts as one free spin.

The number of FS symbols that land during the Cursed FS Respin determines the number of extra free spins awarded in this bonus. If an FS Upgrade symbol lands, it awards an upgrade into the GHOSTLY GALLOWS bonus. When upgrading to a new bonus, the existing free spins and the awarded free spins will be transferred to the new bonus.

In this bonus, the additional free spins awarded during the Cursed FS Respin are between 2-10.

BONUS GAME - GHOSTLY GALLOWS

Activate this bonus with 7-15 free spins by landing FS scatter symbols and 1 FS Upgrade symbol at the same time during the Cursed FS Respin.

This bonus keeps the base game's mechanics. In addition, when a Pot symbol lands, it will activate the corresponding Chest on that spin.

When activated, the Cursed Coins Bonus sequence counts as one free spin.

The number of FS symbols that land during the Cursed FS Respin determines the number of extra free spins awarded in this bonus.

In this bonus, the additional free spins awarded during the Cursed FS Respin are between 2-10.

The maximum win occurs when the Green Chest is activated, triggering a Cursed Wilds Respin, creating a full grid of Wild and Wild Multipliers with a combined multiplier value of at least 19x that is applied to the winning combinations to reach the max win. INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
