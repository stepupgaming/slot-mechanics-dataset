FEATURES

THUNDER RESPINS

Land at least 5 Coin symbols of any kind at the same time to trigger the Thunder Respins. This Respin sequence contains only dead symbols, Coins, and Collector Chests. During Thunder Respins, all Coins and Collector Chests that land are sticky until the Respin sequence ends. Dead symbols are non-paying and have no other effect.

Continue to land Coin symbols of any kind or Collector Chests to continue the Thunder Respins. When no new Coin symbols or Collector Chests land, the Respin sequence will end. At the end of the Thunder Respins, all Coins on the grid reveal a random cash value depending on the Coin symbol. The revealed values are calculated based on your current bet.

Bronze Coins can reveal values based on the following bet multipliers: 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Coins can reveal values based on the following bet multipliers: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x

Gold Coins can reveal values based on the following bet multipliers: 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 250x, 500x

The Collector Chest symbols can land with or without a Multiplier. After all Coin symbols reveal their cash values, the Collector Chest activates and collects all Coin symbols on the grid. If a Collector Chest lands with a Multiplier, it first collects the Coin symbols, then reveals the Multiplier value and applies it to the Coin totals to determine the final amount.

If more than 1 Collector Chest lands, each one will apply its own Multiplier to the Coin symbols it collected. Each Collector Chest collects each Coin symbol once. If Coin symbols get collected, they will retain their original values when collected by another Collector Chest. Collector Chests activate one by one, from top to bottom, left to right. The final win is the total value of all collected Coins from all Collector Chests. The possible Multiplier values are: x1, x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x15, x20

Once the Thunder Respin sequence ends and all collections are complete, the grid resets, removing Coin symbols, Collector Chests, and dead symbols before the next spin.

BONUS CHOICE

Landing 3 or 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game brings you into the Bonus Choice menu, where you can choose between 2 bonus games depending on the number of landed FS symbols.

With 3 FS symbols, you may choose either the STORMBREAKER or the PERFECT STORM bonus games.

With 4 FS symbols, you may choose either the LEGACY OF LIGHTNING or the HAMMER OF THE HEAVENS bonus games.

BONUS GAME - STORMBREAKER

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will get 3 free spins. In this bonus game, each free spin triggers a new Thunder Respin sequence. During Thunder Respins, a minimum of 2 Coins and 1 Collector Chest will land during each Thunder Respin sequence!

BONUS GAME - PERFECT STORM

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will get 10 free spins. In this bonus game, all Wild symbols that land are Sticky and stay on the grid throughout the bonus. When a Wild symbol lands, it substitutes for all symbols in the paytable and has a possible Multiplier value of 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 25x, 50x, 100x. When a Wild symbol lands, it can reveal a Multiplier which multiplies the winning payline it is part of. If more than 1 Wild symbol contributes to a win, their values are added together before being applied to the total win.

If 4 Wild symbols land and cover an entire reel, they transform into a full Wild God Reel with a total Multiplier. This total Multiplier is the sum of adding all individual Wild Multiplier values and is applied to the entire Wild God Reel. If a winning combination includes more than 1 Wild God Reel, the Multiplier values will first be added from left to right and then applied to the win. Wild God Reels stick to the grid and remain throughout the bonus.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols award +2 free spins,

3 FS symbols award +4 free spins.

BONUS GAME - LEGACY OF LIGHTNING

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will get 3 free spins. This bonus game keeps the mechanics of STORMBREAKER bonus. During Thunder Respins, a minimum of 4 Coins and 1 Collector Chest will land during each Thunder Respin sequence! In addition, when landing a Mjolnir symbol during a Thunder Respin, it will activate at the end of the spin and multiply a random number of Coin symbols on the grid with a Multiplier of x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x15, x20. The Collector Chests will activate after the Mjolnir symbol has activated.

BONUS GAME - HAMMER OF THE HEAVENS

When you choose this bonus game from the Bonus Choice menu, you will get 10 free spins. This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the PERFECT STORM bonus. In addition, landing a Mjolnir symbol adds a random Multiplier value of x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x15, x20 to a random number of Wild symbols or expanded Wild God Reels on the grid at the end of the current free spin. When the current free spin ends, all Wild symbols will revert to their original Multiplier value for the next free spin.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols award +2 free spins,

3 FS symbols award +4 free spins.

HIDDEN EPIC BONUS - BLESSINGS OF THE BIFROST

Activate this bonus with 5 free spins by landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This Hidden Epic Bonus keeps the mechanics of the LEGACY OF LIGHTNING bonus. In addition, at least 5 Coins, 1 Collector Chest, and 1 Mjolnir symbol lands during each Thunder Respin sequence!

The maximum achievable win in this game is 15 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. MAX WIN

Maximum win: 15 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when at least 5 Coin symbols land at the same time to trigger the Thunder Respins feature, which reveal multipliers based on the current bet level over multiple respins until the maximum win of 15 000x is reached. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
