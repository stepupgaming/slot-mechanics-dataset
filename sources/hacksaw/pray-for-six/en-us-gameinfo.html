ABOUT THE GAME

One would think the worst thing to come out of Hell was the Devil himself. But it looks like someone left Hell’s nursery gates open…

Meet the Devil’s Spawn and his Pyropup, two hellraisers who leave a trail of tears behind them as they run wild across the infernal realm. Together, they tumble through the Devil’s playground, setting souls ablaze and tormenting the tormented.

Pray for Six is a 6-reel, 5-row cascading game with All-Scatter wins and a max win of 20 000 times your bet, achievable in all game modes.

Looks like it’s playtime in Purgatory — and Balthazar and Beelzebub have some competition.

FEATURES

TOTAL WIN BAR

All wins during each spin are collected in the Total Win Bar. The Total Win Bar is located outside the grid and the collected win is awarded when all cascades have ended.

WAILING WHEELS

When a ‘6’ symbol lands, it will activate if there is at least one win on the grid, and after all cascades are complete. When ‘6’ symbols drop in during cascades, they will land activated.

When a ‘6’ symbol activates, it triggers a Wailing Wheel on the same position. The Wailing Wheel spins and lands on a Cash Prize, Additive Multiplier, Multiplicative Multiplier, or a MAX WIN that is instantly awarded. The Cash Prizes are paid as multiples of your bet and are selected from Bronze, Silver, or Gold value brackets.

The three value brackets have the following Cash Prizes:

Bronze Cash Prize: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Cash Prize: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x

Gold Cash Prize: 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x, 333x, 500x, 666x

When the Wailing Wheel lands on a Multiplier, its value is added to the side of the Total Win Bar. If multiple Multiplier values are collected, they are combined into a single total. When the Multiplier is Additive, the new value is added to the existing total Multiplier. When the value is Multiplicative, the new value multiplies the existing total Multiplier. Additive Multipliers can have the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 25x, 33x, 100x, 333x. Multiplicative Multipliers can have the following values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20, x33.

Once all ‘6’ symbols have activated, the final win collected in the Total Win Bar is multiplied by the final Multiplier value on the Total Win Bar. The Total Win Bar resets for the next spin.

SPIN

The game is started by pressing the SPIN symbol button. The spacebar on the keyboard can also be used to initiate a spin.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). Separate wins in a winning combination will be displayed during the win presentation. Symbol payouts are displayed in the paytable and reflect the current bet configuration. Only the highest win per winning way is paid. Some win amounts may be displayed in an abbreviated format using letters to represent large numbers. When a win amount is displayed in this format, the displayed value may be rounded down. The actual amount awarded will never be less than the displayed value.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 20,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 20 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when a high enough win amount is collected in the Total Win Bar after all cascades have ended, which will combine with the multipliers awarded from several Wailing Wheels after being activated by landing 6 symbols until the maximum win of 20 000x is reached. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player.
