FEATURES

DUELREELS™

When a VS symbol lands it will expand into a wild DuelReel if it would be part of a winning combination once expanded.

After expanding, the DuelReel will cover the entire reel and a duel will take place on the reel. Each DuelReel will have two multipliers, connected to the duelists The Outlaw and The Sheriff. The surviving duelists multiplier will be applied across the entire reel and affects any winning combinations that includes the DuelReel.

All expanded DuelReel positions count as WILD symbols

The possible multiplier values for DuelReels™ are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 200x

If more than one DuelReels™ are part of the same winning combination, their multipliers are added together before being applied to the win.

Only 1 VS symbol can land on a reel at a time.

THE OUTLAW FEATURE

When an Outlaw symbol lands, it will expand into a full wild Outlaw reel and show a revolver with 1-6 bullets in its chambers. The number of bullets in the revolver represents the number of Wild symbols that the Outlaw will shoot onto the grid.

If more than one Outlaw is on the grid at the same time, they cannot shoot multiple Wild symbols onto the same positions on the grid, nor can they shoot at each other. A maximum of 2 Outlaw symbols can appear on one spin, and only 1 Outlaw symbol can land on a reel at a time.

Each Outlaw reel will also have a multiplier that can be one of the following values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 200x.

The multiplier affects any winning combination that includes the Outlaw reel. If more than one Outlaw reels are part of the same winning combination, their multipliers are added together before being applied to the win.

WILD WILD WEST

Land 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the WILD WILD WEST bonus feature with 10 free spins.

This bonus feature maintains the mechanics of the base game with an increased chance of getting high-value multipliers on DuelReels™ and Outlaw reels! There is also an increased chance of landing VS symbols and Outlaw symbols!

Landing additional FS symbols during the bonus awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols award 2 additional spins,

3 FS symbols award 4 additional spins.

DUSK ‘TIL DAWN

Land 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate the DUSK ‘TIL DAWN bonus feature with 10 free spins.

This bonus feature offers the chance of earning 4 DuelSpins with guaranteed VS symbols! You unlock each DuelSpin by landing Outlaw symbols and shooting a full revolver of 6 Wild symbols onto the grid.

Each DuelSpin is awarded immediately after the free spin where you unlocked it and does not count towards your number of free spins in the bonus. No more than 2 DuelSpins can be unlocked in one spin; if more than one are unlocked in the same spin, they are awarded in sequence before proceeding to the next free spin.

Each subsequent DuelSpin awards more guaranteed VS symbols:

The first DuelSpin guarantees at least 2 VS symbols;

The second DuelSpin guarantees at least 3 VS symbols;

The third DuelSpin guarantees at least 4 VS symbols;

The fourth DuelSpin guarantees that 5 VS symbols land!

Each unlocked DuelSpin also awards 3 additional free spins.

VS symbols do not appear in this bonus except during DuelSpins.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
