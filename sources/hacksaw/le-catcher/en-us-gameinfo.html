ABOUT THE GAME

Armed with his orange floaties and yellow wellies, Smokey hits the water! He might be looking to put the heists on hold for a weekend and take a relaxing break away, but we know this Frenchie better than that. He isn’t just fishing for dinner; rumor has it there’s some treasure at the bottom of this lake, and if anyone can catch it, it’s Le Catcher!

So if you’re hanging out by the lake this weekend, you might spot him air-jamming with his fishing pole, singing to himself: “Smokeeey on the waaater, duh duh duuuh, duh duh duh duuduuh!”

Tag along on this 6-reel, 5-row game with Cluster and a max win of 15 000 times your bet, available in all game modes.

FEATURES

SUPER CASCADES

When a winning combination occurs, all regular paying symbols of the type that were part of the winning combination are removed from the grid, and new ones drop down to fill the empty spaces.

GOLDEN SQUARES

When a winning combination occurs, the squares behind the winning symbols will be highlighted as Golden Squares.

When a Rainbow symbol lands on the grid, it activates any Golden Squares after all wins have been collected. Activated Golden Squares reveal Special symbols that are either Bronze, Silver, Gold or Diamond Coins and can also reveal Green or Gold Clover symbols and Bucket or Global Bucket symbols.

When an Epic Rainbow symbol lands, it reveals Special symbols on all positions on the grid. The reveal and activation process is the same for both Rainbow symbols.

All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet.

Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x

Gold Coins can have the following values: 25x, 50x, 100x

Diamond Coins can have the following values: 150x, 250x, 500x

After all Golden Squares have been revealed, the Clover symbols will activate. Green Clover symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Bucket and Global Bucket symbols on adjacent squares with one of the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20. Gold Clover symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Bucket and Global Bucket symbols on the grid with one of the following Multiplicative Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20. If there are no Special symbols near the Clover symbols, they will activate once there are Special symbols on the grid to multiply.

Once all Clover symbols have applied their Multipliers, Bucket symbols will activate. When Bucket symbols activate, they collect the values of all Coins and other activated Bucket and Global Bucket symbols on adjacent 3x3 squares and store their total value. Bucket symbols activate top to bottom, left to right.

Golden Squares will reveal new Special symbols once they have been collected by the Bucket symbol, and the process above repeats. If a Bucket symbol has already been activated, it will not collect again.

Once all Bucket symbols have activated, any Global Bucket symbols present on the grid will activate. When Global Bucket symbols activate, they collect the values of all Coins and other Buckets and Global Buckets, and store their total value. Global Bucket symbols activate top to bottom, left to right. Golden Squares will reveal new Special symbols once they have been collected by the Global Bucket symbol, and the process above repeats. If a Global Bucket symbol has already been activated, it will not collect again.

Once all reveals and activations have been completed, all Coins and Bucket symbols will be added together and multiplied by your bet amount to determine the final awarded win. Once all the wins and cash prizes have been awarded, all Special symbols and Golden Squares are removed from the grid for the next spin.

BONUS GAME - ON THIN ICE

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game's mechanics, and all Golden Squares are progressive and will remain on the grid until either Rainbow symbol activates them. In addition, a Big Catch Bar is displayed outside the grid. Each time an FS symbol lands on the grid, the Big Catch Bar increases one step.

When a Rainbow symbol has activated, it will transform into another Special symbol depending on the Level reached. The Big Catch Bar begins at Level 0, where the Rainbow symbol functions as usual. After every 5th FS symbol has landed, a new Level is reached on the Big Catch Bar:

Level 1: After either Rainbow symbol activates, it transforms into a Green Clover symbol.

Level 2: After either Rainbow symbol activates, it transforms into a Global Bucket symbol.

Level 3: After either Rainbow symbol activates, it transforms into a Gold Clover symbol.

Level 4: A Rainbow or Epic Rainbow symbol is guaranteed on every free spin, and after a Rainbow symbol activates, it will transform into a Gold Clover symbol.

For every Level that is reached on the Big Catch Bar, +5 free spins are awarded.

BONUS GAME - SLIPPERY WHEN WET

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus keeps the mechanics of the ON THIN ICE bonus. In addition, all Golden Squares are progressive and stay on the grid throughout the bonus, even after being activated by a Rainbow symbol. When Epic Rainbow symbols activate, only the Golden Squares that were on the grid before will remain after the full grid activation is complete. The minimum Coin value is 1x.

For every Level that is reached on the Big Catch Bar, +5 free spins are awarded.

HIDDEN EPIC BONUS - SMOKEY UNDER WATER

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus keeps the mechanics of the SLIPPERY WHEN WET bonus and the Big Catch Bar starts at Level 4 and remains there for the entire bonus.

FS symbols cannot land in this bonus.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 15 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 15 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs by forming winning combinations that highlight as many grid positions as possible, then landing a Rainbow symbol to activate the highlighted Golden Squares feature that will reveal either Bronze, Silver, Gold or Diamond coins, Bucket, or Global Bucket symbols, Green Clover, or Gold Clover symbols until the maximum win of 15 000x is reached. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
