FEATURES

SUPER CASCADES

When a winning combination occurs, all regular paying symbols of the type that were part of the winning combination are removed from the grid, and new ones drop down to fill the empty spaces.

GOLDEN SQUARES

When a winning combination occurs, the squares behind the winning symbols will be highlighted as Golden Squares.

If there is a Neon Rainbow symbol on the grid, it will activate any Golden Squares after all wins have been collected. Activated Golden Squares reveal either Bronze, Silver, or Gold Coins and can also reveal Treasure Pots, Green or Gold Clover symbols, and Jackpot Markers.

All Coins are cash prizes that are paid as a multiple of your bet.

Bronze Coins can have the following values: 0.2x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver Coins can have the following values: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x

Gold Coins can have the following values: 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x, 500x

After all Golden Squares have been revealed, the Clover symbols will activate. Green Clover symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Treasure Pots on adjacent squares with one of the following Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20. Gold Clover symbols will multiply all Coins or existing Treasure Pots on the grid with one of the following Multipliers: x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20.

Once all Clover symbols have applied their multipliers, Treasure Pot symbols will activate. Treasure Pots collect the values of all Coins and other Treasure Pots and store their total value. Treasure Pots activate top-to-bottom, left-to-right. Treasure Pots will not collect Jackpot Markers.

When the last Treasure Pot on the current grid has activated, all other Golden Squares will activate again to reveal new symbols and the process above repeats.

Once all reveals and activations have been completed, all Coins and Treasure Pots will be added together and multiplied by your bet amount to determine the final awarded win.

JACKPOT MARKERS

Jackpot Markers can land on the grid or be revealed in the Golden Squares. Jackpot Markers award four different jackpot prizes with a value based on your current bet. The four Jackpot Markers are:

MINI: 10x

MAJOR: 100x

MEGA: 1 000x

{maxJackpotLabel}: {highestJackpotMultiplier}x

The current value of each jackpot is displayed outside the grid on the Jackpot Sign.

Jackpot Markers that land during a spin will be activated if they land together with a Neon Rainbow. When a Jackpot Marker is revealed in a Golden Square it will be awarded once all Coins and Treasure Pot values have been awarded. Only 1 of each Jackpot value can appear at the same time, so a maximum of 4 Jackpot Markers can land on a spin. The Jackpot Marker values will be combined before being awarded.

Once all the wins and cash prizes have been awarded, all Special Symbols and Golden Squares are removed from the grid for the next spin.

WIN

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. Multiple wins in one round are added together and the total round win will be displayed in the WIN field (to the right of BALANCE). Separate wins in a winning combination will be displayed during the win presentation. Symbol payouts are displayed in the paytable and reflect the current bet configuration. Only the highest win per winning way is paid. Some win amounts may be displayed in an abbreviated format using letters to represent large numbers. When a win amount is displayed in this format, the displayed value may be rounded down. The actual amount awarded will never be less than the displayed value.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is {maximumWinMultiplier} times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
