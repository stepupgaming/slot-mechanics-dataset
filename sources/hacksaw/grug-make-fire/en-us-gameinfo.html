ABOUT THE GAME

As wildfires blaze across the land and dinosaurs carve their shadows into the earth, the world crackles with raw, primal power. In Grug Make Fire, every spin drags you deeper into an age where survival is earned with fist and fangs.

This 5-reel, 4-row game with paylines harnesses fire, fury and evolution as you hunt for a max win of 10 000 times your bet, achievable in all game modes. Will you rule the prehistoric wilds with Grug?

FEATURES

BURNING WILD SYMBOLS

When a Burning Wild symbol lands on the grid, it activates and burns all paying symbols and FS symbols on the reel above where the symbol landed.

When low-paying symbols are burnt, they transform into Wild symbols.

When high-paying symbols are burnt, they transform into Burnt Wild Multipliers. When Burnt Wild Multipliers are part of a win, the Multiplier value is added to all wins connected to that Burnt Wild Multiplier. If more than one Burnt Wild Multiplier is part of the same winning combination, the values are added together before being applied to the win.

Burnt Wild Multipliers can have the following Additive Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 200x

Each high-paying symbol has its own corresponding Burnt Wild Multiplier.

FS SYMBOLS

When 3 or more FS symbols land simultaneously, each FS symbol will display a free spin value. The total number of free spins awarded for the bonus is determined by adding up all the numbers on positions covered by each FS symbol. FS symbols can have the following values: 2 - 5.

When FS symbols are burnt, they can display a higher free spin value. Burnt FS symbols can have the following values: 3 - 6.

BONUS GAME - GRUG FIND FOOD

Activate this bonus with 6 - 18 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus keeps the base game's mechanics with an increased chance of landing Burning Wild symbols.

FS symbols can land with the following values: 1-3 and Burnt FS symbols can have the following values: 2 - 4.

Landing an additional FS symbol in the bonus awards extra free spins: An FS symbol awards at least 1 free spin and max 4 free spins.

BONUS GAME - GRUG FIGHT DINO

Activate this bonus with 8 - 24 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus keeps the mechanics of the GRUG FIND FOOD bonus. In this bonus, a Wildfire Bar is located outside the grid. The Wildfire Bar has Levels and is progressive throughout the bonus. The Wildfire Bar will reach the next Level when a certain number of winning symbols have been awarded. Each Level will award a certain number of Burnt Reels. Once a new Level is reached, the Bar will reset. The Wildfire Bar starts at Level 0.

  • 12 winning symbols are needed to reach Level 1, which awards 1 Burnt Reel
  • 46 winning symbols are needed to reach Level 2, which awards 2 Burnt Reels
  • 96 winning symbols are needed to reach Level 3, which awards 3 Burnt Reels
  • 150 winning symbols are needed to reach Level 4, which awards 4-5 Burnt Reels

Once Level 4 is reached, the Wildfire Bar will stay at Level 4 until the end of the bonus.

Burnt Reels burn low and high-paying symbols and FS symbols on that reel, and transform them as in the base game. When a Burnt Wild Multiplier burns twice, the existing Multiplier value will be multiplied by x3. If a Burnt FS symbol burns twice, its free spin value can increase to a higher value. The new possible values are: 3 - 5.

When Burning Wild symbols and Burnt Reels are active at the same time, Burning Wild symbols activate first, followed by Burnt Reels. Any wins are awarded and the Wildfire Bar fills. Burnt Reels can only burn a reel once per spin.

Landing an additional FS symbol in the bonus awards extra free spins: An FS symbol awards at least 1 free spin and max 5 free spins.

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10,000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 10 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when one Burning Wild symbol lands on each reel at the same time, and activates by burning multiple high-paying symbols, transforming them into Burnt Wild Multipliers, which apply the total sum of Multipliers to the winning combinations to reach the max win. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game, but is only possible with a lower probability if bonus rounds are excluded.

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar.
