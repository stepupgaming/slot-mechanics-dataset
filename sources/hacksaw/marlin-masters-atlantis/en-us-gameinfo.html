FEATURES

MARLIN SYMBOLS AND LOOTLINES

Marlin symbols are dynamic paying symbols that display cash values. The cash value that is shown on the Marlin symbol represents the payout that is awarded if the symbol is part of a winning LootLine or is collected by a Fisherman symbol. Marlin symbols can have values based on the following bet Multipliers: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x and 100x.

A LootLine is formed when there is a winning payline of Marlin symbols, Jackpot Marlin Symbols or a combined payline including both symbol types on the grid. The payout of the LootLine is determined by the values of the Marlin and Jackpot Marlin symbols that are part of the winning combination.

If a Marlin symbol's value is too large to display fully, it will be shortened to two decimal places. The displayed value represents the minimum possible award, though the actual amount may be higher. For example 12 500 000 may be shown as 12.50M.

JACKPOT MARLINS

Jackpot Marlins are symbols that have different fixed jackpot prizes. A Jackpot Marlin symbol will be activated by being part of a winning LootLine or being collected by a Fisherman symbol.

When activated, the Jackpot Marlin symbol will reveal one of the four following jackpot values, that will be rewarded as a multiple of your bet:

FISHERMAN SYMBOLS

When landing, a Fisherman symbol collects all Marlin symbols currently on the grid. Fisherman symbols activate after regular wins and LootLine wins have been awarded. When activated, Fisherman symbols collect all Marlin symbols on the grid and award the value as a final cash prize.

The Fisherman symbol can also land with one of the following Multiplier values: x1, x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x15 and x20. When the Marlin symbols have been collected, the Fisherman symbol's Multiplier value will then be applied to the total cash value of the collected Marlin symbols, before being rewarded as a final cash prize. Fisherman symbols will not apply their Multiplier value to Jackpot Marlins.

When more than one Fisherman symbol or Fisherman symbol with a Multiplier lands during the same spin, each Fisherman symbol separately collects each Marlin symbol once from top-to-bottom, left-to-right. Each Fisherman symbol then awards their collected value as a final cash prize. There is a maximum of three Fisherman symbols that can land on the grid during the same spin.

KRAKEN COLLECTOR

A Kraken Collector symbol can only land on reel 1. When landing, all regular payline wins will be handled before the symbol expands and covers the entire reel. After that, all Marlin and Jackpot Marlin symbols will remain in their position on the grid, while all regular paying symbols are removed before the reels will respin.

When the Collector is active, the only symbols that will land are Marlin, Jackpot Marlin and Dead Fish symbols. All Marlin and Jackpot Marlin symbols that land will become Sticky and remain at the same position on the grid as they landed until there are no more respins. Jackpot Marlins reveal their value when the final respin is done. The Dead Fish symbol is a dead symbol and will not result in any regular payline wins.

The respins will continue until no new Marlin or Jackpot Marlin symbols land. When the final respin is done, all Marlin and Jackpot Marlin symbols will be collected and the total value of the collected Marlin and Jackpot Marlin symbols will be rewarded as a cash prize.

Marlin and Jackpot Marlin symbols cannot land on the same reel as the Kraken Collector symbol and Fisherman symbols cannot land during the same spin as a Kraken Collector symbol.

BONUS GAME - SUNKEN EMPIRE

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game's mechanics. In addition, a progressive Trident Bar is displayed at the top of the grid. Every time a Fisherman symbol lands on the grid, the Trident Bar will increase by one step. This increase happens regardless of whether or not the Fisherman symbol collects any Marlin or Jackpot Marlin symbols that round.

After every fifth Fisherman symbol has landed, a new upgrade bonus level is reached:

Upgrade 1 awards 8 additional free spins and guarantees that all subsequent Fisherman symbols land with a Multiplier value of at least x2.

Upgrade 2 awards 8 additional free spins and guarantees that all subsequent Fisherman symbols land with a Multiplier value of at least x3.

Upgrade 3 awards 8 additional free spins and guarantees that all subsequent Fisherman symbols land with a Multiplier value of at least x5.

BONUS GAME - POSE FOR POSEIDON

Activate this bonus with 15 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus keeps the mechanics of the SUNKEN EMPIRE bonus, with an increased chance of reaching the different bonus upgrade levels through the Trident Bar!
