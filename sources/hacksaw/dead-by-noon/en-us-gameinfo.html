FEATURES

ROW CASCADES

When a winning combination occurs, all symbols on the bottom row are removed from the grid, and all other symbols move down one row. New symbols then drop in to fill the empty spaces on the top row.

MULTIPLIER CHAMBER

When a Poker Chip symbol lands, it activates if there is a win on the grid or if the Poker Chip symbol would be part of a win. When Poker Chip symbols are active, they will trigger the Multiplier Chamber above that reel before any wins are handled. The five Multiplier Chambers above each reel reveal a number between 1-9 when activated. Once a Poker Chip symbol activates a Multiplier Chamber, it transforms into a Wild symbol. Activated Multiplier Chambers remain active during Row Cascades. New Poker Chips can land during Row Cascades on reels with inactive Multiplier Chambers.

When Row Cascades are complete, wins are multiplied by the combined number created from the individual numbers in the activated Multiplier Chambers, starting from left to right. For example, if the different Multiplier Chamber numbers are 2, 5 and 1, the total multiplier will be 251x. Only numbers that are in a continuous line starting from the leftmost Multiplier Chamber will be combined.

Only one Poker Chip symbol can land on a reel at a time. All Multiplier Chambers will reset between spins.

HIGH-ROLLER FEATURESPINS™

During the HIGH-ROLLER FEATURESPINS™, only Wanted Poster symbols and Dead symbols will land. Dead symbols are non-paying symbols and have no other effect.

When Wanted Poster symbols land, the possible number per reel can be:

  • 3-4 Wanted Posters can land on reel 1
  • 2-4 Wanted Posters can land on reel 2
  • 1-3 Wanted Posters can land on reel 3
  • 1-3 Wanted Posters can land on reel 4
  • 1-2 Wanted Posters can land on reel 5

Once the grid settles, the player is awarded six shots. Five are fired, one on each reel. If the shots hit one Wanted Poster symbol on all five reels, the max win is awarded, and the HIGH-ROLLER FEATURESPINS™ will end.

If four shots hit Wanted Poster symbols and one hits a Dead symbol, the Lucky Shot is triggered and the sixth shot is fired onto the reel where the shot missed the Wanted Poster. Shots cannot hit the same position twice.

The maximum achievable win in this game is {maximumWinMultiplier} times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. MAX WIN

Maximum win: {maximumWinMultiplier} times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when winning combinations form, which activate the Row Cascades feature, which allows Poker Chips to drop on the grid and unlock the Multiplier Chambers above the reels, which then apply their multiplier values to all the wins over subsequent spins. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
