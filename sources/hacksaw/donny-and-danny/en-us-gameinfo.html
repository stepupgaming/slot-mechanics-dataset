FEATURES

LOOTLINES

A LootLine is formed when a winning payline contains at least 3 Donny symbols, or a combination of at least 3 symbols made up of Donny and Danny. In a winning LootLine, each Donny symbol will select a random value from the Cash Board, located outside the grid. Each selected value is added to a Donny symbol. The values are added from left to right, top to bottom, and multiplied by the current bet amount to determine the awarded win. The Cash Board values are based on the following bet Multipliers: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 150x, 250x, 500x, 1000x, 5000x, 12 500x.

DOLLAR-REELS

When a Danny symbol lands, it will expand into a Dollar-Reel if the Danny symbol is part of a LootLine win, or the expanded Dollar-Reel is part of a LootLine win.

Danny symbols only expand upward, to the top of the grid, after all regular wins are collected. Once expanded, the Dollar-Reel and every position it covers can be part of a LootLine. Dollar-Reels will reveal a Multiplier value on every position that the Dollar-Reel covers, positions can have different Multiplier values. Dollar-Reel Multipliers can have the following Multiplying values: x2, x3, x4, x5, x6, x7, x8, x9, x10.

When there is a winning LootLine of Donny symbols and a Dollar-Reel on the grid, the payout of the LootLine will be determined by the values of the symbols that are a part of the win. The values will be applied from left to right, the resulting value is then multiplied by your bet to determine the cash prize. Dollar-Reel Multiplying Multipliers that come first on a winning payline will be treated as Additive Multipliers.

For example, a winning LootLine that consists of 1 Dollar-Reel symbol, 1 Donny symbol and 1 Dollar-Reel symbol with the values x3, 15x and x2 will have a final payout of 3+15*2=36x.

Only 1 Danny symbol can land on a reel at a time. A Dollar-Reel cannot expand over a Donny symbol.

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
