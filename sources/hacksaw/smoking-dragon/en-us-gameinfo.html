ABOUT THE GAME

Awakened from his long slumber, the Smoking Dragon has taken back the skies. With his pipe clutched between his claws, he blows clouds that decide whether the sun will rise or the night will linger a little longer. From dusk till dawn, the world bends to his will.

Below, his treasure gleams—piles of gold, glittering jewels, and the rare golden dragon eggs. He spends his days counting, polishing, and admiring every piece—not because he has to, but because he loves it. So seeker, think you can tame him? Forge an unbreakable bond and celebrate the shared riches in this 5-reel, 4-row game with Ways wins and a max win of 10 000 times your bet, achievable in all game modes.

FEATURES

ROW CASCADE

When a winning combination occurs, all symbols on the bottom row are removed from the grid, and all symbols move down one row. New symbols then drop in to fill the empty spaces on the top row.

A new Row Cascade sequence is triggered when the same win is present on the grid, the same win continues to grow, or new wins are created. When no more wins are present, the Row Cascade will end.

WILD SYMBOLS

When a Wild or Epic Wild symbol lands, it can be only Wild or also have one of the following Additive Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x.

When a Wild or Epic Wild symbol with a Multiplier participates in a winning combination, the value of the win is multiplied by the Multiplier value.

When an Epic Wild symbol is on the grid during a Row Cascade, the Multiplier will double its value on each Row Cascade sequence the symbol is a part of.

If more than one Wild and/or Epic Wild symbols are part of the same winning combination, their multiplier values are added together before being applied to the win.

DRAGON EGGS

When 3 Dragon Egg symbols land on the grid at the same time, they activate and an instant cash prize of 500x is awarded. After the awarded win, the Dragon Egg symbols will become inactive until the Cascades remove them from the grid. No more than 3 active Dragon Eggs can be on the grid at the same time.

BONUS GAME - PUFF OF LUCK

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game's mechanics with an increased chance of landing Wild, Epic Wild, or Dragon Egg symbols!

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols award +2 free spins,

3 FS symbols award +4 free spins.

BONUS GAME - SMOLDERING RICHES

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the PUFF OF LUCK bonus. In addition, a Multiplier Bar is located outside the grid, which indicates the lowest possible Multiplier value of all Wild and Epic Wild symbols that land on the grid. The Multiplier Bar starts with a minimum value of 1x. Each time 10 Row Cascades have been accumulated, the Multiplier Bar will increase by one level (up to the max level). The possible values on each level of the Multiplier Bar are 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 25x.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus awards extra free spins:

2 FS symbols award +2 free spins,

3 FS symbols award +4 free spins.

EPIC BONUS - FIRE AND FORTUNE

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 5 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the mechanics of the SMOLDERING RICHES bonus. In addition, it features one guaranteed win and a Wild and/or Epic Wild on every spin, with the progressive Multiplier Bar starting at 5x.

FS symbols cannot land during this bonus.

WAYS TO WIN

The specified requirements for a winning combination in the base game are also used during bonus features or free spins play unless otherwise stated. Each wager is eligible for all winning combinations unless otherwise stated.

You win if matching symbols appear on adjacent reels from left to right, starting with the leftmost reel. Each winning combination of matching symbols creates a winning way. The number of possible ways in this game is 1024. Please consult the paytable for the number of each symbol required to win.

After every win the winning symbols are removed and new ones drop in. This continues until there are no more new wins on the reels.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 10 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when winning combinations are formed which remove the bottom row allowing new symbols to drop in filling the empty spaces on the top row creating new Row Cascade sequences until the max win of 10 000x is reached. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
