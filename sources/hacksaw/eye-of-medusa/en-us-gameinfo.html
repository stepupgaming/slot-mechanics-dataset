ABOUT THE GAME

Perseus, sword in hand, shield gleaming like polished bronze, stepped into the shadowy lair of the dreaded Gorgon. Medusa, once a beauty favored by the gods, had been cursed, her flowing hair now twisted into slithering snakes. Every step was a petrifying risk, for to meet her eyes was to be sealed in stone forever.

Now, Eye of Medusa invites you to embark on this mythic quest! Join this cascading game with ways-wins, a 5-reel, 5-row grid, and a chance to win up to 10 000 times your bet across all game modes. Will you survive the Gorgon’s gaze—or will you freeze in fear?

When a winning combination occurs, all regular paying symbols of the type that were part of the winning combination are removed from the grid, and new ones drop down to fill the empty spaces.

MEDUSA SYMBOLS

Medusa symbols act as a Wild and substitute for all symbols in the paytable. Medusa symbols can be Silver or Gold.

When a win with low-paying symbols occurs while a Medusa symbol is on the grid, the Medusa symbol will drop to the bottom of the grid and stay until the end of the spin. All other non-Petrified symbols are removed and new ones will drop down to fill the empty spaces. This continues until no further wins occur.

PETRIFIED SYMBOLS

When a win with high-paying symbols occurs while a Medusa symbol is on the grid, those symbols are Petrified and drop to the bottom of the grid along with the Medusa symbol. The Petrified and Medusa symbols remain at the bottom while all other symbols are removed and new ones will drop down to fill the empty spaces. If additional wins occur with high-paying symbols, those symbols will also be added to the bottom. This continues until no further wins occur.

At the end of the spin, all Petrified symbols reveal an Adding Multiplier from the Bronze, Silver, or Gold values below, depending on the high-paying symbols in the win.

Bronze: 0.2x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x

Silver: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x

Gold: 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x, 500x

After the Petrified Adding Multipliers are revealed, the Medusa symbol displays its own Multiplier. The Silver Medusa symbol shows a multiplier value of x1, x2, x3, or x4, while the Gold Medusa symbol shows x5, x6, x7, x8, x9, x10, or x20. The final win is determined by adding the Petrified values and multiplying the total by the Medusa Multiplier, and then multiplying this total with your bet.

When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account.
