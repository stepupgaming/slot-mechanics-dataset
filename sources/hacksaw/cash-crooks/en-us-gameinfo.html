FEATURES

VAULT DIALS

When a Vault symbol lands on the grid, it activates after all regular wins have been handled. Once the Vault symbol has activated, a Vault Dial will appear in the same position.

The Vault Dial spins and lands on 1 of 12 possible Multipliers. If it lands on a MAX WIN, it is instantly awarded. If a Multiplier is awarded, the value will be added to a Piggy Bank outside the grid. The Multipliers will either be Additive Multipliers with possible values of 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 250x. Or Multiplicative Multipliers with possible values of x2, x3, x4, x5, x10, x20.

If multiple Vault Dials are active on the grid during the same spin, their Multiplier values are combined from left to right. If a Multiplicative Multiplier is active on the first reel, it will act as an Additive Multiplier in relation to the next Vault Dial Multiplier. Once all the Vault Dials have activated, the cash prize value in the Piggy Bank will be paid as a multiple of your bet. Vault symbols can only land on reels 1, 3, and 5. Only 1 Vault symbol can land per reel.

BONUS GAMBLE

When you enter the GRAB'N GO and the FIERCE & FUR-IOUS bonus games, you have the choice to either continue to the bonus game or Gamble. If you choose to Gamble, you will be presented with a Deluxe Dial that will spin and land on 1 of 12 outcomes.

The Deluxe Dial can land on 5, 8, 12, 16, or 20 free spins or a Bonus Upgrade. This could result in a gain or loss of free spins compared to the initial award. The Bonus Level Upgrade will award immediate entry into the next bonus game. In GRAB'N GO the upgraded level will be FIERCE & FUR-IOUS. In FIERCE & FUR-IOUS the upgraded level will be VAULT TO VICTORY. A downgrade to a lower bonus game is not possible.

You may Gamble once per bonus and once after a Bonus Upgrade.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: {maximumWinMultiplier} times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when landing multiple Vault symbols and activating the Vault Dials, revealing a combined multiplier value of 10 000x, which is awarded as a final cash prize after all regular wins have been handled. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player's account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds that require additional player input to determine the outcome of the game will remain unfinished for {payoutPeriod}, after which a default choice will automatically be made in order to complete the round and credit any winnings to the player's account. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
