FEATURES

JOLT FRAMES

A Jolt Frame can land on a low-paying, high-paying or Wild symbol at the start of a spin and will activate after all wins have been handled on that spin. When a Jolt Frame activates, it spreads out in straight lines and turns all symbols along the lines into the same symbol type as the symbol the Jolt Frame landed on.

A Jolt Frame can land and spread in two or four directions if there is space, vertically and horizontally, depending on the Jolt Frame that lands.

A vertical Jolt Frame will spread from the symbol it landed on upwards and downwards to the edge of the grid.

A horizontal Jolt Frame will spread from the symbol it landed on to the right and left edges of the grid.

A four-way Jolt Frame will spread from the symbol it landed on upward, downward, right, and left to the edge of the grid.

When the Jolt Frame has finished spreading, the wins will be awarded from the new Clusters that are formed. If a Jolt Frame spreads over a Wild symbol, an Additive 2x Multiplier is added to that Wild symbol. The Multiplier is then added to the Jolt Frame win. The Wild Multiplier will increase by 2x every time it is hit by a Jolt Frame. If there is more than 1 Wild Multiplier, the Multiplier values will be added and then applied to the entire win. Only 1 Jolt Frame can land at the start of a spin.

CHAIN REACTION

If a Jolt Frame spreads over a low-paying, high-paying, or Wild symbol of the same type as the symbol the Jolt Frame landed on, it triggers a Chain Reaction. A Jolt Frame will then appear on the second symbol, which triggers another spread depending on the Jolt Frame that lands. This Chain Reaction can continue until the grid is full.

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 10 000 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when a Jolt Frame lands on the highest paying symbol and spreads out in straight lines turning all symbols along the lines into the same symbol type triggering a Chain Reaction that continues until the grid is full and the maximum win of 10 000x is reached. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
