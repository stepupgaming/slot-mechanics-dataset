FEATURES

EXPANDED BLOODY WILDS

When a Blood symbol lands, it expands into a Bloody Wild if the expanded symbol is a part of at least 1 winning combination once expanded.

Bloody Wilds expand downward, from the top of the grid to the landed Blood symbol. Blood symbols that land on the top row will still be counted as expanded Bloody Wilds if part of a win. Expanded Bloody Wilds count as Wild on every position the expanded symbol covers. Only 1 Blood symbol can land on a reel at a time.

When a Bloody Wild expands, it can just be Wild, or it can reveal a Multiplier, which is applied to the entire Bloody Wild. When an expanded Bloody Wild participates in a winning combination, the value of the win is multiplied by the shown value. If a winning combination includes more than 1 expanded Bloody Wild with a Multiplier, the Multiplier values will be added together and then applied to the win. Expanded Bloody Wilds can have the following Adding Multiplier values: Wild, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 500x.

WILD BAT SYMBOLS

When a Wild Bat symbol lands, it will have one of the following Adding Multiplier values: Wild, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 500x. When a Wild Bat symbol participates in a winning combination, the value of the win is combined with the Wild Bat’s Multiplier value. If more than 1 Wild Bat symbol is part of the same winning combination, their values are added together before being applied to the win. If a winning combination includes an expanded Bloody Wild and a Wild Bat symbol, the two Multiplier values will be added together and then applied to the wins.

When an Epic Wild Bat symbol lands, it will have one of the following Adding Multiplier values: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 10x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 500x. When an Epic Wild Bat symbol lands on the grid, it will activate and spread its value to all expanded Bloody Wilds and other Wild Bat symbols on the grid. Only 1 Epic Wild Bat symbol can land per spin.

BONUS GAME - REST IN PIECES

Activate this bonus with 10 free spins by landing 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time during the base game.

This bonus game keeps the base game's mechanics. Additionally, a progressive Symbol Counter is displayed outside the grid, with 5 sections linked to each of the 5 low-paying symbols. Each time that low-paying symbol creates a win, one section fills up on the corresponding Symbol Counter. When all 5 sections are full, it activates on the next free spin, replacing all corresponding low-paying symbols with Wild Bat symbols for the rest of the bonus! The minimum Multiplier value in this bonus is 2x.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time in the bonus awards extra free spins:

MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 12 500 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

MAX WIN

Maximum win: 12 500 times the total stake. Probability of getting the maximum win: 1:{oddsOfMaxWin}. The maximum win occurs when the multiplier values of the Epic Wild Bat, Expanded Bloody Wilds or the Wild Bat are added together and multiplied by the current bet until reaching the maximum win of 12 500x. The maximum win is the sum of all winning combinations when the reels are stationary, taking into account further winning opportunities such as bonus rounds and free spins. The maximum win can be achieved in the base game but is only possible with a lower probability, if bonus rounds are excluded.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
