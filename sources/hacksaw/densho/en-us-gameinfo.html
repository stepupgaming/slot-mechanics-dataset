ABOUT THE GAME

Welcome to the enchanting world of Densho, where beautiful depictions of the natural world will accompany you on your mission to shape your legacy. Densho is a 5-reel, 4-row paylines game featuring a wild Multiplier Reel mechanic and an exciting bonus game with progressively increasing multiplier values for the Multiplier Reels! The max win in this game is 10,000 times your bet! The max win can be reached in the base game, Free Spins and Super Free Spins game modes.

FEATURES

WILD MULTIPLIER REELS

When a Densho symbol lands, it expands into a Multiplier Reel if the expanded reel would be part of at least one winning combination once expanded.

Multiplier Reels are wild and substitute for all other paying symbols, and apply a multiplier to each winning combination they are part of. If a winning combination includes more than one Multiplier Reel, their multipliers are added together before being applied to the win. The multiplier value on each Multiplier Reel is determined by the quality of the Densho symbol that created it:

The possible multiplier values from the Common Densho symbol are: 2x - 10x

The possible multiplier values from the Rare Densho symbol are: 5x - 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x

The possible multiplier values from the Legendary Densho symbol are: 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x

Each reel can land only one Densho symbol at once.

FREE SPINS

Get 3 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate this bonus game with 10 free spins.

In this bonus game there are progression trackers above each reel. The level of each progression tracker indicates the lowest possible quality of Densho symbol that can land on that reel.

Land Upgrade symbols to level up the progression tracker of the corresponding reel! As the minimum quality of Densho symbols increase, so do the minimum multiplier values on the Multiplier Reels.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time during the bonus game awards additional free spins:

2 FS symbols awards 2 extra spins;

3 FS symbols awards 4 extra spins.

SUPER FREE SPINS

Get 4 FS scatter symbols at the same time in the base game to activate this bonus game with 10 free spins.

This bonus game follows the rules for Free Spins above. In this version of the bonus game, progression trackers start at level 2.

Landing additional FS symbols at the same time during the bonus game awards additional free spins:

2 FS symbols awards 2 extra spins;

3 FS symbols awards 4 extra spins.

Winnings and winning combinations are paid according to the paytable. MAX WIN

The maximum achievable win in this game is 10 000 times your bet. The maximum win may be achieved by triggering specific combinations and/or features. When this amount is reached the game will display a message indicating the maximum win has been reached. The current round will then immediately end and your winnings will be awarded. If this occurs during free spins and/or other bonus feature(s), the game will return to the main screen and any remaining free spins and/or other bonus feature(s) will be discarded.

FREE SPINS

In Free spins the total win for each spin is shown in the WIN field while the accumulating win for all free spins is shown in the TOTAL WIN field next to the number of remaining spins.

INTERRUPTED GAME

In the event of disconnection, a previously started round that has been interrupted may be resumed immediately by starting the game again. The results of a completed round (where display of the visual result was interrupted) will only be re-displayed within the game for winning rounds, but all game rounds may be reviewed in History. Any amount wagered on an unfinished game will remain paused until you either complete the game or the game is void as a result of account inactivity or system maintenance. When an unfinished game is void, your bet will be refunded to your account. Any awards earned on an unfinished round will not be credited unless you resume and complete the game. Winnings on unfinished game rounds where the player cannot further influence the outcome of the game will be automatically credited to the player’s account after {payoutPeriod}. Game rounds will never be affected by unforeseen external errors due to hardware, bandwidth, network errors or similar. Game rounds will be either stored and completed by the player at a later time or they will be closed and the wager will be refunded to the player. If an incomplete game round has not been resumed within {rollbackPeriod}, the round will be rolled back and the wager will be refunded.
