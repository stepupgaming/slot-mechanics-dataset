GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)

Gemstones reveal multipliers in Jewel Rush™.

Played across 7×7 reels, various gemstones glisten across the reels with five adjacent
matching symbols or more needed to create a winning cluster. Once landed, these wins
are removed from play to trigger a cascade of new symbols to fall from the top of the
game board.

Whenever a cluster of symbols is created, there is the chance to be awarded a wild
multiplier that can have its multiplier increased for every additional win it is a part of.
Three scatters are needed to unlock 10 free spins, where wild multiplier values are tripled
for each win they are a part of to create lucrative prizes
Game Information

Screen size:

   •   Screen size 7x7
   •   Bet multiplier x20
   •   Symbols pay if connected horizontally or vertically


8 Symbols:

   •   7 Regular symbols
   •   1 Scatter
Game Information

Tumbling:
   •   After a win, the symbols that have been part of the win will disappear and the symbols above will fall down and
       take their place.
   •   The game will then play this new matrix of symbols as a new game with no cost to the player (tumbling respin).
       This process may be repeated until there is no win.
   •   [SCATTER] symbols do not tumble and FG will be calculated at the end of the spin (after all tumblings )



Free Game:
   •   3 or more scatters at base game gives 10 FS
   •   During FS 3 or more scatter will give additional FS. For 3: +10, for 4: +11, for 5: +12 etc.
   •   In FS, each block involved in a win has a chance to carry a MULT.
   •   After the spin finishes, the win of the block is multiplied by the total value of the multiplier landed within that
       block.
   •   Landing more than one Multiplier symbol will compound the multiplier.
   •   Max multiplier is 256
