Game Overview
Rocket Blast Megaways™
Game Description (for Players)
Blast off into space for a fun-filled journey with Rocket Blast Megaways™.

Aliens and colourful gems appear across six ever-changing reels, alongside
Wilds which can substitute for all symbols except Scatters, appearing on every
reel aside from the first. Scatters will trigger the free spins round whenever four
or more land on any spin.

The tumble feature sees winning combinations removed from play, with the
remaining symbols falling to the bottom of the screen and empty positions
replaced with new ones. A special rocket feature also makes an appearance,
transforming any symbols it lands on into Wilds. These symbols are then locked
onto the reels, nudging down one position after each tumble and adding
further Wilds to the top of the grid until the spin has finished which can
accelerate the winning potential of the slot.

Game Information
 Re-spins with tumbling:
     •   Triggered by any winning combination.

     •   All symbols in winning combinations will disappear.

     •   Empty spaces are filled as symbols tumble in sequence from above.

     •   Can be triggered during base game and free game.


 Rocket symbol:
     •   Doesn’t pay.

     •   Appears on both base game and free game.

     •   Appears at reels 2-6.

     •   Doesn’t appear during tumble.

     •   Transforms itself and all symbols in the reel below it in the wilds.
