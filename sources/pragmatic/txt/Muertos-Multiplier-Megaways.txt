                                                TUMBLE FEATURE
  The TUMBLE FEATURE means that after every spin, winning combinations are paid and all winning symbols
disappear. The remaining symbols fall to the bottom of the screen and the empty positions are replaced with new
    symbols coming from above. New symbols are of the same size as the initial spin result, before tumbles.
            Tumbling will continue until no more winning combinations appear as a result of a tumble.
   All wins are added to the player's balance after all the tumbles resulted from a base spin have been played.

                                                WILD MULTIPLIER
Whenever a WILD symbol hits it has a random multiplier of x2 or x3. The multiplier applies to the win of the current
 tumble and all subsequent tumbles and it gets collected in a meter. Whenever more WILDS hit during the tumble
                     sequence the collected value is multiplied by the value of the new ones.
                    The total multiplier value is reset to x1 at the end of all tumbles in a spin.

                                               FREE SPINS RULES
                                     This is the BONUS symbol. It appears on all reels.
                                     Hit 3 or more BONUS symbols anywhere on the reels to
                                     trigger the FREE SPINS ROUND.
                        Before the round starts, a random number of free spins is selected.
                    6x BONUS symbols awards a random value from 8, 10, 12 or 14 free spins
                    5x BONUS symbols awards a random value from 7, 8, 10, 12 or 14 free spins
                    4x BONUS symbols awards a random value from 6, 7, 8, 10, 12 or 14 free spins
                    3x BONUS symbols awards a random value from 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 12 or 14 free spins
            After a random number of spins is selected, if the number is below 14, you can gamble to win
                more free spins or collect the current number of spins at any time and start the round.
      If you gamble, a wheel spins and stop on either a win or a lose position. If it stops on a win the number
       of free spins won increases by 1. If it stops on a lose all free spins are lost and the round ends. It can
                                         only be gambled up to 14 free spins.
                           When gambling from 5 to 6 free spins the chance to win is 64.43%.
                           When gambling from 6 to 7 free spins the chance to win is 68.37%.
                           When gambling from 7 to 8 free spins the chance to win is 72.05%.
                           When gambling from 8 to 9 free spins the chance to win is 75.39%.
                           When gambling from 9 to 10 free spins the chance to win is 78.27%.
                           When gambling from 10 to 11 free spins the chance to win is 80.80%.
                           When gambling from 11 to 12 free spins the chance to win is 82.99%.
                           When gambling from 12 to 13 free spins the chance to win is 84.83%.
                           When gambling from 13 to 14 free spins the chance to win is 86.41%.
  During the free spins round WILD symbols hit with a random multiplier of x2 or x3. Every time a WILD symbol
   hits the multiplier is used for the win of the current tumble and it gets collected in a meter. Every time other
                             WILD symbols hit, they will multiply the value in the meter.
  Every tumble that happens during the FREE SPINS round also increases the multiplier in the meter by x1. The
                           meter starts from x1 at the beginning of the FREE SPINS round.
              The total multiplier in the meter is kept and used for every win until the end of the round.
   Hit 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 more BONUS symbols during the feature to win 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 more free spins respectively.
                               There are no limits to the number of possible retriggers.
                                 Special reels are in play during FREE SPINS feature.
