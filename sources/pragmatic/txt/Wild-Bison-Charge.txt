Game Description (for Players)



Almighty bison stampede and award wins in Wild Bison Charge™!

A herd of bison roam across the Grand Canyon in this 5×5 reel slot, featuring a variety of wild life species saturating the
board. At least six Eagles, wolves, bears, coyotes and bison symbols must be present anywhere on the reels to award a
win.

Three scatter symbols trigger the bonus round where sticky wild multipliers remain on the screen once landed
throughout the nine free spins. Players can have up to 25 multipliers on the screen at one time to trigger a maximum
win of 12,000x in this highly volatile slot!
Game Information
Screen size:

   •   Screen size 5x5
   •   20 Bet multiplier is x20



11 Symbols:

   •   9 Regular symbols
   •   1 Wild symbol - doesn’t pay
   •   1 Scatter: doesn’t pay, 3 or more symbols triggers Free Spins, 2 or more symbols on Free Game add extra Free Spins
Game Information
Base game:

   •   Symbols pay anywhere on the screen expect Scatter
   •   The total number of the same symbol on the screen at the end of a spin determines the value of the win


Free Spins:


   •   3 or more Free spins symbols triggeres Free Spins
        •   3 FG triggeres 9 Free Spins
        •   4 FG triggeres 12 Free Spins
        •   5 FG triggeres 15 Free Spins
   •   During free game the wilds appeared on window will be Sticky
   •   Every wild will award 2x or 3x or 5x Multiplier in both Base Game and Free Game
