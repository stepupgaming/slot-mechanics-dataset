                                                 TUMBLE FEATURE
   The TUMBLE FEATURE means that after every spin, winning combinations are paid and all winning symbols
 disappear. The remaining symbols fall to the bottom of the screen and the empty positions are replaced with new
     symbols coming from above. New symbols are of the same size as the initial spin result, before tumbles.
             Tumbling will continue until no more winning combinations appear as a result of a tumble.
    All wins are added to the player's balance after all the tumbles resulted from a base spin have been played.

                                   6x BONUS symbols award 22 free spins.
                                   5x BONUS symbols award 17 free spins
                                   4x BONUS symbols award 12 free spins.
    During the free spins round WILD symbols hit with a random multiplier of 2x, 3x or 5x. Every time a WILD is
involved in a winning combination it's multiplier is accumulated in a tumble multiplier indicator. All WILD multipliers
         that hit during a tumble sequence multiply between each other. At the end of the tumble sequence
                      the accumulated multiplier applies to the total win of that tumble sequence.
                       Hit 3 or more BONUS symbols during the feature to win 5 more free spins.
                                 There are no limits to the number of possible retriggers.
                                  Special reels are in play during FREE SPINS feature.


                                                    ANTE BET
          The player has the option to select the bet multiplier. Depending on the selected bet, the game
                                    behaves differently. The possible values are:
                                           Bet multiplier 20x - Normal Play
   Bet multiplier 25x - The chance to win FREE SPINS naturally is doubled, more BONUS symbols are present on
                 the reels but symbol paytable is not affected and they pay as for 20x bet multiplier.

The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet. If the total win of a FREE SPINS ROUND reaches 5,000x the round
                 immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited


                                                 BUY FREE SPINS
 The FREE SPINS FEATURE can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
              When the 25x ante bet feature is enabled, the BUY FREE SPINS feature is disabled.
            When buying the FREE SPINS feature 4, 5 or 6 BONUS symbols hit randomly awarding
                                     12, 17 or 22 free spins respectively.
