GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description
What riches are hidden away in the safe? Find out in Reel Banks™.

Played across a 5×4 layout, the slot takes players into the heart of a bank with
symbols depicting money, gold and lucky sevens, which must form matching
combinations across the title’s 25 paylines to award a win.

Players are assisted in unlocking these wins with wilds that substitute all other
symbols in game, except for scatters. Additionally, collect symbols are there to
gather instant cash prizes on the reels.

Free spins are awarded when there is at least one bonus coin present on each reel.
Here, the collect mechanic is removed and updated with new in-game modifiers
that can increase coin values, award extra spins, multiply wins, and collect all bank
values, providing players with huge win potential.

Game Information
Base Game:

• Wins paid by win lines
• Wins paid by collected bonus coins
• Bonus coins can appear on the screen on any reel, each coin can has value in range 1 –
500TB. Bonus coins win is the sum of all coins values and paid only when ‘Collect’ symbol
also present on the screen.

Free Game:

•  Free game triggered when at least one bonus coin symbol present on each reel. For
   each reel appears bank which contains initial value corresponding to the award sum of
   the each reel.
These banks are never empty.
• Wins paid by win lines
• During the free game ‘Collect’ symbol (12) does not appear, only bonus coin symbols.
• The following symbols can appear randomly on the screen (called as modifiers):
     • Bonus coin value 1 – 500 (remains the same 13) (adds value to the corresponding
        reel’s bank)
     • Collect bank => 14 (pays the bank amount from the same reel)
     • Add spins => 15 (adds 3 free spins)
     • Multiply bank => 16 (multiplies reel’s bank on provided amount)
     • Multiply all banks => 17 (multiplies all banks on provided amount)
     • Collect all banks => 18 (pays from all banks)
     • Add to all banks => 19 (adds provided amount to all banks)
