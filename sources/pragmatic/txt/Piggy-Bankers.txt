Game Overview
Piggy Bankers™
Game Description (for Players)
Head to the bank for a fun-filled adventure with Piggy Bankers™.

This 5×4 slot features symbols including banknotes, piggy banks and the
bankers themselves, who act as Wilds and can substitute for all other symbols.
An extra reel is included at the top of the grid, and if a full vertical stack of Wilds
appears under one of the banknotes, their value of up to 100x is awarded.

A Wild respin can be triggered whenever a full stack of Wilds hits, with the full
Piggy Banker Wilds moving to either the left or the right of the reels. Landing a
free spins banknote or two stacked Wilds on one spin unlocks a round of free
games, where the Wilds can also be multiplied for incredible winning potential!

Game Information
 Main reel game:
     •   Each press of SPIN button will cause each reel to spin to a random position.

     •   The reels are set to 4x5 with 20 winlines (Kraken).

     •   Wins are made for 3 or more of a kind.


 Top Tracker:
     •   The Top Tracker is available in the game and will either show blank symbols or notes.

     •   This will use the same notes from piggy bank riches, with them only collected if the whole

         reel below them is a Wild Piggy.


 Wild symbols:
     •   The Wild symbols are two Piggys, one brown, one green.

     •   If either land in land fully in view (covering the whole reel) it will pay any wins and initiate

         a respin. During the respin it will shift.
Game Information
 Multiple wilds:
     •   Can land in view and shift simultaneously. If two wilds collides in the same reel, the

         money symbol is collected twice.

     •   The feature is only triggered with colliding wilds


 Two Worlds Collide:
     •   Two world collide as two wilds bump into each other, Free Spins are awarded.

     •   Free Spins will play out with 5 spins, with any wild initiating the respin.

     •   Money symbols in the tracker are collected, as per the base game.

     •   Any collisions of two wilds in Free Spins will cause a x2 super stacked wild, award +1 free

         spin and as a result multiply any wins and piggy notes collected above it in the top

         tracker.
