GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)
A taste of the Orient awaits players in Dragon Hero™.
The title is home to symbols depicting fish, toads and more that must form matching
combinations across the slot’s 5×4 reels to award a win. These are joined by
mythical dragons that hold the key to huge wins, with each symbol being able to
award up to three randomly placed wilds across the game board!

Three scatters are needed to award the free spins round with at least six spins being
awarded. However, spinning wheels prior to entering the thrilling bonus can award
even more spins. In the bonus round, dragon symbols are held in place distributing
their wilds continually for each spin, further boosting the big win potential.
Game Information

14 Symbols:

    o   1 Scatter
    o   2 Wilds
           ▪ Super Wild may have levels 1, 2, 3
           ▪ Normal Wild
    o   11 regular


Scatter:

    •   May appear at all reels
    •   Does not pay
    •   3 Scatters trigger Free game at base spin
    •   Do not trigger free spins during free game
    •   Can be collected by Super Wild during free game
Game Information
Wild:

    o   Pays
    o   It substitutes for all symbols except Scatter
    o   Are not present on reels
    o   Can be thrown by Super Wild


Super Wild:

    o   Pays same way as normal Wild
    o   It substitutes for all symbols except Scatter
    o   Appears on reels 2,3,4
    o   Sticky during free game
    o   Always have a level on it (1, 2 or 3)
    o   Super Wild throws Wilds. The number of thrown Wilds corresponds to Super Wild level
    o   Super Wild can throw Wilds to any position except Super Wild positions.
    o   Several Super Wilds can throw Wild to the same position.
    o   Super Wild can level up themselves by collecting Scatters during free game
    o   Super Wild of level 3 cannot collect Scatters
    o   2 Scatters are required to level up Super Wild to the next level
Game Information
Base game:

    o   One reels set is used
    o   Pay for win lines
    o   Super Wilds are not sticky
    o   Super Wilds throw Wilds according to its level


Free Spins Award:

    o   Each scatter triggers 2 bonus wheels:
          o 3 Scatters trigger 6 bonus wheels
          o 4 Scatters trigger 8 bonus wheels
          o 5 Scatters trigger 10 bonus wheels
    o   Each bonus wheel can generate values 1, 2, 3 by weights
    o   The number of Awarded free spins is the sum of the wheels values
          o Minimum possible number of FS is 6
          o Maximum possible number of FS is 30
    o   Free spins are awarded during Base game only and before Wild throw feature
Game Information
Free game:

    o   Free game is triggered by 3 or more Scatters during Base game
    o   Free game is triggered before Super Wilds throw Wilds
    o   Cannot trigger additional free spins during free game
    o   3 reels sets are used


Buy feature:

    o   Price of buy feature is 100 x Total bet
    o   Game mechanics is the same as for regular game
    o   Has weight tables and reels sets different from regular game
