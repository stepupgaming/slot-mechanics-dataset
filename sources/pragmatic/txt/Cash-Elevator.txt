                                         DEAD LOVERS FREE SPINS
                               Whenever a full 1x3 WILD symbol hits on reel 2 or reel 4, the
                                           DEAD LOVERS free spins is awarded
                            Before the round starts pick 1 out of 3 options to get a number of
                                             free spins from 6 to 12 randomly.
                            If the round started by hitting a 1x3 WILD symbol on both reels 2
                            and 4, in the pick round you can get 12 to 24 free spins randomly.



                         When the round starts, the game moves to a random floor from 1 to 12.
                                  The DEAD LOVERS free spins has a progressive feature.
 The round starts with full 1x3 WILD symbols present on the screen on reels 1 and 5. They remain on the screen until
   the end of the round and move to different reels at different free spins depending on what is hit during the round.
After every free spin with a win containing the lowest symbol of the current floor, the 1x3 Wild symbols move one reel
 towards each other, first to reels 2 and 4, then meeting in reel 3, where they merge into a single symbol and award 5
more free spins. More wins with the lowest symbol of the current floor splits them again, moving them back to reels 2
    and 4, then to 1 and 5, after which they start to move towards each other again. This moving pattern can happen
                               indefinetely until all free spins are played and the round ends.
             After the round ends the game moves back to the floor where it was before free spins started.
                                        Special reels are in play during this feature.
                                     HOLD AND SPIN FEATURE
        Whenever the game moves to floor 13 by any means, the HOLD AND SPIN feature is triggered.
                  Before the round starts, the game moves to a random floor from 1 to 12.
       These are MONEY symbols. During the HOLD AND SPIN feature only MONEY symbols and blank
                                     spaces are present on the reels.

                    MONEY symbol with random value taken from a pre-defined set at every respin.

                    MONEY symbol with a random multiplier that applies to the total win of the round.
                    Possible values are: 2x, 3x, 5x and 10x. If more than one multiplier are present in
                    the same respin round, the values multiply between themselves.
                    MONEY symbol with floor value. A fixed amount depending on the
                    floor the feature is played on.

    Before the round starts, pick one out of 3 options to determine a random number of MONEY symbols to
                start the round with, from 3 to 11, distributed on random positions on the screen.
   The round starts with 4 respins. All MONEY symbols that hit after each respin, remain on the screen until
    the end of the round. Every time at least 1 MONEY symbol hits, the number of respins is reset back to 4.
   The round continues until the all respins are finished or all positions on the screen are filled with MONEY
                                                      symbols.
   After the round is complete the values of all MONEY symbols are added together, multipliers are applied
                                        and the game pays the total amount
                       After the round ends, the game moves to a random floor from 1 to 12.




                                        BASEMENT FEATURE
             When the game moves by any means to a floor lower than the 1st, it is automatically
                               moved to a random floor from 1st to 12th.
