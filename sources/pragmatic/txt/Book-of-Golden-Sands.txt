                                                FREE SPINS RULES
            Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbols anywhere on the screen to trigger the FREE SPINS feature.
                                          The round starts with 10 free spins.
 Before the round starts, a random symbol out of all paying symbols is selected as the special expanding symbol.
During the round, after the payout of regular symbols, the special symbol expand vertically to cover all the positions
    on the reel. Then, on top of each reel where it expanded, one instance of that symbol is collected at the top.
       Future expansions in the same round on reels that have symbols collected on top of them will expand
                           as 3 plus the number of symbols on top, up to a maximum of 10.
     The special expanding symbol pays according to its paytable on all reels according to the number of ways
             given by the number of expanded symbols on each reel, even on non-adjacent positions.
     Expansion is triggered only if at least 3 special symbols are present at the end of a spin on different reels.
    Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbols during the round to get 10 more free spins with the current special symbol.
                             There is no limit to the number of possible retriggers.
                                         Special reels are in play during the feature.




                                                          MAX WIN
The maximum win amount is limited to 10,000x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a FREE SPINS
  round reaches 10,000x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited
