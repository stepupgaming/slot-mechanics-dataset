GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)
Wilds countdown in Gods of Giza™

Varying coloured jewels are joined by Horus and Anubis symbols in this Egyptian-themed
5×5 slot, and they must form an adjacent cluster of three or more symbols to award a win.
Winning symbols are removed from play with a cascade of new characters filling their
position.

Each win also creates a wild allowing for subsequent wins to be created more easily. Wilds
can also have a countdown attached to them which reduces by one for each win it is a
part of before being removed from play. At least four scatters are needed to award 15 free
spins, more scatters unlock additional spins with players being able to be awarded up to
120 spins in total!
Game Information
Screen size:

   •   Screen size 5x5.
   •   10 win lines.
   •   Bet multiplier is x20.

Lines pays:

   •   Symbols pay from Left to Right on adjacent reels starting from any reel or from Top to Bottom on adjacent
       rows starting from any row.
   •   Multiple win combinations may pay per each line:
         • If the line contains two overlapping combinations, both will pay.
         • If the wild combination is included in some longer combination, both will pay.


8 Symbols:

   •   6 Regular symbols
   •   1 Scatter symbol
   •   1 Wild symbol
Game Information
Scatter:

   •    Doesn’t pay
   •    Appears in the base and free game.
   •    Appears on all reels
   •    It is possible to get multiple scatters on the same reel because of tumbling.
   •    Triggers Free Game


Wild:
   •    Pays
   •    It substitutes for all symbols except Scatters
   •    Appears in the base and free game.
   •    Wild is not naturally present on reels, but appears on all reels
   •    Has multipliers (x1, x2, x3, x5, x7, x10)
   •    If a wild is involved in a win line, the win will be multiplied on the multiplier.
   •    If more than 1 wild is part of winning combination, their multipliers will be multiplied together
Game Information
Base Game:

   •   After a win, the symbols ( except Wild ) that have been part of the win will disappear and the symbols above will fall
       down and take their place. The game will then play this new matrix of symbols as a new game with no cost to the
       player. This process may be repeated until there is no win.
   •   Before the new symbols fall in, the winning combination generates one Wild.
   •   Wild will appear on one of the win positions, which are not a part of win line with existing wilds. If there are no such
       positions, wild won’t be generated.
   •   The new wild don’t affect the current win and will play after new symbols fall in.
   •   Wilds can contribute to wins on 3 separate cascades and then they are removed after the third usage in base game.
   •   Wild has a random multiplier and keeps its multiplier for its entire lifespan.
   •   If the base spin doesn’t have any win, there is a chance to activate a random wild replacement:
   •   2-5 Wilds will be randomly placed on non scatter positions with random multipliers assigned to them.
   •   After a regular win evaluation, the cascading will play as usual
   •   Random wild replacement can't happen during cascading
Game Information
Free Game:

   •   3 or more Scatter symbols trigger free spins. Free spins are only triggered after cascades have finished.
   •   3x Scatter in base game trigger 10 free spins, each additional Scatter contributes 5 spins for the Free spins sessions:
   •   3 scatter - 10 spins
   •   4 scatter - 15 spins
   •   5 scatter - 20 spins
   •   …
   •   25 scatters – 120 spins
   •   Free spins can be re-triggered with 3 or more scatters with a fixed amount of 5 spins added to the free spins.
   •   The Wilds can contribute to wins on 5 separate cascades and then they are removed after the fifth usage in free
       spins.
   •   Random wild replacement for non winning spin is active in Free spins
