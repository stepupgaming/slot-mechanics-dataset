                                                 GAME RULES
                All symbols pay from left to right on adjacent reels starting from the leftmost reel.
            The game has two profiles that can be chosen by the player, OLYMPUS and HADES modes.
           Depending on the profile chosen the look of the game changes to match the respective profile.
               When playing in OLYMPUS mode the chance to hit the FREE SPINS feature is higher
                               but the average win of FREE SPINS rounds is lower.
                 When playing in HADES mode the chance to hit the FREE SPINS feature is lower
                              but the average win of FREE SPINS rounds is higher.

                                                  FREE SPINS




          These are SCATTER symbols. They appear only on reels 1, 3 and 5 and only in the base game.
                  Hit 3 SCATTER symbols to trigger the FREE SPINS feature and get 10 free spins.
During the round any expanding WILD symbol that hits will expand, get a random multiplier from the same possible
       values as in the base game and will remain on the screen until the end of the round, for all free spins.
                                       The feature cannot be retriggered.
                                   Special reels are in play during the feature.




                                                     MAX WIN
The maximum win amount is limited to 15,000x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a FREE SPINS
  round reaches 15,000x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited




                                               BUY FREE SPINS
                  The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it.
                         When in OLYMPUS mode the following buy options are available:
                          Cost of 75x total bet will award a standard FREE SPINS feature.
          Cost of 300x total bet will award a special FREE SPINS feature in which one expanding WILD is
       guaranteed to hit with a random multiplier and in a random position on the first free spin of the round.
                           When in HADES mode the following buy options are available:
                          Cost of 150x total bet will award a standard FREE SPINS feature.
          Cost of 300x total bet will award a special FREE SPINS feature in which one expanding WILD is
       guaranteed to hit with a random multiplier and in a random position on the first free spin of the round.
                                                   VOLATILITY

 High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher.

           All symbols pay from left to right on selected paylines.
                  Free spins win is added to the payline win.
                     All wins are multiplied by bet per line.
               All values are expressed as actual wins in coins.
 Only the highest win is paid per line irrespective of any external multiplier.
   When winning on multiple paylines, all wins are added to the total win.
  Free spins round win is awarded to the player after the round completes.
Free spins round total win in the history contains the whole win of the cycle.

                  The theoretical RTP of this game when playing in OLYMPUS mode is 96.05%
                    The theoretical RTP of this game when playing in HADES mode is 96.07%
               The RTP of the game when using "BUY FREE SPINS" in OLYMPUS mode is 96.04%
            The RTP of the game when using "BUY SUPER FREE SPINS" in OLYMPUS mode is 96.01%
                 The RTP of the game when using "BUY FREE SPINS" in HADES mode is 96.14%
             The RTP of the game when using "BUY SUPER FREE SPINS" in HADES mode is 96.08%
