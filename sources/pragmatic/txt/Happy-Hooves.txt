Game Description
(for Players)
Stampeding through the stables comes Happy Hooves™ an equine-
inspired slot.

Played across 5×4 reels and 40 paylines Happy Hooves™ has four
groups of two colour-coded symbols each with a wild that substitutes
for other symbols within the group. Three scatter symbols award the
PowerNudge feature which continues till no mystery symbols are in
play.

Each spin increases the win multiplier by 1x with mystery symbols being
able to reveal base game symbols, instant win prizes and a nudge-
up feature awarding extra spins and further increasing the game’s
multiplier. With 5,000x on offer for players, this is a stallion release!
