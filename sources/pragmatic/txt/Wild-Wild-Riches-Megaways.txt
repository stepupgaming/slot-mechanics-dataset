GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)
The luck of the Irish brings good fortune in Wild Wild Riches Megaways™.

Played across six reels, iconic symbols such as harps, clovers, mushrooms and beer return from
the original release with instant cash prizes. These can be awarded for landing wilds on the first
two reels and an instant cash prize on reel three. To enrich the action even further, instant cash
prizes can also come with lucrative jackpot prizes.

The bonus requires wilds to be on reels one and two with a bonus symbol on reel three. This
unlocks a gamble mechanic where players can try their luck to win up to 20 free spins in the
bonus round!
Game Information
14 symbols:

   •   11 Regular symbols
   •   1 Wild symbol, substitute all symbols except Scatter
   •   1 Scatter: doesn’t pay, starts Free Spins when lands on 3d reel with wilds on 1st and 2nd reels.
   •   1 Money Symbol it appears on reels 3,4,5 and 6 – At every spin all Money symbols take a random
       value from a predefined set.


Screen size:

   •   Screen size 6x7
   •   Reel height is variable
   •   Bet multiplier 20
Game Information
Base Game:

•   Each press of SPIN will cause each reel to spin to a random position. Wins are paid for matching 3 or
    more of the same symbols, left to right.
•   The reels are megaways which can spin any size symbols from 2/7 on reels 1 and 2 and a minimum of
    3 to 7 on reels 3, 4, 5 and 6.

Free Spins:

     •   Land a scatter symbol in reel 3 with WILD WILD on reels 1 and 2 triggers Free Spins.
     •   When free spins are won, after wins are paid, a wheel will be presented to spin, which will award
         the player a number of Free Spins from 6-20.
     •   Once wheel stops, player can gamble the entire free spins to the next level of spins or start free
         spins.
     •   Retriggering Free Spins is possible with the WILD/WILD/Bonus trigger. Retriggering gives 6 more
         Free Spins.
