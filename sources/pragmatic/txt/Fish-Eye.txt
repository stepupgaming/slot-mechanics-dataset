GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)
Fishing on the Nile has its perks in Fish Eye™.

This Egyptian-themed fishing game has symbols depicting the Eye of Horus and fish with instant
cash prizes attached and more which must form matching combinations across the title’s 10 pay
lines to award a win.

Four pharaoh symbols award 10 free spins and additional pharaohs collect the fish symbol’s
instant cash prizes thereafter. Additionally, Cleopatra removes the lowest paying fish and awards
one extra free spin with the biggest fish prize netting players a huge 2,000x!
Game Information
13 Symbols:

   •   9 Regular symbols
   •   1 Wild
   •   1 Scatter
   •   1 Money symbol
   •   1 Collect symbol



Screen size:

   •   3x5 – screen size
   •   10 lines, pay left-to-right
Game Information
Base Game:

•   Once 4 or more fish land on a spin, then the cash values are collected based on the multiplier level
    reached on the fish trail.
•   Once the player lands 4 or more fish, the sum of all fish values in view has is multiplied by a multiplier
    depending on the fish amount.


Free Spins:

      o   At the side of the reels are the number of pharaohs landed so far during free spins. For each 4th
          pharaohs, give an extra 10 free spins immediately.
      o   1 woman in view removes the lowest fish value and awards +1 free spin. 2 women in view remove
          the lowest 2 fish values and award +3 free spins
