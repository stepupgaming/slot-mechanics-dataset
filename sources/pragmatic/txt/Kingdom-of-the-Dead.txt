GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)



Another exciting chapter is ready to be unleashed in Kingdom of The Dead™.

Played across 5×3 reels, this Egyptian-themed slot features ancient symbols, including scarabs, the deity of the
underworld, Anubis, and famed Pharaohs as symbols that must form a matching combination across the slot’s 10 pay
lines to award a win.

Three scatters are required to gain entry to the thrilling bonus, where players can choose from one of two free spin
rounds present. The first bonus will select a special symbol to be expanded across all subsequent spins, whereas the
alternate bonus awards a different icon to be the special expanding symbol for every spin!
Game Information
Screen size:

   •   Screen size 5x3
   •   10 win-lines, left to right
   •   Bet multiplier is x10 for regular game
   •   Bet multiplier is x15 with ante bet turned on



10 Symbols:

   •   9 Regular symbols
   •   1 mystery scatter symbol (wild + scatter, named “Book”). Pay both as a scatter and as a wild, substituting regular
       symbols. 3 or more symbols triggers 10 Free Spins
Game Information
Base Game:

   •   Mystery Scatter (Book)
        •   Acts both as Scatter and Wild
        •   Different pay tables for Scatter behavior and Wild behavior
        •   Appears on all reels
        •   3 or more Book symbols trigger Free Game (10 free spins)
   •   Reels set is selected randomly each spin. Two reels don’t have Book symbols.


Free Game:
   •   When Free Game starts player selects type of Mystery Symbol – fixed or changing
   •   Fixed Mystery Symbol is selected once for all Free Game
   •   Changing Mystery Symbol is selected every free spin
   •   Mystery Symbol is selected randomly without player choice
Game Information
Free Game:

•   Expanding Symbol ( Mystery symbol)

     •   Each free spin one symbol is Mystery Symbol
     •   Appears on all reels
     •   Expands to cover the whole reel
     •   Scatter can not be selected for expanding
     •   It pays all 10 lines. Number of symbols before expanding define paytable.
     •   Special symbol should expand after all the other wins not involving this symbol are awarded.
         Expanding symbol does not pay with normal wins, even if x3 exist on a payline. Expanding
         symbol only pays when expanding.
     •   To award a win the special symbols do not have to be consecutive. This means if only three
         of a kind is needed for the specific symbol, if there is a symbol on the first, fourth and a
         symbol on the fifth reel, they expand to cover their respective reels there is still a three of a
         kind awarded on all 10 winlines. The symbol will only expand if a win for it is going to be
         awarded.
     •   If Mystery Symbol will not produce a win it doesn’t expand
     •   Three or more Scatters add 10 additional free spins
