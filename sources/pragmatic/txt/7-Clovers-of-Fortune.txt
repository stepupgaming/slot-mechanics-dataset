                                           CONNECT & COLLECT FEATURE
                        These are the CONNECT & COLLECT symbols. Randomly, they can carry a random money value.
                        Possible values are: 0.4x, 0.6x, 0.8x, 1x, 1.2x, 1.6x, 2x, 2.4x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 8x, 10x, 12x or 50x total
                        bet. It can appear on any position on reels 1 to 5, on non-special positions.
      Hit adjacent CONNECT & COLLECT symbols, from left to right, starting from the leftmost reel, touching each other
     horizontally or vertically, to win the values on CONNECT & COLLECT symbols or until the chain of symbols reaches
               one of the special positions on reel 3 or reel 6 to win the award from the reached special position.
     In the base game and FREE SPINS the special positions on reel 3 and reel 6 the following symbols can hit randomly:
             RESPINS - Can hit only on the special positions on reel 3. Whenever it hits it triggers a respin round. The round
             has 5 respins. During the round, all the CONNECT & COLLECT symbols hit in the initial spin remain in place and
             all other positions on reels 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 respin. Any CONNECT & COLLECT symbols that hit during respins also
             remain in place until there are no more respins left. If all special positions are linked the RESPINS end. After the
             round, the game pays for all connections.
             MONEY VALUE - Whenever it hits it takes a random money value from a pre-defined set. Whenever it is
             reached by a connection chain its value is awarded. Possible values are 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 8x, 10x, 12x, 20x,
             30x, 40x, 50x, 60x, 80x, 100x, 120x or 160x total bet.

             ADD WILDS - Whenever it is reached by a connection chain it will add WILD symbols on random positions
             on reels 2, 3, 4 or 5 .

             FREE SPINS - Whenever it is reached by a connection chain after all other awards from the current spin are
             paid, it will trigger the FREE SPINS round and award 2x total bet. The FREE SPINS symbol always shows a
             number of free spins on it - 7, 10, 15, 20 or 25 randomly.




                           MINOR, MAJOR and GRAND awards - Whenever one of them is reached by a
                          connection chain their values are awarded. MINOR has a value of 20x total bet.
                           MAJOR has a value of 100x total bet. GRAND has a value of 4000x total bet.


                                                          FREE SPINS
                                               This is the SCATTER symbol.
                                               It appears on all reels.
                                               3 or more SCATTER symbols pay 2x total bet.
   The FREE SPINS feature is triggered by collecting a FREE SPINS symbol by reaching it with CONNECT & COLLECT or
                                            by hitting 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols.
  The round starts with the total number of free spins from all the FREE SPINS symbols collected plus 7, 10 or 15 free spins
             from hitting 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols respectively, on the spin that triggered the feature, if any.
            The CONNECT & COLLECT feature is available and functions in the same way as in the base game.
    When more FREE SPINS symbols are reached by a connection chain during the round, more free spins are awarded
                                        equal to the number on the symbols reached.
                                         Special reels are in play during the feature.

                                                     RANDOM AWARDS
                     The GRAND award can be won randomly at the end of any spin in the base game.
        At random base game spins a random number of WILD symbols can be added to random non-special positions.
   At random base game spins a number of CONNECT & COLLECT symbols can be added to random non-special positions.

                                                              MAX WIN
 The maximum win amount is limited to 4,454x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a round reaches 4,454x
                bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited.
                                                      BUY FREE SPINS
       The FREE SPINS feature can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
When buying the FREE SPINS feature the triggering spin is guaranteed to hit enough CONNECT & COLLECT symbols to form a
 chain that reaches the special positions on reel 3 and at least one of the positions on reel 6. For the triggering spins reel 6
                      contains only FREE SPINS symbols with random amounts of free spins on them.
