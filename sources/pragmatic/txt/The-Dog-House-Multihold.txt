GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)
Fetch an upgraded version of a player favourite in The Dog House Multihold™

Played across 5×3 reels, familiar symbols return with different breeds of dogs, bones and collars needing to be landed
across the title’s 20 paylines to award a win. Three scatter symbols award seven free spins where any multiplier wilds
landed are held throughout play.

During the thrilling free spins round, additional scatter symbols landed are collected with three of these needed to award
a retrigger of up to three spins. This retrigger also grants an extra play screen, onto which all previously held wild
multipliers are transferred. This process can be repeated until there are a total of four individual game boards in play at
one time!
Game Information
14 symbols:

     •   11 Regular symbols
     •   1 Wild
     •   2 Scatter


Screen overview:

•   5x3 – screen size
•   20 win lines, pay left-to-right
•   20 bet multiplier
•   up to 4 additional screens during free game


Scatter (1):

     •   Pays
     •   Appears in the base game only
     •   Appears on reels 1, 3, 5
     •   Triggers Free Game
Game Information
Scatter(2):

•   Doesn’t pay
•   Re-triggers Free Game
•   Appears in the free game only
•   Appears on the last unlocked screen only
•   Appears on all reels
•   Doesn’t appear on level #4


Wild:
•   Doesn’t pay
•   It substitutes for all symbols except Scatters
•   Appears in the base and free game.
•   Appears on reels 2, 3, 4
•   Has multipliers (x2, x3)
•   Wilds are sticky on Free Spins
Game Information
Base Game:

•   Each wild has a multiplier (x2, x3).
•   If a wild is involved in a win line, the win will be multiplied on the multiplier.
•   If more than 1 wild is part of winning combination, their multipliers will be summed up


Free Game:

•   3 FG give 7 Free Spins.
•   Wilds and its multipliers are sticky on the latest unlocked screen.
•   SC symbol are collected during free spins:
      • Each 3 SC level up the free game and award 1-3 additional free spins.
      • Each level up gives +1 screen.
      • The wilds and its multipliers are copied to the same positions from the last screen to the new
         unlocked screen
      • The existing wilds will be kept sticky on all the screens until end of FS
      • The new wilds will be sticky only on the latest screen.
      • It is possible to level up twice on the same spin (level 1 → level 3 or level 2 → level 4):
           • additional free spins will be awarded twice
           • wilds and multipliers will be copied from the initial screen to both unlocked screens
           • new wilds will be sticky only on the one latest screen
