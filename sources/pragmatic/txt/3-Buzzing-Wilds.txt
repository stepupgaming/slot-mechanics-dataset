Game Overview
3 Buzzing Wilds™
Game Description (for Players)
Swarm around a sweet slot with 3 Buzzing Wilds™.

Set in a garden paradise, insects fill this 5x4-reeled title and must form a
matching combination across the game’s 20 paylines to award a win. Wilds
aid players in creating these wins and can trigger various game-altering
effects, such as providing a wild reel or transforming all adjacent symbols into
wilds, increasing the slot’s winning potential!

At least three beehive scatters are needed to unlock the slot’s bonus round,
where players can pick between three alternate features. Here, wilds will
transform and continually provide one of the base game mechanics such as
providing wild reels. This allows players to engage with the slot in their own
way, whilst offering plenty of replayability.
Game Information
Screen size:
    •   4x5 – screen size

    •   20 pay lines

    •   20 bet multiplier

    •   Symbols pay from Left to Right

10 Symbols:
    •   8 Regular symbols

    •   1 Wild symbol pays

    •   1 Scatter symbol ; 3 symbols triggers Free Game
Game Information
 8 Sets of reels:
     •   0-3 – base spin

     •   4-6 – free spins

     •   7 – bonus boost trigger spin


 Purchase:
     •   Before every base spin, the player can choose to buy the Free games feature.

     •   The cost to the player is 100x the bet.

     •   The player will receive 3 scatters.

 Bonus Boost:
     •   Bonus Boost is tied to the Free Spins feature activation.

     •   The player will receive 3, 4 or 5 scatters.
Game Information
Free Game:

   •   When Free Game is triggered, player can choose one from 3 Free Game modes below:

   •   FG1 – “Sticky Random Wild” feature

   •   FG2 – “Sticky Expanding Wild” feature

   •   FG3 – “Sticky Surrounding Wild” feature

   •   Free game features are the same as for base game modes (BG1, BG2, BG3). The
       difference is Wilds become “Sticky”
