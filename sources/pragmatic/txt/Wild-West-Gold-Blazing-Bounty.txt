                                         WILD WITH MULTIPLIERS

                                                           25 - 1500


                    This is the WILD symbol and substitutes for all symbols except for SCATTER.
                               WILD symbol hits with a random multiplier of x2, x3 or x5.

                         of the same winning combination, their multipliers add to each other.
                             The same WILD can be part of multiple winning combinations.


                                                    FREE SPINS
                Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbols to trigger the FREE SPINS feature and win 10 free spins.
If the round is triggered with 3 SCATTER symbols, possible multiplier values for WILD symbols are x2, x3 or x5. If the
   round is triggered with 4 SCATTER symbols, possible multiplier values for WILD symbols are x2, x5 or x10. If the
     round is triggered with 5 SCATTER symbols, possible multiplier values for WILD symbols are x10, x15 or x25.
             During the round any WILD symbol that hits remains on the screen until the end of the round.
                                            Feature cannot be retriggered.
                                      Special reels are in play during the feature.


                                                      MAX WIN
 The maximum win amount is limited to 7,500x. If the total win of a FREE SPINS round reaches 7,500x bet the round
                  immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited.


                                                     ANTE BET
 The player has the option to select the bet multiplier. Depending on the selected bet, the game behaves differently.
                                               The possible values are:
     Bet multiplier 40x - the chance to win the FREE SPINS round naturally is five times higher. More SCATTER
                     symbols are present on the reels. The BUY FREE SPINS feature is disabled.
                                           Bet multiplier 20x - Normal play.


                                               BUY FREE SPINS
                  The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it.
                 Pay 100x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature with 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols
                                   guaranteed to hit randomly on the triggering spin.
             Pay 500x total bet to trigger the SUPER FREE SPINS feature with 5 SCATTER symbols
                                      guaranteed to hit on the triggering spin.




                                                 VOLATILITY

  High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher.

                           Only the highest win is paid per winning combination.
                     When winning with multiple blocks all wins are added to the total win.
                           Free spins and bonus wins are added to the total win.
                                     All wins are multiplied by base bet.
                              All values are expressed as actual wins in coins.
                       Free spins win is awarded to the player after the round completes.
                     Free spins total win in the history contains the whole win of the cycle.

                                  The theoretical RTP of this game is 96.48%
                          The RTP of the game when using the "ANTE BET" is 96.48%
                        The RTP of the game when using "BUY FREE SPINS" is 96.51%
                     The RTP of the game when using "BUY SUPER FREE SPINS" is 96.47%
