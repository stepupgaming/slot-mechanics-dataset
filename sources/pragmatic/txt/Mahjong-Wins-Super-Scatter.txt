                                                   TUMBLE FEATURE
         The TUMBLE FEATURE means that after every spin, winning combinations are paid and all winning
       symbols disappear. The remaining symbols fall to the bottom of the screen and the empty positions are
                                 replaced with new symbols coming from above.
               Tumbling will continue until no more winning combinations appear as a result of a tumble.
      All wins are added to the player's balance after all the tumbles resulted from a base spin have been played.

                                                    GOLD SYMBOLS
  Randomly at any spin, normal paying symbols marked in gold can appear in random positions on reels 2, 3 and 4.
    When winning combinations are formed with gold symbols they will transform into WILD for the next tumble.
                                                TUMBLE MULTIPLIERS
         In the base game a win multiplier that applies to all wins for the current tumble is increasing to 2x, 3x,
          5x after every tumble. From the 4th tumble on, the multiplier remains 5x for all subsequent ones. The
                                       multiplier is reset at the end of all tumbles.

                                                    SUPER SCATTER
                                               This is the SUPER SCATTER symbol.
                                               It can appear on reels 2, 3 and 4.
                                               Combinations of 3 or more of SCATTER and
                                               SUPER SCATTER trigger the FREE SPINS feature.


Combinations of 3 or more SCATTER or SUPER SCATTER, with at least one SUPER SCATTER included, pay as follows:
             1 SUPER SCATTER in combinations of 3 or more SCATTERS of any kind pays 50x total bet.
            2 SUPER SCATTER in combinations of 3 or more SCATTERS of any kind pays 500x total bet.
           3 SUPER SCATTER in combinations of 3 or more SCATTERS of any kind pays 5,000x total bet.
          4 SUPER SCATTER in combinations of 4 or more SCATTERS of any kind pays 100,000x total bet.


                                                  FREE SPINS RULES
         Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbol combinations of any kind to trigger the FREE SPINS feature and win
                    10 free spins plus 2 additional free spins for each SCATTER beyond the third.
                              During the FREE SPINS round, on reel 3, all symbols are gold.
 In the free spins all wins start with a 2x multiplier that applies to all winning combinations. The win multiplier for the
 current tumble is increasing to 4x, 6x and 10x after every tumble. From the 4th tumble on, the multiplier remains 10x
                  for all subsequent ones. The multiplier is reset back to 2x at the end of all tumbles.
Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbols during the round to retrigger the feature and get 10 more free spins plus 2 additional
                                free spins for each SCATTER beyond the third.
                   The SUPER SCATTER symbol does not appear during the FREE SPINS round.
                                   Special reels are in play during the round.

                                                         MAX WIN
   The maximum win amount is limited to 100,000x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a FREE
   SPINS round reaches 100,000x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are
                                                   forfeited.

                                                   BUY FREE SPINS
     The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
      When buying the FREE SPINS round, on the triggering spin 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols can hit randomly.


                                                             VOLATILITY

   High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher.
