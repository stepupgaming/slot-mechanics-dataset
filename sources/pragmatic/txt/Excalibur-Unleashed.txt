GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)



Draw the sword from the stone for legendary wins in Excalibur Unleashed™.

This fantasy-themed slot features an epic crew of animal heroes as its highest-paying symbols, joined by crowns and
magical crystal balls, with players creating matching combinations of these symbols to be awarded a win. Wilds that
land on the first or fifth reel unlocks a wild reel accompanied by a random multiplier to boost win potential into
magnificent proportions!

In the free spins round, wild multiplier reels become sticky and are increased by 1x for each win they are a part of,
making them key to unlocking the slot’s max win.
Game Information
Screen size:

   •   Screen size 3x5
   •   20 win-lines, pays both ways
   •   Bet multiplier is x20



11 Symbols:

   •   10 Regular symbols
   •   1 Wild symbol - doesn’t pay, could appear only on reels 1 and 5
Game Information
Expanding Wild:
   •   In the base game Wilds only appear on reels 1 and 5.
   •   When they land on either reel, the Wild expands, covers the entire reel, and display a random multiplier.
   •   Wins are only multiplied with the value of participating Wild, if both Wilds are part of the win, the multipliers are
       summed up and applied.

Free Spins:
   •   Free Spins is triggered when there are expanded wilds on both reel 1 and 5.
   •   6 free spins are awarded, without retrigger option.
   •   The expanded Wilds stay on reels until the free spins end.
   •   The multipliers of the triggering base game spin get carried over to free spins and serve as initial free spin Wild
       multipliers.
   •   During the spins, the multiplier on each Wild increases by 1 per winning spin where the Wild contributes.
   •   The multipliers get increased after all wins are added to the spin win and apply for the next spin.
   •   Wins are only multiplied with the value of participating Wild. If both Wilds are part of the win, the multipliers are
       summed up and applied.
   •   When both multipliers are increased in one spin +1 spin is added to the free spins
