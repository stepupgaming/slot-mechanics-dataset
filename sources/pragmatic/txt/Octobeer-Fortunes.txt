Game Description
(for Players)
Guten Tag from an Oktoberfest filled with big win potential.

Characteristics and features
- All winning combinations pay from left to right
- All wins are multiplied by bet, only the highest win is
paid per line
- Bets are made in coins per line
- To control bets, user is able to change the value of a
coin in real currency and also increase the number of
coins bet per line
- Balance, bets, wins are all displayed in both coins
and real currency at all times
- There are 11 different symbols in the game.
o 5 high symbols
o 4 low symbols
o 1 Wild symbol that substitutes for all symbol except
                                                            Money symbol. Wild collects Money symbols
                                                            o 1 Money symbol present on all reels
                                                            Money Collection Feature
                                                            - The money symbol, at every spin takes a random
                                                            value from a predefined set
                                                            - The possible values are: 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2.5x, 4x, 5x, 7.5x,
                                                            10x, 20x and 50x total bet
                                                            - Whenever at least one Wild symbol along with
                                                            Money symbols hit, for every wild the total value of
                                                            money symbols on the screen is awarded

Free Spins Feature
- Hit 5 or more money symbols to trigger the free
spins round
- The round starts with 9 Free spins
- Before the round starts, the value of all money
symbols that triggered the round is added together
and collected serving as the starting value for the
progressive feature
- The Free spins round has a progressive feature
- During the round, the values of all Money symbols
that hit are collected and every Wild symbol that hits
during the round awards the currently collected value
- The Free spins round cannot be retriggered
- Special reels are in play during the feature




                                                         Max Win
                                                         - The max win amount is limited to 4500x bet.
                                                         - If the total win of a Free Spins round reaches 4500x
                                                         bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and
                                                         all remaining free spins are forfeited
