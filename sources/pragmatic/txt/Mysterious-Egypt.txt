                                                       FREE SPINS
 10 free spins are awarded. Before the round starts, two special mystery expanding symbols are selected randomly.
During the FREE SPINS, after the payout of regular symbols, the special symbols may expand vertically, one after the
other, to cover all 3 positions on the reel. The special expanding symbols pay according to their paytable, on all reels,
      and even on non-adjacent positions . Expansion is triggered only if enough symbols for a win are present.
         Hitting 3 or more     during the FREE SPINS ROUND awards 10 more free spins with the currently
                   selected special symbols. There is no limit to the number of possible re-triggers.

                                          Special reels are in play during the feature.
             The FREE SPINS ROUND with 10 starting free spins can be instantly triggered from the base
                                game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
