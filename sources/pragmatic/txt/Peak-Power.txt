                  hits on a line that has a multiplier, its win will be multiplied by that multiplier.
             In the base game possible multipliers are: 2x, 5x, 10x, 100x, 200x, 300x, 400x or 500x.


                                                This is the SCATTER symbol.
                                                It appears on all reels.
                                                3 or more SCATTER symbols pays 3x total bet.




                                                   FREE SPINS
                    Hit 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols to trigger 10, 12 or 14 free spins respectively.
   During the FREE SPINS round, all paylines have a minimum multiplier of 5x attached to them. Randomly at any
   free spin, higher random multipliers can appear attached to random paylines. Possible higher multipliers are:
                                      10x, 100x, 200x, 300x, 400x, 500x or 1000x.
                                          The feature cannot be retriggered.
                                      Special reels are in play during the feature.

                                                        MAX WIN
The maximum win amount is limited to 10,000x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a FREE SPINS
  round reaches 10,000x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited


                                               BUY FREE SPINS
                 The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it.
                                     There are two options to buy the FREE SPINS:
    Pay 100x total bet to trigger the STANDARD FREE SPINS feature with minimum 5x multiplier on each payline.
     Pay 300x total bet to trigger the SUPER FREE SPINS feature with minimum 100x multiplier on each payline.




                                                  VOLATILITY

  High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher.

           All symbols pay from left to right on selected paylines. All wins are multiplied by bet per line.
                             Free spins and bonus wins are added to the payline win.
                                 All values are expressed as actual wins in coins.
                                        Only the highest win is paid per line.
                     When winning on multiple paylines, all wins are added to the total win.
                        Free spins win is awarded to the player after the round completes.
                      Free spins total win in the history contains the whole win of the cycle.
