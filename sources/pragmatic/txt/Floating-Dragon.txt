                         The fish paying symbols are also FISH MONEY symbols. At every spin, the fish take

                         Possible values are: 0.2x, 0.5x, 1x, 1.5x, 2x, 2.5x, 5x or 500x total bet.

                                                    FREE SPINS
                        Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbols to trigger the FREE SPINS feature.
                                            5x SCATTER awards 20 free spins
                                            4x SCATTER awards 15 free spins
                                            3x SCATTER awards 10 free spins
                   During the FREE SPINS feature each WILD symbol also collects all the values
                                     from FISH MONEY symbols on the screen.
             All the WILD symbols that hit during the feature are collected until the end of the round.
 Every 4th WILD symbol collected, retriggers the feature, awards 10 more free spins and the multiplier for MONEY
symbol collection increases to 2x for the first retrigger, 3x for the second retrigger and 10x for the third retrigger.
                   The retriggered spins are played after the previous batch of free spins ends.
                                   The multiplier applies to the retriggered spins.
                        After the third retrigger, the feature cannot be retriggerd anymore.
    Randomly, when there are one or more WILD symbols on the screen, at the end of a free spin, fish MONEY
    symbols can appear in random positions on the screen replacing current symbols from that positions.
