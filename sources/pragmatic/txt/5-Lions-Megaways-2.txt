                                                   TUMBLE FEATURE
    The TUMBLE FEATURE means that after every spin, winning combinations are paid and all winning symbols
  disappear. The remaining symbols fall to the bottom of the screen and the empty positions are replaced with new
      symbols coming from above. New symbols are of the same size as the initial spin result, before tumbles.
              Tumbling will continue until no more winning combinations appear as a result of a tumble.
     All wins are added to the player's balance after all the tumbles resulted from a base spin have been played.



                                                    WILD MULTIPLIER
   Whenever at least a WILD symbol hits and it's part of a winning combination, a random multiplier is applied to
    all winning combinations using WILD during base spins and tumbles. In the base game, possible multiplier
                           values are: 1x, 2x, 3x, 5x, 8x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 30x, 40x or 100x.

                                                This is the LEVEL UP COIN symbol.
  All LEVEL UP COIN symbols that hit are collected in a meter. The first time 3 of them are collected, the round goes to
   level 2, 3 additional free spins are awarded and the lowest WILD multiplier is removed from the possible multipliers
  until the end of the round. The second time 3 of them are collected, the round goes to level 3, 3 additional free spins
    are awarded and the next lowest WILD multiplier is also removed from the possible multipliers until the end of the
   round. The third time 3 of them are collected, the round goes to level 4, 3 additional free spins are awarded and the
     next lowest WILD multiplier is also removed from the possible multipliers until the end of the round. After level 4,
               every time 3 more LEVEL UP COIN symbols are collected, 3 additional free spins are awarded.
      If the round was triggered with only SUPER SCATTER symbols, no regular SCATTER symbols in the combination,
                       minimum 3, the triggered FREE SPINS round will be a MAX FREE SPINS feature.
  If the round is a MAX FREE SPINS feature, there are no options to choose from before the round starts and instead the
round starts with 35 free spins and 100x WILD multiplier for the round. There is no LEVEL UP mechanic during this feature
                        and for every 3 LEVEL UP COINS collected, 3 additional free spins are awarded.
                                   Special reels are in play during the FREE SPINS features.




                                                       MAX WIN
    The maximum win amount is limited to 8,000x. If the total win of a round reaches 8,000x bet the round immediately
                         ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited.


                                                      ANTE BET
   The player has the option to select the bet multiplier. Depending on the selected bet, the game behaves differently.
                                                 The possible values are:
              Bet multiplier 50x - the chance to win the FREE SPINS round naturally is seven times higher.
                   The chance to hit tumbles is increased. The BUY FREE SPINS feature is disabled.
                                             Bet multiplier 20x - Normal play.



                                                  BUY FREE SPINS
                 The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it.
                BUY FREE SPINS - Pay 100x total bet to trigger the regular FREE SPINS feature with
                   minimum 3 regular SCATTER symbols guaranteed to hit on the triggering spin.
      BUY SUPER - Pay 150x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature with at least one SUPER SCATTER symbol
              along with enough regular SCATTER symbols to trigger the SUPER FREE SPINS feature.
               BUY MYSTERY - Pay 500x total bet to trigger randomly a regular FREE SPINS feature, a
                             SUPER FREE SPINS feature or a MAX FREE SPINS feature.




                                                    VOLATILITY

    High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher.
