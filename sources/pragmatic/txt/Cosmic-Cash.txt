                                         COSMIC RESPINS FEATURE
                        Hit 6 or more MONEY symbols to trigger the COSMIC RESPINS feature.
When the feature starts, the base game reels are replaced with special reels containing only MONEY symbols and
          blanks. During the round, only MONEY symbols or blanks can stop on any position on the grid.
            At the start of the round, the MONEY symbols hit in the base game will move to the end of the
                      screen, to the right in landscape mode and to the bottom in portrait mode.
On the left side of the screen in landscape mode and top of the screen in portrait mode, next to each reel, there is a
              UFO with different awards on it that can be won through the feature. Possible awards are:

                            - Direct money award - Randomly between 10x and 250x total bet.

- Free spins - A random number of free spins from 10 to 100. Winning this award will trigger the FREE SPINS round
                          with the won number of spins after the current feature ends.

          - Multiplier - A multiplier from 2x to 50x that will apply to all MONEY symbols under the awarded
                                              UFO at the end of the feature.
         - GRAND - A direct MONEY award of 2,500x total bet that will be collected at the end of the feature.
          - MAJOR - A direct MONEY award of 250x total bet that will be collected at the end of the feature.

          - MINOR - A direct MONEY award of 50x total bet that will be collected at the end of the feature.

            - MINI - A direct MONEY award of 20x total bet that will be collected at the end of the feature.

              The round starts with 3 respins. All symbols that hit after each respin remain on the screen until
                 the end of the round. Whenever any symbol hits, the number of respins is reset back to 3.
After stopping, hit symbols move towards the end of the screen, and the UFO next to it opens one more empty spot,
 in it's direction extending the number of possible positions on the grid, up to a maximum of 2 spots on each reel.
       If all spots on one of the reels gets filled by MONEY symbols, the award of the UFO next to it is unlocked.
                The round ends when there are no more respins remaining or all the positions on the grid,
                                             including extensions are fully filled.
                     At the end of the round all amounts from all MONEY symbols along with unlocked
                                          UFO awards are added together and paid.


                                                      FREE SPINS
         Hit 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols to trigger 10, 20 or 50 free spins respectively. The FREE SPINS round
                         can also be won in the COSMIC RESPINS feature from an UFO award.
 Hit 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols during the round to retrigger the feature and get 10, 20 or 50 additional free spins.
        Special reels are in play during the feature that have more stacked WILD symbols on reels 2, 3 and 4.

  The maximum win amount is limited to 2,650x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a round
    reaches 2,650x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited.

                                            BUY COSMIC RESPINS
The COSMIC RESPINS feature can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
    When buying the COSMIC RESPINS feature the round will start with 6 MONEY symbols with random value.
