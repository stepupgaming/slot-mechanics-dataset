Game Overview
Diamond Cascade™
Game Description (for Players)
Precious gems and shining chances to win await with Diamond
Cascade.
Match at least five jewels with the same background colour in a
cluster and watch as the symbols explode and trigger a cascade. For
every 20 symbols collected, a 1x multiplier is added to the reels until
no more clusters form.
Hitting three or more diamond scatter symbols triggers the free spins
feature, where the Colour Tumble multiplier also applies for brilliant
win potential.

Game Information
Free Game:
   •   Collect 3 or more than 3 Scatter will trigger Free Game.

   •   Scatter pays is there for the initial FG triggered. For 3,4,5 or more than 5 scatters 2x, 5x and

       15x is paid to player respectively.

   •   Retrigger is there. When 3,4, 5 or more than 5 scatters appears then 5, 10, 15 spins are

       awarded respectively.



Tumbling:
   •   The Tumble Mechanics starts only when there is a line pays win on the initial reel window

       both in BG as well as in FG spins.

   •   There are 4 colors, each paying symbol have overlay color.

   •   C-Tumble can only start if there is any line win on first screen.
