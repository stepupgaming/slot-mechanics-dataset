                                                FREE SPINS RULES
                                       This is the SCATTER symbol. It appears on all reels.
                                       Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbols anywhere on the
                                       screen to trigger the FREE SPINS round.
                          3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols award 10, 15 or 20 free spins respectively.
       The FREE SPINS round has a progressive feature. All gorilla and golden versions that hit, are collected.
                                    Every 40 gorilla heads, the level increases as follows:
             The round starts with level 1. In level 1, golden gorilla symbols can hit as x1, x2 or x3 randomly.
In level 2, GOLDEN GORILLA symbols can hit only as x2 or x3 randomly. Achieving level 2 awards 3 more free spins.
         In level 3, GOLDEN GORILLA symbols can hit only as x3. Achieving level 3 awards 3 more free spins.
      After level 3 is achieved, all remaining free spins and subsequent possible retriggers are played in level 3.
      Hit 2, 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols during the round to retrigger the feature and get 5, 10, 15 or 20 free spins
                                                         respectively.
                                       Special reels are in play during the feature.


                                                      MAX WIN
     The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet. If the total win of a round reaches 5,000x bet the round
                  immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.
