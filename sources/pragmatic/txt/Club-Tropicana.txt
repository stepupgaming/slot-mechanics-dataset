                                                  MONEY SYMBOL
                                This is the MONEY symbol.
                                At every spin the MONEY symbol, takes a random money value
                                from the following: 2x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x or 4000x total bet.


                                                      FREE SPINS
         Hit 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols anywhere on the screen to win 10, 15 or 20 free spins respectively.
Whenever only 2 SCATTER symbols hit in the base game there is a chance that randomly a beer glass will appear and
                              bring a third SCATTER into the screen to win the feature.
 During the FREE SPINS feature each WILD symbol also collects all the values from MONEY symbols on the screen.
               All the WILD symbols that hit during the feature are collected until the end of the round.
 Every 4th WILD symbol collected, retriggers the feature, awards 10 more free spins and the multiplier for MONEY
 symbol collection increases to 2x for the first retrigger, 3x for the second retrigger and 10x for the third retrigger.
                    The retriggered spins are played after the previous batch of free spins ends.
                                    The multiplier applies to the retriggered spins.
                         After the third retrigger, the feature cannot be retriggerd anymore.
       Randomly, when there are only 1 or 2 WILD symbols on the screen, at the end of a free spin, MONEY
                                       symbols can appear in random positions.
    Randomly, 1 or 2 beers can appear at the end of a spin and push the reels up to reveal WILD and MONEY
                                                         symbols.
                                         Special reels are in play during the feature.




                                                        MAX WIN
          The maximum win amount is limited to 4,000x bet. If the total win of a round reaches 4,000x bet
               the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.



                                                       ANTE BET
         The player has the option to increase the bet multiplier to 18x bet per line. When playing with ante
                             bet active, the chance to hit SCATTER symbols increases.
