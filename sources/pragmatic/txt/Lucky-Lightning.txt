                                                MONEY SYMBOLS


                         These are MONEY symbols, they appear on reels 3, 4, 5 and 6.
  At each spin, MONEY symbols take a random value from a pre-defined set or a SILVER, GOLD or PLATINUM fixed
                                                jackpot prize.
                              WILD RESPIN AND COLLECTION FEATURE
Whenever a WILD symbol hits on reel 1 along with a WILD symbol on reel 2, the game has a random chance to extend
      the screen to 3x6, 3x3x4x4x4 or 3x3x4x4x4x4 for the current spin. When available, reel 6 contains only
                                                    MONEY symbols.
   After winning combinations are paid, all MONEY symbols present on the screen are locked in place and reels
                                        3, 4 and 5 respin to award new symbols.
       After respin, the values of all MONEY symbols that are adjacent to the WILD symbols on reels 1 and 2,
                    forming a left-to-right payway, are collected and the total amount is awarded.
                                     The possible values for MONEY symbols are:
       Direct money award of: 1x, 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 8x, 10x, 20x, 40x, 50x, 100x, 200x, 400x or 500x total bet.
     Jackpot values are: SILVER, GOLD and PLATINUM. SILVER jackpot value is 50x total bet. GOLD jackpot
                         value is 250x total bet. PLATINUM jackpot value is 10,000x total bet.
                                                     COVERALL
 Whenever a WILD symbol hits on reel 1 along with a WILD symbol on reel 2 and reels 3, 4, 5 and 6 (if available) are
 covered with MONEY symbols at the end of the spin, the total value of MONEY symbols on the screen is paid once,
                                             then paid once again.
                                                     FREE SPINS
                              Hit 3 or more BONUS symbols to trigger the free spins feature.
                                     5 BONUS symbols award 50 free spins.
                                     4 BONUS symbols award 15 free spins.
                                     3 BONUS symbols award 10 free spins.
                        The FREE SPINS feature always starts with a grid of 3x6 symbols.
The WILD RESPIN AND COLLECTION FEATURE is available during the free spins round and when it happens, there is
                a random chance for the screen to increase to 3x3x4x4x4x4 for the current free spin.
Hit 3 or more BONUS symbols during the round, to retrigger the feature. A number of additional free spins is awarded
                       based on the number of BONUS symbols, same as in the base game.
                                   Special reels are in play during the feature.
                                                      MAX WIN
The maximum win amount is limited to 10,100x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a FREE SPINS
 ROUND reaches 10,100x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited

The FREE SPINS ROUND can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
  Bought free spins rounds, are always played on a 3x3x4x4x4x4 grid, for the whole duration of the round.

                  High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big
                                       wins in a short time span is higher
