                                              MONEY SYMBOL
                       These are the MONEY symbols. At every spin, the MONEY symbols take a
                       random money value which can be won during the FREE SPINS feature.
                       Possible values are: 2x, 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 100x, 200x, 500x
                       1666x, 2500x or 5000x total bet.


                                                  FREE SPINS
    Hit 3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols to trigger the FREE SPINS feature and win 10, 15 or 20 free spins respectively.
         In the base game whenever 2 SCATTER symbols hit without a third, there is a chance for another one
                                 to be brought onto the screen by a random feature:
 - Randomly, if the SCATTERS on the screen can move down one position without leaving the reel area, a respin is
 triggered where the reels with SCATTERS move one position down and the reels without SCATTERS respin.
 - Also randomly, one more SCATTER can appear on the screen in a random position, after the initial spin result.
      Before the round starts, pick a card from 12 possible cards to reveal the type of FREE SPINS round won.
If a BONUS card is revealed, a regular FREE SPINS round is triggered. If a SECRETS OF THE GOLDEN LAKE card is
                            revealed, the GOLDEN LAKE FREE SPINS feature is triggered.
               During the regular FREE SPINS feature each FISHERMAN WILD symbol also collects all
                                   the values from MONEY symbols on the screen.
        All the FISHERMAN WILD symbols that hit during the feature are collected until the end of the round.
Every 4th FISHERMAN WILD symbol collected retriggers the feature, awards 10 more free spins and the multiplier for
   MONEY symbol collection increases to 2x for the second level, 3x for the third level and 10x for the fourth level.
                          After the fourth level, the feature cannot be retriggered anymore.




         During the GOLDEN LAKE FREE SPINS feature normal symbols fade away and only an empty grid
              remains. The normal reels are replaced with special reels containing only MONEY symbols,
                                         FISHERMAN WILD symbols and blanks.
                                           The round starts with 10 free spins.
             At every spin, all winlines or winning combinations with MONEY symbols are paid and every
              FISHERMAN WILD symbol that hits collects the value of all MONEY symbols on the screen.
        All the FISHERMAN WILD symbols that hit during the feature are collected until the end of the round.
Every 4th FISHERMAN WILD symbol collected retriggers the feature, awards 10 more free spins and the multiplier for
  MONEY symbol collection increases to 2x for the second level, 3x for the third level and 10x for the fourth level.
                           After the fourth level, the feature cannot be retriggered anymore.
                     During any type of FREE SPINS, the following random features can happen:
  Randomly, when there are FISHERMAN WILD symbols on the screen but no MONEY symbols, at the end of a free
                                 spin, MONEY symbols can appear in random positions.
  Randomly, when there are MONEY symbols on the screen but no FISHERMAN WILD symbols, at the end of a free
       spin, a hook will appear pulling a random reel up to bring FISHERMAN WILD symbols onto the screen.
    Randomly, when there are FISHERMAN WILD symbols on the screen but no MONEY symbols, at the end of a
         free spin, a curtains animation can appear and change all the symbols from the screen, except for
                                      FISHERMAN WILD symbols to something else.
                                        Special reels are in play during the feature.
                                                      MAX WIN
 The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet. If the total win of a FREE SPINS ROUND reaches 5,000x the round
                  immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited

                                                      ANTE BET
The player has the option to select the bet multiplier. Depending on the selected bet, the game behaves differently. The
                                                    possible values are:
 Bet multiplier 15x - the chance to win the FREE SPINS round naturally is higher. More SCATTER symbols are present
                                  on the reels. The BUY FREE SPINS feature is disabled.
                                              Bet multiplier 10x - Normal play.
    When playing with ante bet on, there is a chance for 3 SCATTERS to be added in random positions on the screen
at the end of a spin or respin resulted from 2 SCATTERS hitting without a third. These SCATTERS win the FREE SPINS
                                     feature but the spin result does not pay for lines.

                                                 BUY FREE SPINS
                  The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it.
                                     There are two options to buy the FREE SPINS:
 Pay 100x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature. When buying the FREE SPINS feature, before the round starts,
       choose a card from 12 possible cards to get the regular FREE SPINS or the GOLDEN LAKE FREE SPINS.
 Pay 270x total bet to trigger the GOLDEN LAKE FREE SPINS feature. When buying this feature, there is no option to
            pick a card before the round starts, and the GOLDEN LAKE FREE SPINS is triggered directly.

                                                  VOLATILITY

   High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher
