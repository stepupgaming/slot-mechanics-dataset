                                              TUMBLE FEATURE
   The TUMBLE FEATURE means that after every spin, winning combinations are paid and all winning symbols
 disappear. The remaining symbols fall to the bottom of the screen and the empty positions are replaced with new
     symbols coming from above. New symbols are of the same size as the inital spin result, before tumbles.
            Tumbling will continue until no more winning combinations appear as a result of a tumble.
    All wins are added to the player's balance after all the tumbles resulted from a base spin have been played.

                                              HAMMER FEATURE
                                     This is the HAMMER symbol. The HAMMER symbol does not pay.
                                     The HAMMER symbol always takes 2 horizontal positions on the
                                     screen and can appear only on the top row of reels 2, 3, 4 and 5
    Whenever the HAMMER hits fully, as a 2 positions wide symbol, as a result of a normal spin or a tumble, all
     symbols on the reels below it are transformed into WILDS. If as a result of a tumble, the HAMMER symbol
      changes to a new position on the top row, all symbols on the reels below it are transformed into WILDS.
    The HAMMER feature does not trigger if the HAMMER symbol is not fully visible on the top row or does not
                                           change position after a tumble.

                                                    MAX WIN
The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet. If the total win of a FREE SPINS ROUND reaches 5,000x the round
                 immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited

                                                  FREE SPINS
                               This is the SCATTER symbol. It appears on all reels and can
                               hit of any size that is possible on the reel.
                               Hit 4 or more SCATTER symbols anywhere on the screen,
                               forming the word THOR to trigger the FREE SPINS feature.
            4 SCATTER symbols forms the word "THOR" and awards 10 free spins.
            5 SCATTER symbols forms the word "THOR" and one "+4" and awards 14 free spins.
            6 SCATTER symbols forms the word "THOR" and two "+4" and awards 18 free spins.
            7 SCATTER symbols forms the word "THOR" and three "+4" and awards 22 free spins.
            8 SCATTER symbols forms the word "THOR" and four "+4" and awards 26 free spins.
            9 SCATTER symbols forms the word "THOR" and five "+4" and awards 30 free spins.
     Before the round starts, if 10 to 18 free spins were won, you can gamble to win more free spins or collect at
      any time to start the round. Gambling is not possible if 26 or 30 free spins were won from the base game.
   If you gamble, a wheel spins and stops on either a win or lose position. If it stops on a win, the number of free
        spins won increases by 4. If it stops on a lose all free spins are lost and the round ends. It can only be
                                        gambled up to 22 free spins.
                      When gambling from 10 to 14 free spins the chance to win is 62.81%.
                      When gambling from 14 to 18 free spins the chance to win is 70.39%.
                      When gambling from 18 to 22 free spins the chance to win is 75.02%.
The FREE SPINS round starts when you collect the free spins from gamble or enter it directly from the base game.
The round starts with a 1x win multiplier. During the FREE SPINS feature, every tumble increases the multiplier by 1x
 until the end of the round. After every tumble the total win of that tumble is multiplied by the current win multiplier.
                          Hit 3 SCATTER symbols during the round to win 4 more free spins.
                          The HAMMER FEATURE is available during the FREE SPINS round.
                                       Special reels are in play during the feature.
