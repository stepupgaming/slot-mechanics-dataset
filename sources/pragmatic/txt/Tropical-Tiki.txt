                                          WINNING SYMBOLS TUMBLE
    After every spin, all symbols from all winning combinations and all SCATTERS lock on the screen and non
    winning symbols disappear. All remaining symbols fall to the bottom and empty positions are replaced with
                                            new symbols coming from above.
           If more symbols of the same type or SCATTERS are added to existing winning combinations
                            they will also remain on the screen and tumbling will continue.
           Tumbling ends when there are no new symbols added to the existing winning combinations.
                    All wins are added to the players balance after all the tumbles have ended.

                                                      MAX WIN
          The maximum win amount is limited to 3,000x bet. If the total win of a round reaches 3,000x bet
               the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.




                                                BUY FREE SPINS
   The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
               The amount of SCATTER symbols that hit on the triggering spin is always 4 and
                                the triggering spin will not result in tumbles.
