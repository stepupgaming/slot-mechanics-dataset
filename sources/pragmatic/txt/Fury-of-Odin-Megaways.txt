Game Information
Battle with the Norse Gods in Fury of Odin Megaways™

Played across six reels this Megaways title has changing reel sizes with up to 117,649
winning ways. Symbols depict various Norse Gods including Odin, Thor, and Freya
which must form matching combinations to award a win, triggering a tumble
feature with new symbols replacing winning ones. Win potential is increased by wilds
which substitute all symbols except the scatters making it easier for players to strike
it lucky. The Fury icon grants a wild reel to all positions below it and Mystery symbols
can also reveal matching symbols.

Four or more scatters are required to enter the free spins round with each win
awarding an increasing 1x multiplier. This round is also home to the Fury and Mystery
symbol features from the base game which are key to unlocking the title’s huge max
win of 15,000x!
Game Information

Screen size:

    •   Screen size 8x6. Consists of two parts — secondary screen and top tracker.
    •   Secondary screen 7x6 reel sizes are switched randomly each spin (except tumbling) and may
        have length from 2 to 7 for reels 1 and 6, and length from 2 to 6 for reels 2,3,4,5.
    •   Top tracker 1x4 is a single reel placed horizontally in the highest screen row on reels 2,3,4,5.

15 Symbols:

    •   10 Regular symbols
          • 4 high {H1, H2, H3, H4}
          • 6 low {A, K, Q, J, T, N}
    •   Fury is a special symbol. Triggers Fury re-spins.
    •   Wild. Present only in top tracker. Spawned by Fury symbol.
    •   Scatter. Triggers free game.
    •   Mystery is a special symbol which is converted to one of regular symbols (only one regular
    •   symbol for all mysteries on the screen during spins in a particular round).
    •   Overlay symbol.
Game Information
Base Game:

•   Can trigger fury re-spins, free games and Max Megaways.

Re-spins with tumbling:

•   Triggered by any winning combination.
•   All symbols in winning combinations will disappear.
•   Empty spaces are filled as symbols tumble in sequence from above for secondary
    screen and from right for top tracker.
•   The reels are evaluated again for winning symbol combinations
•   Tumbles continue until no winning symbol combinations appear
•   Can be triggered during base game, free game, fury re-spins and max megaways
    modifier.

Max Megaways modifier:
•   Can appear during base game.
•   Replaces screen having no winnings with screen having max reel sizes and a win 10x
    total bet or higher.
Game Information
Free Game:

•   10 free spins are awarded when 4 Scatters appear (these should be shown as O-D-I-
    N.), any additional Scatter gives +2 free spins.
•   • Any scatter landed during free spin gives additional +2 free spins.
•   • Every win is multiplied by tumbling multiplier which is increased by +1 every tumbling,
    this multiplier is kept till all free spins are finished. Multiplier starts from 1.
•   Fury symbol can land during free game. If after all tumblings there is stacked wilds on
    any reel then fury re-spins are started.
•   Fury re-spins do not deduct from the player free spins.
•   Free game tumbling multiplier is also active during re-spins (multiplies tumbling win
    and increases +1 each tumbling).
•   After the re-spin has finished the minimum number of ways guaranteed is kept during
    left free games. If the respin is triggered again, then the minimum ways guaranteed is
    started at next level from the previous one kept.
