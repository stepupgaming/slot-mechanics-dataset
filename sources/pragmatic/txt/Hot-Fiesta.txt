                                                 FREE SPINS RULES
                     Before the round starts, a random number of free spins is awarded from 9 to 27.
          On a grid of 3x3 pinatas each will explode and reveal a number of spins. The numbers revealed
                             by each pinata are added together and a total is awarded.
         During the FREE SPINS round all WILD symbols that hit remain on the screen until the end of the
       round and change to a new random position on every free spin. The STICKY ROAMING WILDS have a
          random multiplier of 2x, 3x and 5x that apply to the total win of all paylines that they are part of.
                          BONUS symbols are not present on the reels during the round.
                                                Feature cannot be retriggered.
                                     Special reels are in play during the free spins round.




                                                        ANTE BET
            The player has the option to increase the bet multiplier to 35x bet per line. When playing with ante
                                bet active, the chance to hit BONUS symbols increases.




                                                           MAX WIN
The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total win of a FREE SPINS
 ROUND reaches 5,000x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free spins are forfeited
