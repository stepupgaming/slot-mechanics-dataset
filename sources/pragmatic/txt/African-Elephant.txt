GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)

Patterns of wilds call the savannah home in African Elephant™.

Played across 5×4 reels, this slot is home to symbols depicting rhinos, leopards, zebra and
other African animals, which are also joined by elephant wilds that substitute all other
symbols in the game except the free spin awarding scatters.

African Elephant™ also has an innovative pattern mechanic. During any spin, patterns form
across the reels marking certain positions, which transform into wilds once the spin is over.
Three scatters are needed to award 10 free spins where the pattern mechanic is activated
more often and boosts the game’s winning potential to majestic new heights.
Game Information
Screen size:
   •   Screen size 4x5.
   •   20 win lines.
   •   Bet multiplier is x20.

Lines pays:
   •   Symbols pay from Left to Right on adjacent reels starting from any reel or from Top to Bottom on adjacent
       rows starting from any row.
   •   Multiple win combinations may pay per each line:
         •   If the line contains two overlapping combinations, both will pay.
         •   If the wild combination is included in some longer combination, both will pay.


11 Symbols:
   •   9 Regular symbols
   •   1 Scatter symbol doesn’t pay
   •   1 Wild symbol pays
Game Information
Free Spins:

   •   Hitting 3, 4, or 5 Bonus symbols either in the base game or the bonus round itself awards 10, 15, or 20 free
       spins, respectively. During the free spins bonus round, a Wild Shape is guaranteed to trigger on every single
       spin.
   •   On every spin, a shape can land fully, partially, or not at all over the symbols.
   •   The game will choose one of the covered symbols at random and transform all symbols into that same
       selected symbol.
