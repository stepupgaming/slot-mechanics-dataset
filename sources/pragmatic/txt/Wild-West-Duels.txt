GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)
It’s high noon… Draw and fire to secure wild reels!

There’s a new sheriff in town in Wild West Duels™, a cowboy-themed slot with multiple bonuses
available to players. The 5×5 reels are filled with cowboy hats, boots and more which must form
matching combinations across the 15-payline grid for players to be awarded a win.

There are three different bonuses packed into this release. In the Lost Relic bonus, players dig for
wilds and multipliers which will be available across 3 subsequent spins.

The Duel bonus has 10 spins available and expanding wild reels with multipliers attached that can
offer varying prizes depending on the outcome of a duel. The Beer free spins have multiplier wilds
which are held across all 10 spins. Prepare for many wild wins in this western style hit.
Game Information
12 symbols:

   •   10 Regular symbols
   •   1 Wild ( pays )
   •   1 Scatter ( doesn’t pay )


Scatter:

   •   Doesn’t pay
   •   Appears only in base game
   •   Triggers free spins
   •   Three types of scatters. Type chosen randomly each spin. Depending on type one of three types
       of free spins may be started.

Screen overview:

   •   5x5 screen size
   •   Pay Direction – left to right, only one highest winner paid per line
   •   Bet multiplier – 20
   •   Lines – 15
Game Information
Base Game:

•   Regular game with 15 win lines.
•   Scatters that appear may be one of three types.
•   Each type of scatter if landed more than three starts the corresponding free spin game of specific
    type. Number of freespins given depends on type.
•   There is a small chance that a duel feature will be triggered

Duel Feature:

•   Lands special wild2s on screen (1 to 5)
•   Wild2s expand to cover the whole reel they are at.
•   Each reel with wild2 will have a randomized multiplier.
•   Several multipliers on win line sum up.
•   Doesn’t trigger if there are 3+ scatters on screen.

Beer Game (Free game type 1):
•   3 to 5 scatters in a base game start free spins.
•   10 free spins regardless of number of scatters.
•   All wilds that land get a multiplier 2x or 3x and become sticky for the whole free spin session.
•   No duel feature and no wild2s.
•   Multiple multipliers sum up.
Game Information
Duel Game (Free spins type 2):

•   3 to 5 scatters in a base game start free spins.
•   10 free spins regardless of number of scatters.
•   Duel feature is guaranteed on every spin.

Lost Relic (Free spins type 3):

•   3 to 5 scatters in a base game start free spins.
•   3 free spins regardless of number of scatters.
•   Starts with a pick&click game.
•   Player has 9 clicks and on every click gets a random award either a multiplier or a number of wilds.
•   The multiplier or number of wilds may be +1/+2/+3 or +5.
•   After 9 clicks the free spins start.
•   Every free spin the accumulated number of pick&click wilds is placed on a screen in random
    positions.
•   Every free spin there is a global win multiplier equal to 1 + number of accumulated multipliers in
    pick&click.
•   No duel feature and no wild2s.
