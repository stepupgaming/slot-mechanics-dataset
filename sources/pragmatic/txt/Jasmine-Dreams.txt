GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)
Rest assured, Jasmine Dreams™ is packed full of wild wins.

Played across 5×3 reels Jasmine Dreams™ has symbols depicting rings, treasure chests, swords
and more, that are also joined by a wild symbol. This wild is large enough to fill an entire reel if you
are lucky enough to land it in the right spot and will make it easier for players to create wins.

Three scatters award six exciting free spins where these large wilds can nudge up or down the
reels, once landed, to award a wild reel. The bonus round also has an overall game multiplier that
increases with each additional wild symbol in view.

With wild reels in play in both the base game and free spins round, this slot is not to be slept on!
Game Information
10 symbols:

      •   8 Regular symbols
      •   1 Wild
      •   1 Scatter


Screen Overview:

•   5x3 – screen size
•   20 win lines, pay left-to-right


Base Game:

      •   Spin regular reels. If there are win lines – pay for them.
      •   Free game is triggered by 3 to 5 scatter symbols.
      •   Free game includes 6 free spins. Scatter combinations are paid in total bets
Game Information
Free Game:

•   Free game is triggered by 3 to 5 scatter symbols and 6 free spins are awarded.
•   Free game consists of spinning regular reels and Moving Wild feature.
•   In free game wilds appear on reels only as stacks with a size of 3 – this is important for Moving Wild
    feature.


Moving Wild Feature:
•   Possible only in free game.
•   If any Wild appears on the reel and doesn't fill up the whole reel, then "Respin" is triggered.
•   At the beginning of the Respin, Wild will shift 1 position toward the direction that can fill up the whole
    reel, other reels spin randomly.
•   Keep in mind that wilds are stacked, this shift will reveal one more wild symbol.
•   If Wilds fill up the whole Reel but Respin is still triggered due to Wilds on other reels that are not fully
    filled, then those Wilds on the full reel will stick on the reel until feature ends.
•   Once reel is fully filled with wilds, Win Multiplier feature is applied, the same way as in regular free
    spin.
•   Respin feature continues until Wilds fill up the whole reel and no additional Wilds appear (also do not
    fill up the whole reel).
