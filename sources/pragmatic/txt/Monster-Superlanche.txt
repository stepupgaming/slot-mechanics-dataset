                                       WINNING SYMBOLS TUMBLE
       After every spin, all symbols from all winning combinations and all SCATTERS lock on the screen and
         non winning symbols disappear. All remaining symbols fall to the bottom and empty positions are
                                   replaced with new symbols coming from above.
 If more symbols of the same type or SCATTERS are added to existing winning combinations they will also remain
                                       on the screen and tumbling will continue.
            Tumbling ends when there are no new symbols added to the existing winning combinations.
                     All wins are added to the players balance after all the tumbles have ended.
At every spin a random multiplier appears on top of each reel. Whenever a reel is filled with winning symbols at the
  end of a tumbling sequence, the multiplier from that reel is applied to the win of all winning combinations on the
           screen, except for SCATTER pays. When more multipliers apply their values add to each other.
         Possible multipliers are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, 7x, 8x, 9x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 30x, 40x, 50x, 75x or 100x.

                                                       FREE SPINS
       Hit 4, 5 or 6 SCATTER symbols anywhere on the screen to trigger 8, 12 or 20 free spins respectively.
During the FREE SPINS round, whenever a multiplier is applied to a win, it's value is added also to a total multiplier
     meter. Whenever a new multiplier is achieved during the round, the total multiplier value is also added to
                                            the multiplier of that spin.
     Hit 3, 4, 5 or 6 SCATTER symbols anywhere on the screen to trigger 3, 8, 12 or 20 free spins respectively.
                                   Special reels are in play during the feature.

                                                            MAX WIN
                         The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet in both base game
                        and free spins. If the total win of a FREE SPINS round reaches 5,000x
                        bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining free
                                                   spins are forfeited.

                                                            ANTE BET
The player has the option to select the bet multiplier. Depending on the selected bet, the game behaves differently.
                                              The possible values are:
    Bet multiplier 25x - the chance to win the free spin round naturally is higher. More SCATTER symbols are
                           present on the reels. The BUY FREE SPINS feature is disabled.
                                          Bet multiplier 20x - Normal play.

                                                   BUY FREE SPINS
   The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
        When buying the FREE SPINS round, on the triggering spin 4, 5 or 6 SCATTERS can hit randomly.
