                                                    MAX WIN
  The maximum win amount is limited to 5,163x bet in both base game and respins. If the total win of a RESPIN
 feature reaches 5,163x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining respins are forfeited.


                                      BUY MONEY RESPIN FEATURE
The MONEY RESPIN feature can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
               When buying the MONEY RESPIN feature the round will start with a random
                  number of MONEY symbols on the spin that starts the bought round.


                                               VOLATILITY

High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher
