GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)

Hearts will race with the release of The Red Queen™.

Familiar literary characters fill this slot, which sees the white rabbit, kooky hatter
and The Red Queen™ make up the symbols of this 5×3 release. These are joined
by a wild which substitutes all other symbols in game except the scatters. There
are numerous ways to increase the wins gained in the base game with stacked
symbols boosting the winning ways, while a queen respin can grant wilds
across the reels and a sixth reel can be unlocked.

Three scatters award five free spins and provide an extra row on top of the
game board becoming a 5×4 slot, this helps unlock winning combinations of
symbols. The features found in the base game are also present in the free spins
round and hold the key to awarding big wins!
Game Information

Screen size:
    •   Screen size 3x5 during base game.
    •   Screen size 3x6 during queen re-spin and free game.
    •   Bet multiplier is x20.
    •   Lines pay from Left-to-Right.

13 Symbols:
    •   10 Regular symbols
    •   1 Wild symbol
    •   1 Mystery symbol
    •   1 Bonus symbol
Game Information
Free Game:
    •   Can be triggered with 3 or 4 scatters (3 on the reels and 4th appears on
        6th reel) during base game or respin.
    •   The player will be presented with the White Rabbit clock, which will spin
        round and reveal the number of Free Spins.
    •   6th reel is active and contains regular symbols.
    •   Free game ends when the spins reach 0. All wins are paid to the player.



Queen respin:

    •   Can be triggered during base game with 2 or 3 scatters on the reels and
        Red Queen symbol on the 6th reel.

    •   If respin was triggered with 3 scatters on reels then after respin the
        multi-wilds are converted back to 3 scatters and Free game is awarded.
