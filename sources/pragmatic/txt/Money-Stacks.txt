                                                    FREE SPINS
                     Hit 3, 4, 5 or 6 SCATTER symbols anywhere on the screen to trigger the feature.
                               Before the round starts, the type of FREE SPINS can be chosen.
                             FREE SPINS WITH RANDOM WILD MULTIPLIERS
  If the round was triggered with 3, 4, 5 or 6 SCATTER symbols 8, 12, 20 or 30 free spins are awarded respectively.
   During the round, Wild symbols carry a multiplier of x2, x3 or x5 randomly. These wilds increase the amount of
    symbols they substitute for with a number equal to the multiplier, thus increasing the amount of ways winning
                                                  combinations are formed.
                Hit 2, 3, 4, 5 or 6 SCATTER symbols during the round to retrigger the feature and win
                                    5, 8, 12, 20 or 30 additional free spins respectively.
                             The MONEY COLLECT feature is unavailable during this round.
                                         Special reels are in play during the feature.
                                                   MONEY SPINS
  If the round was triggered with 3, 4, 5 or 6 SCATTER symbols 2, 3, 5 or 7 spins are awarded respectively. During
                      the round, only MONEY, COLLECT and blank symbols spin and can stop.
                           At least one COLLECT symbol is guaranteed to hit at every spin.
                                           The feature cannot be retriggered.
                                      Special reels are in play during the feature.
                                                    MAX WIN
       The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet. If the total win of a round reaches 5,000x bet the
               round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.


                                                   ANTE BET
The player has the option to select the bet multiplier. Depending on the selected bet, the game behaves differently.
                                              The possible values are:
  Bet multiplier 40x - the chance to win the FREE SPINS round naturally is tripled. More SCATTER symbols are
                           present on the reels. The BUY FREE SPINS feature is disabled.
                                          Bet multiplier 20x - Normal play.


                                                BUY FEATURE
                 The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it.
                        Pay 80x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature with at least 3
                           SCATTER symbols guaranteed to hit on the triggering spin.
                        Pay 120x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature with at least 4
                           SCATTER symbols guaranteed to hit on the triggering spin.
                        Pay 200x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature with at least 5
                           SCATTER symbols guaranteed to hit on the triggering spin.
                       Pay 240x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature with 6 SCATTER
                                 symbols guaranteed to hit on the triggering spin.




                                                 VOLATILITY

 High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher.
