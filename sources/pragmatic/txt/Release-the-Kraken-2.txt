                                              RANDOM FEATURES
       In the base game randomly, at any spin there is a chance that one of the following features will hit:
                                                     WILD RESPIN
           If 4 or more WILD symbols hit, after paying the win the game goes into a free respin mode.
      The WILDS remain on the screen and move to a random position while the rest of the symbols respin.
                            The game pays all winning combinations after each respin.
                         If more WILDS hit after the respin, one more respin is awarded.
                             The feature ends when no more WILDS hit after a respin.
                                          SHIFTING STACKED WILDS
                             This is the SHIFTING STACKED WILD symbol.
                             It appears on reels 2, 3 and 4.
                             The SHIFTING STACKED WILD can hit randomly.
                             When the spin stops the SHIFTING STACKED WILDS are brought fully into
                             the screen to cover all position on their reel. For each position moved a
                             multiplier is increased by 1x for each SHIFTING STACKED WILD.
                             After all are moved fully into the screen, winning combinations
                             are paid, multiplied by the sum of all multipliers.
                                                 GUARANTEED WIN
                     Randomly in a spin that results in no win the Kraken can come into the
                    screen and change the symbols to an outcome of 20x to 60x total bet win.
                    Randomly after a spin 3 BONUS symbols can appear on the screen as the
                            result of an explosion awarding the FREE SPINS feature.

                                              FREE SPINS FEATURE

              Before the round starts the player has the option to play a mini bonus game to win a
                       combination of free spins and multiplier for the subsequent round.

                     The player is presented with 16 boxes each containing one of the combinations.
     At the start of the feature, a random box is selected and removed from the pool but its result is not revealed.
  The player can open this box, reveal the result and play that number of free spins with that multiplier or reject this
offer and instead remove chests and combinations from the pool manually, in groups of 4, 4, 3 and 3 picks and take a
                      new offer based on the average of the remaining chests between each group.
   After every block of picks, a new offer is made that is equal to the average of the remaining combinations of free
spins and multiplier, rounded down. The player can accept and start the feature with the offered number of free spins
                              and multiplier or reject again and do one more block of picks.
 This continues until only one chest remains unpicked. When that happens the player is made an offer to either open
  the first randomly awarded chest or open the remaining one. After this, the round will start with the number of free
                                         spins and multiplier awarded by his choice.
           During the FREE SPINS round, any win is multiplied by the multiplier won in the mini bonus game.
                                     In the FREE SPINS feature 40 paylines are in play.
  Any WILD symbol that hits during the round remains on the screen until the end of the round, moving to a random
    position after each spin. If a GOLDEN WILD symbol hits, it increases the multiplier for the current round by 1x.
                                             The feature cannot be retriggered.
                                         Special reels are in play during the feature.

                                                     MAX WIN
      The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet. If the total win of a round reaches 5,000x bet the round
                   immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.

                                                    ANTE BET
           The player has the option to select the bet multiplier. Depending on the selected bet, the game
                                    behaves differently. The possible values are:
                   Bet multiplier 25x - the chance to win the free spins feature naturally is higher.
                                      The BUY FREE SPINS feature is disabled.
                                            Bet multiplier 20x - Normal play.

                                               BUY FREE SPINS
                 The FREE SPINS round can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it.
                                     There are two options to buy the FREE SPINS:
       Pay 100x total bet to trigger the STANDARD FREE SPINS feature with 3 to 5 BONUS symbols randomly.
            Pay 250x total bet to trigger the VIP FREE SPINS feature with 5 BONUS symbols guaranteed.




                                                 VOLATILITY
