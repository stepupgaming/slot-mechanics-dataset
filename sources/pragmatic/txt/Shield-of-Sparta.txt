GAME OVERVIEW
Game Information
Reach eternal glory in Shield of Sparta™.

Set across a 5×3 grid featuring 20 paylines, Shield of Sparta™ splits its play
between a rugged coastal location in the base game and a battle scene in
the bonus round.

The experience is framed by grand Ancient Greek architecture that
complements the various warrior, helm, sword, ship, and chariot symbols, but
what sets it apart is the two types of Bonus symbols on offer.

Bonus and Magnetic Bonus symbols will appear on all reels, and when two of
either symbol hit, they are locked in place, while the rest of the symbols go
through another respin. Landing three or more Bonus or Magnetic Bonus
symbols triggers the free spins.

Landing three, four, or five of the two types of Bonus symbols awards 10, 15, or
20 free spins, respectively. For the free spins round, the positions where the
Bonus symbols landed are highlighted with a frame. Following this, whenever
a new bonus symbol lands in a marked position, it is upgraded.

Game Information
Free Game:

•   3+ [SCATTER/Magnetic SCATTER] trigger the Free Games with 10,15,20 freespins for 3,4,5
    scatters respectively
•   Scatter positions that triggered freespins become level 1 Hot Spotz
•   During freespins you can land extra scatters
•   If a scatter lands onto a Hot Spot, that spot gets upgraded
•   If a scatter lands on same reel as magnetic Hot Spot, after win evaluation reel gets nudged and
    scatter is pushed to Hot Spot, thus upgrading it
•   Max level for Hot Spot is 3, after that even if you land a scatter on it, it doesn't get upgraded
    Landing 3+ scatters in freespins awards retrigger with same 10,15,20 freespins as in base game
•   During freespins you can land normal line wins
•   In the end of freespins Hot Spotz are opened, each giving multipliers, respective to their level
•   These multipliers are summed up and your total freespin win is multiplied by that sum
