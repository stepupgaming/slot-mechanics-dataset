                                              LION REEL BONUS
                           This symbol is the LION REEL symbol.
                           It appears on reels 3, 4 and 5.
                           Hit 3 LION REEL symbols to trigger the BONUS.
                           When the feature starts, reels 1 and 2 become a single fully expanded reel that
                           contains only direct money awards expressed as multipliers of total bet.
                           The LION REEL spins once and awards a random value.
                           The possible values are: 5x, 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 50x, 75x, 100x, 150x or 200x.
