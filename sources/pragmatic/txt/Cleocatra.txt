                                               RESPIN FEATURE
  In the base game, whenever a full vertical stack of one of the cat symbols hits on reel 1, it can trigger a respin
feature. All cat symbols of the same type and WILD symbols remain on the screen until the end of respins and all
       other positions respin until at least one winning combination with the stacked cat symbol is formed.


                                                   FREE SPINS
            3, 4 or 5 SCATTER symbols hit in the base game award 8, 12 or 16 free spins respectively.
During the FREE SPINS round, all WILD symbols that hit on reels 2, 3, 4 and 5 remain on the screen for the whole
 duration of the round. These STICKY WILD symbols have a random multiplier of x2 or x3 that remains the same
                                           until the end of the round.
   If a full stack of any cat symbol hits on reel 1 during the FREE SPINS round 2 more free spins are awarded.
                                     Special reels are in play during the feature.




                                                     MAX WIN
    The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet during the base game and any feature. If the total win
    reaches 5,000x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.




                                               BUY FREE SPINS
 The FREE SPINS ROUND can be instantly triggered from the base game by buying it for 100x current total bet.
      When buying the FREE SPINS feature 3, 4 or 5 SCATTERS can hit randomly on the triggering spin.
