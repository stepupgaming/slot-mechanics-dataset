                                         following features is awarded:

            COLLECT - The values of all MONEY SYMBOLS currently on the screen are added together and paid.



            EXTRA CREDIT COLLECT - The value from the EXTRA CREDIT COLLECT symbol is added to all MONEY
            SYMBOLS currently present on the screen, then all values are added together and paid.


            MULTIPLIER COLLECT - The value of all MONEY SYMBOLS currently on the screen is multiplied by the value
            of the MULTIPLIER COLLECT SYMBOL, then all values are added together and paid.




            EXPANDING COLLECT - All reels that contain at least one MONEY SYMBOL expand to fill the whole reel with
            MONEY SYMBOLS. The MONEY SYMBOLS added take a new random value, then all values are added
            together and paid.

            RESPIN COLLECT - When the RESPIN COLLECT SYMBOL hits, all MONEY SYMBOLS currently on the screen
            remain in place and all remaining cells respin to reveal a new result. Then the value of all MONEY SYMBOLS
            present on the screen at the end of the respin are added together and paid.




                                          FREE SPINS FEATURE
  Hit 3 BONUS SYMBOLS on reels 2, 3 and 4 to trigger 8 free spins.

  During the feature the value of all MONEY SYMBOLS that hit is added into a bonus pot. After all free spins end the
  value of the bonus pot is returned to the reels in the form of a giant MONEY SYMBOL on reels 1 - 4, and one final
  respin is triggered. If COLLECT is hit on reel 5 the value of the bonus pot is paid.
