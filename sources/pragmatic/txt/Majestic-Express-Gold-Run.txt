         Whenever a COLLECT or SUPER COLLECT symbol hits along with MONEY or TRAIN MONEY symbols on
                   other reels, the values of all MONEY or TRAIN MONEY symbols are collected and paid.
  The value of each TRAIN MONEY symbol is determined through a train animation of its color, with a random number of

 When a SUPER COLLECT symbol hits along with TRAIN MONEY symbols on other reels after the values of the trains are
 determined, a new golden train animation appears with a random number of carts each carrying a 1x multiplier. After the
     animation, the amount of all TRAIN MONEY symbols on the screen are multiplied by the total multiplier and paid.


                                                      FREE SPINS
                  Hit 3 SCATTER symbols to trigger the FREE SPINS feature and win 10 free spins.
             The MONEY and TRAIN MONEY symbol collection feature is also possible during the round.
                                      The feature cannot be retriggered.
                                   Special reels are in play during the feature.


                                                        MAX WIN
     The maximum win amount is limited to 20,000x bet. If the total win of a round reaches 20,000x bet the round
                   immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.
