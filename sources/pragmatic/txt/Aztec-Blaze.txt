GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)

Colossal symbols will have you feeling the burn in Aztec Blaze™!

Played across 6×4 reels Aztec Blaze™’s symbols depict numerous beasts
which must form matching combinations across the title’s 25 paylines to
award a win. Colossal symbols can randomly trigger and change the reel
sizes beginning at 2×2, they grow for each win gained containing the
colossal symbol up to 4×4 at which point additional wins gained with the
colossal symbol gain an increasing multiplier of up to 10x.

The free spins round is triggered for landing two scatters and plays like the
colossal symbol feature, with 5 lives being gained initially and these
reducing each time a spin that doesn’t create a win inclusive of the
colossal symbol occurs. This allows additional opportunities to grow the
colossal symbol and increase its multiplier throughout the free spins round!
Game Information
•   4x6 – screen size, with colossal reels
•   10 symbols:
      • 8 Regular symbols: 4 high and 4 low symbols
      • 1 Wild
      • 1 Scatter
•   Lines description
      • 25-win lines
      • 25/40(ante) bet multiplier
      • Left-to-Right
      • Only one highest winner paid per pay line
•   1 set of reels:
      • 0 - base set
•   Scatter
      • Pays
      • Appears on reels 1 and 6
      • Trigger Free Game
•   Wild
      • Pays
      • It substitutes for all symbols except Scatter.
      • Appears on all reels
•   Max winning in points
      • 50,000c
Respin Feature
•   The winning spin (regular or scatters’ win) triggers the respin.
•   The respins continue until losing spin. The progress resets after a loss.
•   Each win increases the level. The max level is 6, all the following spins
    are played on 6th level.
•   Respins have 6 levels:
     • 1 level – colossal reel with symbols 2x2
     • 2 level – colossal reel 3x3
     • 3 level – colossal reel 4x4, win multiplier – x2
     • 4 level – colossal reel 4x4, win multiplier – x3
     • 5 level – colossal reel 4x4, win multiplier – x5
     • 6 level – colossal reel 4x4, win multiplier – x10
•   Colossal reels appear on middle 4 reels at random positions

Free Game
· 2 Scatters trigger Free Spins and award 5 lives.
· Free Spins play out the same as respins.
· The player losses a life in the case of losing spin.
· After running out of all the lives and the losing spin, the progress will be
reset.
· 2 Scatters during the Free Spins re-fill lives to 5
Ante Bet
•   Before every spin, the player can choose to pay extra credits for the spin.
•   The cost to the player is 40 credits instead of 25.
•   It will give the player an extra life during respins.
•   If the base spin is losing, the extra life is not applied.
•   A life from ante bet goes away first and it can’t be refilled.
•   If the extra life isn’t spent and free spins are triggered, the player will get 6
    lives.
•   FS re-trigger refill lives to 5 only.

Purchase
•   Before every base spin, the player can choose to buy the Free games
    feature.
•   The player will get 5 lives
•   The cost to the player is 60x the bet.

Bonus Boost
•   Bonus Boost is tied to the Free Spins feature activation.

Win limit
•   Game has max win limit for one round – 2000 x TB.
