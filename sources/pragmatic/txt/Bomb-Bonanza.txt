Game Description
(for Players)
There’s gold in those hills!

Pick up your shovel and get digging with the lucky miner as you delve
underground into his goldmine which is home to 5×5 reels and symbols
depicting Pickaxes, Gold Pans, Wild Bombs and more.

Three free spin symbols are key to unlocking six free spins. Here players
will be collecting as much gold as possible to upgrade the wild bombs,
awarding extra free spins and expanding the bombs into different-
sized patterns.

These extra wilds help in winning the treasures hidden within the mine
and unlocking Bomb Bonanza™’s max win of 2,015x!
