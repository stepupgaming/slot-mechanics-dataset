GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)


Swarm around wild wins and harvest sweet rewards in Sticky Bees™

Create matching clusters of sweet fruits, from cherries and plums to strawberries
and watermelons to unlock a win and trigger a cascade of new fruits from the top of
the game board. At every spin, between 12 to 19 positions are randomly marked and
upon a bee symbol landing on one of these circle spots, they will transform all other
circles on the game board into wilds to create additional winning opportunities.

Seven free spins are awarded in the bonus where up to three bees can be triggered
on each spin, upon activating a circle and creating a wild, these will remain active
throughout all subsequent spins allowing for more wild positions to become
available.
Game Information

Screen size:

   •   Screen size 7x7
   •   Bet multiplier x20
   •   Tumbling from top to down


13 Symbols:

   •   10 Regular symbols
   •   1 Wild
   •   1 Scatter: doesn’t pay, triggers Free Spins
   •   1 Super Wild Activates pattern position
Game Information


Tumbling:
   •   Triggered by any winning combination (with 5 or more symbols of the same type in a stack).
   •   All symbols in winning combinations will disappear.
   •   Empty spaces are filled as symbols tumble in sequence from above.
   •   The reels are evaluated again for winning symbol combinations.
   •   Tumbles continue until no winning symbol combinations appear.
   •   Can be triggered during base game and free game.

Screen Pattern:
   •   Screen position can have 3 states: blank, deactivated, and activated.
   •   Blank position acts as usual.
   •   Deactivated position acts as usual but can be activated.
   •   When a position is activated, it becomes sticky wild.
   •   Pattern determines which positions are blank and which can be activated.
   •   Pattern selected randomly on the start of base spin (purchase or bonus boost trigger spin as well) and
       remains for all the base spin tumblings.
Game Information

Screen Pattern :
   •   Pattern selected randomly on the first Free game spin remains the entire FG feature.
   •   Activated position can be selected to be activated again, but that doesn't change anything for it.
   •   Activated position can’t be deactivated and remains sticky wild till the end of base spin or the entire Free
       Game feature.
   •   If scatter on a position that has become activated, or if scatter falls down during tumbling on an activated
       position, or if scatter during free spin lands on the activated position — in all these cases the scatter has been
       collected before converting to the wild symbol.

Super Wild symbol mechanics:
   •   Can land only during base game, purchase or bonus boost trigger spin and their tumblings.
   •   When lands on deactivated or activated position, SWD activates it and additionally 2-6 non-blank positions
       (already activated positions can be selected, and the position of SWD can be selected).
   •   If it lands on a blank position, it acts as usual wild and burns in case it is part of a win.
   •   Can fall down on a non-blank position during tumbling, and then it also provides the activation process.
