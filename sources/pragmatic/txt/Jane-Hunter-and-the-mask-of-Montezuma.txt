GAME OVERVIEW
Game Description (for Players)



Discover the hidden relics of the Aztec tombs in Jane Hunter and the Mask of Montezuma™.

Our female protagonist, Jane Hunter, explores the secrets of Montezuma’s empire to help players uncover hidden gems
that lead to fantastic wins. Players will be immersed in an ancient Aztec city with rich visuals and an atmospheric
soundtrack that brings to life a variety of archaeological essentials, from binoculars and backpacks to pickaxes, which
are present across the slot’s 5×3 reels and 10 paylines.

Three Aztec tomb scatter symbols gives players access to a hidden lair in the bonus round, where 10 free spins are
awarded. During the round, players will be collecting instant cash prizes from each high paying symbol whenever a
collector symbol is present on screen. A progressive meter situated at the top of the game board requires numerous
versions of these symbols to be collected in order to upgrade the instant cash prizes attributed to each and to
unlocking the slot’s epic riches!
Game Information
Screen size:

   •   Screen size 3x5.
   •   10 win-lines, left to right.
   •   Bet multiplier is x10.



12 Symbols:

   •   9 Regular symbols
   •   1 Wild symbol. Wild symbols pay, also act as Collect symbols in free games
   •   1 Scatter. 3 or more scatters trigger Free Spins4 Wild symbol
   •   1 Bonus symbol, it triggers Bonus game
Game Information
Base Game:

   Scatter

   •   2 scatters pay, 3 or more scatters don’t pay
   •   3 Scatters trigger 10, 4 trigger 15 and 5 trigger 20 free spins.
   •   Scatters can appear on any reel

   Wild

   •   Wild symbols pay
   •   Wild is not naturally present on reels
   •   Each spin 0 to 10 Wilds are selected to land displayed over existing stop positions
   •   Wild substitutes for all line pay

   Bonus

   •   Bonus symbols doesn’t pay
   •   Bonus can land only on reel 5
   •   Bunus only lands on Base game
   •   Bonus symbol triggers Treasure chest bonus game.
Game Information
Treasure chest bonus game:

   •   The player is given a choice of 5 prizes from which the player can choose 1.
   •   It can be Money award or Free spins.
   •   Money awards: 2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 40 total bet
   •   Free spins: 10, 15, 20 free spins


Free Game:
   Free spins can be retriggered.


   Scatter:
   ◦ 2 scatters pay, 3 or more scatters don’t pay
   ◦ 3 Scatters retrigger 10, 4 retrigger 15 and 5 retrigger 20 free spins.
   ◦ Scatters can appear on any reel
Game Information
Free Game:

Wild
       •   Wild symbols pay
       •   Wild is not naturally present on reels
       •   Each spin 0 to 10 Wilds are selected to land displayed over existing stop positions
       •   Wild substitutes for all line pay
       •   Each Wild acts as Collect symbol


Progressive Symbols
       •   Pay as regular symbols
       •   Also act as Money symbols: have a fixed “x Bet value” attached to them
       •   When Wild acting also as collect symbol lands in the same spin, the current value for each of
           progressive symbol pays on screen which also appears, is collected as a win
       •   If there is a catch and matching symbols on a line, both are paid out
       •   In addition, each progressive symbol has a meter corresponding to it. When a progressive
           symbol land contributes +1 to its meter, a win is not required. Each time the meter is filled for
           a symbol, the x Bet value on that symbol is increased by amount and the meter resets.
