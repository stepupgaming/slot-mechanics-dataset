Bigger Bass Blizzard Christmas Catch™
The lake may be frozen over but wins can still be caught in Bigger Bass Blizzard Christmas Catch™
The slot is played across 5x4 reels which sees iconic returning symbols have a winter makeover as rods,
reels and now snowmobile symbols fill the board. These are joined by scatters with three or more
needed to unlock the free spins round.
Here, instant cash prizes are collected by the fisherman wild with four needing to be collected to
progress to the next round, retriggering the spins and awarding an incrementally increasing multiplier.
Celebrate the winter season in style with Bigger Bass Blizzard Christmas Catch™.
Game Information

Screen size:

    •   4x5 – screen size
    •   12 win lines, pay left-to-right
    •   12/18(ante) bet multiplier

12 Symbols:

    •   9 regular symbols
    •   1 Wild: doesn’t pay
    •   1 Scatter: doesn’t pay, triggers Free Spins
    •   1 Money symbol: pays as regular too

Base Game:

    •   Regular symbols pay left-to-right according to the paytable
    •   Line of 5 fish pays the sum of cash values.
    •   Hook feature: when there is two or more scatters on the screen, there is a chance that a hook will
        drop at the top of the aperture to then move that reel up to award one more scatter
Game Information
Free Game:

•   3 or more Scatters trigger the Free Spins:
      • 3 Scatters give 10 Free Spins
      • 4 Scatters – 15 Free Spins
      • 5 Scatters – 20 Free Spins
•   Fisherman collects the money values from fish
•   On every spin we collect the number of landed fishermen: each 4th fisherman triggers
    the next stage and add +10 free spins when other freespins at the current stage have
    been exhausted.
•   Free Game has 4 stages:
      • stage 1 – fisherman win multiplier is x1
      • stage 2 – fisherman win multiplier is x2
      • stage 3 – fisherman win multiplier is x3
      • stage 4 – fisherman win multiplier is x10.
•   The player can only move 1 stage at a time if the right number of fishermen have been
•   collected.
•   The maximum number of collected fishermen – 12. More fishermen won’t affect the
    game flow or add additional free spins.
Game Information
Free Game:

•   Dynamite feature: additional feature only during freespins, is that a spin can present
    one or two fishermen with no fish cash symbols in view. The fishermen / fisherman will
    throw a dynamite, and some fruit symbols will explode to be replaced by fish with cash
    values. The cash fish is then collected in the normal way.

•   Hook feature: A hook will drop from the top of the reel aperture (1 or 2 hooks) and
    essentially “hook” the reel up to show the fish and fishermen that were hiding below the
    reel aperture. The fishermen now collect the fish cash values as normal.


Ante Game:

•   Before every spin, the player can choose to pay extra credits for the spin.
•   This will give the player a higher chance to trigger the Free Games Feature
•   The cost to the player is 18 credits instead of 12.
•   Purchase is unavailable
