                                               FREE SPINS RULES
           Hit 3 or more BONUS symbols to trigger the FREE SPINS FEATURE. Hit 2 or more BONUS
                                   symbols during the feature to retrigger.
           The number of free spins won is based on the number of BONUS symbols hit, as follows
                        In base game:                                  During the feature:
                  6       awards 100 free spins                           6     awards 100 free spins

                  5       awards 25 free spins                            5     awards 25 free spins
                  4       awards 15 free spins                            4     awards 15 free spins

                  3       awards 8 free spins                             3     awards 8 free spins
                                                                          2     awards 5 free spins
          During the FREE SPINS round all WILD symbols have a random multiplier of 2x, 3x or 5x. All
         winning combinations that contain at least one WILD symbol are multiplied by the product of all
                                    WILD multipliers from that combination.
                                 Special reels are in play during FREE SPINS feature.
