                                   MONEY COLLECT FEATURE
                          This is the MONEY SYMBOL and it                    There are five types of COLLECT
                          appears on reels 1, 2, 3 and 4.                  SYMBOLS. They appear only on reel 5.
                          On each spin the MONEY SYMBOL takes
                          a random value from a predefined set.
   If one or more MONEY SYMBOLS hit on the screen and any COLLECT SYMBOL hits on reel 5, one of
                               the following features is awarded:
                     COLLECT - The values of all MONEY SYMBOLS currently on the screen are added together and paid.

                     EXTRA CREDIT COLLECT - The value from the EXTRA CREDIT COLLECT symbol is added to all
                     MONEY SYMBOLS currently present on the screen, then all values are added together and paid.
                     MULTIPLIER COLLECT - The value of all MONEY SYMBOLS currently on the screen is multiplied by
                     the value of the MULTIPLIER COLLECT SYMBOL, then all values are added together and paid.




                     EXPANDING COLLECT - All reels that contain at least one MONEY SYMBOL expand to fill the
                     whole reel with MONEY SYMBOLS. The MONEY SYMBOLS added take a new random value,
                     then all values are added together and paid.
                     RESPIN COLLECT - When the RESPIN COLLECT SYMBOL hits, all MONEY SYMBOLS and COLLECT
                     symbols currently on the screen remain in place and all remaining cells respin to reveal a new result.
                     After the respin, the value of all MONEY SYMBOLS present on the screen are added together and
                     paid.
                                        FREE SPINS FEATURE
                  Hit 3 BONUS symbols on reels 2, 3 and 4 to trigger the free spins feature.
                                       The round starts with 8 free spins.
            MONEY symbols and all COLLECT features are active during the FREE SPINS round.
  The FREE SPINS round has a PROGRESSIVE feature. All the COLLECT symbols, of any type, that hit
                                    during the round are collected in a trail.
 Every 4th COLLECT symbol added to the trail, retriggers the feature, awards 8 more free spins and the
multiplier for MONEY symbols collections increases to x2 for the first retrigger, x3 for the second retrigger
                                            and x5 for third retrigger.
         The retriggered spins are played after the previous batch of free spins end. The multiplier
                                         applies to the retriggered spins.
                     After the third retrigger, the feature cannot be retriggered anymore.
                               Special reels are in play during the FREE SPINS round.
                                                  MAX WIN
     The maximum win amount is limited to 5,000x bet in both base game and free spins. If the total
      win of a FREE SPINS round reaches 5,000x bet the round immediately ends, win is awarded
                             and all remaining free spins are forfeited
