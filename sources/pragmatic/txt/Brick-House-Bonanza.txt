                                                       FREE SPINS
        Hit 6 or more SCATTER symbols to trigger the FREE SPINS round. The round starts with 6 free spins.
   When the round starts the positions of all SCATTERS that triggered the feature are marked with a square made
     of straw. During the round whenever a SCATTER symbol hits its position will be either marked with a straw
   square if is not already marked or the square will be progressively upgraded to wood, then brick. If a SCATTER
     hits in a position that is already marked with brick, then a random position on the screen is upgraded to the
     next marking in order of value. If SCATTERS hit when all the positions on the screen are marked with brick,
      nothing upgrades, and an extra award of 10x total bet is given for each of these SCATTERS. The markings
                                      remain in position until the end of the round.
        At the end of the round, after all free spins have finished, the marked positions transform into houses
      and a wolf comes into the screen, blows the houses away and reveals random money prizes behind each
                                 house. The prizes are higher based on the type of house.
                              Possible values for straw houses are: 0.6x, 1x and 1.5x total bet.
  Possible values for wood houses are: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x, MINI for 12x, 2xMINI for 24x total bet or MINOR for 60x total bet.
         Possible values for brick houses are: 10x, 15x, 20x, 25x, 100x, 250x, MINI for 12x, 2xMINI for 24x total bet,
                                 MINOR for 60x, MAJOR for 500x, GRAND for 5000x total bet.
       Hit 3 or more SCATTER symbols during the round to retrigger the feature and win 1 additional free spin.
                                        Special reels are in play during the round.




                                                  RANDOM AWARDS
                                               This is the WHEEL BONUS symbol.
                                               It can appear on all reels.
                                               This wheel exists only as a graphic representation for
                                               entertainment purposes.
             Hit 3 or more WHEEL BONUS symbols in the base game to trigger a randomly revealed award.
                                        Possible award that can be revealed are:
MEGA BANK FEATURE - When it is revealed, a big 2x2, 3x3, 3x5 or two 2x2 SCATTER symbols can hit on the screen and
   the FREE SPINS feature is awarded, with starting markings based on all positions covered by the big SCATTER.
   WINDMILL FEATURE - When it is revealed, the WHEEL BONUS symbols that hit to trigger the award roll towards
    the right edge of the screen leaving behind marks until they leave the screen. If more WHEEL BONUS symbols
   roll over the same spots, they upgrade the spot to the next mark. After this, the FREE SPINS feature is triggered
                                           with those marked spots in place.
        BRICK HOUSE FEATURE - When it is revealed, the WHEEL BONUS symbols that hit to trigger the award
        transform into brick marks, then the FREE SPINS feature is triggered with those marked spots in place.
      During a FREE SPINS feature triggered this way, all new SCATTER symbols that hit, immediately mark their
                                               position with a brick mark.
          A RANDOM JACKPOT - A random MINI, MINOR, MAJOR or GRAND Jackpot is immediately awarded.
                                                    MAX WIN
 The maximum win amount is limited to 10,000x bet. If the total win of a feature reaches 10,000x bet the round
               immediately ends, win is awarded and all remaining features are forfeited.




                                               BUY FEATURES
      Pay 100x total bet to trigger the FREE SPINS feature with a random number of SCATTER symbols,
                              from 6 to 15, guaranteed to hit on the triggering spin.
    Pay 200x total bet to trigger the WHEEL BONUS feature where one of the MEGA BANK, the WINDMILL,
       the BRICK HOUSE or RANDOM JACKPOT awards are randomly revealed on the triggering spin.




                                                VOLATILITY

High volatility games pay out less often on average but the chance to hit big wins in a short time span is higher.
